THE Theocracy has at all times been violently opposed by the Devil and his agents, visible and invisible. There has been a conflict between the two since the very beginning and always Jehovah has had his faithful servants on the earth who have been unafraid of the enemy and have carried the fight right into his camp. In Australia the religio-political combine made an attempt to hinder the Lord's work on January 17th, 1941 when the Society's various corporations, and Jehovah's witnesses, were declared unlawful in the Commonwealth. The Ambassadors of the Theocracy, however, did not take this lying down. In fact a more powerful witness than ever has been given, especially to the rulers.

Hereinafter follow some of the letters and telegrams that have passed between the Society's attorney in Australasia and various officials of the Commonwealth.

Representatives of the Sydney newspapers first advised the Society's office of the proposed "ban" on Thursday evening January 16th, 1941. Newspapers and radio stations blared forth the news bright and early on Friday morning, but the "ban" had not yet been gazetted and for the time being the authorities were powerless!

Your Excellency,

News has just reached me per medium of the press to the effect that the Menzies Government has declared Jehovah's witnesses an illegal organization. In a country which has ever been a bulwark of liberty this is surely most disquieting and as attorney for the Watch Tower Bible & Tract Society, Brooklyn, New York, I wish to enter a vigorous protest against such arbitrary action.
