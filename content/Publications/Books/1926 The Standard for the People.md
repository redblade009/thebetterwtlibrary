# The Standard for the People

MILLIONS of people on earth honestly and sincerely desire to rally to that standard which will guarantee to them security from harm and bring to them lasting blessings. They are greatly perplexed because not knowing to which standard they; should rally. My purpose is to direct the attention of the people to the divine standard. Knowing and following the truth will lead the people into freedom. I am convinced that the time has come for the people to understand the truth.

There is a common enemy who has for centuries blinded the people to the truth. The evidence at hand proves conclusively that the enemy shall soon be shorn of his power and shall not be permitted to further deceive mankind.

The great Creator made of one blood all the peoples that dwell upon the earth. The real interest of one is the interest of all. When the people come to know this and then learn to walk in the right way, there will be no more wars; and no longer will right be determined by might. The time must come when man will enjoy the full possession of these divine provisions.
