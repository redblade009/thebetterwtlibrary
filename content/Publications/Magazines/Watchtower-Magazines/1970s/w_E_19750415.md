● Do Jehovah's witnesses feel that it is proper to consult a psychiatrist?

Because, despite much research, comparatively little is known of the workings of the human mind. The book _The Mind_ states: 'The causes of \[various mental illnesses] constitute the most important unsettled question of modern psychiatry.
