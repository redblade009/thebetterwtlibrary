# Can Technology Bring World Peace?

## WHY SOME SAY YES

**They believe that technology can promote communication that unites people.** With the help of the Internet, social media, mobile phones, and apps that assist with language translation, people from different countries and cultures can talk to one another instantly. In some cases, technology has been used to host conversations between conflicting groups when it might not be possible or safe to meet in person.

# Economic reforms cannot eliminate human greed

This verse highlights an important truth: The love of money, or greed, can lead to harmful actions. When people are mainly driven by the desire for wealth, they often make decisions that hurt themselves and others.—Ecclesiastes 5:10.

Many conflicts begin with greed. Countries may fight over resources such as water, oil, or land that can bring economic wealth. Businesses may prioritize profits over people. Nations may exploit trade for selfish gain, fueling mistrust and tension. Even powerful countries may use their economic strength to dominate weaker ones, threatening global peace.

Economic reforms cannot eliminate human greed. The Bible explains that true peace will come when people are taught God’s ways and not because of trade deals or economic reforms. (Micah 4:2, 3) Those willing to be taught by God will no longer learn war, and they will make changes to their deep-seated way of thinking. They will change their attitude toward war and violence. They will learn how to be peacemakers instead of learning how to fight.—Romans 12:18.
