# ‘MY FOOD IS TO DO GOD’S WILL’

## What gives you great delight?

Is it some aspect of human relations, such as marriage, raising a family, or nurturing a friendship? Very likely, you find it delightful to share a meal with loved ones. As a servant of Jehovah, though, is it not especially rewarding to do God’s will, study his Word, and preach the good news?

Of course, David was not the only worshipper of Jehovah who took pleasure in serving the true God.
