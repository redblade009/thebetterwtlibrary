I am an Australian pioneer, and work alone, using only a push-bike in my work.

By that means I have covered considerably more than 10,000 miles. My method is to go to a main town on my assignment of territory, rent a room, or stay with friends, and from there work all the territory for 40 or 50 or sometimes for 100 miles around.

The phonograph I carry on my back; the literature, 40 books and 100 booklets, on racks; my towel, soap, shaving kit, a clean collar and a pound of dates are in a leather bag. This equipment usually lasts me ten days, but I keep going until all the literature is placed or until I run out of territory, when I return to my room and load up for another trip. I stay wherever night overtakes me, eat where I can, and in all my travels in the Australian bush I have never yet been without a meal or a bed, though sometimes I have had to keep witnessing until 9:00 or 10:00 at night before someone would be kind enough to give me a shakedown.
