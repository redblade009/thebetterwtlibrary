## Checking Up on a Jesuit

![img-19.jpeg](img-19.jpeg)

The _Catholic Telegraph Register_ had a nice column story entitled 'Jesuit's Work Removes Black Hole of Death'. Also, the story, which bore a San Antonio date line, contained this convincing statement:

The situation was desperate. Father Tranchese set up the Catholic Relief organization, opened food depots throughout the district, fought starvation with bread and other food.
