# HOW VALUABLE IS THE BIBLE?

## Book Extraordinary

The oldest book in the world, the Bible, is still the world's 'best seller'. Its circulation is now reaching toward the three-billion mark. No other book, in whole or in part, has been translated into as many languages—more than 1,125! No other book has traveled all over Africa, reached the heart of Asia, found its way into remote islands of the far-flung Pacific, penetrated the frozen wilderness of Eskimoland, and, besides, is found on the shelves of every scholarly library in the world. No other book has exercised such overpowering influence on the lives, history and culture of whole races of people.

The historical value of the Bible cannot be over-emphasized, for no other book reaches into the past as distantly or gives such a detailed account of ancient happenings. In its opening chapters, written more than 3,460 years ago, it quotes nearly a dozen written documents dating back hundreds, some thousands of years earlier. Whereas there are only fragments, or possibly one or two manuscripts of other ancient history books, the Bible can boast of having more than 14,000 manuscripts in the libraries today, some of which were written before the birth of Christ.

In literary value, scholars give the Bible the highest rating as the world's finest literature. They say it surpasses the writings of Homer, the orations of Cicero, the poetry and lyrics of Pindar, and the philosophy of Plato. The finest specimens of narratives, historical records, legal documents, songs, poetry, proverbs, oratory, sermons, romances, dramas, and other literary forms are found in the Bible. However, surprising as it may seem, this book's greatest worth to us does not lie in its historical or literary merits.

# Proof of Divine Inspiration

The Bible is of the greatest importance to people in this atomic age because it is God's inspired Word of truth. This fact, though not believed by scoffers, can be proved beyond a question of doubt. Archaeology has dug up a host of ancient coins, monuments and clay tablets with inscriptions verifying the Bible account of events. Geology and its findings in the fossil deposits and rock strata have added an abundance of testimony in support of the Biblical record. And many other scientific facts known to man have likewise given credence and veracity to what is written in the Bible. Non-Christian writers of ancient times—men like Tacitus, Celsus, Lucian, Suetonius, Josephus, Pliny the Younger, etc.—confirm many of the historical names, places and events mentioned in the Scriptures. All of which proves that the Bible is not a collection of fictitious folklore and mythical fairy tales, but rather is indisputably true.

But the Bible is more than just the writings of honest men; it is more than just an accurate history of ancient happenings. Much of it is prophecy, a predicting of events of the future. Now, no human imagination can foresee and foretell the future with any degree of accuracy or in any exact detail, but the Bible does. This therefore furnishes the most positive kind of proof that the Bible was written under divine inspiration, that its real Author is Jehovah God, as it is written: 'Prophecy was at no time brought by man's will, but men spoke from God as they were borne along by holy spirit.'—2 Peter 1:21.
