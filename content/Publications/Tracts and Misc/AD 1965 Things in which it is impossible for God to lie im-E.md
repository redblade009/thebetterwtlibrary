## Have you ever asked these questions?

Why should one believe in God when no man has ever seen him?

—This question is answered in chapter one.

What evidence is there that the Bible is the only book truly inspired by God, when there are many other sacred books?

—Chapter two treats this subject.

In concise form, what is the message of the Bible?

—Chapter three presents a delightful synopsis of the Bible.

What is the soul? What happens to us when we die? Will the dead ever live again?

—The Bible's clear answers to these questions are presented in chapters five and eighteen.

What good purpose could God possibly have had in mind in instructing Abraham to sacrifice his only son? Why were the Israelites commanded to sacrifice animals to God? Is the belief that Jesus Christ died for us out-of-date?

—How these sacrifices marvelously reflect God's love for mankind is explained in chapters seven through ten.

Why has God permitted wickedness? When will it end?

—These questions are answered in chapter fifteen in a way that satisfies the mind and heart.

Are events of our time a sign that Christ will soon return?

—Chapters sixteen and seventeen present clear evidence that the happenings of our day are a sign, not that Christ will soon return, but that he has already come, is present for the second time, now as a heavenly king, and that soon the present wicked system of things will be brought to its end by God.

In addition, this fine book discusses such subjects as the founding of the true Christian congregation, what the Law of Moses means to Christians, the earth's future, judgment day, and the way to salvation.
