## Just What You Have Long Wanted

For forty-two years Pastor Russell preached the "Glad Tidings of Great Joy"—a message which must yet be heard by all. Thousands whose hearts were gladdened by his preaching passed the good news on to others. A public demand was thus created for his sermons, until eventually over 2,000 newspapers were led to publish his discourses weekly.

Since the venerable Pastor's demise multitudes have hungered for more of that mental and spiritual food which his discourses supplied. To meet the demand this selection of his sermons is issued in book form.
