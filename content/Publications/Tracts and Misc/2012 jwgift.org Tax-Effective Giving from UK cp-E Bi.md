## Benefits of the Worldwide Work

All of our work is carried out by unpaid volunteers, so all of your contributions will be used for charitable purposes. These include funding the following in Britain and other parts of the world where there is a need:

- The supply of Bibles and Bible-based literature to help people fill their spiritual need
- Missionary, special pioneer and travelling overseer activity
- Kingdom Halls
- Branch operations
- Relief work

## You Can Give Tax Effectively by:

Completing the standing order form and Gift Aid Declaration (see over) for monthly donations and sending it to the branch office. (The order can be changed or cancelled at any time you wish by contacting your bank.)

Making a credit or debit card donation over the phone to the branch office. The telephone number is 020 8371 3359.

Logging on to www.jwgift.org

Sending a cheque payable to "IBSA" to the branch office.

Donating stocks and shares. We can sell them without tax thus increasing their benefit. (Please contact us for further information on this.)

Putting money into a "Worldwide Work" contribution box at one of our assemblies or conventions along with a Gift Aid Declaration. (Putting money into the worldwide work contribution box at your Kingdom Hall is not tax effective.)

Nominating us as the beneficiary of a policy (e.g. life assurance or retirement. Please advise us of the details).

Nominating us as the beneficiary of a will.—See leaflet Making a Bequest Supporting the Worldwide Work of Jehovah's Witnesses.
