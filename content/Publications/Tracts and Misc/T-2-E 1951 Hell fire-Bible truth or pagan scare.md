## HELL-FIRE—BIBLE TRUTH OR PAGAN SCARE?

One finds similar torture doctrines also taught by the ancient Babylonians, Persians and Phoenicians.

Yes, the word 'hell' is in the common English version of the Bible, but the expression 'hell-fire' is not translated from the same original Greek words, which makes a vast difference (_gehenna_ is used, not _hades_).
