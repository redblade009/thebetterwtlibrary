# How Will Militarism Be Forever Destroyed?

## Present Day Events Reviewed in the Light of Divine Prophecy.

_By Hon. J. F. Rutherford._

The question we have for consideration here is uppermost in the minds of a majority of the thinking people of earth. For more than a year millions of men have been upon the battle field engaged in a deadly conflict. Thirty millions of men are there now. Other millions are occupied in the manufacture of ammunition and instruments of destruction, while many more millions are looking on with bated breath and sad heart and asking, Why is all this war necessary! What will be the end of these things? May the world ever hope to see the day when it will be free from Militarism?

It is both appropriate and timely that we discuss these questions in a calm and dispassionate manner, and we hope that this discussion and consideration may result beneficially to all of us.

In July, 1914, the countries of Europe were in an extremely nervous condition. Internal labor and governmental questions were threatening the peace and tranquility of Great Britain; Socialism was menacing Germany, while revolution was fomenting in Russia. But aside from these home troubles another and greater storm was threatening and which was destined to break shortly thereafter.

More than a quarter of a century prior thereto the Imperial Government of Germany began to build a great military and naval power. Other nations assuming that armament is necessary in self defense followed the example of Germany. Year after year new instruments of destruction were devised. Year after year the strong Kingdoms of Europe increased both in armament and trained soldiers. The thoughtful and observing people foresaw that a great international war was not only threatened, but was almost a certainty. The public press for several years had been predicting a great conflict. In the spring time it was announced that the war would come in all probability before autumn. In the autumn it was announced through the public press that surely the war could not be held off until spring.

Some, however, with less wisdom, were reasoning thus: Oh, there can never be another war, because each nation will be afraid to go to war; this great preparation for war is the surest guarantee for peace. When the war began I was in Hamburg, Germany. The people of that beautiful city were quietly pursuing their respective vocations at 3 o'clock in the afternoon. I saw hundreds of Russians, leaving behind their property and German friends, fleeing across the border into Denmark and into Sweden trying to get back to their native land. Train after train bore these unfortunate refugees away. I traveled with many from the border to Stockholm, Sweden. They were not a vengeful people, they desired to harm no one. They did not want the war.

Denmark and Sweden were mobilizing their armies expecting to be drawn into the right. I talked with both soldiers and citizens who were hoping and praying their nations would not have to go to war. I saw many of England's citizens fleeing out of Germany whose stories would make sad the heart of any man. For several weeks I visited many parts of England and Scotland and witnessed many sad affairs. Business men were trying to induce their younger employees to go to the front; placards marked every station or moving vehicle urging young men to enlist; young girls occupied in persuading the young men to go to war; and, worst of all, preachers, claiming to be the representatives of Messiah's Kingdom, urging young men to enlist, marry and beget children, and then go to the front, that there might be other generations to war after the present one is gone. The people of the Colonies of Great Britain have sent thousands of their sons to battle, yet of all the people on the earth that stop to calmly consider the situation none of them really want war.

No sane man wants to be killed, because life is the dearest thing to him. No honest man wants to kill because he realizes that he is taking away that which he is not able to give.

### The Cause.

Who is responsible for this awful war? Some say the Kaiser is the one to bear the blame. Others who are the enemies of the British say that England instigated the war, while still others lay the blame at the door of Russia. All are in some measure to blame. One nation could not fight a battle alone. If a man arms himself and goes forth to look for his adversary he will likely find some occasion to use his weapon. If a nation arms with the intention of engaging in war she is likely to find some occasion to engage therein.

Under the laws of all civilized nations, if two men arm themselves for the purpose of engaging in and do engage in a mutual combat and one dies as the result thereof, the sur-

vivor is guilty of murder. In principle there is no difference between individual combats and national combats, because nations are composed of men. If one is murder the other is murder. If one is justified the other is justified.

Interest in this great war is intensified when we recall that God in the Bible foretold more than 2,500 years ago such an international conflict. The same wisdom foretold that it would begin in the year 1914. It came on schedule time. Long prior thereto the same authority—God in the Bible—pointed out the cause of the war and likewise foretold what will be the result and what shall follow thereafter.

Why have these things not long ago been understood by the people that they might be avoided? There are at least two potent reasons. One, because the prophetic statements of the Bible cannot be properly understood until God's due time, and second, because comparatively few have been diligently seeking to understand. The teachers have been largely overcome by the spirit of the world-desire for fame and riches—and have neglected their duty.

If God long ago set forth in His Word the certainty of this great war and the cause thereof, may we not expect to find in the same authority a foregleam of how Militarism will be eliminated from the earth and what shall follow? If we believe the Bible to be true (and I do, and assume that you, so believe) and if we can find the evidence therein settling these great questions, then I submit that the vital question before the peoples of earth today who love righteousness and hate iniquity, is, Shall we not lay aside our man made creeds and turn to the Bible? What does the Bible say on these subjects?

And I will shake all nations, and THEN THE DESIRE OF ALL NATIONS SHALL COME.'—Hag. 2:6, 7.

Thus will the Lord shake out all the kingdoms and dominions of this earth ecclesiastical and political, that upon the ruins thereof He may establish His kingdom for which Christians have long hoped and prayed. This was long ago foretold in the words of the Prophet, saying:

'And in the days of these kings the God of heaven shall set up a kingdom which shall never be destroyed; and the kingdom shall not be left to other people, but it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand forever.'—Dan. 2:44.

#### Prince of Peace to Reign.

God is working out His great plan in his own orderly way. Soon the great Prince of Peace will have fully established His Kingdom for the blessing of all the families of the earth. God never fails in one of His promises. Remember that He long ago said this of Jesus Christ.

'Unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given, and THE GOVERNMENT SHALL BE UPON HIS SHOULDER, and His name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The Mighty God, The Everlasting Father, THE PRINCE OF PEACE. And of the increase of His Government and PEACE there shall be no end.'—Isa. 9:6, 7.

#### End of Militarism.

When Messiah's Kingdom is established in the earth 'then will the inhabitants learn righteousness.' All learning to do right, selfishness will be supplanted by love. There will be no more need for the building of battleships, submarines and the equipping of great armies, but instead the people will turn their attention to reconstruction and beautifying the earth. Mark the certain proof thereof:

'But in the last days (and we are in those days now, the last of the Gentile reign) it shall come to pass that the Kingdom (mountain means kingdom) of the house of the Lord shall be established on the top of the kingdoms, and it shall be exalted above the hills, and the people shall flow into it. And many nations shall come and say, 'Come, and let us go up to the kingdom of the Lord, and to the house of the God of Jacob, and He will teach us of His way, and we will walk in His paths; for the law shall go forth of Zion (the Christ) and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem. #### Peace and Plenty.

Messiah once established in His Kingdom, peace will know no end and nothing shall be permitted that will do injury to another; for

'Righteousness shall be the girdle of His loins, and faithfulness the girdle of His reins. And 'Nothing shall hurt nor destroy in all that holy kingdom, for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea.'—Isa. 11:5-9.

There will be none then crying for bread, nor weeping because of the desolation of the land; for

'The wilderness and the solitary place shall be glad for them and the desert shall rejoice and blossom as the rose. Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, the ears of the deaf unstopped; then shall the lame man (who was crippled by militarism) leap as a hart and the tongue of the dumb shall sing (because of joy). #### Happiness.

There will be great rejoicing when the people learn there is no more harsh and mailed fist to rule them, and that the oppressor will no more

be permitted. Happiness will reign supreme when they learn that the Lord of righteousness is directing the affairs of society. The Lord puts the words of praise in their mouths and causes them to say

O, worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness; reverence Him all the earth. Say among the nations the Lord reigneth; the world also (as well as the heaven) shall be established that it shall not be moved; He shall judge the people righteously. Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; let the sea roar and the fullness thereof. Let the field be joyful and all that is therein; then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice before the Lord, for He cometh to judge the earth; He shall judge the world with righteousness and the people with His truth.'—Psa. 96:9-13.

#### Life.

Adam by disobedience lost the right to life for himself and all the human race. Jesus died, giving up His human life that the human race might have life (John 10:10, Luke 19:10, 1 Tim. 2:3-6, Acts 3:19-22) His reign will not only destroy militarism and wrong-doing, but will destroy death itself by lifting up the race out of death and restoring them to life; 'For He must reign until He hath put all enemies under His feet, and the last enemy that shall be destroyed is death.'—I Cor. 15:25-28.

'And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain, for the former things are passed away.'—Rev. 21:4-6.

In this last hour of great sorrow upon the earth, let us point the people to the glorious day just beyond the dark night.
