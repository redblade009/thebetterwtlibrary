## IN 750,000 TESTIMONIES WEEK

### 1 in Every 32 Homes to Get the Witness

#### Every Worker to Average 5 Testimonies an Hour Light a Big Inspiration

As outlined in the August Bulletin, if each one does his part during this drive there is not the slightest doubt about attaining the 750,000 testimonies quota. The results of the 150,000-Hour Week are a clear indication that the anointed throughout the country have a keener appreciation of the issue than ever before.

The books Light clarify many features of the great battle now impending and the significance of our activity in the Lord's service in a way that we did not previously appreciate. There may be some who, when they read Light and see the significance of the various campaigns in the past in which they might have had a part but for one reason or another did not, will feel disappointed because they permitted something else to crowd out their privileges of service at that time; and it is unquestionably right that one should feel badly about missing any opportunities of service that the Lord affords. The apostle says that "godly sorrow worketh repentance unto salvation", and one who could have had a part in the Lord's service and did not because of some negligence on his part would not have a proper appreciation of the Lord's organization and his part therein if he did not feel badly about losing these privileges. It is evident that as we approach closer to the great climax, when Jehovah himself will arise to give the great witness, every privilege of service is fraught with greater responsibility and brings correspondingly greater blessings. Therefore it behooves everyone who has been taken into the covenant for the Kingdom to give greater diligence than ever before, seeing to it that he renders unto the Lord the things that are His by diligently watching the time and devoting his faculties to His service. The 750,000 Testimonies Week is just what its name implies; a witness week wherein, by the Lord's grace, it is expected that that many testimonies pertaining to Jehovah and the establishment of his kingdom will be given to the people.

### 117 Adams Street

#### All Records for Bookmaking Broken!

#### Working 11 Hours a Day

During the last week of July and the first week of August the entire factory and office force has been working on Light, putting in eleven hours a day—and, by the way, it is hot in New York. We wish you could see these boys working, and watch the sweat roll off of their brows, backs and arms. They are giving all this energy in the service of the Lord, and they believe the Lord wants their "heart, mind, soul and strength". We feel sure this is pleasing to him. They are the happiest family on earth. We thought you would be interested if we told you what they have been doing during the first week of the extra-time work in the factory, and that it would make your heart glad to know this. During the first week they finished, on an average, 20,240 books and 37,851 booklets each day. In fact, one day they made 21,000 bound books, breaking all previous records. This makes a total of 58,001 pieces of literature completed in eleven hours. As to the work accomplished in this one week, from July 28 to August 2, inclusive, there were produced at 117 Adams Street, 332,750 pieces of literature. They believe that that on which they are working it is "necessary to have done speedily". We hope that during the 750,000 Testimonies Week the friends will place the books as fast as they were made here during these two weeks. We will be in that drive, too.

# MY! OH MY! WHAT A WEEK!

## Think of It—154,450 Hours in 10 Days!

### All Records Smashed to Smithereens Everybody Did Splendidly

Well, you did it, even though many of the friends may have had a little doubt in the back of their heads that it couldn't be done. Nor do we blame them; for 150,000 hours is a lot of time. But the joyful part is that you beat that mark by 4,450 hours! Just look at the table of results if you want to see how it was done.

While we anticipated 8,500 might participate in the class work, even more than this joined the ranks, averaging 9.58 hours each in the field. This was 42 of an hour less per worker than the 10 hours quota, resulting in the class workers coming short 1,723 hours of the 85,000 goal. The 83,277 hours, though, which they did put in is so far in excess of all previous records for class workers that we are exceedingly joyful. Just look at the work done by the pioneers! Honestly, it thrills our hearts to see the way that they entered into this great work in singing forth the praises of Jehovah. You might think that those who are in the work all the time would look at this week as it were nothing out of the ordinary. Not so with the pioneers. They are not the job because they love the Lord, and whenever the opportunity arises to demonstrate to the Devil and his crowd that they are 100 percent on the Lord's side, they respond with all their heart, soul, mind and strength. Instead of only 1,100 participating, 1,140 reported 52,550 hours, bettering the quota by 4,050 hours. This made the average time per colporteur mount up to almost 2 hours better than the 44,000 hours for the week that we hoped they would put in. Isn't that splendid? To make an average life that means a lot of long hours by many of the pioneers to make up the short time of those who were handicapped by sickness, etc. To all the workers in the field who put forth the effort in an unselfish desire to magnify the name of Jehovah, we should like to say to you. God bless you, your labors are not in vain.

The auxiliaries were as anxious to do their part, too. The 750, the number of those who participated, corresponded exactly with the quota figure, but these put in so many more hours that we were really astounded. Their average time was 24.82 hours per worker during the 10 days, with a grand total of 18,614 hours. That's just 2,114 hours better than the quota, and 2.82 hours better for each auxiliary. One auxiliary put in 72 hours during the 10 days. Adding all branches of the work to-

gether, we have 154,450 hours devoted to preaching this Kingdom gospel which Jesus said must be preached as a witness. The Devil may have thought that he could discourage the remnant by his attack in Germany, but the faithful came back with the greatest counter attack, witnessing to more people than ever before in any similar period of time.

As a result of this tremendous effort, the IDSA Week quota of 300,000 books and booklets, which was not made at that time, was surpassed during 150,000-Hour Week by 85,000. Think of it! 335,495 books and booklets! This means not only that the hour quota was far surpassed, but that all previous records for books and booklets placed were smashed to smithereens.

We have been so keenly interested in compiling these figures that when the final totals were obtained we felt like shouting with joy so that all could hear.

150,000-Hour Week is in the past. While we rejoice in its accomplishments, let this but arouse us to make 750,000 Testimonies Week, a corresponding success. With Light for the people we have every reason to believe that it will be, for if any one can read Light without wanting to have a greater part in singing forth the praises of Jehovah than he ever did in the past, there's surely something wrong somewhere.

## September Brings Joyful Labor Days

### 3-Day Canvassing Parties

The month of September is about the most nearly ideal month of the year for our work. In fact, as far as field activity is concerned, September leads every other month. The intense heat is a thing of the past, children have returned to school, and parents are freer to go out during the daytime in the witness work than they are at any other time. The weather also is inviting. Therefore we feel sure that September of this year is going to be as in former years, the best month that we have. Labor Day covers a period of three "off" days, inasmuch as Saturday is half a holiday, and a whole holiday for many. Sunday will likewise be a holiday, and Monday, Labor Day, will be the third. This would prove an ideal time for the final big drive in the zarals.

### Identification Card Never Be Rude to People

In many cases where the friends are stopped by local police officials the police claim that they do this because they are not certain that the friends are born to be representative of the Society. They claim that there is such a wave of lawlessness everywhere that they have to take this precaution and insist on stopping the friends until they have established the fact that they are really what they claim to be.

To overcome this difficulty and avoid the embarrassment that arises out of such circumstances, the Society is pointing a neat identification card signed by Brother Hutterford. This card identifies the worker and states his mission. The cards will be sent to the classes on the basis of the number of Bulletins they are now receiving. Pioneer and auxiliary colporteurs and sharpshooters will receive their card filled out by this office. The director is to fill in the name of each worker in the space provided we have some other competent member of the class do so. The worker will then keep this card, and if he is stopped he is to use it as his means of identification as a representative of the Society.

### COURTESY

Reports come to the Society from time to time that some of the field workers are discourteous to the people. This is much to be regretted. The truth should be presented with courtesy and respect to all, and see to it that no one is personally rebuked or illiterated.

The mere fact that it is one's privilege to tell the people the truth does not warrant one's being discourteous. It injures the field for those who come afterwards, and is not in keeping with the proper course of those who hear the testimony of the kingdom.

Unified in reading the new book, as you will be when you read it, and are anxious to offer these new publications to the people. We have set for ourselves a quota of 12,000 testimonies. It is going to take an earnest and hard effort on our part to make it, but we are going after it. You too, are going to try to make the 750,000 Testimonies Week a big success, aren't you?

Any class within a radius of 500 miles of New York city that desires to have a service leader sent to serve them for these three days, please write to the regional service department. These brethren will lead the friends into the field, and in the evening give them service talks.
