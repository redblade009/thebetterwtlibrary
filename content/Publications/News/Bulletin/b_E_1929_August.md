# NEW BOOK PUTS "LIFE" INTO IBSA WEEK

## Internation'l Network

Inaugurates Campaign

Millions to Be Informed of Broadcast on Aug. 25

It is with great pleasure that we tell you that at least 75 stations are to be linked together on August 25 in this great international network. The message to be given by Brother Rutherford, "Health and Life for the People," will inaugurate the IBSA Week campaign. This will prepare the minds of the people so that they will be in a receptive mood when the workers call with the literature.

### ADVERTISING LIFE

In order to increase the effectiveness of the hook-up, the Society has prepared a new radio flier specially for this occasion. On one side appears the announcement of the hook-up, giving the time, date, subject, and list of stations. On the reverse side is the picture of a man reading Life, and a word or two about the book. The announcement is attractive, simple, and to the point. We are sending a few samples to the classes so that the friends can get a better idea of it. Beneath the announcement of Life is the statement that a world-wide distribution begins August 25. Colporteurs desiring to have a part in this work should send in their orders soon.

We here remind you of the instructions contained in the July 1 Bulletin regarding the distribution of the announcement slips. The workers should begin using them about three weeks before the date set for the hook-up, handing them to those on whom they call, at the conclusion of the canvass. One week before IBSA Week concentrate on the larger cities and towns, giving special attention to those sections that have been set aside for IBSA Week canvassing. The largest number should be circulated during the three days just before the broadcast. In this way the broadcast will be fresh in the minds of the people, and also the new book Life.

For newspaper advertising the Society will be glad to furnish plates at 50c each, or mats at 10c. Before ordering make sure whether the newspaper you intend to use requires the plate or the mat.

Inclosed with this Bulletin the director will find a suggestion for advertising the broadcast on the spare tire of your automobiles. You can follow the design suggested, having some painter in your vicinity do the work for you.

By giving the hook-up wide publicity in the above-mentioned ways it is hoped that a larger number of people will be reached and prepared (Continued on page 2, column 1)

## Three Features of Drive Aug. 25 - Sept. 2

Life, Big Hook-up, and Make Your Plans Early for the Field Work

August is by far the most outstanding month of the year 1929 as far as the Lord's anointed and their commission to be his witnesses are concerned. The new book, Life, is to be released for public distribution during IBSA Week of that month, giving us a combination of the six most attractive and valuable books in the market to offer to the people, the value of which can not be estimated in dollars and cents, because they contain a message of hope, peace and life, without which all else is worthless. IBSA Week starts on August 25, and a great international broadcast on the subject "Life and Health for the People" will introduce the book Life and inaugurate the drive. This broadcast will take place from ten to eleven a. m., Eastern Daylight Saving Time. With these three great contributing factors the August IBSA Week should result in the greatest of all witnesses to date. Brother Rutherford has so arranged the time of the broadcast that practically every worker in the United States, Canada and the Isles of the sea may be able to listen in on the talk before they start out in the work.

### TUNE IN

Wherever possible the classes should arrange to have a good receiving set in their places of meeting, so that they can hear the message while they are assembled for service. The friends should gather at the meeting-place an hour, or at least half an hour, before the broadcast takes place, so that all arrangements, such as territory distribution, transportation, supplies of literature, and any other details pertaining to the day's activities, may be completed before the broadcast commences. As soon as the program is concluded let each one take his place, advancing as a part of the mighty army of Jehovah, singing the honor of his name. In this manner all the Lord's anointed from coast to coast will start as one mighty unit in declaring the purpose of Jehovah. Surely the prospects of having part in this great drive will thrill the heart of each anointed child of God and will induce each one to make a special effort to have part in the opening of the campaign as well as continuing the united effort throughout the nine days of IBSA Week.

We would suggest that on the opening day, particularly, the friends east of the Rocky Mountains take their lunch along with them when

### Six-Book Combination

they assemble for service in the morning, and, immediately following the broadcast, go forth into the field, spending the remainder of the day in the service. This will avoid the necessity of returning home for their meals and will enable each one to make the opening day the big day of the drive.

### THREE BIG DAYS

As a climax to August IBSA Week we have Saturday, Sunday and Monday, August 31 to September 2, during which practically all the friends will be released from their secular employment and thus be enabled to devote the entire time to the witness work. There will, consequently, be more time available for the work than there was during May IBSA Week.

A method that has been found very beneficial in using periods like this has been to have a number of the friends get together on Friday evening or Saturday morning and go to some of the larger towns in the outlying territory and establish a headquarters, and work from that headquarters during the three days. Thus they save time and energy and give a much more effective witness than if they had to drive to the territory every morning.

We would recommend that in setting apart territory for IBSA Week the service committee consider this proposition and lay aside some territory for those who wish to undertake a week-end drive as suggested here. Definite provision should be made to supply sufficient territory for every brother and sister in the class who has all or part of these three days available for service. The responsibility to make these provisions rests primarily upon the service committee in each class. However, if no provisions are made by the committee, it would be the duty of the class to request that such arrangements be taken care of.

### YOUR VACATION

Another matter that should be given consideration during this period is that at this season of the year a number of friends are enabled to take their vacations. Surely nothing can bring greater joy or satisfaction to the Lord's people than denoting a certain part of their vacation period to the work of magnifying the name of Jehovah. The joy that this brings is not to be compared with anything else on earth. ## "Simply Beautiful"

Adds One More Color to Our Attractive Set

You all know that Brother Rutherford has written a new book, Life, because the July 15 Watch Tower announced it. But we just must say something about it. It is a cloth-bound book of 364 pages, is printed in a little larger type than our other books, and contains six tinted pictures illustrating different passages in the book. There are also eight paintings, reproduced in four colors, which add to its attractiveness. As for the cover, it is of beautiful red cloth, upon which "LIFE" is stamped in brilliant gold. The center panel is embossed in a most striking way. But we will not say any more about it, except to repeat that "It's simply beautiful". As for the most important part of the book, the message it contains, it will thrill your heart as you read it, refresh you with its timely truths, and increase your love for the Lord and his cause. This message is not for only the consecrated, however, because everybody wants life. When canvassing, try asking the people, "You want life, don't you?" Of course they must say "Yes". Then offer them the book and show them how life can be obtained. We believe these books will sell like pink lemonade on a hot day at the circus.

"When can we start?" Wait a minute; we're coming to that. The "Author's Edition" has been sent to all organized classes, as announced, and the regular edition will follow, so that every one will be supplied before IBSA Week. On August 25 IBSA Week will be introduced by a nation-wide broadcast, and when advertising this hook-up Life will be advertised at the same time. The new radio flier will prepare territory for Life. Then on August 25 the campaign with this new book begins, and is to be stressed all through IBSA Week. The people want "LIFE", and now we have it for them.

### COLPORTEURS TO ORDER

The colporteurs are going to have it too. Beginning August 1, we can fill orders for colporteurs from all depots, except Oakland, which will have Life on August 5. This will be in ample time for a supply to reach you.

Isn't it great how the Lord provides these good things to give the people from time to time! We know you will agree and say, "It surely is!"

# Colp's Prepare for IBSA Week with "Life"

## Possibility of Surpassing May's High Record

On your marks! Get set! All are awaiting with anticipation the starting signal of the next great attack, which opens with the big radio broadcast.

The colporteurs are urged to hurry in their orders for _Life_, so that after the lecture they will be ready to rush out into the field with this wonderful new book added to their combination.

What are you going to do during this coming IBSA Week, colporteurs? Can you surprise that wonderful record you made in May? Let's see what the possibilities are.

### NINE-DAY DRIVE

This coming IBSA Week you will have _nine_ days instead of only _eight_. With one Saturday, two Sundays, and a holiday, there is an advantage of a holiday over last May. With the additional day you will have the opportunity to talk to more persons than usual. May was a rainy month, whereas during August the country roads are usually good and the weather better. Thus there should be a good chance for improvement in the average number of hours in the work per colporteur. The number enrolled is about the same. In May there were 1,248 regulars and 636 auxiliaries. Now there are 1,253 regulars and 617 auxiliaries. But of those enrolled in May, only 1,049 regulars and 474 auxiliaries sent in their reports for IBSA Week. These figures include the few struggling ones which were received after tabulating the figures for the June _Bulletin_. Therefore there is another opportunity for surpassing May's figures.

And don't forget this GREAT BIG ADVANTAGE. _Life_ will be added to the campaign. Isn't that a wonderful title? When you see the book, with its encouraging message and its beautiful binding, pictures and pages, you'll be so anxious for the 25th to arrive that you'll find it difficult to wait until the starting time for offering it to the people.

Everything points to an even greater IBSA Week for the colporteurs than that record-breaker during May. To accomplish this, however, it will mean a lot of work, for remember what you did in May. In that IBSA Week 1,040 regular colporteurs averaged 32.81 hours in actual canvassing and sold 85,680 books and booklets. Then 474 auxiliaries averaged 10.08 in the actual canvassing and sold 21,940 pieces of literature. That makes a grand total for all the colporteurs of 107,020 for the _eight_ days' work. Can you surprise it? We know that you want to because of your love for the Lord and your desire to have an ever-increasing part in spreading the message of his kingdom. The total number of 42,800 hours given in the service by all the colporteurs thrills us as much as the number of books actually sold; for it is really the effort put forth that is most pleasing to the Lord.

The time is not far off. Begin to make your preparations now, so that you can be in the field as much as possible during these _nine_ days. If you have to move to a new territory, you should be there before August 25.

### Director Appointments

On July 15 we forwarded to the secretary of each organized class the annual request for the names of three brethren from which the Society will choose one to serve as director for 1939. If this matter has not been brought to the attention of the class by the time you receive this _Bulletin_, please ascertain the reason, and if the secretary did not get the above-mentioned letter, advise us immediately and we will forward another.
