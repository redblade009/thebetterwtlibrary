# Jehovah's Witnesses' Praise Period, Nov. 18-26

## The Name of JEHOVAH

### Is a Strong Tower

"in the Day of His Preparation"

The name of Jehovah is a strong tower to those who really use their faculties in praising his name and take advantage of his provision for participating in his service in the manner that he provides.

All of the anointed and Jonadahs who have received Preparation and read it will appreciate as never before how Jehovah's name is a strong tower. The paragraph to the point in this connection, taken from the Preparation book, page 310, reads: "The hour of battle draws night! Jehovah's great Field Marshal is in the van of his army. 'His eyes \[flash] as a flame of fire.' He is fully prepared, ready and eager to lead the assault upon the enemy and to completely vindicate his Father's holy name. His armies in heaven,

including the risen saints and his host of holy angels, follow after him, riding righteous war mounts and marching on to victory. (Rev. 19:11-14) On earth are the remnant in evidence, and these are the only ones really seen by the enemy; and these are singing along the praises of the victorious Warrior whose name is now to be vindicated. Over and above his entire militant organization is the great Jehovah God, the mighty Warrior, who knows no defeat and who is directing the operation of his army."

Let all who have been lifted up far above the sad conditions of this world and who see the incontrovertible proof that the time of deliverance is at hand manifest their appreciation and love by joining in the ranks of this victorious army and go forth with Preparation, which discloses to all Jehovah's provision for the preservation of those who love him in the battle of the great day of God Almighty.

## You Can Have More for Catholic Territory

### Use Escape and Purgatory Booklets in November

A study of September activities will undoubtedly bring joy to everyone who had any part in them. Over two million pieces of literature were placed during the month. # "Preparation" to Be Featured

## Can 75,000 Be Placed in Nine Days?

Will Workers Average Four?
Pioneers, Auxiliaries, More!

### Be Sure You Get "Time, Territory and Books"

November 18-26 is "Jehovah's Witnesses' Praise Period"; and what an occasion for praise? Looking back over the past year and seeing the Lord's leanings in every branch of the work in which we have been privileged to participate is great cause for praise. The last few months have witnessed the most desperate attempt on the part of the enemy to stop the work in this country; but, despite all their efforts, Brother Rutherford has been enabled, by the Lord's grace, to put on, on three consecutive Sundays, three of the largest chain broadcasts we have ever had. The month of September has witnessed the violent distribution of the Kingdom message, by means of the booklets and special Golden Age No. 368, that has ever taken place in any one month, and on top of these was the world-wide campaign with the Escape to the Kingdom booklet, which early reports indicate was a phenomenal success.

These thrilling facts clearly reveal to the anointed the supremacy of our God and the onward victorious march of the Kingdom. And now, as a grand climax of the year, Jehovah has provided us with Preparation, with its panoramic view of the preparation, the battle, and the aftermath; truths vital to the existence of the anointed and to the existence of every creature who hopes to have the Lord's protection in the battle of the great day of God Almighty.

With such evidence of the Lord's favor and blessing, who could withhold praising his name? Praise, however, is not a matter of lip service only. In fact, words are the least effective method of praising the Lord. The apostle tells us that "faith without works is dead". The same may be said of praise. Our thankfulness and gratitude to Jehovah are best expressed in diligent service. During Jehovah's Witnesses' Praise Period we are afforded another opportunity to show forth our love and faith in Jehovah and his kingdom in a very special way. Therefore let us arrange now to set aside every possible moment for field service during that period. The Scriptures indicate that the time is

short. We must work speedily. In Preparation we have the most important message ever committed to the anointed to deliver. The message that book contains must be delivered before Armageddon because it is a message of preparation for Armageddon. Not only is this message for the anointed, but it is also for the honest-hearted people of the world whom we call Jonadahs and for the "prisoner" class. The only way this message is to be delivered to these classes is by going from house to house, knocking at the door and telling them about it. Thus we place within their reach the message of Preparation, which, if taken advantage of, will enable them to meet the great crisis on the Lord's side.

Work city and town territory during this campaign. Leave rural territory until afterwards. The directors should arrange to have back calls made on those who manifested exceptional interest when presented with the Escape booklet. This work should be done in the evening. During the daytime the regular campaign activity should be pushed strenuously. Let everyone have a part in this work. To the Jonadahs who have been blessed with a knowledge of Jehovah's purposes undoubtedly the words of the apostle Paul to the anointed will strongly appeal: "Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort; who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God," and a great many of them will want to express their love and appreciation to Jehovah by having a part in this special praise period. Service directors will therefore make preparations to assist all of these good people into the service; likewise sharpshooters, auxiliaries and pioneers. In fact, the Lord's anointed everywhere should put forth every effort to assist these good people in rendering their heart devotion unto Jehovah. A large number of Jonadahs have been participating in the service campaigns and special witnessing work during the last few months. This leads us to believe that we shall have approximately 20,000 workers in the field during Jehovah's Witnesses' Praise Period.
