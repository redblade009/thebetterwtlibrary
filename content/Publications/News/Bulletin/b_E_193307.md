# BULLETIN for Jehovah's Witnesses

JULY 1, 1933

TESTIMONIES TO DATE 11,511,318

## ALL RECORDS EXCEEDED

### International Thanksgiving Period the Greatest Ever Held

#### World-wide Report Shows 3,920,190 Books and Booklets Placed

6,682,439 Persons Receive Testimony

##### Brother Rutherford Heard by Vast Audience

The figures for The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period, April 8-16, are ready at last, and here they are, a whole page of them. Reports have been received from 76 different countries and all bear out the same conclusion, that this was the most wonderful service period ever enjoyed. Everywhere the brethren responded to the call to service as never before. Appreciating the tremendous importance of the message to be brought to the people, and that they were in a covenant with the Lord to do this, the brethren simply could not stay at home, for it was as a 'fire in their bones' and stirred their zeal for the Lord to a fever pitch.

During the spring Thanksgiving Period of 1932, when an international report of the results was tabulated, we rejoiced greatly as we beheld 38,585 workers in the field delivering 2,447,540 testimonies and placing 340,340 pieces of literature in the hands of 710,638 persons. These workers reported 330,833 hours in the service. That was a mighty effort and a wonderful period. The figures for the fall Thanksgiving Period (Kingdom Testimony Period) of the same year showed a decrease in number of workers to 37,411, total literature 677,122, obtained 400,522, testimonies 2,202,970, but those who participated worked diligently and reported 384,190 hours. With these figures as a basis the quota of 3,000,000 testimonies and 1,000,000 pieces of literature was set at first for The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period of April 8-16. As it became known that more and more countries were going to be able to have the Crisis booklet for the campaign we became more hopeful for bigger results. The various printing plants of the Society rushed the translation of the booklet and its publication in as many languages as possible, and as a result 52 different countries, included in this report, had The Crisis in some language to place with the people.

And then the reports began to come in. In a very short time the million mark, in pieces of literature placed, was passed. The figures climbed steadily toward the two-million mark. Incoming reports kept saying 'It's the best period yet'; 'More workers than ever before, more zeal and joy in the service,

and splendid results in the placement of literature.' But even at that, when the figures showed that 3,920,190 books and booklets had actually been placed and 6,682,439 testimonies given within a short period of nine days, we could hardly believe our eyes. That means that almost 4.7 times as many pieces of literature were placed as during the same period of the preceding year, and about 5.8 times as many as during the Kingdom Testimony Period of October 1-9, 1932.

This amazing result was possible because of the Lord's blessing upon the efforts of his people who stood ready to take advantage of every opportunity. His time had come for the people to be informed concerning the meaning of 'The Crisis', and they were. To Him is due all the honor and glory. Instead of around 38,000 workers, the number participating rose to 58,804, an increase of almost 53 percent, and these averaged better than 12 hours each in the witness work. The number of pioneers, auxiliaries and company workers all increased as each one seemed to appreciate the privilege and responsibility of being in the work and making his report. No doubt many of these new workers are of the Jonadah class, which is making itself more and more manifest. There are still a great many who participate in the Memorial emblems who do not appreciate their true significance, as is evidenced by the 84,170 reported for Memorial, compared with this 58,804. This Memorial report shows a slight increase over the preceding year, at which time 81,778 were reported.

Then, to cap the climax, on the night of April 16, at the close of this glorious period, when all the other workers had returned from the field, Brother Rutherford delivered a stirring lecture on 'The Way of Escape', over a large network of radio stations. During the week millions of radio folders announcing the lecture were distributed in the United States and Canada and every effort was made to see that the people had an opportunity to listen to this important message. Shortly after the lecture thousands of letters and telegrams were received expressing appreciation and requesting a copy of the address.

It was surely a wonderful Thanksgiving Period and the brethren throughout the earth give thanks and praise to Jehovah for these inspiring results accomplished. But for His spirit, His blessings and His

protection, none of this would have been possible. Knowing that you will delight in hearing a word from some of the various countries as to how they got along in this campaign, we give you some excerpts from their reports.

##### AUSTRIA

'We have never before had so much joy to send in our report, which is beyond all expectations. The former service weeks showed good results, but as compared with Thanksgiving Period they are far behind. In the Kingdom Week of last year we placed 22,000 booklets, but now the company workers alone distributed 34,000 booklets; and whereas last year the highest number of company workers was 400, this year 400 brethren have participated in the service. We are grateful to the Lord and rejoicing over this accomplishment.'

##### BELGIUM

'The Thanksgiving Period has passed very well and I was impressed by the way in which all the brethren went joyfully and earnestly to the work, spurred on by the 'Pearl of Jehovah'. During the week express letters and telegrams were received from brethren for more booklets, for they had not anticipated that The Crisis would have a better distribution than the Kingdom booklet.'

##### BRITISH ISLES

'This has been a unique experience. The brethren have never risen to an occasion as to this, nor has there ever been the same spirit manifested and continued through a like period of time. The total number of booklets placed is 220,588. We anticipated we might reach to 200,000, but the number has gone 10 percent in excess of that. The number of Watchtower subscribers who responded to the invitation contained in Brother Rutherford's letter was more than expected. Some of these have now become regular workers. The presence of these new workers undoubtedly affected the regular workers. The grouping of pioneers in parties of from 6 to 18 in the cities, under the care and guidance of a regional service director or member of the Bethel family, was a great success and an inspiration to all the country.'

##### Order Early

In harmony with the notice appearing in the July 1 Watchtower, that the Bethel factory, office and home will be closed from August 21 to September 4, to allow the brethren a change from their steady occupation here, it will be necessary, therefore, for orders for literature by companies, pioneers, auxiliaries and sharpshooters to be in this office by the second week in August to insure their being shipped before the close-down. Do not wait until the last minute to put through your order and then mark it 'rush' or wire in, expecting it to receive preference over the orders of brethren who have complied with this request and put in their orders in time. It would not be just or fair to them to hold up their orders in order to permit some delinquent one to get a supply to emergency due to his own fault. Therefore, let each company, pioneer, auxiliary and sharpshooter take inventory of the stock on hand following Ambassador's Devotional Period and estimate generously the stock you will require to run you until September 30, then forward your order to this office promptly. Do not skimp on your order so that you will have only enough to carry you over. See to it that you order enough to have a reasonable surplus for any emergency that may occur, and on account of the financial crisis the workers could not place any booklets in the hands of the people; but we had at hand about 1,000 copies of the Cedar Point Resolution of 1922 which we placed in the hands of the people.'

# Great Work to Be Done During Next Three Months

## Summer Vacations Mean More Time to Witness

Finish Rural Territory by September 30

### Vindication and Preservation to Be Stressed

The manner in which Jehovah is going before his anointed and literally plowing, breaking and watering the earth with the three stirring radio speeches, first, over a national network of about 60 radio stations, next a nationwide broadcast over 170 stations, and following that over hundreds of portable transcription machines in every book and corner of the country, indicates the importance of this summer's activities. The King of Eternity is riding majestically into action. The position of the anointed now is that of witnesses and heralds of the King. Every highway and byway, every city, town and hamlet must be told of the significance of the time in which we are living. God's remnant are prepared for that purpose. Every one who would be faithful to Jehovah at this time must use his every power to that end. As Brother Rutherford very definitely stated in his speech 'The Way of Escape', the rulers have had their witness and we are now calling upon the people that those who love righteousness may escape and that the issue may be squarely presented to every creature, giving them an opportunity to take their stand on the Lord's side and have his protection or to reject the Lord's provision and suffer the consequences.

To do this properly, this summer must witness the greatest house-to-house campaign ever staged. Every section of the rural territory held by companies should be finished before September 30, and where the portable transcription machine meetings are held this territory should be witnessed to twice: once before the meeting, doing the double work of witnessing and inviting the people you call upon to attend the meeting; and the second time within a week or ten days after the transcription meeting, so that advantage may be taken of the interest developed by the meeting.

The literature to be stressed is the _Vindication_ and _Preservation_ books and the booklets, with our objective being that of leaving at least a _Crisis_ in every home, but be sure always to present the bound books first. This is particularly necessary in rural territory, where much time, energy and money is spent in reaching the people. The responsibility devolves upon each service director and service committee, pioneer and sharpshooter to plan this summer's work so that the greatest possible witness will be given. The service director is particularly charged with the responsibility of seeing that adequate provisions are made for every one in the company to have a part in those campaigns. The KINGDOM is the big thing in the lives of the anointed now. It is not a matter of 10 percent, 20 percent, or 60 percent, but each one must be 100 percent for the Lord and his cause, first, last and all the time.

In many companies there are a great many interested who love the Lord and his cause and who do not claim to be of the anointed, who are termed the 'Jonadah' class. Provisions should be made so that every one of these can avail himself of the opportunity of demonstrating his love for the Lord and

his cause by participating in this activity to the extent of his ability.

During the next three months there will be vacations for those who are employed in secular pursuits and because of this work are unable to devote all of their time to the Lord's kingdom service. What is going to be done with this vacation? Is it going to be used to the praise of Jehovah's name or to greatly sense personal desire along other lines? This is the question each one must face and answer before the Lord. Then there are Labor Day and the previous Saturday and Sunday, during which practically every one is released from all secular employment. How is this time to be used? Will it simply mean it drive to some divisional campaign, work 2½ or 3 hours and come home, or will it mean a real witness for Jehovah? These are serious questions for each one to ask himself or herself; for the issue is put squarely before us by Jesus in these words. 'Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit.' Fruit-bearing is carrying the Kingdom message to the people to the extent of our ability. Therefore, during the Labor Day weekend at least six hours should be devoted to field service by company workers and sharpshooters, with auxiliaries increasing their time proportionately. The divisional leader, in arranging for territory for September 3 and 4 divisional campaign, is to allow at least 400 homes for each car. This does not need to be in one city, of course. The campaign can be extended to include sufficient territory to provide that many homes for each car in order that a thorough witness may be given. See to it, however, that the territory is within the company and sharpshooter assignments of your division. In this way adequate provisions will be made for all to have part in the campaign and also to give a real witness to the praise of Jehovah during this period.

#### Outdoor Meetings During Summer

A special portable transcription machine campaign with the chain speeches will commence Sunday, July 16.
