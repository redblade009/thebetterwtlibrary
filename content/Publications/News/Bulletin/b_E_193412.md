# Every Worker to Share in Special Campaign

## December and January

### 3 Clothbound Books on a 50c Contribution

Use Any of Four: _Harp, Deliverance, Reconciliation, Life_

#### Order Immediately a Good Supply

Jehovah has graciously made provision that another tremendous testimony may be given this fall and winter. The unedited and all others who have taken their stand on Jehovah's side will be clarified with the opportunities that this special witness, which will afford the exuding the name of Jehovah. The months of December and January are to be devoted exclusively to the distribution of three clothbound books on a contribution of 50c. The workers may choose any three of the following four books only: _The Harp of God, Deliverance, Reconciliation, and Life_.

The past few months (in fact, nearly the whole year) have been devoted exclusively to an exceptionally wide distribution of booklets, and during the past two months the _Prosperity-Fusion_ booklet has exceeded anything in this line. The field is now ripe for an extensive distribution of clothbound books. Therefore the call goes forth to everyone to participate in this campaign as outlined below.

Every pioneer, auxiliary, company worker, sharpshooters and Jonadah should see to it that he has a good supply of the above-mentioned books. Carefully plan your territory so that during the two months the greatest possible number of people can be called on and given this extraordinary offer. Ordinarily one clothbound book on a contribution of 25c. but now three on only 50c contributed. A special testimony card is being prepared for this combination. We feel sure Jehovah will bless this witness as he has all effects hitherto put forth by people who are unselfishly devoted to him and his kingdom.

There will be special wrappers supplied for holding three books. These wrappers will have a cut of Brother Rutherford on them and will call attention to the fact that this is an extraordinary offer.

All territory will be new territory for this combination, for in most places _The Harp of God, Deliverance, Reconciliation and Life_ have not been offered to the people for years. Now that the winter season draws on and many people remain at home to read and study, every effort should be made to place these books which set out clearly the

Kingdom as the only hope of the world.

Immediately after December 2, which closes the _Angels_ booklet campaign, start with this one, three books on 50c contribution, and continue up to February 1. During these two months it is expected that several hundred thousand clothbound books will be placed. The Lord has richly blessed the quote arrangement for his people in the past. It is well that each individual plan his work for these two months and strive earnestly to do that which he sets out to do. During the last bound-book campaign, which was four bound books on \$100 contributed, our placements were slightly under one book an hour. Now, with this most attractive combination of three on a 50c contribution, and the fact that there are millions of people mostly disappointed in and fully realizing the fallacy of human schemes to aid them in the present distress, an excellent opportunity is afforded for this offer. The Kingdom is the only message for the people now. It seems reasonable that in a period of three hours of steady witnessing at least one combination per worker should be placed; and if this be so, that would mean an average of one clothbound book per hour. Let each one put forth an extraordinary effort to put in many hours in the field service during December and January; and according to the number of hours in the field, so we may expect will be the placements of bound books. Let's make this the greatest witness for a winter period to the honor of Jehovah's name!

#### Please Report Promptly and Accurately; This Is Important

Every director, pioneer and auxiliary, as well as sharpshooters, is supplied with order blanks. Your stock should be checked over immediately as to the quantity of these four books, _The Harp of God, Deliverance, Reconciliation, and Life_, that you have on hand, and then fill out your order blank and send it to the Society immediately. If your account is not in good condition, accompany your order with a remittance. The Society has on hand a good supply of these four books at 117 Adams St., Brooklyn, as well as at the depots, and orders can be filled immediately.

The companies and sharpshooters, in most instances, have a large stock on hand which they have been storing for a year or so. The companies and sharpshooters should begin immediately to work on this stock and use these four books. In the meantime they can order more, so that they will be on hand if required. In the case of pioneers and auxiliaries who are in company territory, if they do not have a supply on hand they can borrow from the company through the director or stockkeeper and then order a supply from the Brooklyn office, and they can replace the books which they borrowed when they receive their filled order. Anyone who runs out of the storage stock of these books should not stop

this campaign, but should order a supply for December and January for this special combination. It is hoped that it will not be necessary to make any transfers between the companies and pioneers, as this causes a lot of extra work in the office; therefore we suggest that pioneers and auxiliaries order stock, and when they have borrowed from companies, replace the borrowed literature.

So that the brethren might continue to realize their expense allowances in going from place to place, these publications are being given to the companies, sharpshooters, pioneers and auxiliaries at a reduced rate. When you order these books they will be involved to you at the same rate as heretofore. Credit will be placed on your account from your December report card as well as your report for January on these four books. After January all of these books will again be offered on the regular contribution of 25c each. Therefore we cannot ship these books to you at the reduced rates, but a credit will be given at the end of each month.

As shown above, where books are reported the space is divided into two sections. On the top line are the books that are placed in combination of three on 50c contribution. On the lower line are books that were placed singly on the 25c contribution, and all foreign books. Credit will be given to the companies on only the four books that are mentioned on the top line; namely, _The Harp of God, Deliverance, Reconciliation, and Life_. Pioneers will receive a credit of 2c for each book mentioned; auxiliaries, 5c; and companies, 6c. Company workers should receive their books from the stockkeeper for the instead of the usual 20c. It is therefore urged that the directors, sharpshooters, auxiliaries and pioneers carefully check their report cards before sending them in, making them out accurately and reporting promptly. This is very important. No adjustments will be made on accounts except from your monthly report cards.

We look forward to this special campaign with joy and enthusiasm, knowing that the Lord's favor will continue upon his people as they make effort to spread the Kingdom message.

## Brethren to Eng.

## Each Thursday of Testimony

### Thursdays as Well as Saturdays and Sundays Should Be Regular Witness Days

Most of the activity among the companies is now carried on during Saturdays and Sundays. There is some work done by a few during the middle of the week, but we do not believe that there is nearly as much being done during this time as might be done. This is particularly true in the special service periods.

The Society has made a new arrangement to take effect immediately, and that is, that during the special service periods the factory, office and Bethel home will be closed all day Thursday to enable the Bethel family to have one whole day in the middle of the week in the field witness work. The arrangements are: The family will start with breakfast in the morning at 7:00 o'clock. Each one will take a lunch with him and go into the field service, having his lunch in the field and working until well into the afternoon and return to Bethel for supper at 5:00 o'clock in the evening. We believe a similar

arrangement can be made, but of the brethren there, a country and know that it will result in a more effective and greater witness to the name and purposes of Jehovah and will, therefore, have the Lord's blessing. This method is strongly recommended to the brethren throughout the country. In addition to Thursday, of course, the Bethel family will also have Saturday afternoon and Sunday to participate in the field work with the rest of the Lord's people throughout the earth.

Service leaders sent out from Bethel within an area of 200 miles, instead of going on Friday night or Saturday morning as formerly, will now leave after work Saturday noon. Wherever possible the brethren will leave immediately after dinner and be with you to have some part in the service Saturday afternoon and serve the local company Saturday evening, participate with them in the field service on Sunday, returning home Sunday night. Therefore in your request for brethren to serve your company during the "Servant's Testimony Period" Nov. 24 to Dec. 2, do not expect them before Saturday afternoon.

The work of December and January will be like starting with a new book or a new booklet, and we surely do have a new extraordinary combination. Territory which has been so thoroughly covered with booklets during the past year should be in a very good condition for bound books, and every effort should be put forth by such individual worker to see to it that this offer is made and put within reach of the people.

Almost all pioneers have been working with Preparation, Prosecution, Government and booklets, and now, with this new arrangement for two months, it will save them traveling expenses by their covering this same territory with this special offer. Consider these points and see what will be to the best advantage in the interest of the Lord's kingdom.

### A New Booklet for a Midwinter Campaign

#### Transcription Meetings

##### Should Be Started

##### According to Instructions

The importance of this campaign cannot be overstated. The truth of Jehovah is now forcing the people to take their stand on one side or the other of the great issue; thus they are being divided and forced to identify themselves. He has committed into the hands of his remnant the privilege of proclaiming this great truth, and has blessed us with every necessary requirement to do the work.

Upon the service directors and sharpshooters rests the responsibility of making the detailed preparations for this work so that others associated with them may in no wise be hindered. The other brethren in the companies have the responsibility of fully cooperating in making the campaign a real success by devoting as much time to it as they possibly can and also seeing to it that unnecessary hindrances are set aside and that they are not deterred by inclement weather or other obstacles which a few years back we considered as insurmountable, but which the isolated now recognize as only another opportunity to demonstrate their zeal and love for Jehovah by overcoming these. Pioneers and auxiliaries will, of course, make their personal arrangements to push this campaign vigorously, especially if they are in isolated territory.

In choosing territory keep in mind that this campaign also inaugurates the special transcription activity. This will mean a slight departure from the general outline presented in the special transcription bulletin. In this, that _Dividing the People_ will be presented the first time you go over the territory, instead of the other literature as recommended in the special bulletin. In other words, you will be presenting the _Dividing the People_ booklet the first time and the last time you go over the territory set aside for the transcription program. Afterwards, of course, you will return to the plan presented in the special transcription bulletin. Choose your territory, therefore, and arrange for a portable transcription machine meeting and advertise the meeting as you go along with the booklet in a given district. If your company is large enough, arrangements can be made for a number of portable transcription meetings, provided you have the machines to handle them.

In the work with the _Dividing the People_ booklet cover as much territory as you possibly can and in the

transcription meeting district also advertise the transcription lecture with the first announcement.

# There Is Plenty of Work to Do!

## The Year's Quota 22,500,000 Testimonies

### More Workers Expected in the Field

Auxiliary Division
Should Increase

Transcription
One of the Big Features

There are certain features in every year's work that are outstanding, and certain objectives that the Lord's moulted will earnestly strive to accomplish. The purpose of the organization which the Lord has formed is to give a testimony to the nations of the earth concerning God's purposes. He has blessed us with the great privilege of being his witnesses that he is Jehovah and that his kingdom shall shortly be established in all the world for the blessing of those who love righteousness. Due to the fact that the remnant are commissioned to give the testimony, it is appropriate that the Society should set as its goal each year a quota in testimonies. In arranging for the quota, which is set at 22,500,000 testimonies given in the United States, the number of company workers, of pioneers, of auxiliaries and of sharpshooters has been carefully considered. The time they are putting into the service has been used as a basis for the quota, as well as the number of testimonies they are delivering each hour. There has been a marvelous increase in the number of workers this year. This is due mostly to those of good will, the Jonadah class, who are taking in the water of life freely and then in turn passing it on to others. These have been richly blessed, and should be aided by all of the Lord's people, throughout the coming year, to continue in this privilege of honoring Jehovah's name in the earth.

The Workers

It is estimated that during the fiscal year 1934 there will be an average of 14,000 company workers and sharpshooters engaging in the field service and reporting monthly. We hope that this will be increased, however, due to the interest shown by the Jonadah class, which results would be included in company results. It is hard to estimate this figure. In considering the number of pioneers that might be expected in the service, the estimate is 1,850 reporting each month.
