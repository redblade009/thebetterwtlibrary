## A Suggestion to Consider

In many instances pioneers leave the literature with people in exchange for produce and foodstuffs, such as chickens, eggs, milk, preserved fruits, canned vegetables, etc. To aid the pioneers in turning these into cash with which to pay their necessary expenses, one company has arranged a section in their meeting place where these things taken in exchange are placed by the pioneers, and the members of the company purchase such at reasonable market values. This saves the time of the pioneers since ordinarily they must take the produce to market and dispose of it. It also works to the advantage of those purchasing, since they get good, fresh country products at proper prices. The brethren everywhere must buy various articles of food, and it is suggested that this arrangement, which is reported as working very satisfactorily, could be undertaken by other companies where there are pioneers working in their vicinity.

## Radio Folders and Back Calls

The new radio folder has proved very effective, and many people are listening in because they have received these announcements. It is well to leave these with all the people you need, so that more will be informed concerning the immense number of radio transcription programs that are put on in the United States.

The back calls on those who write in to the office for literature or further information have proved very successful. Workers tell us that quite often when they have made a back call they have left an entire set of ten books, as well as many booklets. These back calls are very encouraging and should be taken care of as soon as you receive the slip from the office.

We would recommend that, whenever pioneers are in a company territory, the director gave these slips to them if they desire to make the back calls. This will aid them somewhat in their expenses, because it may be that they can place a set of books, and this will be of help to them. Instead of giving the back call slips to the company workers or even those who do not go out regularly, it is preferable to give them to the pioneers in your territory if they are conveniently make the calls. Otherwise, active workers should take care of these immediately, so that the witness may be properly presented and literature left with the interested parties.
