The light shining upon the Kingdom privileges and requirements is such that daily the issue assumes a clearer and deeper meaning. The work of a witness is to give testimony; and that is why the Kingdom work during recent years is forecast in testimonies rather than on the amount of literature placed. If Company Wishes to Work
Their Own Business
Territory, Notify the Office

## What Is

"Business Territory"?

Manifestly it is not only to the interests of the work in general for all territory to be thoroughly covered, but to the interests of the pioneers themselves, as they will not have to move so often. Business districts are part of pioneer assignments, and we encourage those who hold assignments not in company territory to thoroughly cover the business sections before the assignment is turned back to this office. Business districts have proven to be a fruitful field for many pioneers.

In company and sharpshooter territory the business districts are open for pioneers who cannot go to isolated territory. Business districts are not to be covered by the company workers unless the service director or sharpshooter makes a special request stating that the company workers can cover the business sections, and receives the business district assignment from this office. Auxiliaries wishing to work generally in business districts should get a special assignment from this office, as do the pioneers.

Business districts refer not only to retail stores, but also to offices, factories, water fronts, in fact, all but the residential territory of a city. In many places there are streets of stores with apartments above. The rows of stores are business districts; the apartments above are residential. Often, away from the main, downtown businesses, are groups of business houses of all kinds, and these also are included in the term "business districts", and are pioneer territory unless the company specially requests them. What about the isolated neighborhood stores, those located by themselves throughout the residential section? The service directors should see that these are witnessed to, and the best way would be for the worker having the adjoining residence to take those little stores as he comes to them. Sometimes directors report that business districts are covered, when only some of the first-floor stores are entered offices, etc., above being untouched; but these professional men are included in business districts.

## THE NEW CATALOG

Can Be Used
in Witness Work

About January 15 the Society will have completed the publication of a revised catalog. It will be practically the same as the one used last year, with a few additions for the new books. A new colored insert will appear in the middle, which will give a better display of the books published by the Society than the previous one.

Many of the friends have found this catalog a real help in placing the literature by using it the same as they would a prospectus. Some of the sisters and older brothers find it impossible to carry a full set of the ten bound books and all the booklets when witnessing from door to door. They have found that by carrying a few of the books with this catalog they have been able to place complete sets of ten.

The Society publishes the catalog primarily for answering radio inquiries. All listeners desiring to know more about our work and the literature we publish are mailed a copy of this catalog, and it has proved effective in this manner. It can be used not only in answer to radio inquiries, but as a prospectus and the Society is pleased to offer these booklets to the friends at cost of production. They can again be had at two for 5c.

We should very much appreciate it if the director of each company would order at one time the number the workers desire for their use, with some order for books, so as to save shipping charges. The same would be well for the pioneers and auxiliaries also.

## Testimony Booklets

A Convenience
for Every Worker

A request has been made several times for a testimony to be printed in different languages. The Society is now preparing to have the testimony we now have for bound books printed in several different languages in a small 16-page folder. On each page there will appear in a different language an exact translation of the English testimony, which will appear on the first page of this booklet. These little booklets will be shipped during January to all companies, sharpshooters, pioneers and auxiliaries, for their use when meeting people speaking languages other than they can speak. This little booklet should be supplied to each worker, so that when witnessing to a certain territory and meeting, for example, an Italian who can not understand English, all that is necessary is to turn to the page in the testimony booklet marked "Italian" and request the one who answers at the door to read the testimony in his own language. If interested you can either take an order or, if you have the literature in the language required with you, leave some of the books. In this manner there will be a greater witness given among the foreign-speaking people in this country than heretofore.
