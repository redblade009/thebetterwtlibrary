## SPECIAL BLESSING FOR THANKSGIVING PERIOD

### Brother Rutherford Will Talk to Anointed in Many Countries

#### WORLD CONTROL

##### THE RADIO ANNOUNCEMENTS

As has been previously announced, on March 25 there will be a coast-to-coast hook-up comprising more than 115 radio stations. These will carry Brother Rutherford's lecture on "WORLD CONTROL". Announcement folders for this chain are being consigned for all the workers. Pioneers and auxiliaries addressed in company or sharpshooter territory will receive an allotment from the director or sharpshooter; which likewise applies to foreign-speaking and colored companies. These isolated will receive theirs direct from the Society. The directors and sharpshooters should be sure to notify all the workers, whether they be auxiliaries, pioneers or company workers, that the folders are on hand and should be used in their regular witness work from door to door from the time they are received up until the broadcast. All of the folders should be distributed no later than Sunday morning, March 25.

##### "DIVIDING THE PEOPLE" TESTIMONY CARDS

The foreign-speaking brethren will be provided with a testimony card with the _Dividing the People_ testimony in their respective languages. These will be sent to the directors as soon as they are ready.

Because of the extreme amount of other printing before us, it is impossible to provide the testimony card for the English-speaking brethren. They may use the testimony as printed in the March _Bulletin_ or have a supply of their own printed.

##### CELLULOID ENVELOPES

The Society can now supply the brethren with a celluloid envelope for holding a testimony card. These envelopes keep the testimony card nice and clean, so that it is always in a neat and presentable condition. They will take a card 3 1/2 by 5 1/2 inches. These envelopes may be had at 10c each or three for a quarter.

##### TESTIMONY BOOKLETS

The "Testimony" booklet, containing a testimony (in sixteen languages) for the books and booklets, is now ready and is being sent with this _Bulletin_, for distribution of one booklet to each worker.

##### CANADA

Our brethren in Canada and Mexico will be working shoulder to shoulder with those of Jehovah's witnesses in the rest of the world. North America will be well informed of the Kingdom.

#### Lecture "Requirements" a Provision of the Lord

##### Every Morning Field Service at Los Angeles

##### Field Service, Chain Hook-Up and Record "Requirements" Make Los Angeles Convention a World-wide Gathering

Friday night, March 23, is the opening of the Los Angeles convention. All of the Lord's anointed who can possibly get there will be there for the opening meeting of the convention so that they will be ready for the service on Saturday morning, which is the beginning of the International Thanksgiving Testimony of Jehovah's witnesses. Thousands of the brethren are expected, and abundant blessings are in store for those who attend this convention.

All the meetings will be held in the Shrine Auditorium Building. Every morning from 8:00 until 12:00 has been set aside for field service work. There will be meetings in the afternoon and evening which will aid the brethren in the understanding of the Lord's Word. Sunday afternoon, at 3:30 Pacific Standard Time, Brother Rutherford, by the Lord's grace, will lecture on the subject "WORLD CONTROL" and at this time arrangements include more than 115 radio stations in a coast-to-coast network that will serve the millions of people eager to hear Judge Rutherford who live in the United States and Canada. All of the remnant will make arrangements to listen in. This is one of the blessings with which everyone will be favored who can hear.

For those who will not be privileged to attend this convention, the Lord in his goodness has provided a special blessing for this Thanksgiving Period. It is now arranged by means of an electrical transcription record that Brother Rutherford will be able to talk to all of the anointed in many countries throughout the earth. In every English-speaking country where there are transcription machines the lecture "Requirements" has been shipped. The Lord's people will assemble together either on Saturday, March 24, or on Sunday, March 25, and the anointed and people of good will will be overjoyed to hear this message which the Lord has surely provided for his people at this time. Never before has such an arrangement been made whereby a greater part of those who are in a covenant relationship with the Lord could hear a message provided by Jehovah at the same time.

With this at the beginning of the week, everyone will undoubtedly put forth an extraordinary effort to serve the only true God by giving testimony from door to door, which will be to the vindication of Jehovah's name. Many countries, especially all of the English-speaking countries, have been provided with the booklet _Dividing the People_. Why not make arrangements during this period to spend at least four hours every day in the field service, the same as it has been arranged at the convention in Los Angeles. There are many of the brethren who will be able to do this. Those who cannot do this can surely put in Saturday afternoon and the best part of both Sundays in the field service work.

This is really a time of _thanksgiving_, for it will be during this week that everyone who is in line for the Kingdom will be partaking of the Memorial supper. Just let us recall the many features of the convention that many will enjoy even though they cannot attend:

(1) "REQUIREMENTS," a provision of the Lord. This half-hour lecture will be given by Brother Rutherford to more than 400 assemblies throughout the earth, either on Saturday the 24th or Sunday the 25th. It is not to be heard before this time by anyone.

(2) Listening in to Brother Rutherford's lecture on "WORLD CONTROL" over a coast-to-coast network.

(3) Everyone of the anointed will be participating in the Memorial.

(4) Witnessing from door to door with the same testimony and the same literature.

The Lord has seen fit to have this booklet translated into many languages so that _Dividing the People_ may be in the hands of those who want to know which side they shall take. After reading this booklet they can be assured of knowing whether or not they are on the right side. What a blessed period this will be for those who now know Jehovah and will put forth every effort to sing forth his praises!

Remember, if you can get to the Los Angeles convention, get there. If not, then be sure to enjoy the further special blessings Jehovah has provided for his people, not only at the convention, but throughout the world.

#### GOOD NEWS

##### NORTHERN EUROPE

Jehovah's witnesses in the countries of northern Europe broke all records for a nine-day period during the midwinter campaign, January 20-28. During that period 2,071 brethren were out in the service, and they placed 1,547 books and 132,449 booklets. That was more than 250 additional workers, and about 17,000 pieces of literature more than ever before for those countries. Now these brethren are preparing for the greatest campaign yet, the THANKSGIVING TESTIMONY OF JEHOVAH'S WITNESSES.

##### MOST DISTANT POINTS

200,000 _Dividing the People_ booklets have been shipped to Australia, and 40,000 to New Zealand, and the brethren there will have them for the international campaign. 35,000 have gone to South Africa, 10,000 to West Africa, and 10,000 to India.

##### CZECHOSLOVAKIA

The Slovak edition of _Dividing the People_ is being rushed through so that Jehovah's witnesses in Czechoslovakia will have them by March 24.

##### SPANISH-SPEAKING COUNTRIES

Consignments of Spanish _Dividing the People_ are going forward to Mexico, Cuba, Chile, Argentina, and other countries.

##### COUNTRIES OF THE EAST

_Dividing the People_ will be published in Arabic, Albanian and Greek. Shipments of these booklets will soon be on their way to Syria, Albania, and Egypt, and it is hoped they will arrive there in time for the THANKSGIVING period.

##### CENTRAL EUROPE

The Berne factory is working hard to get out _Dividing the People_ in German, French, Dutch, and other languages, and it is expected that the anointed in France, Holland, Belgium, Switzerland, Poland, Romania, Austria, and Yugoslavia will have _Dividing the People_.

##### ENGLAND

A half million _Dividing the People_ booklets were shipped to England in January for the midwinter campaign. This was considered a large quantity, but all of them were shipped out to the brethren, and now the London office has cabled for an additional supply. The factory at Brooklyn has just completed a week of working extra time in the evenings from 7:00 till 9:30 in order to supply the heavy demand.

# Are We Going to Reach the Mark?

1,000 Auxiliaries by March 24

Since the first of the year many questionnaires have been mailed to brethren responding to the call to enter either the pioneer or the auxiliary work. Doubtless you have considered what arrangements you must make to get in auxiliary time of 52 hours every month in house-to-house work, or to enter the service full time as pioneer. Have you made application yet?

It is still hoped that the auxiliary list will increase to at least 1,000 by Memorial time. This figure has not yet been reached; just now there are 872 enrolled. We earnestly hope the applications will arrive speedily.

Pioneer time is full time in the field, and 110 hours each month is the established minimum. All the brethren will be interested in this quotation from a letter sent to the pioneers some time ago: "Let us stop to consider for just a minute how much 110 hours a month is. Say you work 6 days a week, 5 hours a day, only 2½ in the morning and 2½ in the afternoon. That would make 30 hours every week. There are 4 weeks in every month, and that makes 120 hours in the door-to-door witnessing."

Many who are really feeble, or otherwise physically handicapped, have been enabled to serve as auxiliaries or pioneers by using the printed testimony, as they have written to us. We shall be very happy to send you information regarding this service upon hearing from you. The territory mentioned in the February Bulletin is not all assigned.

# Service Leaders for March 31 and April 1

As usual, the factory, office and home will be closed during the first and the last Saturday of this period to enable the Bethel brethren to join with you to a greater extent in the field activity, and it is anticipated that, by the Lord's grace, this period is going to exceed any up to the present in the field activity of the Bethel family.

In view of the fact that Brother Rutherford has arranged to provide a special record for the opening of the THANKSGIVING TESTIMONY OF JERUSALD'S WITNESSES, and that his nation-wide radio talk comes on Sunday, the usual custom of sending service leaders from Bethel during the first part of this period will be dispensed with. However, any company within the 200-mile radius of Bethel desiring a service leader for the latter part of this testimony period, Saturday March 31 and Sunday April 1, may have one, provided they write in and request one.

Since this is to be an international period of united witnessing, the brethren everywhere will be presenting the Dividing the People booklet. Please place your orders at once, so that by March 24 you will have what you need of any of these booklets.

# A Good Suggestion for Transcription Meetings

The portable transcription machine activity throughout the earth is going ahead with leaps and bounds.

A method that has been found very practical in the large cities and should also work out advantageously in the smaller places, is the following:

The brethren come in contact with a home where considerable interest is manifest by the people. Inquiry is made of that party if they would like to have a transcription meeting in their home to which they could invite their neighbors, friends and relatives and if they would be willing to have any other outsiders who would be interested in such a meeting also attend. If they give assent they are provided with as many invitations as they think they can use among their relatives, friends and neighbors and are requested to personally invite these people to this meeting. # Thanksgiving Testimony of Jehovah's witnesses Reports

Every service director, sharpshooter, auxiliary and pioneer is to make a special report for "THANKSGIVING TESTIMONY OF JERUSALD'S WITNESSES, on one of the regular report cards. From the card cross out the words "For the entire month of" and write "Thanksgiving Testimony of Jehovah's witnesses, March 24 to April 1". Enter on the card all work for the nine days, showing the total of all booklets, including Dividing the People, in the space needed "Miss Booklets". Please report promptly.

When you report for the entire month of March include the work done during the testimony period, including April 1. Two report cards are to be sent to us for work in March; one for the testimony period only, and the other for the entire month (including the testimony period). We have never yet received testimony period reports from every one as we should. Let each company worker cooperate with the service director in reporting promptly, and let each service director, sharpshooter, auxiliary and pioneer see to it that he is not one of the delinquent brethren who do not report.

# Divisional Campaign Reports

Pioneers, auxiliaries, company workers and sharpshooters will participate in the divisional campaign of Saturday and Sunday March 3 and 4, and, as usual, all these brethren will make a special report to the divisional leader of their work during the two days of the campaign. The divisional leaders are the only ones who are to make special separate reports to this office, showing the work done during these two days only. This work done during the two days of the campaign is to be included in the regular monthly reports of all sharpshooters, companies, auxiliaries and pioneers. Except for the divisional leaders' reports, to one should report separately to this office the work of these two days. Following the last divisional campaign, many pioneers, auxiliaries and sharpshooters sent as cards for their work during the campaign only. This should not be done. The work of the divisional campaigns is to be included in the regular monthly reports these workers send to this office, and not reported separately. At the end of March, every pioneer, auxiliary, sharpshooter and company service director should send to this office a report of the work done during the entire month (including the two days of the divisional campaign).
