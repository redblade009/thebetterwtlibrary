# REMNANT'S THANKSGIVING PERIOD, APRIL 8-16

# Brother Rutherford Writes Special Message to Jehovah's witnesses

"THE CRISIS"—A New Booklet—To Feature Campaign in English-Speaking Nations

# The Memorial Season Will Be a Feast of Joy and Kingdom Service

Our Goal Three Million Testimonies in 94 Countries

Can We Place 1,000,000 Booklets in 9 Days for Greatest Assault Against the Devil's Organization to the Present Time?

Never before in the history of the church has Jehovah God brought his people to such a state of unity. The remnant is now preparing to go forth as one army in the greatest assault against the Devil's organization ever made up to the present time. Jehovah will vindicate his name, and God's faithful people in the earth are enjoying the privilege of having part in this great and marvelous work by serving notice on the enemy.

The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period begins April 8 and continues to the 10th. During this period of time the Memorial Feast will be celebrated with much joy. On Sunday, April 9, after 6 p.m., the anointed will have the pleasure of commemorating their communion with their Lord in drinking the new wine of the Kingdom. This feast they will greatly appreciate after having spent two days or parts of two days in witnessing for the Kingdom. After this supper they will continue to drink the new wine by zealously carrying out their commission of bearing much fruit to the glory of Jehovah.

Brother Rutherford believes that this particular testimony period is of such great importance that he has written a letter addressing it "To Jehovah's witnesses throughout the earth". His letter is reprinted in this Bulletin, and every one of the remnant will be filled with joy upon reading his message and noting the importance of this period as set forth therein. Each one who reads it will realize the necessity of going forth in united action without fear of man or devil. Every reader of The Watchtower who professes to be one of Jehovah's wit-

nesses is being given the opportunity to read this message and make arrangements for his participation in this special Remnant's Thanksgiving Period.

# The Crisis Has Come!

Brother Rutherford has just completed a new 64-page booklet, and it is hoped that during this period of 9 days all of the English-speaking countries and as many as possible of the other countries throughout the world will have been supplied with them so that all of them will have the privilege of concentrating on the distribution of The Crisis to the people and the rulers. A day and a night shift have been working at the Brooklyn factory to make this possible. Freight shipments are being raided in England, Australia, South Africa, India, Canada and other English-speaking countries. And why? Because the message it contains is of greatest importance to all. In the first place, it contains the much demanded lecture, "Can the American Government Endure?" This question is one of vital importance, not only to the United States, but to every other country upon the earth; for if the American Government, the world's banker, cannot endure, what hope is there for the debtor nations? Only God's righteous government, his kingdom, can and will endure. The nations of earth are facing a tremendous crisis. Those who are looking for a remedy should know the only possible one. The Crisis not only gives them this, but will afford them the opportunity of taking their stand either on the Lord's side or on that of the Devil and his organization. It is a marvelous booklet. In addition to this thrilling lecture, it also contains "Jehovah's witnesses: Why Persecuted?" as well as a powerful lecture on "Love" which presents the issue so clearly that there is no room for oppression. Its cover is one that will relieve the hearts of the anointed, inspire the people with awe, and strike fear into the hearts of the Devil's organization as God's army moves into triumphant action.

TO JEHOVAH'S WITNESSES THROUGHOUT THE EARTH

My beloved Brethren:

Jehovah discloses to his people that Satan and his agents have formed a conspiracy against him and against his anointed, the purpose of which is to cut off Jehovah's people "from being a nation". Then he makes known his purpose to destroy Satan's organization and to cover himself with victory and glory that "they may know that \[he] whose name alone is Jehovah \[is] the Most High over all the earth". The time for the vindication of his name is at hand. He commands his witnesses to go forward now with full confidence and absolute courage and to deliver his testimony to the rulers and to the people before he executes his judgment upon the enemy.

A specific time has been set aside for the united action of all who truly love Jehovah. That time is April 8-16 inclusive and is designated "The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period". I am sending this message of greetings to all countries of the earth where the consecrated are known to be, and this includes ninety-four different countries of the earth. Remember that Jehovah commands his people that love him to have boldness in this day of judgment in declaring his message. Will you join this company of faithful witnesses to his name? The church is now at unity. Let all move out in exact and harmonious action to do this work as God commands it. He not afraid of man or devil. (Deut. 33:27) Trust in Jehovah and do his work with joy. Let all workers be organized and move orderly and sing the praises of Jehovah. The day of deliverance is at hand.

A blank for your report is sent to you. At the end of the testimony period please fill it out carefully and mail immediately to the Society's office at 117 Adams St., Brooklyn, New York.

May the grace of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ and of our Father, Jehovah God, abide with you richly now and for evermore.

With much love and best wishes, I remain

Your brother and servant by His grace,

President,

# The Name of JEHOVAH Is a Strong Tower

# In the Time of The Crisis

The time has come to call upon all those to whom the name of Jehovah is a strong tower to put forth another great effort, in fact the greatest effort ever. To free into this strong tower indicates that they will make haste to respond. The special call to action comes for The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period, April 8-16.

What arrangements should be made? First: During this period every witness should endeavor to place The Crisis in the hands of as many people as possible, whether such a witness be a company worker, sharpshooter, pioneer or auxiliary. To do this an adequate supply of The Crisis should be on hand. (Read the article on consignment and ordering in this Bulletin.)

Second: Thickly populated territory should be set aside for this particular period. Plans should be made for covering the territory thoroughly and quickly. Pioneers should do this as well as companies. If they have covered their city territory with the other literature and have only runals left, then go over the city territory with the booklet. Pioneers as well as all other workers will be counting their efforts to The Crisis for this period. If they have been saving their city territory, a preliminary witness with The Crisis will be just the thing for an immediate follow-up with the rest of the literature.

Third: To reach more people with The Crisis will mean more time than ever must be devoted to the field work. Directors should arrange for parties to go out into the work for every day of the campaign. Don't be content. If you are a company worker, with two or three days of a few hours each in the service. If you can't get out during the daytime, plan to get out an hour or so each evening. In fact, seize every possible moment for notifying the people that "The Crisis" is here.

Can you boost this to 50 hours? During the same period the auxiliaries reported an average of 24.82 hours each. What do you say about making this an average of 28 hours? Company workers and sharpshooters, you made your best average during Witness Week, September, 1931, at which time you averaged 9.9 hours each. Can you increase this to 12 hours each?

Jehovah has placed this message in the hands of the remnant. His work must be done speedily.

# Special Notice

April 2 Divisional Campaign Canceled

The importance of The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period, and the necessity of eliminating everything that might in any way interfere with it, is further emphasized by the fact that Brother Rutherford has instructed us to cancel all divisional campaigns for April 2.

This, therefore, is to advise all divisional leaders and all field workers that the campaigns scheduled for that date are hereby canceled, and that, instead, you use the time for regular service in your own territory and for making arrangements for The Remnant's Testimony Period. Everything that can be taken care of in advance, so as to allow more time for field service during this special period, should be taken care of during the week preceding it. Let our slogan be: Everyone in the service at least some part of each day.

# Special Report for The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period

EACH service director, sharpshooter, pioneer and auxiliary is to make out and PROMPTLY send in to the Society a report card covering the field witness activities during The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period. # Transcription Machines

# To Be Used in Public Witness Work

The Lord is richly blessing the progress of the work of manufacturing the portable transcription machines, and they are now being assembled and shipped. We hope to have a considerable number of them in use within a short while.

These machines will afford another splendid opportunity to give public witness to the Kingdom message in the territory. Pioneers who have a machine, as well as others, are advised to arrange for schoolhouses, empty churches, or other free buildings, to which the public may be invited, and to put on a program of one or two transcription records and then present the books to the people. There are many who are becoming too poor to afford radio sets, and this will afford a way for them to hear the truth.

Under no circumstances are transcription records supplied for radio broadcasting to be used on the portable transcription machines. This is very important. Use on the portable transcription machines only such records as have been supplied you for that purpose.

Care should be taken that only needles specially prepared for "33-1/3" revolutions per minute records are used. Use a new needle for each running. Should records become cracked or scratched so that the presentation is to any degree distorted, advise this office, with a full description of the damage. The clarity and effectiveness of the presentation of the Kingdom message by this means depends upon the full cooperation of each one. Therefore we request that those who own and use the portable transcription machines give careful consideration to the above.
