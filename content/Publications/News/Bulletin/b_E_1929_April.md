# MAY 19 TO 26 IBSA WEEK

## Biggest Witness in History of the Church

### All Kindreds and Tongues to Engage in World-Wide Message

The first IBSA Week of this year is set for May 19 to 26. It should be viewed as a spring drive of the Lord's army on the enemy's camp. From November to April the inclement weather and short days retarded the activities of the Lord's people, or at least limited their activity to their home city and a few of the larger towns in their assignment. The winter season always has a more or less depressing effect upon the public whom we have to canvass. When they open the door cold winds pierce them and, due to the fact that the workers may have either snow or mud on their shoes, the people often do not feel like inviting them in. Consequently we are handicapped during that period. However, spring is now here, with its sunshine, and the people are in a more responsive mood. Every one seems to be enjoying life. This season brings hope and cheer; an awakening to life is in evidence everywhere. The Lord seems to be tuning the world for the Kingdom message. Every anointed servant of the Lord should be imbued with the spirit of the season, much more so than any one in the world could hope to be, due to the fact that not only have we the sure evidences of spring, but behind it all we can clearly see the great awakening which it symbolizes and the great God, our Father, who arranged it all. In this way the Lord is going before his people, preparing the way for them. The Lord also assures us that in this day of judgment he will cause the wrath of men to praise him. Never has this been brought home to us more forcefully than in considering plans for IBSA Week.

### PENTECOST

The first point that stands out in this connection is the manner in which the Lord overrules the agencies of Satan to his own glory. In their efforts to confuse the people regarding Jehovah God and his purposes and to relegate his name to the background, Satan and his representatives have set Easter Sunday this year to take place on the 31st of March. Pentecost, which is 50 days later, will therefore, according to their reckoning, fall on May 20. Throughout Europe and in South America Pentecost is extensively observed. May 19 and 20 are set apart this year as holidays during which most of the people are relieved from their secular employment. The Lord's remnant in those countries where Pentecost is so observed, appreciating their privilege of magnifying the name of Jehovah, will undoubtedly be glad to take advantage of the arrangement of the adversary

and stage a counter-attack during these two days particularly, which are the first two days of IBSA Week, and also the remainder of the week.

If concerted action is taken by the friends throughout the world, undoubtedly the witness will make itself felt from one end of Satan's organization to the other.

### VICTORIA DAY

The second occasion whereon the Devil and his associates are going to find themselves confronted with the Lord's army pushing the battle to the gate will be on May 24, which also comes within IBSA Week. May 24 is observed throughout the British Empire as Victoria Day. The British Empire is the world-power of our day, and therefore represents the visible phase of the Devil's organization. What could be more appropriate than that the Lord should arrange that our spring drive should open with a direct attack upon the enemy's main stronghold. Without doubt the majority of the friends throughout the British Isles, Canada and Australia will be released from secular employment from Friday. May 24, until Monday, May 27. ## A Special Week for Colporteurs

### Flash-tone Cartons

#### Not to Be Used Before May 19

The flash-tone cartons for the five-book combinations are not to be used before IBSA Week, since they have been obtained as a special feature of the drive. We do not fill orders for cartons alone, but only when the combinations of the five books, _The Harp of God_, _Deliverance_, _Creation_, _Reconciliation_ and _Government_, are ordered packed in these special cartons. ### Forty Hours in Eight Days to Be the Average for Pioneers

Heretofore no special record has been kept of the activities of the regular colporteurs during IBSA Week. Not in view of the fact that all the anointed throughout the earth are going to be putting forth a special effort at that time, we thought that you too would like to do the same and to know the results of your activities. Consequently we are making arrangements to tabulate the results.

Now here's the plan. Since IBSA Week is to be from May 19 to 26, including two Sundays, we ask that you make out your report for that week to include the entire eight-day period instead of the usual seven-day week. This will mean that your report for the previous week will be for only six days. Then send us your Report Card Sunday night, May 26, before you go to bed. The auxiliary colporteurs are asked to do the same, as we are going to keep a special record for them too.

CLASSES VS. COLPORTEURS
The classes are entering this campaign with a determination to equal the colporteurs. Since the auxiliary colporteurs' sales are included in the class reports also, the regulars will have to put forth a real effort if they don't want to be left behind, judging from the results of last year's May IBSA Week. Then, besides, new recruits are joining the colporteur ranks daily.

Remember, also, the consecrated throughout the entire earth are going to be working with you, singing forth the praises of our God. They are going to be putting forth a more strenuous effort than ever. What a great shout it will be!

What will be the final report from the battle front at the conclusion of this great attack? We are looking for a big advance in the front line sector held by the colporteurs.

# Many Joining the Colporteur Ranks

## Increased Enrollment Stresses Importance of Canvassing Territory Thoroughly

It makes us very happy to see the way in which the friends have been responding to the call for more colporteurs. Each mail is bringing in more and more applications and requests for information on how to become a pioneer or an auxiliary. Since the mailing of 'The Time is at Hand', February 23, 329 applications have been sent out and 105 new colporteurs have been enrolled. Surely the time is at hand for the greatest with the yet to be given.

As the colporteur list increases, it becomes doubly important for those who have a territory assigned to them to endeavor to work it thoroughly. In times past some have just skimmed over their assignments, taking a few of the more important towns and waving the rurals and out-of-the-way places untouched. They have made frequent requests for additional territory, sending in uncompleted assignments. When only a few colporteurs were in the field, such territory would not be reassigned the same year; but now it means that when the territory is turned over to some one else to complete, the cream has been taken and the one following has to bear the burden. Surely those in the remote sections are as well entitled to receive the message as those in towns and on the main thoroughfares.

Of course, during certain seasons of the year it is extremely difficult to work the rural sections on account of mud and bad roads. We do not expect the impossible, but bring this to your attention in the hope that those who have not been attempting to cover their assignments will put forth an effort to do so. Some write us that they can't

get into the rurals because of having no automobile. At the same time a frail colporteur sister, whose report was given in the October 2 Bulletin, wrote us how thoroughly and satisfactorily she was able to cover such territory afloat, using the parcel post to keep her supplied with books. She has found this to be so successful that she has come to prefer it to using an automobile. Other colporteurs who can not afford to operate an automobile have worked their rural territory and outlying towns by means of a bicycle. Their reports as to this means of travel have been very favorable and encouraging.

Recently the following letter was received from a colporteur in Idaho: 'I was feeling a bit discouraged because the roads were still blocked with snow. Even the highways have been closed part of the time on account of snow-drifts. I was thinking that I would have to wait till the roads got good in the spring before I could again work rural territory. But rain, snow, bad roads cold, or even the Devil, can not hold the Lord's army back; so I thought, 'Why should I be a slacker?' I know that is what the Devil would like to see: slackers in the Lord's army. So, after I had finished the town territory, I started out afloat and canvassed about four miles out in the country and then canvassed back on another road, and did the same thing today, and came in happy in the Lord. The farmers about all ask a fellow in on a cold day, and one has a chance to warm and rest while showing the books. Although the houses are quite far apart, so that I could not make nearly as many calls as in town, I sold more bound books in the two days than I had sold in two successive days in town for some time. So I say to myself, 'Why sit down and wait for the roads to get good enough to ride around in a car?'

Many of the colporteurs are becoming quite enthusiastic about offering the $1.00 Bible with the combination of 5 cloth books, saying that it aids them considerably in placing more books.
