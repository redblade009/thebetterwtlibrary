# EXTRA! EXTRA! EXTRA! BULLETIN for Jehovah's witnesses

SPECIAL

APRIL 4, 1980

## CLIMAX TO THE WEEK!

### The Thanksgiving Period Ends with 'The Way of Escape'

#### WBBR Will Broadcast Dramas of Special Interest

Jehovah's witnesses Everywhere Should Listen In

Please Make Special Report of Reception

These dramas are of special interest to the brethren throughout the country because they are the exact reproduction of two court trials recently held in New Jersey.

The first drama, 'Conspiracy,' reenacts the trial at Bergenfield, N. J., in which 31 of our brethren were tried and 23 of them convicted and locked up for 7 days in Hackensack jail. The second drama, 'Hypocrisy,' portrays the trial of 18 of our brethren at Long Branch, N. J., in which all of them were tried and convicted and given suspended sentence. To the anointed the real value of these broadcasts is that they do not degrade the absolute fearlessness and implicit confidence which Jehovah's witnesses must and do now manifest as they are brought before the rulers to deliver the testimony for which the Lord has them brought there. Note also how these brethren always tried to bring in as evidence portions of the Order of Trial that the brethren who appeared before them were unable to present, and further, how each one stayed right with the Order of Trial even though not permitted to read it, and all questions were answered from the testimony appearing therein, and were not turned aside from this either by the brazen and arbitrary attitude of the conspirators or the suave hypocrisy of the latter. Jehovah's witnesses now realize keenly that 'politeness' and 'soft words' cunningly spoken by agents of Satan's tottering organization are not to be taken seriously.

#### Brother Rutherford to Speak on Large Hook-up April 16, 7 p.m.

Surely It Will Be the Greatest Witness Week Ever Prepare for Action

The Lord has another big surprise for his witnesses for The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period; and here it is,

A BIG CHAIN RADIO HOOK-UP ON SUNDAY, APRIL 16, from 7 to 8 p.m., Eastern Standard time. Brother Rutherford himself will speak on the subject 'The Way of Escape'.

What a dessert for a Thanksgiving feast! Since we just received the good news ourselves, we are rushing through this special Bulletin so that you may share with us in the pleasure of anticipation and PREPARE FOR ACTION.

Surely the Lord is good to his people. For their Thanksgiving day he spreads a feast lasting for 9 days. On the second day, the remnant parlour of the Memorial emblems not only of their participation in the death of his beloved Son but also

of their blessed privilege of sharing with him in the Kingdom Joy. Jehovah arms them with the new booklet, _The Crisis_, a subject which is daily being blazed forth in the headlines of all the papers. He puts the words in their mouths with the special testimony, which is short, to the point, brings the question squarely before the people as to why this present crisis exists, and will enable his witnesses to cover the territory quickly. AND NOW he adds to their joy by permitting them to arrange for another big radio chain broadcast that the people may receive additional information on 'The Way of Escape'.

This big hook-up will mean that every witness in the United States and Canada will have the opportunity of sharing in making it a success. How? Millions of radio announcements are to be printed. For the people to know about this important lecture, these announcements must be distributed. The lecture coming at the end of the Thanksgiving Period will mean that their distribution can be made at the same time the testimony for _The Crisis_

is given. To get out all of them before the lecture will necessitate quick action, long hours in the field, and close adherence to the printed testimony. Long-winded arguments and discussions will have to be curtailed; for haste must be made from door to door to cover as much territory as possible.

The importance of leaving a _Crisis_ booklet in every home daily increases as the people are coming to realize that they are faced with such a crisis as never before. During The Remnant's Thanksgiving Period special effort is to be made to acquaint the people with the real issue involved. The booklet will make them more anxious than ever to listen to Brother Rutherford Sunday evening and to find out 'The Way of Escape'.

The time for the lecture has been set at the best hour of the day, so that all who desire to listen may have the opportunity. From 7 to 8 p.m. Eastern Standard time will mean 6 to 7 p.m. Central time, 5 to 6 p.m. Mountain time, and 4 to 5 p.m. Pacific time. Never have we had a broadcast at a more favorable hour; never has there been one on a more timely subject; never was it more important for Jehovah's witnesses to notify the people concerning it; and never were conditions better for doing this, with the special nine-day service period preceding it. We can therefore joyfully anticipate the greatest audience ever listening to the Kingdom message; for 'THE CRISIS' is at hand. The people want to know 'the way of escape'.

THE LORD CALLS HIS PEOPLE TO ACTION. He has placed within their hands the message, 'The Crisis' is here. The time to notify the people concerning it and 'The Way of Escape' must not be put off. Nothing is more important to the remnant than the doing of that which the Lord has given them to do, at the time and in the way appointed. #### Wide Advertisement to Be Made of Hook-up

Radio Folders to Be Furnished Free

In order to get as large an audience as possible listening to the big broadcast of the message 'The Way of Escape', on April 16, the Society is going to consign free of charge to every company, sharp-shooter, pioneer and auxiliary special radio folders announcing this big hook-up. The people must know about it, if they are to hear it. The Devil-controlled press cannot be relied upon to notify them concerning Jehovah's message. Jehovah's witnesses must do it themselves. You will have a part in this work throughout The Remnant's Thanksgiving

Period, April 8-10, but remember it is to be done while you are giving the people the testimony concerning 'The Crisis'. First give the testimony suggested by Brother Rutherford, then an invitation to listen to the lecture, accompanied by a radio folder.

If toward the end of the week you find that by proceeding in this manner you will not have distributed all the folders before the time of the lecture, arrange to spend a few hours in the evening distributing the folders exclusively. While it is important that all the announcements get into the hands of the people, this work should be done so as not to interfere with the distribution of the _Crisis_ booklet.

# "THE CRISIS" CAMPAIGN IN WASHINGTON, D.C.

## Do You Want a Part in Serving the Rulers at the Capital?

Read Instructions Carefully and Be There for Action

What a Week
This One Will Be!

The issue is now clearly drawn. The claim is made that present governments shall endure. Jehovah says they cannot endure, but that on their ruins he will establish a new and perfect government which will stand forever. Notice of this issue must be given to the rulers and the people. They must know the crisis is here and that there is no alternative; Jehovah's kingdom only shall prevail.

That this issue may be placed squarely before the rulers as well as the people, a special campaign is being arranged for Washington, D.C., on Saturday, April 15, in which it is hoped that the president, every member of the cabinet, all congressmen, senators, and government employees, and the members of foreign legations, in fact, every person who is connected with this or any other government represented in Washington, will be served with the Crisis booklet and an invitation to listen in to a nation-wide special chain radio broadcast on "THE WAY OF ESCAPE".

Do you wish to have a part in that campaign? If you do and can comply with the conditions, you may have a part therein. Here is the arrangement.

We are inviting the three divisions immediately adjacent to Washington to assemble as divisions. These are the divisions with headquarters at Philadelphia, Pa., Baltimore, Md., and Richmond, Va. Every worker in those divisions who can be in Washington by 7:00 o'clock Friday evening, April 14, is invited to come and participate in the campaign. All other individuals or car groups outside of these divisions who wish to participate in the campaign and can be in Washington at 7:00 p.m. Friday, April 14, may also come. It must be kept in mind, however, that this work is of great

importance, and the detailed instructions covering it are so essential that they must be thoroughly assimilated by each one who goes into action, and that anyone who cannot get to Washington by 7:00 o'clock Friday evening, April 14, to get these instructions cannot have a part in the campaign. Therefore, if you are unable to make it by that time, do not go at all. It creates considerable confusion to try to work last-minute arrivals into the organization. The big job at Washington will be on Saturday morning, when the workers who are thoroughly instructed, and who can be depended upon to carry out in detail those instructions, will be dispatched to their territory.

There will be sufficient territory for those who are in the work at Washington on Saturday to stay over and work in the residential sections on Sunday; but do not come to Washington simply for Sunday's activity, for the witness is just as important in the residential sections of your own territory as it is in those of Washington, and the additional time, effort and expense that would be required in going to Washington for Sunday's activity may be devoted to witnessing at your home. But for those who are there on Saturday and wish to stay over for Sunday there will be plenty of territory; and if any of the brethren wish to come to Washington earlier in the week and work with the Washington company, they may do so. Pioneers driving to this campaign and wishing to make a few stops en route may apply to any company service director for territory and this will be the authority for the service directors to fully cooperate with them by supplying territory and any other assistance they may be able to give. We hope to have at least 1,000 workers ready for service Friday evening; but may we again here emphasize that if you cannot get to Washington by Friday evening, don't go at all.

Workers who have been cooperating with the divisional leaders at Baltimore, Philadelphia or Richmond will please fill in the "Registration Slip" (printed below in this Bulletin) and forward the necessary information to your divisional leader. ## Needles for Portable Transcription Machine Records

Great care must be taken in playing Brother Rutherford's transcription records and in the type of needle used. The clarity and effectiveness of the presentation is dependent upon the attention given to these details.

For the very best results the Columbia electrical transcription needles should be used. Do not use any other type of needle on Brother Rutherford's records, and when purchasing your needles clearly state that they are to be used for playing "333 revolutions per minute" records.

If you are unable to obtain this type of needle locally, the Society will be glad to procure them and send them to you. The Columbia needles come in packages of 100, at 25c a package.
