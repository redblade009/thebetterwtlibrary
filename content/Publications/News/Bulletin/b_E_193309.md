The appropriateness of this title, Kingdom Proclamation Period, at this time is emphasized by the statement appearing in the August 1 Watchtower: "Now there is revealed to those who have taken their stand on Jehovah's side the great truth that there is rapidly approaching a time of unprecedented prosperity and blessings for the human race. Those who are diligent to grasp the truths that are placed before them are lifted for above the present sad conditions of this world and see the incontrovertible proof that the time of deliverance is at hand."

This marvelous vision is given to the anointed now for their encouragement in pushing the battle to the gate. The insufferable arrogance and defiance of the Devil and his representatives before Jehovah is increasing daily. They are desperately straining every nerve to stop the activity of Jehovah's witnesses. The opposition is world-wide, and it can truly be said now that 'all nations are being gathered against Jerusalem to battle'. God is desperately trying to keep the people in ignorance of the Kingdom, but he cannot succeed. With the vision of the Kingdom which the Lord has given his anointed, enabling them to see the Inspector General of Jehovah's host inspecting the city of Jerusalem and measuring the earth, they realize that he is cognizant of all that is taking place and is directing the issue to the praise of Jehovah's name. They have implicit confidence in him and those of the truly anointed cannot be turned aside.

In Escape to the Kingdom Jehovah has equipped the anointed with the most pointed message ever delivered upon the earth since the days of our Lord, proving beyond all doubt that the battle of the great day of God Almighty is imminent; that the so-called "Holy Year" will provide no way of escape for the people and that the kingdom of God under Christ is here and it is the only refuge. This message, when

broadcast over the chain hook-up by Brother Rutherford, created panic among the enemy's forces. They did everything within their power, bringing all conceivable pressure to bear upon the radio stations to keep them from broadcasting it, but to no avail. It went forth under the Lord's direction. Afterwards, when it was broadcast by transcription over more than 150 radio stations, the enemy's forces renewed their efforts, increased their opposition by bringing intimidation, coercion, and every other weapon they had in their control, to bear upon the radio stations in an endeavor to have the message taken off the air; and now, when this message is going forth by portable transcription machine, we are in receipt of reports already advising that the Catholics are putting forth every effort to have this message stopped in their respective local communities. Where local business men or school boards control the auditorium where the message is to be given they try to bring pressure to bear on these to have the permit to use the auditorium withdrawn; they try to bring pressure to bear on local authorities to have the message prohibited from public parks and other public places; but just as sure as the Lord had the message broadcast over the radio stations in the chain hook-up and over more than 150 stations by electrical transcriptions, just so surely may his people expect success in putting this message on by portable transcription machine in their respective areas. Do not permit anything to stop you. Go forward with implicit confidence. Insist on the various ones with whom you contract to use their hail that they carry out their part of the contract. This is a real fight and must be pushed with determination and zeal.

My beloved Brethren:

The Scriptures and the physical facts abundantly prove that the battle of the great day of God Almighty is near at hand. There are millions of persons of good will who desire to learn the way of escape. Jehovah has committed to his witnesses the great privilege of informing the people that he is the Almighty God, and that the only way of escape is to his kingdom.

The enemy will make a desperate effort to prevent you from doing this witness work, but remember that you are a representative of Jehovah and against such no weapon can prevail. (Isaiah 54:17) Be of good courage and continue to sing Jehovah's praises.

Your brother and servant by His grace,

## Service Leaders from Bethel for Kingdom Proclamation Period

Appointments will be made of service leaders from the Bethel family to participate in the work with companies within a radius of 200 miles of Brooklyn for the first and second week-ends of Kingdom Proclamation Period. Companies within this radius desiring to have one of these brethren serve with them should write to the Service Department as early as possible, so that the schedule may be made up. ## No. 116 Bible Reduced to 75c

We are pleased to announce that from now on the No. 116 Bible may be placed with the people whenever a contribution of 75c is received, instead of \$1.00 as heretofore. So that the brethren can afford to do this, there will be a corresponding reduction in the company, pioneer and auxiliary rates.

In receiving the Kingdom message from Jehovah's witnesses, many of the people wish to have a Bible to prove that which they hear and read in the books. This special No. 116 Bible has been purchased from an outside concern to meet this demand, but it is to be remembered that the placing of these Bibles with the people should be along with some of the books. Unless the people have some key to understanding that which they read therein, there is little likelihood of their getting an idea of the real issues which are important for everyone to understand at the present time.
