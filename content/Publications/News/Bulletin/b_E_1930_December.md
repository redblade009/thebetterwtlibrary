# WATCH TOWER

# BULLETIN

December 1, 1930

## 10,000,000 TESTIMONIES 1931 QUOTA

### Expect 6,125 Workers in the Field Weekly

#### Four Testimonies per Worker Each Hour

Hope to Place 5,590,000

Pieces of Literature

#### New Prices a Good Thing for 1931 Witness

Last year's report shows a splendid amount of work done in the interest of the Lord's kingdom and for the magnifying of the name and word of Jehovah. We are firmly convinced that the witness work will increase throughout the coming years, even though there are distressing conditions in the world. The results of the past year would make glad the heart of any unstinted son of God and should create in such a one an even greater soul and determination to serve him. The Lord, through his organization, blessed his people throughout the year as they made efforts to honor his name. The plans set forth by the Society last year received the Lord's rich blessing and approval, and this is most manifest in the results of the work accomplished by the workers in the field. Again it is the privilege of the Society to set forth, in this Bulletin, the outline of the work that we hope to accomplish to the story of God during the coming year, 1931.

First of all, we have set our quota for this new year at "ten million testimonies". This sounds like a lot, and it is, but by the Lord's grace this tremendous witness can be given in the United States by those of the remnant in this country. This quota was set on the following basis:

The average number of class workers in the field during the past year was 4,375 reporting weekly. This was an increase of 475 class workers over the previous year, or a 12-percent increase. During the 150,000-Hour Week there were 8,692 class workers who engaged in door-to-door witnessing for that week alone. This number of workers also included the various foreign-speaking brethren, who, during the past year, have taken a very active part in the field work, and who also have been greatly blessed by the Lord. We believe that during the coming year many more of these foreign-speaking brethren will see their opportunity of service more clearly, as will also many of the English-speaking brethren who in the past have participated in special service weeks only. This being true, and we feel sure it will be, because of the increasing light, we estimate that we would have at least an 8-percent increase for 1931. This would bring the class workers up to an average of 4,725 reporting

regularly, each week. However, from the ranks of these class workers we hope that during the year we shall have on our list 81 new auxiliary colporteurs and also 75 new pioneer colporteurs. These, of course, we would have to deduct from the number of class workers. This would leave 4,567 class workers who we anticipate will be actively engaging in the Lord's service each week. We have therefore set the quota of class workers at 4,500 reporting to the class directors each week. We can do it. Look at October's figures and keep up the good work.

#### AUXILIARY COLOPORTURE WORKERS

During 1930 an average of 544 auxiliary colporteurs reported weekly. This is an increase of 33 percent over 1929. That is an increase. If we have had half of this increase during the coming year, or 15 percent, we may expect to have 81 additional auxiliary colporteurs reporting each week during 1931. This would make a total of 625 auxiliaries reporting weekly. During the

winter months some of the auxiliary colporteurs drop off, but we hope that this will not take place during this coming year, because in the cities many of the apartments and homes with vestibules can be worked during these months. Why not try them this winter? We are setting the quota of auxiliary colporteurs at 625 reporting each week, and we hope that sometime during the year there will be a peak of a thousand auxiliary workers actively engaged in the field service. Do you think we can make it by the middle of the summer in 1931?

Why not write to the Society and investigate the splendid arrangement which the Lord has made to enable you to spend twelve hours in the witness work each week, and be one of the thousand?

Every week during the past year an average of 938 pioneer colporteurs have been reporting to this office. This is an increase of 11 percent over the previous year. ### New Prices on Books Effective January 1

#### Three Books for \$1.00, Special Combination

#### Other Combinations Very Attractive

Haven't the times been hard during this past year? Yet, in spite of all the unemployment and perplexing conditions of this country the Lord's people have gone on as never before, and now, in the midst of all this, the Lord is ready to meet the needs of the people. It has been made possible during the past year, only by the Lord's grace, to bring down slightly the cost of production and distribution of our books, and it would be just like the Lord and his organization to pass this reduction on to the groaning creation of mankind.

On January 1, 1931, the new prices of the Society's literature will go into effect. The Harp of God, Deliverance, Creation, Reconciliation, Government, Life and Prophecy can be placed with the people of 25c each, or any three of these books for $1.00. Isn't that a wonderful offer? All seven of them can be offered for $2.20. The Light books (One and Two) may be offered to the people for only 75c. These two books with four booklets will make another ideal combination for \$1.00. With the depressing con-

ditions and the shortage of money among the poor, the dollar combination will seem reasonable to them, and we urge that during the coming year all the friends stress this combination particularly. If you are in some territory where the people are a little better situated financially, it would be better to offer the nine books for the new price of $2.00, or any six books, including Light, for $1.95. We believe that with these new prices going into effect January 1 many more books will be placed in the hands of the people, as well as booklets. The booklets are going over fine at the present time, and will continue to do so, because of the attractive offer of nine booklets for fifty cents or four for a quarter.

We have the greatest message to give to the people, a message of real comfort and cheer to everyone, and at the same time we have the message in a permanent form at a most reasonable price. The Lord is good to his people. With this splendid new arrangement for the coming year it seems quite evident that the number of books and booklets mentioned in our estimate, as given in the "Quota" article of the Bulletin, will be reached.

### Your Year Book and Calendar

#### Each of the Anointed Should Have One

#### President's Report Excellent

These will be ready for you shortly, and we should like to say a few words about them. First of all we will talk about your Year Book. This Year Book has in it the best report ever made by the president of the Society. It is because the friends are actively engaged in the field service proclaiming the message of the kingdom that makes this report what it is. So then every one of the Lord's anointed should avail himself of this new Year Book for 1931, which contains Brother Rutherford's report and also an explanation of the year text chosen by the Society. There is also a text for each day of the year, together with a brief comment in consideration and explanation of the text. A very interesting and helpful feature of the Year Book this year is that at the end of each daily comment reference is made to the Watch Tower or book from which the comment is taken. This will give you an opportunity to look up points that may have slipped your mind or to more fully examine some detail of the text. The Year Book is offered to all at 50c a copy.

And now for your calendar. The Society is printing a very beautiful calendar for the year, and we know that everyone will like it. It is truly a calendar for the Lord's people because it gives an outline of the big features of the year, giving the most important dates as far as the Lord's people are concerned. You will want this calendar hanging on your wall, where you can refer to it from time to time. These calendars are offered in lots of 50 at 25c apiece, or when bought singly, at 30c. You will need both the Year Book and the calendar during this coming year.

#### Directors, Please Note

This Bulletin is being mailed to all Watch Tower subscribers. We are sending only a limited supply to the class directors. Each director will please inquire and see that each one in the class has a copy of the Bulletin. If in his additional supply he has not enough for all those who do not receive it through the Watch Tower list, he should write to the Service Department for as many additional copies as are required.

# Biggest Electrically Recorded Program in the World

## Over 150 Stations to Begin December 7

By January 4 More Will Be on the Air

Did You Get a Station?
If Not, Try Again

### Radio Folders Being Shipped

Responses coming in from classes all over the country in regard to the new feature of radio broadcasting are most encouraging. Up to the present three are over 150 stations signed up, and on December 7 these will begin broadcasting the electrically recorded lectures of Brother Rutherford. These lectures are short, only 1½ minutes, but they are full of punch and drive home the joint, giving a logical and clear explanation of God's purpose.

These classes that begin broadcasting these lectures in their localities on December 7 will, we feel sure, have a splendid introduction to their field service work. With the 150 or more stations now prepared to broadcast these lectures the entire population of the United States will have an opportunity to listen in.

Many classes have asked whether they cannot go on the air with these lectures at a later date if they can then make the arrangements. We are glad to say "Yes" in answer to this question. If any classes can make arrangements to go on the air beginning with January 4, please let us know at the very earliest possible date. If you have a radio station in your city and it does not carry the WAYCITRONES programs you might see whether or not it is possible to make arrangements to have these splendid lectures broadcast. Stations that heretofore would not take any of our programs are now doing so. This is the Lord's work and it will prosper. If you want any information in regard to how to go about this matter and you have not received such information from the Society, please write us. The Society has written to many classes explaining this means of getting the truth into the homes of the people, but we have not received a response from all the classes to whom we wrote. We feel sure that after you know that such a tremendous effort has been put forth to make this broadcast a success you will want to try to get the best available stations and put on these WAYCITRONES programs.

New radio folders are ready for shipment. These were held up in order that as many as possible of the stations engaged for the new transcription service might be listed on the folder. The first edition of the folder contains a total of 143 stations carrying the WAYCITRONES programs and the time when they are on the air with this program. Whether or not there is a station in your immediate vicinity on this list, you should use these radio slips in connection with your door-to-door witnessing. The Lord has opened up to us a wonderful field for service in the interest of his Kingdom. We are happy that we were privileged to take advantage of this means of spreading the Kingdom message. These classes and colporteurs who have placed orders with us for 1500 and under will have their orders filled complete, but all orders over 1500 we have taken the liberty to cut approximately one-third and even less in some cases. This was

done that you might not be overstocked with slips showing the old prices of the literature. If your order is filled short, please keep in mind that within a few weeks we will fill the balance of your order with a new slip which will have all of the radio stations listed and also give the new prices on the literature. We believe this will be satisfactory to you.

This will be the highest average pioneer reporting each week for a year we shall have ever had, if we do it. It is possible. What do you say, you pioneers?

The average number of pioneer colporteurs reporting each week during the last five months has been 1,025. But the winter is coming on, and in the past some felt that they must hibernate. Why cease working? Why not try to stick through the winter? The Lord will bless you. He promises that to everyone who really wants to serve. However, we hope that by the time the fiscal year 1931 ends we shall have on our list many more than a thousand colporteurs, setting as the peak 1,400 pioneers. They are a happy crowd and the Lord blesses them.

Having in mind now the average number of class workers for the year, which is to be 4,500, we expect that they will put in an average of 4.2 hours in the service each week. This is an increase of only 1/10 of an hour, or just six minutes more in the service each week this year than last. But by the end of the year, if this number of class workers diligently engage in the Lord's service and put in the average of 4.2 hours each week, it will amount to 982,800 hours.

The same is true of the auxiliary colporteurs. If we add just 1/10 of an hour, just six minutes, to what was done last year, which was 10.4 hours, the 625 auxiliary colporteurs reporting each week could make a total of 341,250 hours. If they really put in their twelve hours each, which every one should average weekly, we would go away over that. Let's keep pulling at that 12-hour mark. Many of them make it and do better, and that helps the average.

The same applies also to the pioneer colporteurs, who averaged much better in 1930 than in 1929. They put in 28.44 hours each week. If they add 3½ minutes and bring this up to 23½ hours each week for 1931, one thousand pioneer colporteurs would put in 1,222,000 hours during the year. Adding the totals of the class workers, pioneers and auxiliary colporteurs, we find that the grand total is 2,546,050 hours. This is equivalent to nearly 201 years, three centuries, for one worker. That is a long time. If each worker and colporteur gives 4 testimonies each hour, and 2,546,050 hours are put in during the year, then it will be an easy matter for us to reach our quota, which is 10,000,000 testimonies. Think of it! At the end of 1931 you will have spoken to 1/11 of the population of the United States!

With these hard times and depressing conditions among the people, there is one thing that the Lord's people can do, and that is, call from door to door and explain to the people the Kingdom and its blessings. The definition of a testimony was given in the last Bulletin. We hope that all will have this clearly in mind, so that their reports will be accurate. New workers slips are being prepared by the Society, and these will enable each worker to keep track of his testimonies and the result of each one. During the coming year let every one make an earnest effort to report to the director the number of testimonies, or if you are a colporteur, report them on your weekly report card to this office. If 10,000,000 testimonies are given during the coming year, the books are bound to be placed, because some will be waiting for the truth, being prepared in advance by the wonderful radio arrangement of broadcasting the truth. We feel sure that there will be an increase in the distribution of the literature. In addition to the 10,000,000 testimonies given by the workers, the radio will most likely reach many millions more during the year.

An estimate has also been made of what the Society believes we may expect to place along the line of books and booklets for the year, but we are not setting this as a quota. We believe, however, that you will be interested in knowing our calculations. Of course, the Society must make some plans and arrangements and try to determine in advance the number of books and booklets we hope to place with the people, so that contracts can be made with the paper manufacturers, cloth makers, and other manufacturers, in order to get the lowest possible prices on materials used. This will permit the making of the books at the lowest cost. In considering the distribution of books and booklets you must keep in mind that the Society has made a new arrangement as to the price of the books for the year 1931. Another article in this Bulletin goes a little more into detail in regard to this splendid provision which the Lord's organization has made for the benefit of the public. Having these prices in mind, then, we believe that the class workers, 4,500 brethren organized for

service in different cities, will place 700,000 clothbound books, as well as 1,000,000 booklets. The auxiliary colporteurs, we believe, will place during the year 1931, 240,000 clothbound books and 350,000 booklets. The pioneers, it is estimated, will place 1,300,000 clothbound books and 1,400,000 booklets. The total number of clothbound books which will probably be placed in the United States during the fiscal year 1931 should be 2,246,000, and booklets 3,250,000, making a grand total of 5,300,000 books and booklets. But then, if we can do better, we will do it, won't we?

The report for the month of October, which is the first month of our fiscal year, is most encouraging. Read that report and you will rejoice to see that the first month of the year is the best one we have ever had, so the prospects are very good.

We have covered almost everything now as to the details of the year's work and what, by the Lord's grace, we hope to do. Let each one of the unnoticed put his shoulder to the wheel and 'help push the chariot up Zion's hill'. Every additional hour put in the field will assist wonderfully in doing this. From time to time, however, the Bulletin will bring to you the different special drive weeks that have been arranged. We do not want to mention here the names or dates of these drive weeks, because we are going to have a very attractive calendar this year, and you will find it all outlined on that calendar. But we will say at this time that the first drive week of the year will be from January 31 up to and including February 8. This week is going to be called "Midwinter Week", and is outlined in detail in this Bulletin. Do not forget to put your order in for the new calendar, so that you will be posted regarding these special weeks, which bring such a tremendous amount of joy to you at different times during the year. Our hope is that every one of the Lord's unnoticed throughout the earth will make every effort during this coming year to honor the name and word of Jehovah as they have never done before; and it should be the earnest desire of every one to press on, fighting for the Lord's kingdom with the whole heart, mind, soul and strength. Let us keep at it until the end.

## Something About Two Carloads of Freight

### Don't Forget Your 100-Pound Order

We still find that quite a few of the class directors and colporteurs who place orders with the Society send in orders for less than a hundred pounds. We are glad to say, however, that within the last six or eight months there has been a great improvement and these cases are becoming fewer. The classes and colporteurs can greatly help the Society in maintaining the new reduced rates which were made, and which go into effect January 1, by placing orders for a hundred pounds of books, or more, each time. Always keep in mind that we can send a hundred pounds of freight to you just as cheaply as we can send fifty, sixty or seventy pounds, because freight rates are based on a hun-

dred-pound minimum. Last year alone we could have shipped two carloads of freight absolutely free if all the friends had ordered books and booklets in hundred-pound lots instead of ordering fifty or sixty pounds at a time. In other words, we had to pay freight on two carloads of books which we did not ship. Had all cooperated, this needless expense would have been saved for the Kingdom work. We again ask all the friends to bear in mind when ordering that if possible you order at least 140 clothbound books, which is equal to 100 pounds. The booklets weigh 15 pounds to a hundred booklets. It takes about 750 booklets to make a hundred pounds of freight. We feel sure that during the coming year, with your hearty cooperation, we shall not lose the charges on two carloads of freight.

# Two Months of Radio Service Will Have Prepared Field

## Midwinter Week - January 31 to February 8

### \$1.00 Combination to Be Stressed

#### 'In or Out of Season' the Lord's People Serve

Midwinter Week, January 31 to February 8, is the next big thing to look forward to. Have your warmest clothes ready. Arrange to have as much time set aside as possible, so that during this 9-day week you can be in the field with all those who really love the Lord and his Kingdom, singing forth his praises. Some may wonder at a special service week's coming in the heart of winter; but why should God's people, of all people, cease from fulfilling their divine commission because of the seasons, especially when the Bible gives the assurance that neither winter nor anything else will stop the onward flow of God's river of life-giving water. Unlike other rivers, which freeze up in the wintertime, the Lord has truly foretold that 'in that day . . . living waters shall go out from Jerusalem; half of them toward the former sea, and half of them toward the hinder sea; in summer and in winter shall it be'. (Zech. 14:8) Jehovah foreknow that 'in that day' which has already begun, his people would be willing to 'preach the gospel in season and out of season', and that in winter or summer they would be joyfully inviting 'those who hear', 'those who thirst' and 'whosoever will' to come and take off the water of life freely. When one considers that more than 150 radio stations in this country alone are broadcasting the Kingdom message each week, raising their voices together and together singing the same message, and the thousands of class workers, sharpshooters, pioneer and auxiliary colporteurs who are bearing the same life-giving message to the people throughout the earth, stopping neither for winter nor for summer, one can better realize that we actually are living 'in that day' long ago foretold when the opportunity to partake of the living waters would be given to all regardless of seasons. No wonder the prequel of old in ecstasy exclaimed, 'How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good things, that publisheth peace!' (Isa. 52:7) It gives us a thrill of joy, therefore, to announce this Midwinter Week, because we know how enthusiastically these who really love the Lord will respond, and the blessings that will come to them because they do. Because of this assurance in God's Word, we are looking forward to many testimonies being given to the honor of Jehovah's name.

Last year the anointed put forth a special effort during the last week of January with the book _Prophecy_. The results were very encouraging. This year the plan for Midwinter Week is to offer the people the new \$1.00 combinations which are to be made up of any three of the books _The Harp, Deliverance, Creation, Reconciliation, Government, Life, and Prophecy_, or of _Light_ (Books One and Two) and four booklets. It is suggested that each worker canvass with the entire set of nine books and booklets, offering each one the opportunity of making his own selection of the particular combination desired. By presenting all the books, the entire set is displayed;

and if rest interest is shown, the worker is in a good position to explain that six books may be had for $1.35, or all nine for $2.00. But bear in mind that special stress should be placed on the $1.00 combinations. We believe that this amount will be more within the reach of the majority of the people at this particular time of the year than a larger combination of books. If the $1.00 combination is not desired, then drop to four booklets for 25c. Remember that, as our commission is to preach the Kingdom gospel, a record is to be kept of the number of testimonies given.

That week being in the middle of winter, service directors should begin to map out their territory early, so that, if possible, there will be plenty of territory to work in apartment houses, dwellings over stores, or houses with vestibules, enabling the workers to be out of the cold or inclement weather as much as possible. It would be well for each service committee to actually go over the ground which is to be covered, to see that it is the best available for meeting these conditions. In order that the workers can spend as much time as possible in the witness work.

It has come to our attention that during these special service weeks some of the classes have arranged canvassing parties which meet and have lunch prepared for them at the home of one of the friends. In some cases elaborate meals have been prepared, thus taking out of the field service many of the workers who have had to work hard all morning to prepare the food and all afternoon to clean up. This has encouraged some to come out to eat, and sit around and talk, giving more attention to the social end than to the field service. While it is not the _Bulletin's_ desire to criticize, we suggest that instead of making such elaborate social occasions it would be far better that each one bring his own lunch or that a few simple things be prepared. Let the real purpose of the work be kept in mind. Those who do not have the strength to return to the field work in the afternoon, after having been out all morning, should go home and rest up so that they may have the strength to go out the next day, rather than sit around and visit for several hours.

To serve as witness for the great

Jehovah is the greatest privilege that any creature could possibly enjoy; and when Jehovah through his organization arranges that a special effort be put forth at any time, it is really important that all those who are in line for the Kingdom and wish to show forth his praises consider such arrangements carefully and prayerfully to see that he is using his time and strength to best advantage.

#### 26 Years in Colporteur Service and Still Going

We have just read an interesting letter from a brother who has been in the colporteur work for years. It was so encouraging to us that we thought you would like to read part of it. He says: 'I am now 20 years old and in good physical condition, and still in the colporteur service, in which I have been for 20 years. I thank our dear Lord for this privilege of service. I will now make a radical change in which I expect to leave here after Christmas for — and will stay in the service there until death takes too far to my reward.'

Such a spirit of devotion to the Lord and his cause is conclusive proof that the Lord has some on the earth who think more of him and proving their integrity to him than they do of a life of ease and comfort. Eighty years old, in good health, and all this after twenty-six years in the colporteur work! That's a record few can equal. And the best part of it all is that he's just as determined now as he was twenty-six years ago. If not more so, to stick to the finish. When you behold such devotion, is there any doubt in your mind that the Lord is 'gathering out a people for his name'?

#### How to Report Light

_Light_, Book One and Book Two, is a two-book combination. When _Light_, Book One and Book Two, is placed with an individual, it is to be reported on the class weekly report and workers weekly report slip under the respective headings, and is also to be reported as a two-book combination. Some of the directors are failing to report _Light_, Book One and Book Two, as a two-book combination. These are also to be reported as two books when included in a placement with other books. For illustration: _If The Harp, Deliverance and Light_ (Books One and Two) are placed, they constitute a four-book combination.

### Information Regarding Invoices

#### and Credit Memos

#### Colporteurs and Classes Should Check Monthly Statements

Some of the colporteurs and classes have written to us asking why they have not received a credit memo in acknowledgment of remittances sent in together with their orders. If they will look at the bottom of the invoice for such orders, they will find this acknowledgment. Only when remittances are sent in alone or merely with subscriptions are they acknowledged on credit memos.

The invoices and credit memos should be kept and checked back

with the monthly statement, to see that all entries have been made correctly. Compare the date and number with those appearing on the statement, as well as the amount. If you have invoices and credit memos for December and have just received your November statement, do not expect to see December transactions on your November statement. Considerable undue correspondence has been caused by inquiries regarding such matters. The monthly statement shows only the transactions during the current month for which the statement is issued, added to the previous balance of the account. Any transactions occurring after the last day of that month will appear on the next month's statement.

### Correspondents

We are continually receiving mail from service directors, service-treasurers, colporteurs and sharpshooters in which the only thing in the envelope is a money order or check. Usually these brethren put their name on the outside of the envelope; that is the only way we have of knowing where it comes from. They do not say what is to be done with the check, whether it is for Good Hope, for radio fund, or for their account. This causes considerable confusion and work. We would ask that both one sending any remittance to this office send an accompanying letter stating what is to be done with the money. Be sure that your letters are always addressed to the proper department. Many times colporteur correspondence is held up because it is not addressed to the colporteur department. Therefore, in order that we may cooperate most effectively in magnifying the name of Jehovah at this time, see that each letter you forward is addressed to whatever department you intend it to be for. Another point is that much of the mail coming to this office does not contain the address of the party writing the letter, making it necessary to search through Watch Tower files in order to ascertain the address before we can answer the correspondence. Each letter should contain, in the upper right hand corner, the address of the party writing the letter. This also will save considerable work and confusion.

### How to Report Time

Some of the classes and colporteurs have been confused regarding the method for reporting the time spent in witnessing. When an individual goes out into the service, he should begin to reckon his time from the moment he makes his first call until the last one is completed, taking time out for lunch, of course. If he is out over this period. The only exception to this rule is in the case of canvassing parties which must together at some central point and then leave for the territory. In such cases only, the time each worker puts in begins to count at the moment the party leaves the place of meeting until he individually completes his last call. In such cases the time of the driver of the car should also be reckoned provided he makes a reasonable number of calls, by which is meant that he does not merely go out to drive the car and does not pass by his opportunities of doing some of the witnessing.

#### Watch Tower and Golden Age Subscriptions

The office would appreciate it very much if the director, when sending in subscriptions for _The Watch Tower_ and _The Golden Age_, would kindly write them all out on Watch Tower or Golden Age slips. It will assist us in keeping a proper check if the names of the subscribers, forwarded by the directors, are listed in their letters when transmitting the subscriptions to us.

# GOD'S ANOINTED ALWAYS GLAD TO SERVE

## Many Privileges of Service Opened

### Door-to-Door Witnessing Is Essential

Why Not Be a Pioneer,
an Auxiliary, or a
Class Worker?

#### Write the Society Regarding the Field Service

We believe that the manner in which the Lord used his people during the past year is going to be an inspiration to each one who is of the anointed. That the great God of the universe should condescend to use human creatures in the vindication of his name and giving a witness to the people regarding what he is going to do, should thrill the heart of each one who is thus honored.

Through his Word he says, "My son, he wise, and make my heart glad, that I may answer him that reproacheth me." The Devil has been reproaching the heavenly Father for centuries. He raised an issue which never should have been raised, namely, whether the great Jehovah God is worthy of the love and allegiance of his creatures. Jehovah is the Giver of every good and perfect gift. Those who have been taken into the covenant for the Kingdom and have tasted of his goodness know this to be true. Jehovah says that it is possible for these to make his heart glad by being wise (the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom). Therefore, fear of disguising Jehovah, and obedience to his commands now, is really being wise, and following this course brings joy to Jehovah because it proves that he can place upon the earth a few individuals who will maintain their integrity under any and every test the Devil brings upon them.

During the past year Satan has used every power of his command to turn the people away from God. He has caused oppression of every description. Unemployment is prevalent throughout the country, causing intense suffering from want of food and clothing among the poor people. Many of the Lord's people who were dependent for their sustenance on some one of the branches of worldly occupations have been thrown out of employment. They have diligently searched for something to do and are unable to find it. We are wondering if the Lord is not permitting this particular condition in order to awaken some of his people who are able to devote their time and energy to his service but who instead of so doing have been using their energies in some branch of Satan's organization. Many of the Lord's people spend days, weeks, and even months, going all over the country trying to find employment in some worldly pursuit, overlooking entirely the fact that every _Welsh Tower_ and practically every _Bulletin_ issued by the Society calls attention to the provision the Lord has for the employment of his people now. The Lord tells us that "the steps of a good man are ordered of the Lord, and he delietheth in his way." Again, God's prophet says, "I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous for-sales, nor his seed begging bread." And again, he says, "Prove me now

herewith, saith Jehovah, and see if I will not pour out upon you a blessing such as you will not be able to hold."

Surely the time has come when each one who is a part of God's family and of his anointed should take the Lord at his word, instead of permitting Satan to keep them inactive or divert their energies to some branch of his organization. They should realize the fallacy of depending upon that thing which is an abomination to the Lord and will be utterly wiped out root and branch. "Seek ye first the Kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you." We are convinced that if many of the friends will do this their blessing and joy in the service and the Lord's provision for their temporal needs will be such that they will never again return to secular employment but will continue joyfully in the Lord's service.

In order to help all who wish to participate in any branch of the Lord's service, we are consecrating in this _Bulletin_ the different provisions the Lord is making through the Society for participating in the Kingdom work. The first and great objective of every one of God's anointed should be, if possible, to have all his time spent in the Lord's service. In order that such might be able to enjoy the blessing of giving their entire time and strength in singing forth his praises, the Lord has opened up the pioneer colporteur service. To all whose chief aim in life is to magnify the name of Jehovah, the pioneer colporteur service offers them a wonderful opportunity. No one, however, should enroll with the idea that this is a loafer's job. Experience has shown that the Lord's blessings are on those who are diligent in the service, those who are on the job regularly each day, the same as they would be if working at some secular employment. The requirement for pioneer colporteurs is full time in the service, averaging at least 25 hours a week in actual canvassing. This is not the maximum, but is the very lowest one may do to continue in the pioneer service; and unless one feels that this average can be maintained, we do not believe that the pioneer service should be attempted. For those who are desirous of availing themselves of this, the Society has made special arrangements to carry part of the burden by offering to those who are duly enrolled as pioneer colporteurs the privilege of obtaining literature at less than cost. Those who wish to enter this branch of the service, please write the Colporteur Department for further information.

For those who cannot give their entire time to the Lord's service, because of infirmities or because they find that they must devote some time to secular employment in providing for Scriptural dependents. Here is the auxiliary service. The auxiliary service requirements are 12 hours each week in door-to-door field service. Auxiliary colporteurs may work in conjunction with the local ecclesia or, if they are isolated, they may receive an assignment of territory direct from this office. To help those who

enter this branch of the service to meet their expenses while in the work special rates are also granted, which may be had upon request. There are still other friends who are living in isolated sections of the country and not associated with any ecclesia, or some who may be associated with an ecclesia but the ecclesia is not organized for service. We recommend to such that if there are four or more friends in the class who can devote a reasonable amount of time to service work each week, they should arrange to organize into a service class, with a service organization of director, stockkeeper, and, if the class is large enough, a service treasurer, so that they can unfitly cooperate in magnifying the name of Jehovah and declaring the purposes of his Kingdom. Where there are fewer than four who can reasonably be expected to participate in the service work the Society has provided a sharpshooter arrangement, so that an individual, either brother or sister, who is able to engage in house-to-house witness work for an average of two hours a week may be enrolled as a sharpshooter and receive literature, bulletins and regional director visits the same as the organized classes. If there are two or three in the class who can average between them two or more hours a week they may appoint one of the friends as sharpshooter, and the account will be opened in that individual's name, and territory assigned and books shipped to them, and each one can do what he is able in the way of service, receiv-

ing books and territory assignment from the sharpshooter and reporting back to him the amount of territory covered and literature placed so that the sharpshooter can include it in his report to the Society.

Further details covering all the branches of service mentioned above will be gladly supplied by writing to the Service Department or the Colporteur Department. Each one of the above branches of service requires regular reports from those who participate in it. All colporteurs and classes are required to report once a week, regularly, and sharpshooters are required to report once a month, showing the literature placed and the number of workers participating in the work.

Jesus made this statement: "Here is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit." The bearing of fruit means carrying to others the life-sustaining fruit of the Kingdom, which is the truth concerning God and his purposes. All who really love God are anxious to increase their opportunities of bearing this fruit because it vindicates the name of Jehovah, it gives a witness concerning the purposes of Jehovah, and it brings hope and peace to the hearts of those who receive it as of the Lord. Surely, then, every one will want to avail himself of one of these branches of the service. If you have been desiring to participate in some branch of the service in the past and were not familiar with the requirements, write us now regarding any one of these branches and we shall be glad to cooperate with you in getting started.

## Your Work and Its Effects

### "Joy to the World"

Wouldn't you sometimes like to look into the homes of some of those with whom you have placed the books and see just how the message they contain is being received? Haven't you often wondered whether or not the people were sharing in the happiness which you had when placing the books with them? The following letter has just been received from a man who recently obtained the set of 9 childhood books from an auxiliary colporteur.

"Nov. 16, 1930
(International Bible Students Ass'n,
Dear Brethren in Christ Jesus:
"September 29, 1930, we purchased from Mrs. B. a full set of books. . . . We are thanking God for them and will say that it was God answering prayer. We were members of a church and all the while there was no comfort there; so we were praying and asking God to show us the light. So we know now that on Sept. 30, 1930, God did answer our prayer, and now we understand and know the truth of God and his great love. Oh, it means so much to us at this time; because last April we gave our little girl up, and now we understand just how we are going to meet her and that it will not be long! Although we have had the books only six weeks, we have learned a wonderful lot from God's Word with the aid of the books. We can't thank you enough for your effort and that you have been willing for God to use you in this wonderful work; and I am praying that the Devil will never be able to squeeze in on your work.

"Now there are a few important questions we would like to ask you. What stations do you broadcast your programs over? Also where will your next convention be held, and on what date? . . . Another thing, we understand you print a magazine known as _The Golden Age_. If satisfactory, we would like to have a copy of this. Also, we have four children that we have taken out of the Sunday school: ages, a boy 17, girls 13, 10 and 7; and since the books we have are out of reach of some of them, we thought it possible you might have some study they could comprehend. May God bless you, is our prayer.

"Yours in Christ,
(Signed) —

"P.S. In answering this letter we want you to feel free to speak plainly, as you have in your books, and give us the best information you have at hand."

As a result of this one testimony made by the sister who placed these books, real happiness is being enjoyed by this family of six.

#### What Constitutes a "Worker"

In some cases directors report an individual as a worker for every time he goes out in the service. If Brother B. went out seven different times a week, some directors report seven workers. It should not be reported in this way. Each individual is to be reported as a worker; and if he goes out five, six or seven times a week, he is still to be reported as one worker on that week's report card.
