# Each Worker Should Obtain

14 Signatures Daily

The brethren assembled in a glorious convention at Atlanta, Ga., and there were more than four thousand. At the Saturday afternoon session a "Warning Declaration" was unanimously passed by all the conventioners. There was one dissenting vote, a Catholic priest.

A new booklet is being prepared containing the "warning declaration", and after reading this every one of the Lord's anointed and the Jonahah brethren will want to go right down the line with the petition, the three million booklets and several hundred thousand clothbound books. It will be a glorious fight, and even though winter is coming on, the weather is cold and inclement, each and every one will want to perform his part of the work to the honor of Jehovah's name.

There are three very important duties to carry forward during December and January: the petition, the new booklet, and the bound-book campaign. Each one has its part in the arrangement for the field service, and the outline set out herein should be followed closely by each company, sharpshooter arrangement, auxiliary and pioneer.

The quota of signers to be obtained has been set at ten million. It is easy to imagine someone, whose faith is weak, saying it can't be done, but the Lord through his Word makes void any such attitude. "WE CAN DO ALL THINGS IN HIM THAT STRENGTHENETH US."—R.V.

Here are the facts. There are approximately 24,000 witnesses in the United States. If each one obtains 417 signers we shall exceed ten million. But someone will say, "Oh, yes, but how are we going to get 417 each? That is some job." That is not so difficult when it is viewed from its proper angle. All of the brethren will have at least 30 days in which to do this work; some will have more, but, allowing just 30 days each, your quota can be obtained by getting 14 signers every day. So you see it is not impossible, by any means, it is true each one will have to do it during noon hours, work

evenings and every available moment, but this is a time of war and a time when everything else must be subservient to the great issue. Always have a Petition sheet with you.

However, these are not all the possibilities that are available for obtaining signatures. The Kingdom work has expanded considerably since last year's petition campaign. The following agencies are brought into action this year: Every radio station carrying transcription records is being supplied with special announcements calling attention to the petition and inviting all who wish to do so to send to the station for one or more of these and circulate them among their friends. The radio representative who is contacting that station will be charged with the responsibility of seeing that he gets these letters from the station and supplies the people with the necessary forms as they require them.

Next, The Golden Age (No. 397) which carries Brother Rutherford's radio speech "TRUTH: SHALL IT BE SUPPRESSED?" will also carry one page as a petition form with a special invitation to all Golden Age subscribers to cut out this page and circulate it among their friends and also write us for as many more forms as they may require. Every sound car meeting is to contain a number of announcements regarding this petition, and the service director or the one who has charge of the meeting is to see to it that adequate provisions are made for those present to sign the petition and obtain additional copies to circulate among their friends as well as giving them an opportunity to get the literature. Sound cars and transcription machines broadcasting on streets and public places while the brethren are witnessing throughout the neighborhood will also make periodic announcements of the petition, that the people may know what it is all about by the time the workers call upon them. Likewise all transcription and phonograph meetings are to include an introduction of the petition with an invitation to all present to have a part in circulating them.

By this means it will be readily seen that the Lord is making adequate provision for all persons of good will in this country to take their stand and to declare them

selves on the Lord's side. Do not, however, depend upon the other agencies to do your part. Set as your quota 14 signers each day; make it your business to get them. If the petition exceeds ten million, well and good; let each one do his part, and the Lord will undoubtedly add His blessing and make this campaign a stupendous witness to the praise of His name.

On the petition sheets is space for the names and addresses of 20 individuals. Be sure to have also the street number, the city, and the state, so that when the time comes the United States Government will have no excuse for not mailing this information to the people of this land. Consignments of the petition blanks have already been made to the companies, pioneers and auxiliaries. Where there are many pioneers, auxiliaries and foreign groups in English company territory the shipment is being made to the director. The first shipment is made up so that each company worker can have 5 sheets, each auxiliary 10, and pioneers 15, to start with. As soon as you need more sheets, write in to the Society and they will be shipped to you immediately.

# How to Obtain the Names

Hand the individual on whom you call the special testimony card. Let him read this. Hand him the books in the wrapper as well as the special booklet. If he is seeking the truth and has 50c he will undoubtedly obtain a combination, which combination will include the special booklet, TRUTH: SHALL IT BE SUPPRESSED, OR WILL CONGRESS PROTECT THE

PEOPLE'S RIGHTS? Whether or not the people decide to take the combination, point out to the person to whom you are witnessing the foreword on the second page, which foreword reads: "One great issue before the American people is whether or not this shall continue to be a land of freedom of speech; or shall the people be like dumb, driven cattle? Read carefully this booklet and then write your congressman what you expect him to do to safeguard your interests." This statement immediately leads to the petition, which petition should then be presented for the name and address. If the person does not contribute 50c for the three bound books, he will, in most cases, contribute 5c for the booklet, and if not, and he is interested in our work, the booklet should be left free if he promises to read it. The petition he will undoubtedly sign.

Do not spend any time explaining details or giving long-winded speeches. Make your calls as short as possible. Let the person read the testimony, and the foreword of the booklet, and sign the petition. Several in one place may wish to sign. As soon as this is done, go to the next-door neighbor.

# Jehovah's witnesses Richly Blessed at Atlanta

## Splendid Witness Given

Jehovah's witnesses assembled at Atlanta, Georgia, for a three-day convention, and Jehovah richly blessed all who attended and millions who were not able to attend. The big feature of the convention was a radio chain hook-up which covered the southern and eastern parts of the United States. The short-wave stations used carried the message almost everywhere. From Maine to Florida, from the Gulf states to California, into the northern parts of Canada, across the oceans to points remote, Hawaii, Switzerland, South Africa, the message was heard at the same instant that the large audience that packed the auditorium heard the lecture. Four sound cars amplifying the message were located at different points in Atlanta. A mighty shout of acclamation went up in all parts of the world when the lovers of righteous

ness were asked if they wanted a righteous ruler.

This was truly a thrilling convention.

The report of the work accomplished in Atlanta is very gratifying. The largest number of workers engaging in the field service at one time was 1,514. There were 23,184 pieces of literature distributed. The brethren during three days worked 11,570 hours and gave 76,418 testimonies. Literature was left in 20,815 homes. What a splendid witness! The pioneers flocked to the convention, 931 of them registering. A regular village was formed by the house-cars and trailers of these pioneers. Everyone was richly blessed. And now, with plenty of work ahead, and refreshed by the truth that has been furnished to Jehovah's witnesses through his organization, one and all will press on in the witness work.
