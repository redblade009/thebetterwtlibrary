### THOSE WHO KNOW JEHOVAH

Make Special Efforts in
"Testimony Periods"

What Will You Be Doing During
International Thanksgiving
Testimony Period?

Jehovah's witnesses have but one
ambition in life, and that is to
honor the name of their great God.
They want to see his name vindicated, and they know that now they
have a part in that most wonderful
work. At times some of the infirm
and elderly brethren cannot get out
into the witness work, but they do
make "special efforts" to sing praises
to their God. Generally during Testimony Periods there are more workers in the field than at any other
time. This is due to the fact that
all of the brethren put forth a special effort to put in time witnessing
for the Kingdom. We wonder how
many workers will be in the field
during International Thanksgiving
Testimony Period? You know that
on the first day of this period, March
20, the Lord's anointed will be celebrating the Memorial feast. All of
Jehovah's witnesses will make a
special effort to associate themselves
with a group of the Lord's people
so that they may partake of the
Lord's Supper that evening. This
will be impossible for some, but they
will observe the Memorial, just the
same as the companies. Everyone
who partakes of the Memorial feast
should that day make a special effort to engage in the Lord's service.
In that way, all will, we feel sure,
appreciate that evening's feast as
they never did before in their lives.
Would it not be delightful if the report for service workers throughout
the United States were the same as
the Memorial report? Would it not
afford you much joy to know that
the total number of workers during
International Thanksgiving Testimony Period throughout the entire
earth was the same as that of the
Memorial participants? Really that
is what it should be. All those who
partake of the Memorial know Jehovah, and the only ones who know
him will seek to honor him and obey
his commands. They are willing in
the day of his power.

Eight days are set aside for International Thanksgiving Testimony
Period; and what a blessed period
that will be for the Lord's anointed?
At this season of the year all of the
Lord's remnant in the earth will
make a special effort to go forth as
a united company singing his praises
even as they are the same evening
partaking of the Lord's supper as
a united company.

This period, of all testimony periods, should be an outstanding one,
because of climatic conditions. It is

spring in the northern hemisphere
and fall in the southern, giving
equal opportunity for the workers
all over the earth to engage in field
activity. We feel sure that especially at this time every one will put
forth a special effort to engage in
the witness work, and then continue
throughout the year to make arrangements and special efforts to
engage in the work until this gospel
of the Kingdom is preached in all
the world.

## A World-wide Witness for 8 Days

## Can 2,000,000 Testimonies Be Given?

The United Ranks of the
Lord's Army Will,
We Believe

Unity is the keynote of harmony,
cooperation and effectiveness. It is
the very opposite of discord, strife
and waste. Since the Cedar Point
convention in September 1922, the
visible army of the Lord has been
marching on towards greater harmony, cooperation and effectiveness. The local organizations have been
perfected and strengthened; the regional service activity and service
conventions, local and national, have
done much to build up the national
organizations of the Lord's people
throughout the earth, and with the
International Convention Period of
July 1931, an international unity
was established, so that throughout
the earth the Lord's people are now
going forward as a unit. The Year
Book for 1932 brings clearly to our
attention the unity of Jehovah's
witnesses. It matters not whether
they are in the frigid zones of the
north or the torrid zones of the
south; they are unitedly proclaiming
the same message at the same time.
Countries ago the prophet of the
Lord was given a vision of what is
actually taking place among the
Lord's anointed now, and in ecstasy
he cried out, "How beautiful upon
the mountains are the feet of him?"
If a mere vision of these events
thrilled the prophet of God to that
degree, how much more should we
who are now privileged to participate in the actual thing, be thrilled!

How appropriate that this International Thanksgiving Testimony
Period should commence with the
Memorial date this year. The Memorial symbols depict our Lord being
broken for us and our privilege of
being broken with him and drinking
of the cup which he hands us. This
cup, as The Welleblower has clearly
established from the Scriptures, represents the course of action which
the Lord outlines for his people, that
is, to be his witnesses, and the joy
that the anointed have in bearing
testimony to his name. What keener
joy or satisfaction of soul can be
imagined than that of assembling
to partake of the emblems with the
consciousness of having spent the
day in drinking the actual cup which
the Lord has poured and each one
determining that, by the Lord's
grace, he will continue to drink with
the Lord's anointed throughout the
earth during the remainder of this
Thanksgiving Testimony Period, and
throughout eternity, for that matter.
Just imagine, imagine, the Lord's
army scattered in all lands, thousands of them, holding forth the
banner of truth! Will these thou-

sands of witnesses give testimony
to 2,000,000 people? We believe the
Lord's army can and will, in these
night days. Undoubtedly this would
make the participation in the Memorial this year an event of greatest solemnity and joy.

The preparations for this period
will, of necessity, vary according to
the climatic conditions at that season of the year. In the far north
the frost will still be in the ground,
enabling the friends to get around
fairly well, so that they can reach
the majority of the towns in their
territory assignment. In the south
it will be quite warm. The friends
there can work in any portion of
their territory. The same is true of
the west coast. In sections where
the thaw has set in and country
roads are in poor condition and impassable, the friends will, of course,
be limited to the larger towns in
their assignment. Local service committees are acquainted with local
conditions; consequently they should
commence now to make their plans,
taking into consideration their own
particular conditions and the kind
of weather they would naturally expect at that season of the year, and
make their plans accordingly. Everyone who partakes of the emblems at
Memorial season, of course, wants
to take part in the actuality also;
otherwise partaking of the emblems
means nothing. Therefore, unless
physically incapacitated so as to be
unable to get around, everyone who
intends to partake of the Memorial
should be found as one of God's
witnesses carrying the Kingdom
message to the people during this
period. We are living in the last
days, there can be no doubt, and Jehovah's witnesses will press the battle to the gate.

The ideal combination to be offered during this period is the
bound-book combination. The Kingdom booklet has been widely distributed, and in the minds of those who
have read it it will undoubtedly
have created a great desire for
further knowledge of the Lord's purpose and the outcome of the crisis
that is now facing the world. Therefore the opening presentation should
be the bound books. Where the territory justifies it, the ten books for
$2.50 is the ideal offer and should
be emphasized. However, in territory where the depression has affected the people, the four bound
books for $1.00 should be stressed; and where four books cannot be
placed, then one for 30c may be offered to the people, or four booklets
for 25c. Try to leave at least the Kingdom booklet in every home, if
they haven't it already.

# Splendid Results in the Field

## Jehovah's Witnesses Put in 955,650 Hours

In Three Months They Gave 4,584,396 Testimonies

### Only Total Below Last Year Is on Bound Books

In reviewing the results of the first three months' work by Jehovah's witnesses during this fiscal year, and comparing them with those of the same period last year, a wonderful increase is noted both in activity and work accomplished. First, there were more brethren engaging in the door-to-door service they put in more time in the field, they placed more literature and in almost triple the number of persons' hands, and gave 1,957,356 more testimonies than in the first three months of the previous fiscal year!

The number of pioneers in the service increased by 440, of auxiliaries by 48, and of company workers and sharpshooters by 655 during these three months over the numbers of a year ago. Likewise, each one of these groups of workers put in a larger amount of time in the work, showing a total of 955,650 hours in the three months. This is 255,651 hours more than were spent in the field during the same months of the fiscal year 1931.

Literature was placed in the hands of 1,767,018 people. This was

an increase of 1,152,375 over the number of obtained of literature during October, November and December of the year 1931. Testimonies delivered totaled 4,584,396, which is 1,957,356 more than the total for the same time in the fiscal year preceding.

The total placement for the three months of this fiscal year was 468,823 books and 1,804,350 booklets, or a grand total of 2,268,170 pieces of literature. This shows a total increase of 670,444 over the output in the same three months of last year, although it is to be seen that 108,951 fewer clothbound books were placed this year than last. This is to be accounted for by the fact that there was an increase of 688,405 booklets placed in the hands of the people, and which were practically all Kingdom booklets. However, the Bound Book Testimony Period will provide a good opportunity for the brethren to make up this drop in clothbound books.

This is truly a remarkable showing and should encourage the pioneers, auxiliaries, company workers and sharpshooters all to press on joyously in the service of Jehovah God, returning thanks to him for the inestimable privilege of having some small share in making known that Jehovah is God!

### A Suggestion and a Request

As an aid to pioneers and auxiliaries in disposing of chickens and other foodstuffs, it is suggested that institutions such as hospitals, homes of various kinds, including old soldiers' homes, old people's homes, convalescent homes, all use large quantities of food, and probably these could be sold to them. While carrying on the witness work, the location of these places can be noted and then called upon when a supply of produce has been accumulated. Pioneers who are following this practice suggest it for the benefit of others.

Much time is lost looking up ad-

dresses, and it is again requested that the writer's name and full address be placed in a prominent place on each letter and order sent in to the office. Your cooperation will considerably expedite the work of handling correspondence and orders.

### New "Authorization and Permit" Cards

These cards are being sent to the pioneers, auxiliaries, and sharpshooters with their next statements of account. The pioneers and auxiliaries are to carefully and neatly PRINT their own names on these cards, and also date them.

Sharpshooters are likewise to PRINT their own names and date the cards sent for their use. In instances where one or more brethren assist the sharpshooter, sufficient cards are being sent for them, the same number of cards as of Bulletin. The sharpshooter is to make out the cards for these brethren, and on line marked "Counterstaged" the name of the sharpshooter is to be written, not printed.

The company supply of cards is being sent with this Bulletin. The name of the company worker is to be PRINTED by the director, but the counterstaged signature is to be written, not printed.

## You'll Enjoy This!

Dear Brethren,

"We received your kind letter dated December 16 and are pleased that we are going further south this weather. We are leaving with the people a wonderful witness wherever we go, because it is our Father's due time for this to be done. At the credit is due to Him. We have had the privilege of serving Jehovah in France for about a year, and the varied experiences we have had in that time have gladdened our hearts. We have been arrested a few times, and were glad of it, because we were able to witness to the local rulers, and invariably more books were placed after these free advertisements.

"We continue to use the Testimony card which was sent to us at the commencement of our work out here, because those who read it are able to concentrate their thoughts on the message contained thereon. We have been very successful in placing the books during the past year, and we attribute part of our success to the fact that we keep the books out of sight.

"A few years ago a Special Bulletin, 'Sugarwives for Workers,' was issued in which was shown how to make a 'cannassing jacket'. We went one of those under our coats, the pockets of which hold 5 books and 20 booklets. We can knock at the doors of houses, enter shops and maroons, and march along the garden paths, without anyone's knowing our business until they have read the card. We leave our cases at the side of the doors, so that they cannot be seen. While the person is reading the card, we gently draw out of the pocket the set of books and booklets we have previously put handy.

"There are three English pioneers working in Belgium using this method who are having great success. We have got into the habit of walking, we are not to be able to do so, because, after we have left them, they may think that they wished they had been nicer with us, and endeavor to get the books indirectly.

"When starting new territory we procure a map of the department, and also maps of the towns we work in that department. Then we go to work systematically, and each evening mark in red pencil the streets we have done. This avoids confusion and enables us to make a thorough witness, which is necessary, because we often find in the most unlikely places those who are ready for the Truth. We have placed many foreign places of literature by approaching men working on new buildings. They are generally a mixed company and are glad to meet someone who can bring them something to read in their own language. Every town of over 2,000 inhabitants within reach of our center is visited by cycle or train, and a quick witness given in the main centers.

"The Lord has richly blessed these efforts. We realize that all our efforts would be useless without divine aid, and therefore we humbly ask the Father each morning that He will use us to His honor and glory. Another cause of our success is, we endeavor to quickly obey the instructions coming from the 'powers that be', realizing that we are being guided in this manner by the Lord. A quota card is a great help. We set ourselves a quota for the year 1931, and by the Lord's grace we exceeded it by a good number. Faith will bring its own reward.

"May God bless you as you continue to be His witnesses. Your fellow witnesses of Jehovah, (Two Pioneers in France)
