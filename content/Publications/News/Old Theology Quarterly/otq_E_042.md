# Crucified With Christ.

SAVIOR, thy dying love
Thou gavest me,
Nor would I aught withhold,
Dear Lord from thee;
In love my soul would bow,
My heart fulfil its vow,
Myself an offering now,
I bring to thee.

Jesus our mercy-seat,
Covering me,
My grateful faith looks up,
Savior, to thee;
Help me the news to bear,
Thy wondrous love declare,
Spread thy truth everywhere,
Dear Lord, for thee.

Give me a faithful heart,
Likeness to thee,
That each departing day
Henceforth may see

Thy work of love well done,
Thy praise on earth begun,
Some victory for truth won,
Some work for thee.

Lord, I would follow thee
In all the way
Thy weary feet have trod;
Yes, if I may.
Help me the cross to bear,
All thy fair graces wear,
Close watching unto prayer,
Following thee.

All that I am and have—
Thy gifts so free—
All of my ransomed life,
Dear Lord, for thee!
And when thy face I see,
Thy sweet "Well done" shall be,
Through all eternity,
Enough for me.

# CROSSES TRUE AND FALSE

## FALSE CHRISTIANITY.

"Thou art weighed in the balances and art found wanting."

FAITH, WORKS.

![img-1.jpeg](img-1.jpeg)

I owe Christ nothing, but deserve heaven for my good works.

"Without faith it is impossible to please God."

"False teachers . . . even denying the Lord that bought them."

## TRUE CHRISTIANITY.

The perfect balance.

"Freely ye have received, freely give."

![img-2.jpeg](img-2.jpeg)

Christ died for me, I live unto him.

Justification (reconciliation) by the blood.

Sanctification (consecration) by the spirit.

"Love is the fulfilling of the law."

## FALSE CHRISTIANITY.

"Thou art weighed in the balances and art found wanting."

THEORY, FAITH—PRACTICE, WORKS.

![img-3.jpeg](img-3.jpeg)

Christ died for me, I may live as I like.

"Faith without works is dead."

"Why call ye me 'Lord,' and do not the things which I say?"

"See that ye receive not the grace of God in vain."
