## EDUCATION FOR LIFE in the New World

![img-0.jpeg](img-0.jpeg)

YOU might incline to think that preparing for the new world means preparing to die. That is a mistake, due to religious miseducation. Religion has taught that the new world is another

world on the other side of death, and that we must enter it through the portals of death. And so, no matter how desirable religion may picture such a world, there lurks always the awful experience of going through death; which is quite depressing.

Death is a gruesome subject, and we are all more interested in how to live under satisfying conditions and in company with good friends. We want to live, and banish death from our minds. We want to learn how to live in happiness and with benefit to everybody else around. Education for the new world is an education for living, because the new world is a place to live forever. That wonderful world, where death itself will be wiped out, is on its way here, and not a long way off. It is due to come whether rulers of this present generation like it or not. It will be a world of unity, not unity of just all of earth's inhabitants, but unity of heaven and earth; for it takes both to make a world. The surprising fact is that you may pass on into that new world without dying, because in the creation of the said world our literal earth, our globe, is not to be destroyed by a catastrophe spelling death to all on it.

Of course, there will be a new heaven and a new earth; and the inspired educator, the apostle Peter, said that those acquainted with the promise of the almighty Creator look for a 'new heavens and a new earth'. (2 Peter 3:13) But to get to that new world you will not necessarily have

to transfer to another globe, newly made. Like Noah and his household in the ark, you can remain on this earth and, under divine protection, pass out from the old world and into the new. That may sound novel, but it is just what that history-making experience of the inmates of Noah's ark was recorded in the Bible to help us to understand. It was done back there. It can be done now, for this old world that humanity has known now for more than four thousand years since Noah's day is on its way out. Yes, OUT, with its total wars, its injustices and inequalities, its religious mix-up and sectarian rivalries, its political differences and bunglings and crookedness, its disasters and calamities, its diseases and pain, its poverty and want, and even its causes of death. Anyone who will honestly read the physical evidences which the greatest prophet foretold as due to appear within our generation can see that we have reached the limit of this world of imperfection, wickedness and death.—Matthew chapters 24 and 25; Mark 13; Luke 21.

As you look about and see the things that the great Prophet described nineteen centuries in advance, you can take to heart his explanation of what it all means: 'So likewise ye, when ye see these things come to pass, know ye that the kingdom of God is nigh at hand. Verily I say unto you, This generation shall not pass away, till all be fulfilled. Heaven and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away.' (Luke 21:31-33) A Government higher than man, and not set up by scheming politicians, is about to usher in a world that satisfies all righteously disposed 'men of good-will' and that is to the glory of a righteous God.

Heaven is higher than earth, and so the Kingdom of God with Messiah Christ ruling on the throne in God's name is higher than earth, because it is the kingdom of heaven. Hence it

will be the 'new heavens'. Since that means that the present political systems of men will have to move out, and commercial exploitation and the Babel of religions with them, a new visible organization is bound to come into effect on this earth, in harmony with the heavenly Government of righteousness. And there you have the 'new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness'.

### IS THAT WORTH LIVING FOR?

It is, even if it be that the time of the passing out of the old visible organization and the invisible demons supervising it means 'days of tribulation'. There yet remains a 'time of trouble' such as the world has never yet known and will not survive. But certain individuals will be hid under the shadow of God's power and will survive, just as Noah and his seven companions survived the end of the 'world that then was'. You and I are interested in being among those survivors. Knowing that the 'great tribulation' yet to come is for the purpose of clearing the way for bringing in a new world robs the tribulation outlook of its terror and arouses our interest in getting through it alive.

To prepare for living in that 'world without end' certainly must require a different education and educational system from what are now in operation serving the aims of this world. What, then, is the required education for living without end in happiness and peace? Religious education must absolutely be out of the question.
