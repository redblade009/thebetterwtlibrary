#### WHAT THE I. S. S. A. IS

The International Bible Students Association is a name used by Bible Students throughout the world who adhere to the interpretation of the Bible readings for many years by the late Pastor Russell. The Watch Tower Bible and Trust Society is a corporation organized under the laws of Pennsylvania, existing since 1916, and handles the business and is the work and the publications of the International Bible Students Association generally.

In 1910 the Peoples Pulpit Association was incorporated under the Mississippi corporation Law of the State of New York, to handle the same work in that state, for the reason that the Watch Tower Bible and Trust Society being a non-stock corporation organized in another state could not hold title to real estate in the State of New York.
