## Handbills Successfully Used with "The New World Society in Action"

¹ In an effort to determine the effectiveness of using handbills to advertise showings of "The New World Society in Action" the following experiment was conducted by one of the New York city units, under the Society's direction.

² When the film was first released in April, 1954, publishers with the unit personally invited persons of good will to attend. A total of 253, a good attendance, saw the film.

³ Then came the possibility of using handbills. How would they work? As an experiment the unit scheduled the film two nights in succession. Both dates were printed on the handbills so that people could come the evening of their convenience.

⁴ Extensive and special advertising arrangements were made. Five times the usual number of handbills ordered to advertise a public meeting were obtained and used. Street advertising was done with handbills Friday. Saturday morning all stores and businessmen in the area were called on. Saturday afternoon and Sunday morning house-to-house work

was carried out with only handbills being used. No literature was offered for this special activity. Beginning from the Kingdom Hall, publishers worked out as far as possible, in a friendly way inviting householders to see the film.

⁵ Initial response to this special advertising seemed good. But what would the results prove to be? We are happy to report that 356 persons saw the film the first evening and 347 the following evening, making a combined attendance of 703. Over three hundred booklets, _Basis for Belief in a New World_, were given out. It was gratifying to note that the vast majority were strangers who never before had attended any meetings of Jehovah's witnesses. The expressions of attenders on both evenings were of appreciation and thanks for the congregation's kind consideration in inviting them. Many have returned for other meetings.

## Around the World

with Jehovah's Witnesses

"The New World Society in Action"

### Part I

The New World society is a going concern in all parts of the earth. To show its true magnitude and to enable all of Jehovah's witnesses to become better acquainted with functions of the organization Jehovah has built up on earth in these last days, the Society sent the film to all countries possible. What are the early results as shown in the 1955 "Yearbook"?

Without exception all who see it are thrilled and feel as do the brothers in Northern Rhodesia (223) and Argentina (84). They now have a much better appreciation of the New World society and the theocratic organization.

If you were a publisher working with a small congregation and never had an opportunity to attend a large assembly of any kind and had no conception of the work done at headquarters, how would you feel upon seeing the film? Probably as the publishers you read about in Chile feel. (118, 119)

Is seeing the film once sufficient? There is so much to see in it that, as expressed by the Belgium report, "This film can be seen two, three, five or ten times. It never becomes monotonous." (95, 96) Although the showing of it will not go on indefinitely the Society does wish to arrange for as many brothers and persons of good will as possible to see it. Therefore, it will be shown at all circuit assemblies during the remaining part of the 1955 service year. Do not miss seeing it at your next circuit assembly and be sure to take persons of good will with you. Let them see and hear of Jehovah's expanding New World society.

### Effective Witnessing

From house to house Jehovah's witnesses faithfully go, using Jehovah's Word skillfully in giving short sermons, in an effort to find and feed the other sheep. Only knowledge and understanding of Jehovah's purposes will enable people of good will to brace up their minds and join in new-nation activity.

How much more effective is our witnessing when we use the Bible and give sermons? A small town in South Africa was witnessed to over a year ago. The total of book placements was one. And what resulted recently when Bible sermons were used? Not one, but sixty books were placed before the town was completed. (248)

"What else can we say?" was the reply of the occupants of a home in Fiji after listening to an effective sermon. As you use sermons you will find this to be the reaction of many good-will persons. (88)

Sermons are being used world-wide by Jehovah's witnesses. With interest you will read the reports on Bechuanaland (250) and French Equatorial Africa (76) and see how our brothers are learning how to use sermons in order to become more effective ministers.

Is not a minister expected to give a sermon? Yes, and that is what a householder in Egypt expected on hearing one of Jehovah's witnesses say he was a minister. He was invited in and the household was called together to listen to his sermon. Such a good sermon was given that the people were deeply impressed. Arrangements were made for return visits. (139, 140)

Are you using the Bible and giving sermons from house to house in order to be most effective in your witnessing?

## With The Watchtower

All of us want to share in making it the greatest campaign to date, and we can by regularly offering the _Watchtower_ subscription during the next four months. Since _The Watchtower_ is a Bible magazine of outstanding value it is fitting that publishers introduce it by using three- to eight-minute Bible sermons. The following sermon, entitled "God's Purpose," may be used with good effect. 2 "Good morning. I am calling to share with you some information in which I am sure you will be interested. He lost a beautiful paradise home, the hope of filling the earth with righteous children and the prospects of everlasting life. This Jesus said he came to restore. The Bible promises that these blessings will again exist under God's kingdom. \[Rev. 21:3, 4] Would you not like to live under such righteous conditions? # YOUR SERVICE MEETING

## Starting Bible Studies. Using 'This Good New of the Kingdom'

**Chairman (3 min.)** Chairman reviews urgent need for more to feed sheep, as outlined in November 'Informant.' New Instrument 'This Good News of the Kingdom' provided by the Society is especially designed to feed sheep. Using this booklet properly will open up the way to start many more home Bible studies. Observe how theocratic companions use booklet in regular witnessing work and in making return calls.

**SCENE I (5 min.)** Theocratic companions give four-minute sermon and obtain 'Watchtower' subscription. Householder is encouraged to read 'The Watchtower' and also booklets, particularly 'This Good News of the Kingdom', as arrangements are made to make a return call.

**Chairman (2 min.)** Planting done! First step toward aiding sheep accomplished! Watering work now needed, for sheep must be assisted and encouraged to absorb Bible truths. How can this be done? Note how theocratic companions use new booklet on return call.

**SCENE II (10 min.)** Theocratic companions deliver latest copy of 'The Watchtower' and advise householder that subscription copies will soon be coming to him. Having in mind leading into a study of the booklet they channel back-call sermon on Kingdom theme, making points such as: 'Most people know of or have heard Christ's words, 'This good news of the kingdom.' What is the importance of the Kingdom, which is the basic theme of the Bible and for which we pray? Many people desire to know the Bible answer. The booklet 'This Good News of the Kingdom' is especially printed to explain and outline what the Bible teaches on this all-important matter. At their suggestion householder gets his copy so its value may be demonstrated. Theocratic companions then begin study of material in booklet with householder.

**Chairman (2 min.)** So a study is started. Do not try to cover entire booklet at one time. Subheadings and reaction of householder enable alert conductor to determine how much material to cover. After several studies (none exceeding one hour) study of the booklet is completed. What will the alert publisher now have in mind? Let us watch to see.

**SCENE III (3 min.)** Benefits of booklet study are briefly summarized and importance of gaining more Bible knowledge is discussed. 'Let God Be True' is tactfully introduced and arrangements are made for future visits, to consider material in book as was done with booklet. Householder expresses appreciation and looks forward to future visits.

**Chairman (2 min.)** Booklet has served its purpose. Study is now to continue in 'Let God Be True' or any other publication of the publisher's choice. Being familiar with contents of new booklet from considering it at the 'Watchtower' study, let us wisely use this new provision in feeding the many other sheep.

## Using the Magazine

**Chairman (3 min.)** The 'Watchtower' campaign is under way. Realizing the value of this magazine, publishers desire to see it go into every home. What can we do to see this desire fulfilled? Be prepared and participate in the campaign. It has been found by many publishers that the magazine may be very effectively used to

obtain new subscriptions by actually placing a copy of the magazine in the householder's hands after giving a sermon, thus enabling the householder to see for himself the magazine's contents.

**SCENE I (10 min.)** Demonstration opens with publisher giving complete sermon (six to seven minutes) which leads into 'Watchtower' subscription offer. At completion of sermon publisher, who has two copies of same issue in hand, offers one to householder and invites him to see what 'The Watchtower' contains. Publisher opens his copy to first page, saying: 'As you open the magazine you will notice that on the inside cover is stated the purpose of the magazine. \[Mention a point or two from it.] Then, in the very first article, information is given on ... \[very briefly describe its contents]. Next turn to page ... \[give number] and here you will find an excellent and timely discussion on ... You will notice that starting on page ... there are questions at the bottom of each page. \[Explain that their purpose is to help the reader grasp quickly the Biblical points discussed and to understand better and glean gems of truth found in each paragraph.] Another article you will enjoy is this one on page ... And now if you turn to the last inside page you will always find 'Questions from Readers.' This section supplies answers to Bible questions that you have no doubt wondered about yourself. These questions are written in by readers of 'The Watchtower' who wish the Bible's answer to their question. Now this is only one issue of the twenty-four different issues that you will receive, all for a contribution of \$1 a year. 'The Watchtower' comes to you two times a month through the mail. Also, these three booklets, including 'This Good News of the Kingdom', are given free. I have a subscription blank right here and shall be glad to send in your subscription today.'

**Chairman (3 min.)** Chairman stresses advantage and effectiveness of using the magazine being offered and placing it in the hands of the householder. Many people do not know what is contained in the pages of 'The Watchtower.' The above method is an excellent way to acquaint householders with the magazine's contents, and, of course, we want all persons of good will to become acquainted with the contents of 'The Watchtower' and learn for themselves its real value.
