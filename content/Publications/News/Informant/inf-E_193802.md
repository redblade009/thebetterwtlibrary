# INFORMANT

FEBRUARY, 1958

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## Announcing Greatest World-wide Campaign Brother Rutherford Writes a Special Booklet—“Cure”

### “Cure,” “Enemies” & “Consolation” Featured

#### World-wide Quota Set

##### Printing Already Begun for April Distribution

This is an announcement which we believe will thrill every one of the Lord’s people. It is about a new world-wide campaign which will begin with “COMPANIONS” Testimony Period, on April 9, and continue for three months. It is not just an ordinary campaign, but a campaign that will call for the greatest united effort ever put forth by those who love the Lord. Brother Rutherford has outlined this campaign and has done much preparatory work on it. Let everyone who believes that the Kingdom is here carefully consider the following, and then prepare to move into action on April 9.

##### “Cure” Booklet

For this campaign the Lord through Brother Rutherford has provided a special 32-page booklet, entitled _CURE_. It is full of fire and will be a most effective weapon of war to use in exposing the enemy, its front cover is brilliant red, and its appearance and the questions asked will create a desire on the part of everyone who sees it to read it. The questions “Do we need more religion?” and “Is religion the cure for nations and individuals?” will certainly arouse the interest of any thinking person. The Lord’s people know the answers, but it is necessary now for millions of people throughout the world also to know these answers. The people are perplexed and in distress, and _Cure_ contains just the message they need. This booklet is to be included in a special offer to be made during the campaign of three months, and this special offer is set out in the one ad which appears in the booklet.

The manuscript for _Cure_ reached the Brooklyn factory on December 14. Typesetting began immediately, plateauing was rushed, and by January 1 the first press began to turn out this new booklet. During the first two weeks of January the presses turned out over 100,000 daily, and then the production was stepped up to 200,000 daily. This will continue until 10,000,000 copies have been printed and shipped out. The first 530,000 printed will go to Australia and New Zealand. These are on the way and should be there by March 1, 1958. Then will follow shipments to South Africa of 80,000, West Africa 30,000, India 20,000, Philippine Islands 15,000, Hawaii 4,000, England 1,500,000, Jamaica 20,000, and Canada 700,000. For the United States alone more

than 7,000,000 copies will be set aside. Because of the special offer made in the advertisement, which offer expires June 30, all these copies must be distributed by that time. Can we do it? We believe we can, by the Lord’s grace, if everyone will do his part. Everyone at the Brooklyn factory is working at top speed so that the publishers can have this literature by the beginning of the campaign. From then on it will be up to the publishers to carry out their part in the campaign and get these booklets out.

##### The Special Offer

The offer to be made in this campaign is entirely new. Beginning with the “COMPANIONS” Testimony Period, the publishers will offer _Cure_, _Enemies_ (or _Riches_) and a year’s subscription for _Consolation_,

on a contribution of only one dollar. It is a remarkable offer. Just consider for a moment the amount of reading matter a person will receive: a 32-page booklet _Cure_, a 384-page bound book already in its second million edition, and a 32-page magazine 26 times during the year. There is nothing else the people could get with a dollar that would anywhere nearly equal this offer in value. The one advertisement on the back of the booklet will help the publishers in presenting this special offer. If a person cannot contribute a dollar, then the offer will be _Cure_ and _Enemies_ (or _Riches_) on a contribution of a quarter. People who cannot contribute a quarter but want to know the truth about religion will be given the booklet free or for any contribution they desire. Keep in mind that 10,000,000 _Cure_ booklets must be distributed in but three months. That means work, and lots of it; but think of what a wit-

ness will be given! _Cure_ booklets in 10,000,000 homes will undoubtedly cause many of them to forsake religion. Jehovah’s people have a “strange word” to perform, and they are doing it joyfully, and now they will enter into this sabled privilege of service with greater zeal than ever before.

##### World-wide Quota

Every one of the people who claim interest in the Kingdom will want a share in the witness work; and therefore a world-wide quota is set as well as an individual quota. The witness to be accomplished is set out in terms of books, booklets and subscriptions as follows: During the three months of April, May and June, by the Lord’s grace, we hope to distribute 10,000,000 of the _Cure_ booklet and 1,000,000 of the _Enemies_ (or _Riches_) book and obtain 100,000 new subscriptions for _Consolation_. That will mean that every person connected with the Kingdom will necessarily have to put forth an extraordinary effort; in fact, more than double anything that he or she has done heretofore in the same length of time. With this tremendous amount of work just ahead of us, every company organization, auxiliary, pioneer and special pioneer should now begin making plans and arrangements for this witness, the greatest of all witnesses.

##### Your Personal Quota

In order to attain this world-wide quota it will be necessary for each company publisher during these three months to place 200 of the _Cure_ booklet and 20 of the _Enemies_ (or _Riches_) book and obtain three new subscriptions for _Consolation_. Each company publisher should keep in mind that there are three months in which to do this; so, looking at it from a monthly standpoint, it means only 70 booklets, 7 books and 1 subscription per publisher. Can you do it? Will you do it? The quota for auxiliaries is set at 500 booklets, 75 books and 6 _Consolation_ subscriptions for the three months; whereas each pioneer and special pioneer should place 1,000 of the _Cure_ booklet and 150 of the _Enemies_ (or _Riches_) book and take 15 subscriptions for _Consolation_. If every publisher of the Kingdom attains this personal quota for the three months, the world-wide quota will be surpassed.

##### “Cure” and “Consolation” in Foreign Languages

Every branch is already making preparations for this campaign. _Cure_ will be translated into every language in which _Consolation_ is published. The translating work is proceeding with all possible speed. (Continued on page 2, column 1)

### All to Work During “His Work”

#### 30,000 Publishers Midwinter and Much Work

The clarion call continues to sound loudly throughout the land for the 30,000 and more hearers of the wonderful truths concerning Jehovah and his kingdom all to manifest their faith and allegiance by boldly engaging in the “strange” work now in full swing. Truly the pealmist expresses the attitude of all who understand and appreciate what the Lord is doing in the earth today when he says: “I will praise thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will shew forth all thy marvellous works.” In order to obtain and to maintain a correct appreciation of one’s relationship to the King and his kingdom, it is imperative to give heed to and obey the positive commandment to “preach this gospel of the Kingdom”. Satan and all his wicked lust are arrayed against mankind. No one is safe from the attacks of these enemies of man except those in the sanctuary of the Lord. The Lord does protect and sustain his own; his people who are willing and active in this day of battle.

During “His Work”, February 5-13, a strenuous effort is going to be made to bring the heart-choosing Kingdom message to the people of good will who are still in bondage to Satan’s organization. Throughout the world those who acknowledge Jehovah as supreme will be putting forth their very best

efforts to counteract the increasing organized attacks of the religionists. The Lord has amply provided his willing people with the weapons necessary to tan the old “whose” and her allies. It is especially in times like “His Work”, right in the midst of the most unseasonable part of the year, that the concentrated attacks of Jehovah’s witnesses and their associates strike fear in the ranks of the religionists. It’s all a strange work to them. Such persistent world-wide boldness and unity in the part of God’s people in carrying the Kingdom truths to the people in the face of all kinds of opposition and obstacles is completely beyond their comprehension and power to cope with. The enemy is beginning to realize the devastating power and strength of our God. Joyful indeed is the lot of everyone who is really on the Lord’s side, that is, having the vision of the warfare and doing what he can to further the interests of the Kingdom.

##### Be Prepared

For “His Work” the service committee has selected the territory that can be best worked during severe weather. Each one should now set aside such definite time as can possibly be spared from secular and household duties so as to work the territory assigned. Determine to let nothing interfere with your plans, trusting in the Lord to provide whatever may be lacking. Any obstacle that you personally feel you cannot surmount, take up with the company servant or the service. (Continued on page 2, column 3)

## Pioneers 110 Hours a Month

As the Kingdom service for the year 1938 opens up, every one who has taken his stand on the Lord's side, whether of the mointed or of the Jonadabs, will want to use all his faculties to the glory of the Lord's name, or at least all his available privileges to that end.

We believe that there are a great many who are not now on the pioneer list who could arrange to be in that branch of the service. With the thrilling campaign outlined in this Informant before us, each and every one will want to do his part. Let each one, therefore, examine himself and examine his circumstances and see if there are not some apparent hindrances that can be removed, that thus he may be enabled to enter the pioneer service.

Those who cannot enter either the pioneer or auxiliary branch of the work should be company publishers, devoting all the time they possibly can to expanding the Kingdom interests which the Lord has committed into their care.

### May Now Be Ordered Separately if Desired

Heretofore the policy of the Society has been not to ship out records except in complete sets where the lectures are in series, namely, "Exposed," "Religion and Christianity," "Safety," and "Worshiping God".
