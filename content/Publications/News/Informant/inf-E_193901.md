# INFORMANT

JANUARY, 1939

"But they shall not prevail against thee."—Jer. 1:19.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## Thrilling "Watchtower" Campaign Begins!

This Campaign Is the Most Important Ever
Are You Prepared to Do Your Share?

### Time the Main Requisite

With a _Watchtower_, a Book
and _Face the Facts_

In every land throughout the entire world, commencing Sunday, January 1, and continually every day and evening thereafter for four months, till April 30, Jehovah's faithful remnant and their companions will obediently and enthusiastically march forward in the "hottest" world-wide campaign that has ever been prosecuted by Christians on earth. In Germany and like dark places where God's true people are cruelly oppressed and hindered in every conceivable way, they will be united with us to the extent they are able, so that, in reality, all who know of and love the Lord will be found diligently doing with their might what their hands find to do during every hour of this _Watchtower_ Campaign.

This _Watchtower_ Campaign is the most stupendous ever attempted—not merely because within that time, by the Lord's grace, we confidently expect to have the largest number of publishers in the field, putting in the highest number of hours in field service and back-call work, and placing the largest number of pieces of literature, but for reasons more far-reaching than these. This campaign is going to result in bringing the _Watchtower_ magazine regularly into the homes of at least 100,000 more families, which magazine stresses the great issue of serving Jehovah, the great Theocrat, and live, or serving the cruel dictator Satan and his wicked organization, and die. Also, in _Face the Facts_, which is going to be read by millions, is sounded the warning. And there are going to be placed a million bound books which outline Jehovah's gracious provision for oppressed mankind at this crucial time prior to Armageddon. Thus, this campaign has as its objective the bringing to millions timely facts which they must learn in order to know about Jehovah's theocratic organization and the course of action they must take now for their protection and safety.

The publishers must assist the newly interested ones to become "laborers" by showing them just what they can do in advancing this _Watchtower_ Campaign.

At the last service meeting held in December approximately 70,000 persons, we believe, heard the lecture "Fill the Earth". Many of these have never gone out into the field, yet they sincerely love the Lord and are anxious to serve Him to the best of their knowledge and ability. The newly interested must be 'built up', that they may become active and thus enjoy the full favor and protection of the Lord.

#### Time in the Service

It is very essential that each publisher put in much time in the field service each month during this _Watchtower_ Campaign. To do so it will be necessary to brave the cold winds, snow, and chilly rains. Yet all who keenly appreciate the importance of the Kingdom issue will overcome these obstacles by properly clothing themselves and taking pains to work the most protected and sheltered portions of their assigned territory. And when the weather is good, full advantage will be taken of it by working as long as possible, and not being satisfied with putting in three hours, but five and more.

Each morning and each evening

consult the "My Daily Kingdom Service" record sheet to see the progress being made daily. If a publisher finds he is getting behind in his quota, then he should take immediate steps to put in the necessary time to make up this deficit, and not wait until the last few days of the campaign to do so. For such an emergency each publisher should have individual territory assignment close to his home (and place of business) and which can be worked at odd times or whenever the weather is bad and it is impractical to witness with a group.

#### Our Equipment

If at all possible, each time you engage in the field service carry a phonograph along with you and at least the record "Miracles" and "Instruction", P-153, P-154. Take with you the special Testimony card provided for this campaign, a few samples of the current _Watchtower_, some bound books, and a good supply of _Face the Facts_ booklets. Also, take some _Watchtower_ subscription blanks, back-call slips, and cards or slips bearing the name and address of the meeting place where the _Watchtower_ studies are held.

#### Offer the Entire Combination

The special combination to be stressed at each call, if possible, is a year's subscription for _The Watchtower_, the book _Enemies_ (or any other bound book), and the booklet _Face the Facts_, all on a contribution of one dollar. With each person taking this offer leave a sample copy of the current _Watchtower_, a book, a _Face the Facts_ booklet, and a card bearing the name and address of the hall where the _Watchtower_ studies are held; also a duplicate copy of the subscription slip signed by the publisher, which is the subscriber's receipt.

To persons unable to subscribe for a year, endeavor to place a six-month subscription, on a contribution of 50c, with a _Face the Facts_ booklet free. With all who so subscribe leave a current issue of _The Watchtower_, a _Face the Facts_ booklet, the address of the _Watchtower_ study, and (as the subscriber's receipt) a duplicate copy of the subscription slip.

If a subscription for _The Watchtower_ cannot be obtained, then try to place _Enemies_ (or any other book) and a _Face the Facts_ booklet, on a contribution of 25c. Where not even this is possible, then leave _Face the Facts_ for anything they can contribute, or even free if they promise to read the booklet. In all homes visited wherein the people show any interest whatsoever we must try to leave at least the _Face the Facts_ booklet—in order, first, that they may have the message, and second, that we may meet our individual and world-wide quota.

In every case where people show any willingness to listen, even if at the time you can leave only the _Face the Facts_ booklet, try to arrange for a back call with the speeches "Face the Facts", "Fill the Earth," and "Fascism or Freedom".

#### Individual Quota

The "My Daily Kingdom Service" record sheet sets out the quota made for each company publisher and pioneer, which must be met if we are to reach the world-wide quota. This quota for each company publisher is 4 new _Watchtower_ subscriptions, 20 bound books, and 250 of the _Face the Facts_ booklet; for each pioneer it is 20 new _Watchtower_ subscriptions, 200 books, and 1,500 of the _Face the Facts_ booklet.

#### Servants of the Company

The success of each company in this campaign depends much on the soul and leadership of its various servants. If each and every one of these servants does his share in the field service and looks after the other interests committed to him, the witness work of the company will be greatly stimulated and advanced. Be sure that the territory is so assigned and worked that your entire assignment will be covered at least once during the campaign—and twice where possible.

#### Make a Good Start

The _Watchtower_ Campaign begins at a time when most of the publishers can put in full time during the first two days, namely, Sunday, January 1, and Monday, January 2.

### Use New Record

"Miracles", "Instruction"

By January 1, it is hoped, all the publishers will each have one of the new records P-153, P-154 to use in the service. We are swamped with orders, and working day and night to get them filled. Run this record at every place possible in your door-to-door work. One side of this disc, "Miracles," tells about the two speeches of Brother Rutherford from London and why the person should hear them. The other side, "Instruction," explains why one should read and study _The Watchtower_. Run both of these records, if possible, when you visit the people the first time in the house-to-house work, then try to place the special offer as well as to arrange for a back call. Even if the party refuses to give you a subscription, you may be able to place a book or booklet and arrange an appointment to run "Fascism or Freedom" or "Face the Facts".

Another place to use the new disc, P-153, P-154, is on back calls. Some publishers have already obtained subscriptions by doing this. Those persons on your back-call list who are not yet subscribers for _The Watchtower_ should be called on. The record "Instruction" will help you to take their subscription, and "Miracles" will stimulate their interest to hear the new lecture series.

# "Kingdom Activities"

December 1 Watchtower

Article to Be

Carefully Studied

The December 1 Watchtower, on page 364, in the article "Kingdom Activities", brings to our attention the importance of devoting more time to the field service. Everyone having in mind the importance of the Kingdom will surely put forth every possible effort to devote at least two hours a day, on the average, to the field service, in obedience to the King's command to preach this gospel of the Kingdom in all the world for a witness. Suggestions are here offered as to how a publisher can put in more time in the field.

First, publishers should get personal assignments of territory near their homes. Such territory can be so situated that it takes in four blocks each way from one's home. The advantages of getting near-by territory can readily be seen. Persons who have domestic duties to perform can slip out an hour or two each day and do some witnessing work. Practically no time, then, will be spent in traveling to territory. One may find it more convenient to devote an hour or two in the morning; others, two hours in the afternoon. After working in this near-by territory for several days with the phonograph, one will have names and addresses of interested persons to call on when rainy or severe weather makes it almost impossible to go from door to door. On such calls a publisher can spend an hour or an hour and a half, running records, answering questions and assisting the people in Bible study. In the case of married people, the wife can assist the husband to put in more time in the field by witnessing in the territory during the day, finding those who show interest, and turning these names and addresses over to the husband publisher for back calls an hour or two in the evening.

Having a local territory, those who get home early from their daily work can put in an hour or two before supertime. Those persons whose work does not begin until after dinner, because of varying shifts in the industry in which they are employed, can put in a few hours in the morning. Since the 46-hour week is in effect, many factories and offices are closed on Saturdays, giving many of the publishers all day Saturday off. Considerable of this time could be devoted to field service work, either in local territory or in a witnessing party. Those who have only a half day off from their secular work on Saturday can put in at least three or four hours.

Sunday has always been a big day for the publishers, and should continue to be the big day, and bigger, if at all possible, as far as the Kingdom interests are concerned. Many of the publishers are arranging now to put in five to seven hours rather than the usual three, eating lunch in the field. Four to five hours can be spent in door-to-door work, and the remaining time in back calls. Always carry a full set of "Face the Facts", "Fascism or Freedom" or some other series with you in the car, so that you will have them on hand for back calls in the afternoon. If you have no car, carry a set to your territory and leave it with some interested person, preferably the person on whom you intend to make a back call. In this way you let him know in advance that you intend to be there at an appointed time.

# "The Watchtower" a Real Surprise

January Issue a Stimulus to "Watchtower" Campaign

The Watchtower subscribers were pleasantly surprised when they received the January 1 issue. In keeping with the progress of Jehovah's theocratic government, The Watchtower appeared in a new dress. Note the changes that have been made: a new cover in two colors, a revised title, a new scripture text at the head of the "Contents" block, a more vivid picture symbol of Jehovah's organization, and—well, we'll let you see for yourself how many more changes you can find on the cover. On the inside, note the new, larger type, the new kind of paper, the two-color work. But above all, we appreciate the food provided for us: for we know His children are taught of Jehovah. We are sure that the new Watchtower has been received with joy, and that the publishers are now more eager than ever to present it to the people.

Its new form should aid in getting new subscriptions. Its bright-colored

cover is significant of the life-giving message of comfort, hope and joy that The Watchtower contains. As announced by its new title, The Watchtower is truly a "herald of Christ's kingdom", and those who would enjoy the benefits under that kingdom must now inform themselves of the message which Jehovah provides for the people of good will through the columns of The Watchtower.

Another new feature, which we don't want to overlook and which serves as an aid to the publishers, is the record of "Field Experiences" appearing on the back page. Companies should study and discuss these experiences in their service meetings so that other publishers may improve their own methods of carrying on the Kingdom work.

Now that Jehovah through his organization has provided this new, attractive form for The Watchtower, let's show our appreciation by engaging with joy and zeal in the Watchtower Campaign.

Please Use Subscription Blanks

For some months the brethren were very dependable in the use of the Watchtower and Consolation blanks, but in the past few months, some have been sending in their subscriptions on almost anything. This, of course, causes extra work in the office. If the brethren, in renewing their subscriptions, will use the renewal blanks that come in The Watchtower or Consolation when it is expiring, and send these slips in promptly with an accompanying remittance, they will aid the office greatly. Particularly during the Watchtower Campaign stress the importance of using subscription blanks and sending in subscriptions through the company organization, which in turn will list the subscriptions on the transmittal form provided. Your heartily co-operating in this regard will aid the workers in the office, who have all they can do.

Coupons

Jundles should be sent insured instead of registered. Borden coupons are not redeemable for cash and should not be included in packets sent us. Send a post card stating number, kind and value of coupons being sent. Do not inclose this in packages.

You Can Help

When a company servant or unit servant changes his address, he should promptly notify both the Society and the zone servant of this change. When notifying the Society, be sure to show that this change is for your address as company or unit servant; otherwise the address will be changed only on your Watchtower and Consolation magazines. It might be well to send a separate notice concerning The Watchtower and Consolation.

Attending Zone Assemblies

The zone and regional servants' reports coming to the office indicate that many of the brethren do not attend the zone assembly on the occasion of the regional servant's visit. Apparently some do not appreciate the importance of the new organization. Usually such brethren are unable to get effective results in their own territory, and they wonder why. These assemblies are a part of Jehovah's Theocratic Organization for the conduct of his Kingdom service. The instruction provided there is essential for each one in order to properly do the work assigned. Let everyone who possibly can, therefore, attend these assemblies regularly.

# 500 More Pioneers Needed

Can You Arrange for Full Time?

The December 1 Watchtower announces the discontinuance of the Auxiliary branch of the Kingdom service. (See article "Kingdom Activities".) While many of the auxiliaries enrolled as pioneers on January 1, having made definite arrangements to increase their time in the work, many more pioneers are needed to properly care for the Kingdom work in the United States at this time. It will be remembered that many previously serving as pioneers are now active as zone servants.

There is much territory that requires the witness. As a result of the world-wide convention in September, with the accompanying radio broadcasts, many are asking for additional information and instruction. These people must be called on, and there are not sufficient pioneers to do this.

In addition to much isolated territory (in which no company organization exists), a number of small companies throughout the country, composed, in the main, of newly interested persons, require the assistance of live, active pioneers to instruct them and aid them in covering their assigned territory.

The experience that the auxiliaries have had in the field, taking the lead in using all the instruments the Lord has provided, using the phonograph consistently, carrying on model studies, making back calls, etc., equips them remarkably for this work. The great majority of those enrolled as auxiliaries will wish to increase their service to the King and enroll as pioneers. Let us hear from you immediately! A pioneer application will not be necessary in your case; merely say, "Transfer me from auxiliary to pioneer service." Company publishers will write for application and information letter.

Some of those already enrolled as pioneers will wish to assist those now entering. Some may have space in their trailers and will welcome a partner in the Kingdom work. We should like to hear from such, advising us just what accommodations they can offer for these new Kingdom pioneers.

The Messenger

Due to late orders and a very heavy demand for The Messenger, a second edition was put on the press. It is believed that The Messenger will be of real assistance to newly interested persons to appreciate the magnitude of the work done by Jehovah's witnesses. Therefore, extra copies of The Messenger were printed on the second edition, and will be offered at 5c a copy (remittance to accompany order) when five or more are ordered to one address. These can be placed with the newly interested people at 5c a copy, and we know that they will get a rich blessing from it.

Pecans

The December Informant had a notice with reference to the sale of pecans to aid pioneers. There seems to have been a misunderstanding about this notice. It is the privilege of anyone to sell his products to anyone he desires, but the purpose of that notice is to inform others that the Society is not either directly or indirectly engaging in selling pecans, and that neither the Society's name nor that of the pioneers in its service should be used to induce others to purchase. To be sure, anyone can buy pecans as he pleases, but this matter should not be connected with contributions to the Society's pioneers.
