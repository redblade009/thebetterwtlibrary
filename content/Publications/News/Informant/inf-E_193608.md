## NATIONAL PETITION "JEHOVAH'S Praise"

### Listeners Eager for More Broadcasts

#### Hierarchy Threatens "More Drastic Action"

Humble persons of good will who have listened to Jehovah's Kingdom message as broadcast in speeches of Brother Rutherford frequently write the Society that they want to hear those broadcasts oftener. Three years ago the Roman Catholic press made world-wide announcement of the Hierarchy's decision to "drive Rutherford from the air", as they put it. They tried. They staggered in the attempt. "But the end is not yet."

Hating the truth broadcast throughout the world last February, in "Separating the Nations", high officials of the Hierarchy at Vatican City and in this country have conspired anew to take "more drastic action" to accomplish their announced purpose. Fresh attempts to coerce owners of radio stations are being launched. The Catholic press renews its campaign of stout protests, insisting that "Judge Rutherford misrepresents the teachings of the Catholic church" and therefore he must be stopped; or "electrocuted", says one of the Hierarchy's agents. Another of its so-called "fathers", a principal (but unprincipled) representative at New York, blandly proposes death for all of Jehovah's witnesses! And now prayers of "the faithful" during the month of August are called for by the head of the Hierarchy, "to save us from the infamous Judge Rutherford of the Watch Tower movement."

Reasonable persons, including even many of the "Catholic population", are beginning to see the inconsistency of the Hierarchy's position. Catholic leaders are being asked, "Why plan murder? Why not openly show the people wherein Judge Rutherford misrepresents the Catholic teachings?"

At Philadelphia, in June, many of such fair-minded ones signed a petition to WIP, prominent radio station there that had been forced by the Hierarchy to discontinue broadcasting Brother Rutherford's answers to Bible questions, after that station had refused to break its contract to broadcast "Separating the Nations" in February. Now thousands at other places in Pennsylvania and New Jersey are joining in that petition, asking WIP to broadcast a public discussion of the disputed questions, "one side to be taken by a high Catholic spokesman and the other side by Judge Rutherford."

Honest Catholics, as well as others, are reporting that the demand for such an open discussion is by no means limited to the people of Philadelphia and vicinity. We agree, and also believe that throughout this entire land there are millions who are eager not only to hear those disputed questions reasonably discussed but also to sign a petition

asking that such a debate be arranged and widely broadcast.

In the public interest, therefore, and for the public convenience, petition blanks and introductory cards similar to those now in use at Philadelphia have been printed and are being contributed to all who want to use them.

#### ORDERLY CO-OPERATION

Any isolated pioneer or other publisher not associated with a company and who desires to assist persons that want to sign the petition will be furnished blanks and cards on request.

Companies are being supplied a sufficient quantity of cards and blanks for immediate use. When more are needed, let us know well in advance.

Let each publisher be provided with a separate container (celluloid) for the special green introductory card. Also provide each one with a substantial cardboard of home size as petition blank (8½ inches by 11 inches). Upon that board two or more petition blanks can be fastened with rubber bands, to keep blanks neat and clean. Cardboard may be folded in half, lengthwise, with blanks inside (lengthwise), and thus be carried conveniently. Carry also a fountain pen or indelible pencil.

At each home or business place, FIRST present the special green card and petition.

That being disposed of, then introduce the literature, using at all times the current presentation. During this petition service, instead of using testimony card for literature, we recommend a verbal testimony, formed according to the current testimony card, either word for word or including in your own language the points shown in card. (For use wherever desirable, carry with you in a separate celluloid container the literature testimony card, so as to be ready to present that card whenever you see fit.) Let this manner of serving the petition and the literature from now on be carefully considered and clearly understood by each publisher. Note well: Make it a habit to see that each signer of the petition fills in correct address, completely, including city and state.

#### NAMES FOR COMPANY'S FILE

As each petition sheet is filled, let name and address of each signer be copied with care on a separate slip or card, suitable for filing, using paper of uniform size for each name. Such copying by typewriter is, of course, preferable. In due time the other information will be sent as to use of copied names kept in the company's file.

Petition sheets filled turn in promptly to the company servant. (Continued on page 4, column 1)

### "Vindication" Books Real Aid to "Riches" Readers

#### Watch Your Time in August

Those who love the Lord have been looking forward to this period with much joy and anticipation. Just as the psalmist, after having a vision of the vindication of Jehovah's name and the great honor and privileges conferred upon the Lord's people of his day by Jehovah, called upon everything that had breath to join him in praising Jehovah, so, today, Jehovah's witnesses, who have the Kingdom vision and see the vindication of Jehovah's name and their privileges in connection therewith, call upon all who have breath to praise Jehovah. This is done by taking the Kingdom message to the people, that they may see the majesty, power and supremacy of Jehovah and bow down before him. "The dead praise not the Lord," and none of the Lord's people can be classed as among the dead. They are in "the land of the living". They have passed from death unto life and are to use their faculties to exalt and magnify the name of the living God, that is, sing Jehovah's praises.

The time set aside for this period is August 1-9.

Are you making the necessary preparations NOW to have as much time as possible to sing Jehovah's praises during this period?

As a pioneer, are you planning to put in at least 40 hours in the service during that time?

Or, as an auxiliary, are you planning to put in at least 20 hours?

Are you a company publisher and planning to put in 10 or more hours during this period?

NOW is your time to do the planning and arranging; do not wait

until the last minute. This is not a large amount of time, and everyone who really recognizes the issue and appreciates the privilege of praising the name of Jehovah will be glad to do that, and more, if physically able to do so.

What kind of combinations are to be featured during the period?

3 bound books, on a 50c contribution.

6 bound books, on \$1.00 contribution.

Where neither combination can be placed, drop to Riches, on a 25c contribution.

If that cannot be placed, 3 booklets, on a 10c contribution, are to be offered.

This variety of presentations should result in the placement of something in almost every home. In your booklet combinations always be sure to have the Choosing and another colored-cover booklet and one self-covered booklet.

What literature is to be used? Vindication 1, 2 and 3 is the initial combination, on a contribution of 50c. Light 1 and 2 and Prophecy are to be included on a \$1.00 contribution, and in homes where they already have Vindication the latter three may be placed, on a 50c contribution.

The light is on, and the three main things we have to contribute to the battle are, faithfulness, time, and zealous enthusiasm. Let us give all three of these unstintingly. The Lord has graciously arranged for each and every one of us to be supplied with a special testimony card, a special attractive wrapper, and a decided reduction in the literature. The field is ready; the Lord's grace and blessing are assured; the rest is up to each and every one of us. Let us, therefore, respond with zeal and enthusiasm. "Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. Praise ye the LORD."—Ps. 150:6.

In order to equal last year's booklet placements we have a tremendous work to do with booklets; and September is the last month. Let every one have a share in this and PLACE BOOKLETS.

## What We Have Done, and What We Still Have to Accomplish

As the year progresses the comparative reports are of increasing interest to those who have the witness work at heart. You remember that the seven months' comparative report showed we were behind by 71,145 bound books, ahead by 504,224 booklets, ahead by 432,676 pieces of literature, behind by 38 in average number of publishers, and 172,305 hours under the number for the seven months of last year. However, to keep this lead, and increase it if possible, every publisher must remember to leave the booklets in combination with _Riches_, and booklets alone when _Riches_ cannot be taken, and get out those pieces of literature at every call possible, which will increase the witness given. The number of publishers is going in the right direction, and this should certainly increase. No doubt there are many in the companies throughout the land who have not previously awakened to their privileges and responsibilities, and these may join in the work Jehovah is doing, and as the publishers increase as they should a greater witness may be given.

And now the total of hours, that very important item, which each one of the Lord's people can affect one way or the other. It is still under last year by \$1,200, but last month it was 172,305 less than a year ago. The brethren who are really in the war now are making an effort to increase their hours, and the trend in hours is in the right direction. Now we have to keep this up and 'let not our hands be slack', if we bring our time in the field ahead of last year and to the quota. There is still a big loss to be made up, and TIME IN THE FIELD must have the attention of every company servant and publisher.

The report as a whole is very gratifying, and manifests Jehovah's rich blessing on those who are putting first the interests of his kingdom. With the few remaining months of this year the Lord's little army should, in His strength, accomplish a splendid witness, surpassing the summer work of any previous year; and this can be done by diligent planning and effort on the part of each one to spend TIME in actual house-to-house witnessing.

## Provide Studies for Jonadabs

### _Riches_ Invitation Slips Prepared

The importance of the establishing of studies throughout the territory of the company cannot be overstated. These studies must be so arranged for the convenience of the interested. Those who obtain literature should be invited to attend the nearest _Riches_ study. So as to aid the publisher in doing this work, the Society is printing an invitation slip mostly arranged which the publisher or the one in care of the territory can fill out in advance. There should be written on this slip, nearly with pen or typewriter, the street address, the day and time of the _Riches_ study nearest to the territory you will be working in during the day. A slip is to be left with each placement, or with those very much interested and who have previously obtained literature. A copy of the invitation slip is sent along with the _Informant_. Please show it to the publishers, and place your order. In lots of 1,000 they can be had at 3c, the same as the radio folders. They should be ordered with other literature and supplies. Be sure that all publishers are furnished with slips with the proper address written thereon for the territory being worked.

At the meeting place there should always be one of the electric signs furnished by the Society, so that the newly interested will know where to attend. Even though they have the address, they will feel more confident in a home where the electric sign is displayed.

Publishers should always be on the alert to find locations for new _Riches_ studies. In many instances these are begun by the use of the phonograph. Pioneers should arrange them in their territory, and, before leaving, ask one of those present to carry on the meeting in the same manner that they have been conducted. These groups should be reported to the Society if you believe it possible to organize them for field work.

It seems advisable that each company organization keep a card file of all publishers and persons of good will who have attended one or more meetings, and at various times throughout the year send them one of these invitation slips or a post card asking them to come to these meetings. This should be done periodically if they are not regular attendants of the company studies. We want to aid the great multitude all possible in taking their stand for the Kingdom.

## A Respectable Presentation

### of the Kingdom Message

The Kingdom message should be presented to the people in a respectable manner, which means, in a manner deserving of regard, and decent in behavior. Let those who are bearing to the people a message of the Devil's organization conduct themselves in a manner befitting the one they serve. If they so desire; but this is not for Jehovah's messengers.

Sincerely and earnestness in presenting the truth to the people is that which accomplishes good results. In carrying _Riches_ and other literature from house to house, the most effective witness is given in showing the people that you really believe what you tell them. Those

of good will recognize that the publisher is sincere, and, because of real earnestness and enthusiasm coming from courage and conviction, are impressed; and, more than anything else that the publisher can do, this respectable presentation results in placements of the printed message.

How different this presentation is from a bulldozing method, which is wrong. Why should one become angry with those to whom he witnesses, or tell them they 'have no brains', or make any such remark, which is not respectable or decent behavior? Occasionally someone errs in the manner of presentation. Let all carefully observe the rule and not displease Jehovah or dishonor him.

## Report Cards Must Be Properly Marked for Credit Allowance

During the month of August the brethren throughout the United States will have the privilege of offering the three _Vindication_ books on a contribution of 50c. Many of the pioneers and some auxiliaries and company workers will want to offer six bound books on a contribution of a dollar. This may be done by offering _Vindication_ 1, 2 and 3, _Light_ 1 and 2, and _Prophecy_. In order to obtain the proper credit on your account because of the reduction, it is absolutely necessary to mark on the bottom of your report card the number of three-book combinations placed. If you place one six-book combination, that is equal to two three-book combinations and should appear on the report card as follows: '2 combinations of 3 books.' Adjustments will be made on auxiliary and company accounts only. The companies will be allowed a credit on their account of 15c for each combination; the auxiliaries, 18c. It is definitely understood by the Society that you do not want any credit on your account if there is no statement of number of combinations placed marked on the bottom of your report card. Credit will be made on all company and auxiliary accounts where a notation of combinations is made. The treasurer, stockkeeper and company servant should make it their business to check the report cards before they are mailed in to the Society. Send in your report card correctly made out. Companies should use their storage stock, and when auxiliaries and pioneers in company territory run out of literature they may borrow, and replace with books as they desire later. Please have in mind that if the party does not take the combination offered, then you will drop to the _Riches_ book on a contribution of 25c, or 3 booklets on a contribution of 10c. Credit is not given on single placements; only on combinations. Be sure that the publishers turn in this information to you on their report slips.

# 'His Strange Work' Report

## 'Multitude' Showing Themselves

A Quarter Million

Bound Books in Nine Days

The report which is set out in detail below is most interesting, and one could very easily spend an hour examining the contents. The first column points out a real increase as to those associating themselves with Jehovah's organization. More than 71,000 persons attended the Memo-

rial, whereas 53,835 partook. A total of 46,200 publishers engaged in the field work. Where were the other 25,000 who attended the Memorial service? These should be aided so that they might see more clearly their responsibility, and it is hoped that in the October campaign at least 50,000 workers will take part in the "Battle Shout". All the

brethren found that Riches goes so well with the people. Christ Jesus is gathering the "other sheep", and those of us who are proclaiming the Kingdom are specially privileged in bringing to the great multitude the message concerning the Kingdom. Many booklets also were distributed, bringing the total of literature up to 1,258,440 pieces placed; and a splendid witness was given by means of sound equipment, too. Over a half million people heard the message in "Sequencing the Nations". It is very encouraging to have these combined reports, for undoubtedly every one of us now not only will prepare for "Jehovah's Praise" Period but will

look forward with interest to the next international period, "Battle Shout." Reports from far and near are set out herewith.

AUSTRALIA

"The campaign from the book Riches resulted in the placing of more than 20,000 bound books in the territory under the supervision of the Australian branch, during 'His Strange Work' testimony period. This has exceeded by far the previous best bound-book campaign, when 12,238 Jehovah were placed in nine days. From all quarters reports show great enthusiasm among the publishers, and it has been difficult to keep all supplied with books. At the time of writing, most of the 55,000 Riches books already supplied to this branch are either placed with the people or in the hands of the publishers.

"During this campaign it was noticeable that the opposition of the enemy is increasing and that deeds of personal violence were reported from various quarters. On the other hand, many people of good will, at the present time under the control of the Roman Catholic Hierarchy, were glad to accept the publication brought to them by Jehovah's faithful ones and expressed their determination to get out of that wicked organization."

CANADA

"Jehovah's witnesses and Jonadales in Canada and Newfoundland entered into 'His Strange Work' testimony period with a zeal and determination to make it the greatest witness yet given in these countries, and by His grace the results have been most gratifying. A total of 12,010 Riches was placed in the hands of the people, the largest number of bound books placed in any nine-day period since 1930."

JAPAN

"Devil carried a severe attack against Jehovah's witnesses with arrests, sickness, extremely cold weather and very much snow and raining during the whole period. The Roman priests began their activity to use public press. The Osaka Asahi, the largest newspaper in Japan, carried a big article against Jehovah's witnesses. Several pioneers were thrown into the prison during the period. All faithful ones are ever rejoicing to serve Most High and to prove their integrity under this trial condition."

SPAIN

"After several brethren from Madrid had worked a small, near-by town, two brothers went back to an inn to collect the reserve stock left there earlier in the day. A large crowd of infuriated workmen had gathered and, suspecting our work of being Fascist propaganda under a disguise, beat them and handled them roughly. Given though the mayor himself, who happened to be a Communist like the men, interceded for the brothers and provided them with an escort of two civil guards back to the station and actually right into the train itself, they were pursued even there and received blows in the train itself."

SWEDEN

"The arrival of Riches in Swedish on the Monday before the period started was, of course, an item of greatest interest and importance to the publishers. And although we did not receive the whole edition in one shipment, we were nevertheless able to supply almost all publishers with this grand weapon. And it went out well, too! Some had to wire for more already on the evening of the second day. Many reports show that the people have received this message more favorably than ever before.

# DENMARK

"Our last record number of publishers was 804, in February; now we had 843, which is 205 more than April 1935. Is it not fine to see the Jonadabs joining up and taking part in the work? The weather was cold and wet, but the ardor of the friends was red-hot. All were much encouraged."

# FINLAND

"The last testimony period has been the best we ever have had. The brethren were filled with joy and enthusiasm, and more than ever before took part in the work (now 777 workers; the best before, 722), using an average (now 12 hours; the best before, 10½) more time (now 9,370 hours; the best before, 7,642) than before, and, as a result of that, placing more literature (now 43,916; the best before, 38,182) in the hands of the people than ever before."

# HAWAIIAN ISLANDS

"Response from publishers and people called upon with _Riches_ is best yet; praise the Loss! Attack upon sound car by excited women furthered the witness. This is by far the best testimony period, and they seem to get better and better."

# INDIA

"Another intensive witness by eight publishers operating in two sound cars was given in the Nilgiri Hills, where many people are on holiday at this season of the year. The speech 'Separating the Nations' aroused the anger of the missionaries and those of their flocks who are blind, while a few more gave serious attention to the message. From other districts some reports of general opposition by the modern Philistines and general apathy on the part of the common people, but some everywhere are showing interest in the message."

# CHINA

"Two Jonadabs (Chinese) participated in the service with result of 50 booklets placed. Being Easter time, offices all closed for three days, most of people were away from Shanghai for holiday excursion. Two days had showers and heavy rain. _Riches_ did not come on hand during the Period. The lecture 'Separating the Nations' was broadcast over XMHA on the 21st. About 50 orders for _Riches_ obtained during the campaign."

# BRAZIL

"Ten persons were immersed. This is the best report we have had. Jonadabs are coming in. A greater number of publishers entered the field this year than in the last year. The weather was the best. The doors are still open in this part of the field; praised by Jehovah!"

# PHILIPPINE ISLANDS

"Many attended the Memorial supper. A few Jonadabs underwent water immersion. Since the Lord revealed to his people who are the modern Philistines, there was great zeal among the brethren here who are working in perfect unity until the Lord may say 'It is enough'."

# SOUTH AFRICA

"This was the best bound-book effort to date. Comparing our results with the figures for the campaign when the bound book _Jehovah_ was released we find there was an increase of 700 bound volumes and 14,000 booklets on this occasion. The

figures for the sound machine work are also better than anything we have had heretofore, the increase in the hours being 90 and in the attendance 3,500. _Riches_ was enthusiastically received by the publishers, and the public have already clearly demonstrated that they want it, too! The crew of one sound car reports: 'We covered 900 miles and gave lectures in the twelve main dorps of the Northern Transvaal and also on several thickly populated farms. We had visible audiences up to 500 in some dorps, and we could discern the Jonadabs among the crowds. Some bishop had the police come up to arrest us, but when the constables arrived they told us that we had full right to go on. Thus Jehovah protects his people for his name's sake.'"

# NYASALAND

"The work continues to go forward in the face of considerable opposition in some parts. Just lately more than one of our fellows have been rather badly handled. They have had their books matched away and not returned, their testimony cards torn, stones, sticks and sand thrown at them, and one of the sisters was entirely disrobed. These incidents took place during the campaign. A protest has been lodged with the district commissioner, who has oversight of the district concerned."

# NORTHERN RHODESIA

"The misrepresentation and persecution which has been going on in Northern Rhodesia has not discouraged the brethren in any way. Although the greater number of the Society's publications are at present banned, the publishers, by putting forth the best effort to date, equaled the distribution during the corresponding effort of last year. Just before the campaign began all the Europeans in Northern Rhodesia were served with a copy of GA No. 425. This special issue has since been the subject of a question in the Legislative Council. There cannot be any Europeans, and can be only a very few motives, in Northern Rhodesia who have not heard of Jehovah's witnesses and the message of the Lord which they declare."

# SOUTHERN RHODESIA

"The proposed sedition bill and native preachers bill which are now being discussed, and which if they become law will bring conditions in this country into line with those now obtaining in Germany, did not give any of the Lord's true witnesses cold feet in Southern Rhodesia. Judging by the time parliament is taking over the bills they appear to be in a real quandary. They are hoping that the bother in Northern Rhodesia would provide a nice background for these bills, but, the attempt to make us the scapegoat in Northern Rhodesia having failed and the secretary of state for the colonies having just decided that our request for European control in Northern Rhodesia should be granted, the legislators in Southern Rhodesia, having admitted that the bills were aimed at us, do not appear to know now which way to turn. The brethren are in good spirit and pushing on with the work."

# UNITED STATES

On the evening of April 6 throughout the land 35,172 of Jehovah's people assembled to celebrate the memorial of Jehovah's name. Of this number, 25,435 partook. The heart-dressing words of the "Courage and Victory" lecture were heard by most of the brethren at this time, strengthening them to have a share in Jehovah's "strange work". The Memorial attendance this year is the largest on record.

In the United States 24,441 brethren were in the field during "His Strange Work" testimony period, which is the largest on record for any special period. It is interesting to note that this number is more than double the number of publishers in the field during a similar period in 1931 (12,141 publishers), and more than three times that of the publishers for a similar period in 1928 (7,951 publishers).

The release of _Riches_ during "His Strange Work" testimony period in the United States exceeded that of any previous bound book.

The "Separating the Nations" lecture, together with other lectures, was used to broadcast the Kingdom message. There was an increase in sound attendance of 300,000 persons, over the report for last year. It has also been reported that quite a number of _Riches_ study classes have been started to further aid the people of good will in getting a knowledge of the Kingdom.

"His Strange Work" testimony period was certainly a season of great refreshment and joy. It was filled with strenuous activity to the praise of Jehovah's name. It was the greatest rally of Jehovah's people up to this time in this land to raise high the standard of the Kingdom. All praise to Jehovah for continually sustaining his people and granting them the privilege of bearing his name before the nations.

# SEVEN MILLION

To aid each publisher to see a goal, we offer the reminder that in the United States, during the first seven months of the present service year, more than five million persons were served with literature by Jehovah's witnesses and their companions. Much of that work was done during one of the most strenuous of winters. Jehovah sustained His servants.—Psalm 147:17.

As a goal, then, for the work from now on it is suggested that an _average_ of 350 signatures taken by each of 20,000 publishers shows a total of SEVEN MILLION signers. That is the equivalent of about one-half of this country's "Catholic population", not counting babies and young children.

To increase the appreciation of all as to the importance and value of working shoulder to shoulder, it is further suggested that each one who understands that he can serve more than the proposed _average_ of 350 persons with opportunity to sign the petition do so, and also pair together for this special work with one who, by reason of physical infirmity or other unavoidable handicap, believes he can serve less than the average of 350 persons.

Let _thoroughness_ be the keynote of the petition service to fair-minded people throughout the land. Study carefully the entire outline of co-operation here shown.

Let each publisher and each company servant prepare thoughtfully, in advance, his part to the work. Then, in union with all of Jehovah's publishers of the entire country, with joyful and fearless determination pursue the task untriguingly until it is done, confidently believing that the true and living God, who gives the increase, can and will preserve and sustain His faithful servants and greatly prosper the work of their hands, for His own name's sake.

Pioneers should give first attention to their regular work, introducing the petition when it appears to be opportune.
