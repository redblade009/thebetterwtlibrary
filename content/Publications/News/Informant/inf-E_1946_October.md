# INFORMANT

OCTOBER, 1946

"Be glad, ye nations, with his people."—Rom. 15: 10, Ruth.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## Awake! Campaign Opens!

### "New Song" Testimony Period Features New Magazine

Wake Up More than
50,000 Persons

with Awake! Subscriptions

Present world planners continue to sing their old song over and over again. They attempt a slight variation, a new key, and different lyrics, but they still produce the same old discordant and dodeful tune of international collaboration. And no wonder! Only Jehovah deserves a new song! It is only what we have done that is worthy of being sung in joyful tunes. His deeds, being distinctively new to humankind, supply a new theme for the new song. No cheap man-made song is this. No, but Jehovah supplies the truths which make up the theme of the song. The theme, now in the chronicles of humankind, is that Jehovah has taken his sovereign power over the earth and is now reigning through the Kingdom. Now it is up to us to awake to the rousing call "Oh sing unto Jehovah a new song!"—Ps. 98:1, A. S. V.

Indeed Jehovah has graciously supplied the truths for the new song. It is time for the people to be awakened to such. Alert to this fact, the Lord's servants will be awake and awaken others in October by obtaining subscriptions for the new magazine Awake! The subscription rate will be \$1.00 per year. Differing from other subscription campaigns there will be no book premium given. This is strictly an Awake! campaign, and it is hoped that more than 50,000 subscriptions for this magazine will be obtained during the one month of October.

"Awake!"—What Does It Offer?

How unthanked all were when Awake! was released! How impressed with the first article of the first issue, "Wake Up, World!" And as we look through the magazine we appreciate how apt it will be in showing the difference between the joyful and magnificent "New Song" of Jehovah's deeds and the cheap, shallow, worn-out song of would-be world peacemakers. Make no mistake about it. Awake! is an entirely new magazine from cover to cover. It is not an old magazine with a new face. It directs its attention to waking up the people, to showing them what is going on, and to alerting them to the vital issues of the day.

"The Mission" (on page 2) is an excellent talking point. Show the people how Awake! has no fetters, but is free to print facts; how it uses news channels, supplemented by its own correspondents from all continents and scores of nations. Its pages cover many fields of knowledge—government, commerce, religion, history, geography, science, social conditions, and natural wonders. It exposes subtle dangers and reflects the hope of a righteous world.

Then there is the table of contents, showing the articles of the current issue. The feature "The Word Is Truth", with its Scriptural subject, will be of interest. Or "Watching the World", with its world-wide news digest. Yes, Awake! is new, it is up to date. The publisher will never be at a loss to present its advantages for the reader.

All-out Campaign Organization

This one-month campaign requires immediate work. No waiting until the month is over before the publishers wake up to the campaign. Having ordered sufficient supplies of magazines, having lined up territory for witnessing, every publisher should be in the field with Awake! from October 1 until October 31. Those brethren who were full-time "singed" during vacation should not cease their good work, but should present this offer evenings, weekends and all times. Good-will persons should be encouraged to have a few extra copies of Awake! which they will give to their friends, trying to obtain subscriptions from them, thereby joining in singing the "New Song".

Awake! will be presented at the door throughout October. If the people are unable to obtain a year's subscription, offer a six-month subscription for 50c. Then drop down to a copy of Awake! with a Watch-force magazine and a booklet, on a 10c contribution. At least endeavor to leave a copy of Awake! in every home on a 5c contribution. Remember too, keep your House to House Record and call back on all obtainers of the magazine to see if they wish to have it regularly.

October should witness evening Awake! work too. New publishers should be taken in this evening work and assisted to get a magazine route started. They will call back with each issue of the magazine trying to further that person's interest, presenting the subscription offer also in this work at every opportunity.

Regular street witnessing will be stimulated by the magazine. Already brethren report an increase in placements, as a result of the people's interest in this new magazine. Keep up their interest by being alert. Know what is going on. Don't let street work slump into a "silent testimony" or lifeless repetition of some trite phrase. Be up to date. Use the headings of the articles for slogans. Use points from the articles to arouse interest. Make short pithy statements about some of the news found in "Watching the World". Tell it out about the hope of a righteous world. Be positive but tactful, using good judgment in calling out the slogans. A few

suggestions are here given for the use of the brethren, in addition to the above-stated suggestions, but these should not become stereotyped in their use.

"Awake! is free to publish facts."
"Awake! is awake to world conditions."

"Keep awake by reading Awake!"
"Awake! views behind the news."

"Awake to the hope of a righteous world."

"World news in review."
Further, a short announcement regarding the subscription may be made at the close of each public talk during October.

At the doors, on the streets, riding on trains or buses, or just visiting friends or relatives, urge all persons to keep awake by reading Awake! Wherever you are join harmoniously in the swelling chorus now singing the "New Song", "Break forth and sing for joy, yea, sing praises." Make October a banner month. Not at a later date, not at a "more convenient time", but as the apostle says, "Now it is high time to awake!"

### "The Prince of Peace"

New Booklet
for December Work

The month of December will be devoted to the distribution of the new booklet "The Prince of Peace". This will be a 64-page colored-cover booklet containing the talks "The Prince of Peace" and "Fearless Against the World Conspiracy", as well as a section on "Warning" and another on "Resolution". All during December this booklet will be the main offer, being left on a contribution of 5c per copy. Those who heard the talks at the Assembly will keenly appreciate the importance of getting this message into the hands of the people as soon as possible.

Companies and pioneers may order now, so that they will have sufficient supplies on hand for the December campaign.

Awake!
in Foreign Languages

Many brethren are wondering when Awake! will be published in foreign languages, as Consolation has been. It is necessary to prepare plates for headings and also to comply with postal regulations when such changes are made. Be assured that Awake! will be available in all the languages in which Consolation has been published just as soon as it can be arranged, and announcements of such changes will be made.

## Pioneering—a Vocation for Those Who Have Faith

Kingdom Expansion
Calls for More
Full-Time Servants

"The basic requirement for pioneer service is FAITH! That was the firm conviction of thousands of pioneer-minded ministers after hearing Brother Knorr's stirring discourse "Equipped for Every Good Work". Does not Jehovah own heaven and earth? Does not be care for the birds of the air and beasts of the field? Has he not promised that to those who "seek first the kingdom" needed food, shelter and clothing shall be provided? Of course he has. But the publisher must believe that promise. He must have FAITH, and let God prove true to his word that he will sustain his pioneer servant, daily."

Here was the answer, clear-cut, convincing. Scriptural, to those who have asked, "How much money must I save first? Do I need a car and trailer? How can I be sure of food and clothing?" Satisfied that faith in God, and not stored-up material wealth, is the thing required, hundreds of brethren at Cleveland, young and old, single and married, asked for pioneer applications. These and the scores who have written in since the Assembly, armed now with unshakable faith, are going to take God at his word and make pioneering their vocation.

Pioneers Share in Expansion
It is the pioneer that must spearhead the expansion of the Kingdom

witness into foreign fields, and there lay the groundwork for reconstruction of the true worship. Hundreds of full-time ministers, including many who first began service in response to the pioneer call at St. Louis, and even following the "Jephthah's Daughter" drama in 1942, are now Gilead graduates singing forth the New Song in other lands. Hundreds more will yet be selected to share in the vital expansion activity, from among pioneers today who hold fast their faith and do with their might the good work of gospel-publishing.

To share in the expanding Kingdom work, however, the pioneer is not required to move into foreign territory. The United States is fertile land for reaping the fruitage of pioneer preaching. At least 22,000,000 persons in this country were not witnessed to in 1945. In many countries there is not one publisher. Large cities await pioneers, for proper coverage, while companies scattered throughout America, north, south, east and west, write to the Society's office daily and say, "Please send us pioneer help." The Society keenly feels its responsibility to oversee the spreading of "this gospel" into every section of the United States and the world. # "Say \[it] Among the Nations"

## November Features "Let God Be True"

### Cover Outlying Territory

Say what? "Say among the nations. Jehovah reigneth: the world also is established that it cannot be moved: he will judge the peoples with equity." (Ps. 96: 10, A. S. Y.) This, then, is the "new song" that Jehovah's servants are commanded to sing. Its keynote: the paramount issue of Jehovah's universal sovereignty. It rings with the truth that "Jehovah hath become King?" and also that the New World shall be completely established. This inspired about-command is for the "glad nations" to invite men of all nations to declare with them. "Thy word is true from the beginning; and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever." In fact, it narrows down to our own locality and individual selves. Wherever we live on this earth we must send forth the cry "Jehovah ... reigneth" and carry forth the challenge "Let God be true".

### November's Offer

November will have as its feature the new book "Let God Be True". Just think, this is the first new book we have had to offer the public in two years! It is indeed food for starving millions, and the populace of the "glad nations" is favored to let the whole world know that Jehovah reigns, and to have this instrument to use in doing so. Along with "Let God Be True", "Be Glad, Ye Nations" booklet or any other booklet will be given, both on a 25c contribution.

**Stress Coverage of Outlying Territory**
Many outlying towns in company territory are in need of a good witness. In most cases, this must be done before winter weather makes it almost impossible. We shall want

to give a thorough witness in these towns, and no better way can be found than by starting public meetings in them. To get well under way in November will require prompt action in getting an appropriate place to hold the meetings. Line up your speakers and order handbills immediately. Small companies probably can handle only one series in November; the larger can start two or more.

In working these outlying towns, work long hours and hard. Experienced publishers will find this an excellent means of breaking in new publishers and instructing them as to how to best present the message and to overcome barriers. When working, take careful note of interest shown and arrange to call for them; take them to the meeting and acquaint them with other brethren.

Much interest will be found in these out-of-the-way places. After you make the initial contact, call back as many times as possible, starting a study in the new book "Let God Be True". If weather or transportation hindrances prevent your return for a while, urge them to study the book themselves, or with others, using the questions on each page to aid them. Where personal contact is impossible, keep in touch by mail, showing your interest in their welfare.

The book "Let God Be True" is particularly provided by Jehovah that the good-will people of all nations may learn "the first principles of Christ" and be judged on the "right" side of the King. What better way could there be to say among the nations that Jehovah reigns, that He is the true and faithful God, than to say it by works with "Let God Be True"?

### Convention Review

The October 1, 1946, issue of _The Watchtower_ contains a complete review of the Glad Nations Theocratic Assembly. It will not be scheduled for regular _Watchtower_ study but portions of it should be reviewed at each service meeting for a period of four weeks. The material lends itself for the following coverage:

The first week spend fifteen minutes covering the first article from the beginning down to the subheading "By Whom Organized", on page 253. The next week spend fifteen minutes to finish the first article. For the following two weeks devote twenty minutes to cover four convention days at each service meeting. This should not be taken up in question-and-answer form, but these presentations should be well-prepared condensations of the _Watchtower_ material, by one or more brethren.

Encourage all good-will interest to attend these meetings.

### Care in Ordering

Brethren should keep in mind ordering sufficient literature to make up a 100-pound shipment whenever possible. Also, pioneers should endeavor to combine their orders with the company order. In such cases, the pioneer orders should be attached to the company order and be mailed with it. Considerable time is wasted when a pioneer order comes in with a request to send such with the company order sent in a few days prior.

Some pioneers and companies have ordered large quantities of "Equipped for Every Good Work", apparently thinking such is campaign literature. This book is published primarily for the publisher, for his own study and use in preaching the gospel; also for the assistance of many persons of good-will. It is not a campaign book yet; hence it should not be presented as an offer from house to house, but only on back-calls. In most instances, the larger orders for pioneers and some companies have been cut. There is only one rate on "Equipped for Every Good Work", namely 50c.
