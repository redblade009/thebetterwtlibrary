# INFORMANT

SEPTEMBER, 1939

"But they shall not prevail against thee."—Jer. 1: 19.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## Almost Made It

39,771 Publishers New Peak

Publishers of Jehovah's kingdom were so stirred by the world-wide convention that nothing could hold them back. The month following the convention finds a new peak of publishers, 39,771, engaging in the field work. This is very gratifying and brings joy to the hearts of all who are for the Kingdom, because they see the progressive onward march of the Theocratic Government.

Some companies have doubled their number of publishers during the year. This was accomplished by consistently aiding and instructing the inactive ones to do field work. The companies that have the Kingdom uppermost in their minds and have set aside strife and are working together in peace have shown the greatest increase.

The Society must still compile the report for "Divine Mandate" Testimony Period, and it is confidently expected that this month will find that we have reached the 40,000 publishers mark.

In just a short time now the new fiscal year of the Society begins, and it is natural to expect that next year will be the greatest of all, because of our greater understanding of the work to be done and the coming in of the "great multitude". Every regional, zone, and company servant should in every manner assist the publishers to get into the field and should aid them in making 60 hours a month.

The average number of publishers in the field for the last 10 months has been 34,630. This is nearly 9,000 more publishers in the field per month than last year. Let's look forward to next year with over a 40,000 average. "THEOCRACY" Testimony Period, in October, will find a new peak in publishers serving the Kingdom.

## "Government and Peace"

New Booklet Off the Press

You have been wondering when the two lectures given by Brother Rutherford at the New York City convention, "Victory" and "Government and Peace", already available on records, would be ready in print. The new booklet, Government and Peace, containing both lectures, is now ready. The front cover shows a photograph of Brother Rutherford "in action" during the Sunday broadcast, taken just before the Catholic hoodlums staged their abortive attempt to break up the meeting.

Every "locust" will want to have a share in taking this message into the homes of the people, and "take away the veneer of religion that has been smeared over the people to keep them in darkness". Listen to the records, read the booklet, get set for a rally for THE THEOCRACY during international testimony period in October.

## "Theocracy" Testimony Period Begins Oct. 1

New Book, New Booklet, New Records, and a New Year

### More than 40,000 Publishers Expected

Ever since Sunday, June 25, that memorable day when Brother Rutherford delivered the history-making speech "Government and Peace" at Madison Square Garden, the Lord's people throughout the world have been waiting for its release for distribution among the public.

Saturday, October 1, will see your desire fulfilled. Government and Peace will then be offered with Salvation on a contribution of 25c. Where the 25c combination cannot be placed, Government and Peace with two other booklets should be offered on a 10c contribution; and where they are unable to contribute this amount but are interested and wish to read, the booklet Government and Peace should be left on any contribution they are able to offer; and if they are unable to contribute anything, leave the booklet with them free. This important booklet should be in every home in the territory.

This is the most vitally important combination we have ever been privileged to offer to the people; principally, because it is the Lord's message of the hour. We have all

read Salvation and realize its power. We have heard or read Government and Peace, or will read it shortly if we haven't read it already, and are acquainted with the desperate effort made by the Devil through the Hierarchy-Fascist-Conglinist combination to stop it at any cost.

We have the recordings "Government and Peace", and undoubtedly most of the brethren have played them a number of times and have been thrilled by the greatest speech ever delivered on earth since the day of our Lord.

This three-fold combination will rip a hole in the Devil's earthly scheme of things that they will never be able to repair. Never before have we been so thoroughly equipped, nor have we had such a great responsibility.

Let each one therefore, with humility of mind and a consuming zeal for the Lord, accept this great responsibility that the Lord has intrusted to us and go forward in the "THEOCRACY" Testimony Period to deliver the greatest witness ever given by the Lord's people.

The Society suggested 60 hours a month for each company pub-

lisher. Many, realizing the great import of the hour in which we are living, will put in many more hours than that. Others who have not been able to attain the 60-hour quota up to now will find in this campaign the necessary incentive to overcome all obstacles and attain to that peak, and those who are so situated that it is impossible for them to put in 60 hours will be keenly disappointed in that but will gladly put in all the time they possibly can, that this important message may be delivered and this declaration of the Lord's judgment against the enemy may be made manifest.

The vicious and devilish attack at Madison Square Garden has proved a boomerang to the enemy. It makes a most effective background for Brother Rutherford's speech, of which, by the Lord's grace, he took due advantage. Therefore, as a part of this campaign this speech should be played in the hall of every company organization, it should be presented in every possible home where a back-call can be made, and the people in general, particularly the Catholic population, be given a vision of the devilish conditions that exist in that organization.

Plan your time and work so as to give the most effective witness possible. Use the "Snare and Racket" record to introduce Salvation, and also use the opposite side of this record, "Victory No. 13," following "Snare and Racket". These records are an effective testimony for Salvation and Daemate. Carry both books with you at all times.

For those who do not yet have photographs we have a Salvation testimony card, which will go out with this (September) Informant. This will also serve as an identification card for all publishers.

Each publisher should have an assignment of territory around his or her home to work in at odd times throughout the week. Week-ends should be spent in cleaning up the towns and rurals before they become inaccessible because of winter weather.

See that this combination is placed in all your small towns, large towns and rural territory before the winter closes in.

Salvation has broken all records. We have printed over a million copies already, and now find it necessary to work nights also to meet the demand.

In addition to the above we have the "Victory" speech in recorded and printed form also. With all of these provisions of the Lord we have every reason to anticipate that the Kingdom interests will advance tremendously during the "THEOCRACY" Testimony Period, and surely will find more publishers in the field than ever before, more than 40,000 singing the praises of the Lord in the United States alone.

## The September Campaign

September is the close of the fiscal year 1939. Whether this year will result in more literature being placed than in 1938 depends much on the whole-hearted enthusiasm and the time devoted to the house-to-house field service by all the brethren during September. Let's go ahead and do it!

### Place "Salvation" and Booklets

The offer during September has been increased to include Salvation and three booklets on a contribution of 35c. This should result not only in a large number of Salvation books placed, but also in many more booklets distributed.

Companies still have on their shelves old large-size colored-cover booklets (English and foreign) and Face the Facts and Fascism or Freedom or other self-covered booklets. All these should be cleared out of stock, turned over to the publishers, and put out in the field during September. This can easily be done if each and every publisher takes a proportionate share of these booklets and makes it his business to put them out during the month. The stockkeeper and company servant should see that whatever of such booklets are in stock are so disposed of at once. Zone servants should check to see that their respective zones have cleared their shelves of large-size booklets, Face the Facts, etc., so that by October 1

they can wholly concentrate on the Government and Peace booklet campaign.

During the week-ends of September an extensive witness should be given in the rurals and the small towns of your assignment. All cars available should start EARLY for the "rurals". On account of the fewer calls that can be made, and the expense involved, the brethren should be on their territory early and put in an average of from five to seven hours each Saturday and Sunday. During the other days of the week, when the publishers have only a few hours available, territory close by one's home should be worked, so that the time is put to the best use possible.

As soon as the company receives its supply of the new records, they should be put to immediate use. In the rurals especially, you should find it easy to run both sides of record P-204, P-203, "Snare and Racket" and "Victory".

Remember that during September, in addition to stressing the new book, Salvation, the goal of each company is to distribute all the old large-size booklets and self-covered booklets in stock, as well as any Face the Facts and Fascism or Freedom booklets on hand. You should make it a point to leave at least one booklet in each rural home where there is any interest at all manifested.

### 8,885 More Publishers This Year than Last

A comparison of the ten months' report covering the first ten months of 1939 with that of the same months of 1938 is most gratifying. An increase of 8,885 publishers each month is a clear indication of the awakening of the people of good will and Jonadabs to their great privilege of serving the Lord and advancing the Kingdom interests.

This represented an increase of 2,197,070 hours. The bound books, of course, show a decrease, but we believe that with an aggressive campaign with the Salvation book during the month of September, which campaign began in August, this can be overcome by the beginning of the fiscal year. Each and every publisher should accept it as his personal responsibility to see that this deficit in the number of bound books placed is wiped out by October 1.

Some might say, How can the

report be so gratifying if we have a decrease of over 422,258 bound books? If there were nothing else to balance this, of course it would not be very encouraging; but it is when we see that there was over a 400-percent increase in back-calls made during this period, also an increase of approximately 38,000 new subscriptions and almost 370,000 booklets, whereby the decrease of 422,258 bound books is more than offset.

However, the Lord's people are going to zealously endeavor to clean up this deficit of bound books, so that they will have an increase in every branch of the service by the end of the fiscal year. This can be done, and undoubtedly will be done.

## Information Marchers in Groups of Three

### Read Instructions Carefully

The purpose of an information march is to convey to the people information relative to the Kingdom. By having two or three publishers in given territories, with the proper overhead standards and placards, a more effective witness can be given than by a long march of several hundred persons.

### Arrangement of March

Three publishers should be given an assignment of territory in which to march. These three should walk fifteen to twenty feet apart, two of them carrying overhead standards and the third one distributing handbills, and all three wearing the large placards on their bodies. These three marchers should be assigned to one or more blocks and will continue to march in this assigned territory during the time allotted for the march. If advisable in your territory, they can be given just one block in which to march, or even one side of a block. This is left to the discretion of the one arranging the march.

Other groups of three publishers should be given a similar assignment, until all publishers are given territory in which to march and the main section of your city covered.

"Parades" of ten or twenty or more publishers in a line walking down the main street are to be discontinued.

In following out this new arrangement of information marches the publisher comes within the law set down by the Supreme Court in the cases of Lovell vs. Griffin and C.I.O. vs. Hague. At no time should permits be asked for to carry on these information marches, regardless of any local ordinances, because an individual has the right to carry signs on his person and distribute announcements in this manner. This arrangement has been tried in several cities and has proved to be a more effective means of giving the witness and informing the people of the coming lectures.

### New Placard Packet

The Society furnishes for use in information marches a packet of twenty large-size placards and four small cards, size 11 by 14 inches. On the small signs appear the wordings:

- (1) RELIGION IS A SNARE AND A RACKET
- (2) CHRISTIANITY IS YOUR PROTECTION
- (3) SERVE GOD AND CHRIST THE KING
- (4) READ THE BOOK SALVATION AND LEARN THE RIGHT WAY

In arranging your information marches, print on cards 11 by 14 inches the name of your hall, the address and time of meeting, and, if space permits, the subject of the next talk. One of these will back up each of the overhead standards, and by the carrier's continually turning these standards the people on the sidewalk can read both sides, in addition to obtaining a handbill announcing the meetings. The large signs are to be worn by all the publishers who advertise the four latest speeches by Brother Rutherford.

In placing your marches, one territory can have overhead standards Nos. 1 and 2, backed up with the local address, and the territory adjoining it on both sides could have overhead standards with the back and front giving the address of the public meeting. Then, the territory next can have overhead standards Nos. 3 and 4. Thus you distribute throughout the business section the various signs and keep prominently before the eyes of the people the time and place of the next meeting.

The Society is now filling orders for the new packet of placards, and the publishers should put them to use immediately. Arrange for information marches in your own home town and in other towns in your territory where public meetings are arranged.
