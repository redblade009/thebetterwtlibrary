# INFORMANT

MAY, 1941

"Salvation unto our God . . . and unto the Lamb."—Rev. 7: 10.

BROOKLYN, N.Y.

# "Watchtower" Campaign Extended One Month

4-Month Campaign, Ending with May, Should Show Results

# Go over Your Quota on

Books, Booklets and
Subscriptions!!

There is no doubt that the Lord
is directing His organization in the
earth. Manifestly the extension of
the Watchtower Campaign to include
the month of May is a merciful pro-
vision of the Lord to permit us to
make up our quota for the Watch-
tower Campaign. The quota set for
February, March and April was
100,000 new subscriptions, 1,000,000
bound books, and 6,750,000 booklets.
This was a reasonable quota for the
more than 50,000 company publishers
and pioneers now reporting regularly
in the United States. However, ac-
cording to the rate at which new
Watchtower subscriptions are being
received at Brooklyn now, by the
end of April we shall have obtained
just a little better than 50,000, which
is only half the quota for the three-
month campaign. Up to March 31
there were 37,521 new subscriptions
sent in to the Brooklyn office. It
appears, too, that we shall not attain
the quota of books and booklets.
Why?

# 50,000 Subscriptions to Go!

February, March and April have
slipped by and the quota is only half
made. This means there is much
work to be done during May. There
are 50,000 publishers regularly re-
porting in the United States. If
each publisher sees to it that he
obtains at least one subscription
during May (regardless of how many
he has previously obtained), we shall
gain our objective, 100,000 new
Watchtower subscriptions for the
campaign. The very fact that the
Lord has arranged through His or-
ganization for the extension of the
campaign indicates that it is His
will that the quota be made. There-
fore let each publisher of The The-
ocracy soberly determine to make it
his business to get at least one sub-
scription before May 31.

Books and booklets also are low,
but these will show a correspond-
ing increase with the subscriptions.
If the new subscription quota is
reached, undoubtedly the rest of the
quota will be reached. Each com-
pany publisher, therefore, should put
forth every effort during May to
place five bound books and at least
thirty booklets, in addition to getting
subscriptions: pioneers, 40 books and
250 booklets. Elsewhere in this In-
formant suggestion is made as to
how to increase the booklet place-
ments, and other features of the
work. More time in the field is re-
quired. If each individual assumes
his responsibility and takes care of
his share of the quota we shall more
than make the quota. Wherever
you go, always be prepared to take
a subscription. By the Lord's grace
we shall go over the top on every-

thing, books, booklets and subscrip-
tions if we do our part, namely, be
a publisher and put in time in the
field.

There is every reason why the
quota can be reached in May. Weath-
er conditions no longer hinder any-
one in any part of the country. Tu-
ral territory can easily be covered,
usually yielding much fruit. With
favorable weather the doorstep set-
ups using the "Instruction" record
will be unanimously pressed into
service. Evening witnessing will be
carried on all over the country. We
shall have a new peak of publishers.
With the willing publishers of The
Theocracy moving forward unitedly,
each having in mind his share in the
quota, we shall reach our goal.

# Your Quota

By the Lord's grace each company
will go over the top in all three fea-
tures of the Watchtower Campaign

quota. In addition, make a separate
quota for the month of May, reck-
oned at one subscription, five books
and thirty booklets per publisher,
and then permit nothing to prevent
your company from making it. Keep
a weekly check on your progress;
and if you begin to lag behind, put
forth additional effort. Let the serv-
ants take the lead. Undoubtedly May
will witness the greatest effort put
forth by Jehovah's people during the
Watchtower Campaign.

These are serious days. Through-
out the earth freedom of action is
rapidly being taken from Jehovah's
people. This may be the last Watch-
tower Campaign. Do not neglect your
privileges. Do your part. With full
confidence in Jehovah join in the vic-
tory song! Lay aside your "weights"!
Remember your individual quotas!
One subscription, five books, thirty
booklets, during May! Jehovah's
will shall be done.

April 1, 1941

# To the Magazine Publishers:

The witnesses for Jehovah are the representatives of the Most High and
should deport themselves accordingly with dignity and gentility whether on
the street or elsewhere. In the magazine street work avoid anything that
has the appearance of rudeness. It is reported that some call out: "Vitamins
for the mind. Read The WATCHTOWER." That is hardly proper.

The exhibiting of signs during this magazine service seems to have per-
formed its work. Henceforth no more signs will be used. Calling attention in
a dignified way to the headings of the articles in the magazine will be more
effective, no doubt.

Let the people observe from your words and conduct that you desire to
help and comfort them in these days of great stress.

Be fearless of man and trust entirely in Jehovah and his King; therefore
be very courageous. Jehovah and the Lord Jesus guide and bless you.

Your brother and servant by His grace,

# Proper Expressions

for Street Work

The brethren, in offering the mag-
azines on street corners, should use
expressions that will not cause prov-
ocation. The expression "RELIGION
IS A RACKER" has been used exten-
sively in the past, and from now on
should be discontinued, and con-
structive statements be used. The
publisher can express to the passer-
by, "REMIND FOR WORLD BUSINESS.
Read The WATCHTOWER, 5c." This
should not be shouted in one's ear or
be heard across the street, but should
be spoken in a moderate tone of
voice. Publishers may call out the
titles of the articles in the maga-
zines; or, set out below are some
expressions that Brother Rutherford
has approved in connection with
"Read The Watchtower, 5c":

# Remedy for world distress

The Theocracy, the hope of mankind
Escape from death at Armageddon
Bible removes fear of death
Prophecy shows deliverance near
Truth brings light, peace, joy
World peace by God's Kingdom
Comfort for 'men of good-will'

Seek righteousness and meekness
Theocracy means totalitarians' end
Understand God's present purpose
Kingdom benefits for humankind
Your Bible questions answered
Teach children the truth

The Consolation magazine has
printed on it (in large type, display-
ing the contents of the magazine)
expressions that you may use when
on the street corners.

All the publishers of the Kingdom
should be diligent in having a part
in the magazine work. It is hoped
that in this manner, throughout the
United States, more than one million
magazines (of both The Watchtower
and Consolation) will be distributed
each month. We feel sure that every
one of the magazine publishers will
give special heed to Brother Ruther-
ford's letter printed above and follow
the organization instructions.

# June, Bound-Book Month

## "Theocratic Ambassadors" Testimony Period

### The Greatest Yet!

### Be Supplied!!

Concerning the activity of the ambassadors of The Theocratic Government a recent issue of The Watchtower states, "Now the people of God, having been favored in the light of the Mighty One, Christ Jesus' shining upon them, joyfully arise and shine forth the message of the Kingdom. Have you arisen, and is the light shining upon you, and are you using that light to the glory of THE THEORETICS?" All who now take an active part, striving diligently to make the quota which the Lord outlines through his organization, are doing this very thing; and this results in the Lord's approval and rich blessings upon them.

With a new peak in publishers' imminent and everyone on the job doing his part, we should by far exceed our previous record month for bound books January, 1940, when 532,178 bound books were placed. In that month 40,278 publishers reported, whereas around 70,000 or more are expected by the month of June; for which reason many more bound books should be placed than in any other campaign. Additionally, we have the experience from the previous bound-book campaign; so we should be better prepared for this one.

#### Offer

The special offer will include any bound books from The Harp of God to and including Enemies. Three of these bound books will be offered on a contribution of 25c. Also combinations of 6 on contribution of 70c, 9 on $1.65 contribution, and 12 on contribution of $1.40, can be placed. In the foreign languages all bound books except the latest one can be offered, at three on contribution of 40c. During June a special effort should be made to place foreign books. All companies should be prepared and equipped with a sufficient number of books that each publisher can be supplied with at least three combinations of three different books in the English language, making nine books per publisher, and as many different foreign books as are available and can be used in your territory. Companies and pioneers should check their stock to see that they have their supplies on hand in readiness. Jehovah's people have taken the offensive in this warfare and there is no shaking down from one campaign to another. We fight shoulder to shoulder, laying down a constant barrage of truth against demonism.

Companies with Unbalanced Stock
If your company is not equipped with a sufficient supply of all bound books needed and you know of a near-by company that has an over-supply of the books you need, then get to touch with them and, if possible, make an even exchange of stock without using a Transfer of Stock blank. If an even exchange cannot be arranged, then use the regular Transfer of Stock blank. This blank should be properly filled out and signed by both companies and sent promptly to the Brooklyn office. In this the zone servants should assist in the companies at every visit and check carefully over their stock, arranging proper adjustments of literature and using the Transfer of Stock blank in accordance with instructions when necessary. If there is no company near by from whom

you can obtain the needed amount of stock, then send in an order immediately for what you will need. Remember that last year many companies were out of stock long before the month was over. Pioneers also may exchange stock and arrange transfers when necessary.

In ordering literature companies and pioneers may order any literature they desire, but some of our stock will run out. Therefore we will substitute other literature, unless you tell us not to do so. The books Light 1 and 2 and Vindication 1, 2 and 3 should always be placed in sets. If you are short of any one of these books to complete your sets, order what you need to balance them. In various depots throughout the country we have an overstock on certain literature, and would appreciate your ordering this in preference to other publications. On the West Coast, at the Seattle depot we have a good supply of Enemies, Riches, and Jehovah. Other books can be had, but we do not have large quantities there. Extra stock at other places is as follows:

At Los Angeles: Enemies, Riches, Jehovah, Preparation, Preservation.
At St. Louis: Enemies, Riches, Preparation, Prophecy, Government.
At Brooklyn: Enemies, Riches, Jehovah, Preparation, Preservation, Prophecy, Government, Creation. We have the largest stock on the books mentioned, but from Brooklyn we can supply others if requested.

Publishers should work their individual territories during this campaign, and also study groups should cover their territory regularly with this special offer, using the doorstep setup. Those who have magazine routes established should show the people on their routes this special bound-book offer, using the advertisement in the magazine to help them place combinations. Place sets with your friends, neighbors and relatives. All good-will names in your back-call files should be given an opportunity to receive a set, and the back-call servant should see that this is pushed. With every person with whom a set is placed, arrange to call back and thus encourage them to also read their books. Last year, according to instructions, the catalog was used to good advantage, and this has aided in placements. Be sure to have a good supply of bookmarks also.

#### Foreign Literature

Company servants should see that all foreign sections are thoroughly covered. This work can be done by all publishers using the testimony booklet. Wherever possible the foreign-speaking brethren should devote all their time to work amongst the foreigners in their company territory. There is too much foreign literature in storage. Order your supply of foreign books for the June work, and then let's get these into the hands of the people, where they will do some good.

#### Credit to Companies for Combinations Placed

A rebate of 25c will be given to each company for each set of three bound books placed. This should be reported properly on the June report card sent to the Society by the company, by showing on the bottom of the report card the number of sets of three books placed. The company will be given credit on their account for the number of sets placed. In no

other way will this be done. Be sure that your report card is made out properly, so as to avoid unnecessary work or errors. Pioneers will not be given a credit, as they already receive the literature at a very low rate.

Individual publishers will get their rebate from the stockkeeper or treasurer by showing on their daily report slips the number of sets placed of three bound books. These report slips should be turned in to the stockkeeper or treasurer, who will give the proper rebate to the publisher. The treasurer will keep an accurate record of the rebates given the publishers, and this should correspond with the company total at the end of the month. Either the stockkeeper or the treasurer should initial these slips in order to show that a rebate was given, and then turn them over to the secretary, who will keep them on file as long after the end of the month as they feel is necessary.

Prepare now for a lightning attack in June!

## "Willingly Offered Themselves" in May

### 70,000 Publishers Our Quota

The great central theme of the Victory Song now opened up in The Watchtower to thrill the Lord's people, is—the willing volunteers; those 10,000 who joyfully joined Barak and Deborah.

Boiled down to the Kingdom terminology of our day this means those who joyfully offer themselves to the Lord through his organization for any service the Lord requires done at the present time. The fact that the Lord made Deborah to say, "My heart is toward the governors of Israel, that offered themselves willingly among the people," indicates that it is only those who do this that can be pleasing to the Lord and his organization. There is not one word of commendation for the other approximately three million nine hundred and ninety thousand. Only those who wholeheartedly engage in the Lord's service can have the Lord's approval and blessing.

There are associated with the various company organizations well over 70,000 different individuals who have participated in the field service to some degree at least. As Jonadah's heart was with Jehn, so must the heart of the approved ones in our day be with the antityphal Deborah. There is no other way of obtaining deliverance and life. Each one of these seventy thousand and more brethren should be actively engaged in the field service, and this should give us a peak of at least 70,000 publishers during May.

During May we have another month in which to attain our quota for the Watchtower Campaign. Those who have not yet engaged in that branch of the work should take advantage of May to do so. The weather during the month of May is, as a rule, ideal for the witness work; it is neither too cold nor too hot, but splendid to work in city or country. Daylight saving is in effect throughout the country, permitting many to engage in the service during the evening. Evening service should be utilized to the very limit this summer. We have a very important message that must be delivered to the people in a short time. This message is contained in the two new booklets, Theocracy and God and the State. These should be in every home possible. To accomplish this each company organization should plan for evening work immediately. The following procedure is outlined:

#### Evening Witnessing

Arrange one or two evenings for group witnessing from study meeting places or from your headquarters, or from both headquarters and study meeting places, depending upon the convenience and effectiveness of the local arrangement. These groups should cover a given territory with Theocracy, God and the State and another booklet, on a 10c contribution. The people should be urged to read God and the State and Theocracy as soon as possible, emphasizing the importance of the message.

Take the name and address of those obtaining this combination. The following week arrangements can be made for back-call follow-up work on these people. Present the regular Watchtower combination with the recorded message at that time, and follow it through with each name to a regular back-call and model study if possible. This arrangement will afford a splendid opportunity for those who are timid and have not engaged in the service to any great extent in the past to get started in the witness work and also to get started in making back-calls.

The groups that cover the territory with the booklets can make the back-calls; or other back-call groups may be arranged while the initial group goes through each week and places booklet combinations, turning the names over to the back-call group, who will follow up the next week. Much will depend upon the available brethren in arranging for this work. Try to get everyone engaged in it, particularly the people of good-will and newly interested who are associating with the company.

Let our slogan for the month be—ENERGY ASSOCIATED A PUBLISHER, and a peak of 70,000 publishers for the month. Let our Victory Song be a real VICTORY SONG in this day of judgment. All can then say that they have 'willingly offered themselves'.
