# INFORMANT

DECEMBER, 1936

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## All Ready for "His Victory"?

Special Period Begins December 5

Cold Weather Does Not Stop Publishers

### Riches and Three Booklets

Jehovah gives to those who love him a vision of his purpose to completely destroy all who defame his name, and to gain for himself complete victory and vindication at the battle of the great day of God Almighty. With the King, Christ Jesus, and sharing in the victory, are those people upon whom God has put his name. They are now engaged in the preliminary warfare in their work of proclaiming Jehovah's praises and his kingdom. In this war now on their victory is assured. In the battle of Armageddon victory is assured. Those sharing in His victory are the courageous ones, who by faith see it now.

Courage means to have implicit confidence and trust in the supreme power that backs you up. This is shown by willing and joyful obedience to the instructions of the organization of the Higher Towers, on whom you confidently rely. There will be no slacking of the hand by the courageous ones. Using the equipment the Lord has provided these now enter a period of special effort in which, by their efforts in bearing testimony, they proclaim the sure victory of the Lord: "His Victory" testimony period, December 5-13, inclusive.

As has been announced, the book _Riches_ will be stressed during this period, the special combination of literature being _Riches, Choosing, Universal War Near_, and _Loyalty_ or other self-covered booklet, on a contribution of 35c. There is much work to be done with _Riches_. It has been available for but a few months, and while the companies have been using it in their territory during the past summer with good results, it seems they are, as it were, just getting started. "His Victory" period offers another opportunity to concentrate on this publication. The _Watchtower_ states:

"The forward movement throughout the land is progressing. The Lord has caused to be published a book entitled _Riches_, setting forth the plain truths of and concerning his kingdom and fully exposing the enemy, and particularly the modern Philistines. That book is now ready in many languages, and all over the land the faithful go forth as one man joyfully putting that message in the hands of the people, that they may see and know that Jehovah is the only true God and that his kingdom is the only hope of man."

### Who Will Take Part?

How can one share in the victory of the present warfare without making use of the most effective instrument against the modern Philistines to date: _Riches_? "Who will have a part in that witness work? Jehovah's remnant, being of the bride class, will engage in this work. The Jona-

dabs, or great multitude, will hear and also join in the witness work, saying to the people, 'Come,' EVERYONE WHO LOVES GOD will have a part in this witness work."

When _Riches_ cannot be taken, drop to the booklets. This is important, and every publisher is to make a practice of offering booklets, so that in addition to combinations including _Riches_, and _Riches_ books themselves, a large number of booklets will be left with the people. If the person witnessed to cannot take the combination of _Riches_, do not leave at once without calling his attention to the three-booklet combination, or, finally, one booklet. If you do this consistently you will place a good number of booklets. We are responsible for making good use of this literature which Jehovah has put in our hands. So remember the booklets at each call.

Even in the homes where _Riches_ has been placed previously, the work in connection therewith is not finished. Additional calls there will enable the publisher not only to stimulate interest in the book if it should be that some of the family have not read it yet, but to present additional literature to them, explaining the necessity of further Bible study. The phonograph used in these homes will aid in this, and in presenting the _Watchtower_ and _Golden Age_ magazines to them with the view of obtaining the subscriptions.

This means that in addition to _Riches_ and the booklets mentioned, you will, during "His Victory", as at other times, carry a good assortment of booklets, and one or more other bound books if you can do so; also the phonograph. This latter you can introduce and use whether the people have previously procured _Riches_ or not.

### Back Calls During "HIS VICTORY"

During "His Victory" testimony period, make back calls (preferably on those evenings when you are not doing the regular house-to-house witnessing) on interested persons you have found during the day, or on others whose names have been previously obtained. Such names and addresses should be furnished with the territory assignment to the publishers. At all back calls take the phonograph; and if you know you will be back in the territory the following day, you can often leave it overnight with some interested, reliable persons, for them to use until you pick it up the next day for your field service.

### Are You Using One?

### Back Calls Real Aid to Jonadabs

Those who have really taken advantage of the phonograph work find it the most thrilling and effective means of proclaiming the Kingdom message. Similar to all other features of the Lord's work, we must, of course, creep before we can walk or run. We cannot hope to be proficient and have the most effective method all at once. We must keep at it, become accustomed to introducing and using the phonograph so that we do it in a natural and an effective way; and when this is mastered it will be found that this is one of the best means of preaching the Kingdom gospel.

It is one of the best, first, because the phonograph is a provision made by the Lord to be used at this time to the glory of His name, and, secondly, because it is a practical and reasonable method of presenting the Kingdom message. It enables one to make a clear and concise presentation of the truth without interruption, which is something that can rarely be done by the witnesses if they try to do it themselves. No one should become discouraged because the use of the phonograph is a little awkward at first. The same is true of the witness work in general. When we first started out we were awkward and nervous and found it difficult. ### Care of Phonographs

Quite a few of the brethren have lost the set screw which holds the needle in the reproducer of their phonograph. In order to prevent this, so that we shall not have to replace them, we suggest that before you close up your phonograph after using it you turn the needle screw of the reproducer all the way in. Don't turn it too tight, but just tight enough to know it can't be turned any more. If you do this the screw will not be lost. Do not leave needles in the phonograph reproducer head when not in use.
