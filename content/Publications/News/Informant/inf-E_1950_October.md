"Preach the word."—2 Tim. 4:2.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## "It Is Nearer than They Think"

Testimony Period
One-Month Awake! Campaign Featured

### Individual Quota Set

¹ The vast majority of earth's people today either slumber under the stupefying sedative of religious, political and commercial propaganda, or blindly grope amid a woe, bedarkened world, insensible to imminent world destruction. They refuse to believe the words of Jehovah's prophet that 'it is nearer than they think'. (Isa. 13:6, Young) In contrast with these, however, thousands of honest-hearted, God-fearing people have awakened to the realization that Jehovah's wrath is certain to shortly be expressed against this old, satanic world. Believing it, they rejoice, for this world's destruction is in itself a promise of a new world's construction through Jehovah's Theocratic kingdom. Knowing that many thousands more must awake to this fact, the question presents itself. How can we help them and arouse their hopes in God's new world?

² The answer is: Acquaint them with God's Word of truth! They must learn the facts about this evil world, know the truth concerning religion, politics and commerce, understand world happenings in the light of Bible prophecy. The Awake! magazine is one of the chief exponents of truth and effectively meets the demand for reliable information. Recognizing this, we will therefore present a one-year subscription for Awake! for a \$1.00 contribution during the month of October. No premium will be offered.

### Demonstrations Aid in Meeting Quota

³ An individual quota of one subscription per publisher and four subscriptions for each pioneer has been set. The company quota will be equivalent to the quota of publishers for the 1951 service year. Each one will work diligently, striving to meet and surpass his individual quota and make this the best Awake! campaign to date.

⁴ To aid all publishers in effectively presenting the Awake! magazine at the doors, practical demonstrations should be arranged for the service meeting program. Bring out the fine talking points on this outstanding magazine and illustrate how they can be used in obtaining subscriptions. This should be done throughout the month so that the publishers can remain Awake! conscious. Experiences and discussions can also be featured to show how subscriptions were obtained, how objections were overcome and how the campaign has progressed locally.

### "Awake!" Is of Interest to All

⁵ Awake! is unique. Each issue contains interesting and valuable information for all. It appeals to everyone: businessman, youth, aged,

nature-lover, Bible student and members of the family in general. By becoming familiar with Awake! you will be prepared to meet on common ground all whom you contact and to show them the merits of subscribing for such a magazine. Awake! is truly different and stands in contrast to worldly periodicals, newspapers and magazines. It is uncensored and without commercial advertising. It is factual and truthful. Awake! fearlessly points out the fallacies of religion, the corruption of politics, the enslaving greed of commerce as well as other existent evils. However, it does not merely uncover error, but by adhering to truth and righteous principles it arouses men's hope for better things and establishes a keen desire for Jehovah's new world.

⁶ To make an effective presentation of the Awake! magazine, however, you must be convinced that it is what the people really need. Would you be without the Awake! magazine? Certainly not! And it is as important to all lovers of truth and righteousness as it is to you. Impressed by your conviction and sincerity, post-will persons will more readily believe that what you have to offer is vital. Awake! will build up in them further confidence in God's visible organization, the channel that provides the information in Awake! and will, in many instances, open the way for a subscription for The Watchtower later on. Therefore, use Awake! in all features of Kingdom activity, at the doors, on back-calls, in the store-to-store and house-to-house magazine work, and in the streets. At all times stress the full year's subscription before dropping to an offer for six months, on a 50c contribution.

### Follow Up Your Subscriptions

⁷ Whenever a subscription for Awake! is obtained, be sure to call back. Not only are you interested in getting the magazines into the hands of the people but you want them to come to a full knowledge of the truth. Do not let the spark you have kindled die. By returning with the latest issue a few weeks after placing the subscription you will have the opportunity to further kindle that spark until by persistent and consistent effort it too will become a flaming firebrand reflecting God's truth. Display genuine love, proper courtesy, dignity and Theocratic tact when helping these "sheep".

# Work Business Territory

## Winter Months Oportune Time

¹ Is your company working its business districts? Do you work the stores and places of business in your individual territory, or have you avoided these? Does not "all the inhabited earth" in which we are commissioned to preach the good news include the business districts? Certainly, these sections of our territory are not to be overlooked because to some they appear formidable and hard to reach with the Kingdom message. When you meet a businessman at his home while in the service, you do not hesitate to present the truth to him. Hence, view the matter of working your business territory just as positively. Furthermore, the Awake! subscription offer for October and the new book, "This Means Everlasting Life" the literature offer to be featured during November and December, are excellent to present to business people. For they too are interested in world happenings and in preserving and gaining life for themselves and their families.

### How and When

² When working business, as in other territory, we "become all things to people of all kinds". Our conduct must be bold and business-like, yet remain tactful, pleasant and sincere. Try to meet each one on his own level, never feel or an inferior. Be prepared! Know what you are going to say and in your presentation speak directly and to the point. Wear clean clothes that are tidy, have shoes polished and otherwise present a groomed appearance. This will add to your confidence and poise, which will have a good effect on those to whom you present the message. A brief case or neat envelope is preferable for carrying the literature.

⁴ Usually the best procedure in large business houses, offices, stores, etc., is to ask for the person in charge, by his name if you know it, or by his position, such as superintendent, manager, doctor or whatever he may be. If met by a secretary or receptionist wanting to know who you are and the nature of your call, you can give your name and state that your business is personal. After witnessing to the person in charge, he may grant permission to speak to some of the employees that are not too busy. If the person desired is not in, perhaps an appointment for a future call can be made, or you can witness to whomsoever you are able to contact. In event some places are very busy skip them, keep a record of them and return at a more convenient time. While the best time to work business territory varies with the type of business and the locality, it has been found that the early morning hours Monday through Friday are the most favorable.

⁵ Our purpose in working business territory is the same as in other features of Kingdom activity, namely, find and then "feed the sheep". Necessarily this obligates us to make back-calls on business people and conduct home Bible studies with them. So, to the extent that you are able, obtain the names and home addresses of all showing interest and especially those who accept the literature. Where this is not possible, many times these persons can be reached further by telephone. Thereby one can find out what progress they have made in reading the book, extend an invitation to attend meetings and make arrangements for future back-calls or studies. Such concern toward this group of earth's inhabitants will undoubtedly result in a heartening response from those who are sheeplike.

### Servants, Publishers, Pioneers All Take Part

⁶ Small stores, offices and other places of business included in the individual territories should be worked with the rest of the territory. However, in towns and cities where there are large, concentrated business sections, the territory servant, if he has not already done so, will make many of such sections to be worked as business territory. The method for handling this is the same as that of the regular house-house territory. (See paragraphs 95 and 96 of Counsel on Theocratic Organization for Jehovah's witnesses.)

⁷ Demonstrations showing how to approach the manager of a store, a doctor, lawyer or other businessman can be featured on the service meeting program. This will aid the brethren and stimulate them for this work. Business territory is ideal to work during the winter, for in this work the publisher is indoors most of the time.

⁸ Appreciating these facts, the scope of our commission, the need these persons have for the truth, and the fine publications we have to offer them, who can hold back any longer from having a full share in this work? Therefore, servants, make the necessary plans and announce these to the company. Then publishers and pioneers can cooperate in working the business territory. With all getting behind this effort and giving this territory the attention it needs, a grand witness will be given and many more will take in life-giving knowledge, all to the vindication of Jehovah's name and Word.
