## "Be Transformed by Making Your Mind Over"

### New Service Year Begins

#### Organize for Better and More Service

¹ It is delightful to experience the change in thinking from the ways of this old world to those of the new system of things. New world thoughts are healthful and upbuilding. Adopting such thinking and actively putting it into daily practice will result in gaining God's approval and eventually everlasting life. So put into practice new world thinking by aiding others to 'quit fashioning their minds after this system of things' and helping them to become transformed by 'making their minds over'—Rom. 12:2, NW.

² The first step to take in making one's mind over is to learn Jehovah's "pure language". And what better aids for learning the "pure language" could a person have than the New World Translation and the book What Has Religion Done for Mankind? This superb Bible and the study help will be offered during September on a \$2 contribution.

³ With the work in unassigned territory completed (except where some can still engage in it and the territory is still open), all will be working once again in their regular company territory. Use the same good organization in caring for your home territory that was employed in unassigned territory. Since this month opens up a new service year, it is essential to have proper organization of all our service activities to get off to a good start. Open up public meetings in home territory right after the district assemblies. On Sunday, set as your goal two

hours of door-to-door work and then follow this with an hour of making back-calls. Plan your day's service, and start early. Keep a record of names and addresses of persons with whom you leave literature and call back with the objective of starting home Bible studies.

⁴ There is indeed a definite place for organization in the activities of servants in the companies. Results in our new service year will be very gratifying if the servants take the lead, and all publishers work in harmony with company arrangements. Therefore, servants, organize local activity and avoid such negative thinking as, 'These suggestions just won't work in our company.' Think positively and progressively to get the assigned work done. Bear in mind that overseers have a vital part in helping the brothers 'make their minds over' for better thinking and greater service.

⁵ Through the company book study most of us have had the opportunity to study What Has Religion Done for Mankind? Being familiar with its contents we are in position not only to effectively present it but also to start studies in it. While material in this book may not be as familiar to us as that of other books, it does contain information Jehovah has prepared for those who love him. So use it effectively. Talks based on one or two chapters of the book will be well received by newly interested persons and publishers alike. The talks, approximately 45 minutes in length, can be given at company book-study centers, one such talk being given each month

with the regular study being carried on during the other weeks. The company servant and school servant can plan and arrange how the material is to be systematically covered in this manner.

⁶ September, then, provides everyone with opportunities to show to all men that we have made our minds over by our unselfish efforts for new world interests. Aid the mild-tempered ones to also be transformed by making their minds over and you will experience real blessings from Jehovah.

## Around the World with Jehovah's Witnesses

### Pioneer and Missionary Blessings

In reply to Peter's question, 'Look we have left all things and followed you; what actually will there be for us?' Jesus gave the assurance that all who followed such a course would be amply rewarded and that even in this present life they would receive a hundredfold. That Jehovah God in our day makes good this promise of his Son to all who engage in the full-time service is very apparent as we read the 1952 'Yearbook'.

During our subscription campaigns company publishers do not average one subscription in two months; but a pioneer in Bolivia in a two-month 'Awake' subscription campaign obtained 210 subscriptions! And whereas company publishers average but a half study each, this pioneer had so many studies that he could not take care of them all but had to turn some of them over to others who did not have any. And what feature of the service is more enjoyable than conducting a home Bible study? (91)

A missionary in Ethiopia reported that 'it is a strange and wonderful experience to have . . . people coming to the missionary home every day requesting that someone study with them'. Some days as many as 20 come, walking as much as two hours to get there and many want to study two or three hours at a time. (66)

The Gilead district servants in Nigeria travel by plane, car, truck, canoe, bicycle and often, like Jesus, by foot. Reports one 'On Saturday morning about 50 men, women and children followed me around to record with their own eyes the unbelievable sight of a white man preaching the gospel in their homes through an interpreter.' For one meeting 1,000 came, although advertised only by the advertising servant going around the streets at five o'clock in the morning announcing the lecture by means of a trumpet! (185)

### Being Faithful in That Which Is Least

Not all can serve as pioneers, special pioneers, missionaries or at some Bethel home. And some may not even have much time for the regular features of field service. But the least we can do is to be faithful in using the opportunities that come our way to give the witness.—Luke 16:10.

Two energetic publishers in the Sudan, Africa, make many opportunities for themselves to witness at their place of business simply by discussing the truth before they transact their business. As a result a company has been formed and some former Moslems are now conducting Bible studies with other Moslems. (130)

Some of our brothers in Nyasaland who are fishermen likewise put the preaching of the good news ahead of selling their fish. Customers are spreading the news of how they got their fish and what was said on the lake shore by the witnesses. (196)

Not only at places of business but also at home one can show faithfulness in that which is least. A sister in Ecuador makes good use of the patio of the apartment house where she lives to give the witness. The women folk gather there to knit, sew, weave and talk, and so she joins them with a supply of socks needing mending, booklets and a Bible. By being alert and tactful she has been able to start five home Bible studies, with the prospect of two or three more. (127)

Our own circumstances may not be exactly like any of the foregoing, but if we are desirous of being faithful in that which is least we will find opportunities to witness at our home or place of business.

## Presenting THE GOOD NEWS

### To Those Who Obtained September's Offer

'I was in the area so I thought that I would stop by to see if you had a chance to read any of the Bible and the book that I left with you last week.' Many a back-call is introduced with a remark similar to this and, on being advised in many instances that the publication left has not been read, the publisher sometimes finds it difficult to start a constructive discussion about the publications. Are there more effective ways to start a back-call? Yes, there are! The following method has proved to be successful:

After a few general remarks such as 'Hello', 'Good evening,' etc.: 'I'm very glad to find you at home. Mr. Smith. The interest that you showed in obtaining the _New World Translation_ and the book _What Has Religion Done for Mankind?_ prompted me to stop by this evening. When leaving them with you I did not have an opportunity to point out the numerous advantages they provide for personal Bible study. So, if you would get them now, it would be a pleasure to show you a number of things that will help you a great deal in using them.

'You will undoubtedly agree that worshiping God is important. But more than just any worship is required; for, opening your _New World Translation_ to James 1:26, 27, it says: (read). Now note the 'c' following the word 'worship' in the 27th verse. The 'c' refers you to the footnote and there is given the Latin word _religio_ from which comes our present-day term religion. For more detailed information as to what the word religion means we can turn to the book _What Has Religion Done for Mankind?_ and

refer to the subject index. Under the subject 'Religion' the section discussing the meaning is listed, pages 8-10. Turning to page 8, note the question on the bottom of the page: 'From what comes the word religion, and how has it been defined?' Would you like to read that paragraph?' After reading: 'Note how that paragraph helps us to understand where the term religion originated and also to know the different meanings attached to it. Then the next paragraph brings to our attention how it is used in the Bible. The question on paragraph 4 is 'How do the Christians Paul and James apply the word religion?' (Comment, after reading, that Paul shows it can be properly used to describe false religion as well as the true religion, as at James 1:27, A.W.) That brings us to the text that we first discussed, doesn't it?

'Then, too, Bible texts on which you desire information can be found in this book by simply turning to the scripture index. Let's look up James 1:26, 27 and see where it refers us. Yes, page 9, the paragraph we just discussed. So you see, using your Bible with its many fine aids to understanding is important. The book serves its purpose by providing explanation of the Bible texts and may be referred to for particular quick reference by using the subject index or scripture index. The questions at the bottom of the page help us to get the main points.'

If time and circumstances permit, continue your discussion a short while longer by going to the 5th paragraph of the book and considering it. If not, then encourage householder to continue reading from that point and try to make arrangements to call by again next week to discuss additional points.

# COMPANY ORGANIZATION

# The Service Meeting

Lively, enthusiastic service meetings are a vital necessity to a healthy, active company. They help the Lord's people to learn the most practical and effective ways of carrying out the commission to "make disciples of people of all the nations". The company servant is responsible for planning successful service meetings.

Upon receipt of the Informant he should outline the service meetings for the month. All Informant articles should be considered during the month. Whenever possible the article dealing with work for the current month on the front page should be considered at the first service meeting in the month. The article appearing on the back of the Informant considering activity of the following month is to be considered at the last service meeting of the current month. Other articles may be worked into the program in accord with the local company's arrangements. The company servant should look for and encourage other servants to offer suggestions in planning meetings, but the company servant makes the final choice.

A Scriptural theme or a particular objective should be determined for each meeting. The first meeting in the month could have the monthly text as a theme and subsequent meetings other appropriate themes. Often the daily text can be woven in as a theme for the meeting. After the Informant material is apportioned, the company servant, aware of the local problems or needs, should schedule portions on the program that will offer helpful suggestions to remedy the weaknesses.

There are a variety of mediums of

education which can be used to present service-meeting material. After a warm welcome to the strangers and consideration of the daily text, the program one week might start with some form of audience participation, such as by the question-and-answer method or a panel of experts (servants) with questions from the audience. This will serve to quicken the minds of the audience. This can be followed with a demonstration, perhaps a pantomime, a round-table discussion, an interview, a quiz, a forum, a dialogue or a talk. Large companies can assign demonstrations to a group attending an area book study. A well-rounded conclusion will stir the brothers to action in the field service. Participants must watch timing closely. Appropriate field service announcements will be made at the end of the service meeting and the program for next week announced so that the publishers can prepare.

The company servant should continually strive for variety in planning the service meeting program, making it lively and interesting, keeping in mind the spiritual and field service needs of the congregation. Brothers who have dedicated their lives to Jehovah and have the ability may be used in developing such educational meetings. Service meetings that are instructive will be had if everyone will co-operate and assist by preparing diligently for these meetings. The service meeting is a provision from the Lord to aid us in carrying out our divine commission. Support your service meeting by regularly attending, participating and doing your part by accepting assignments given to you by the company servant and preparing them well.
