OCTOBER, 1943

"More than conquerors through him that loved us."—Rom. 8:37

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

# You Are Free to Preach Freedom

## Thereby Defeat Persecution

### First Distribution of "The Truth Shall Make You Free"

With the words "For freedom did Christ set us free" still ringing in our ears and the educating of "men of good-will" in our minds, every publisher of The Theocracy will be ready to push off to a good start in the October campaign, "DEFEAT OF PRESCRIPTION" Testimony Period.

Defeat of persecution does not mean that persecution will stop, but signifies that the Lord's people will keep witnessing in the face of all opposition and that persecution against them will accomplish nothing and will be defeated in its purpose. How appropriate then is the name of this campaign, in view of the many victories the Lord has given his organization during the past year! For example, the many court decisions which were handed down by the courts recognizing the right of Jehovah's witnesses to worship Jehovah. Our "Free Nation's" Theocratic Assembly world-wide was also a great victory defeating persecution. Not only in America is persecution receiving a setback, but all over the earth, such as the lifting of the ban, and the stepped-up activities now, in Australia, and the faithfulness of the Lord's people under restraint in demonized Nazi lands resulting in a great witness there.

Expressing our appreciation for these victories in Kingdom service should make October our biggest month of field activity to date. During this campaign the new book "The Truth Shall Make You Free" and the booklet Freedom in the New World will have their first distribution to the public. The combination will be offered on the regular contribution of 25c. Consignments for the companies and special pioneers are being sent out as rapidly as possible, and to accomplish this the factory is now running an extra shift, working in the evenings. If your consignment does not reach you by the time of the campaign, then use The New World (or any other book) and the latest booklet you have as your offer. We expect to get your consignment to you on time, and, by the Lord's grace, we shall.

### Be Sure to Follow Up

You may not receive in your consignment as many new books as you could place; therefore every book that you place should be followed up with back-calls, having in mind the starting of a book study. We are in a major educational campaign. Since book studies are where the

Lord's "other sheep" are assembled and taught the truth, it makes them of utmost importance. By now you should have read and studied your new book, with the aid of the question booklet, becoming thoroughly familiar with its contents. So you know that this book will be a real help in our studies, as it makes the truth so easily understood.

Be diligent in getting into your individual territory and calling on all the people living there. Use your S-15 House to House Record and make sure everyone has the opportunity to have and study "The Truth Shall Make You Free". It is your obligation to call on these people, "hunting" and "fishing" for the Lord's "other sheep" who are seeking true freedom.

If you do your part in defeating persecution by exercising your freedom to the glorifying of Jehovah, then you may rest assured that Jehovah will bless your efforts as stated in James 1:25: "But whoso looketh into the perfect law of liberty, and continueth therein, be being not a forgetful hearer, but a doer of the work, this man shall be blessed in his doing." (Margia)

The number of books and booklets sent to companies is determined from their past activity. When this supply is used up you may re-order after November 1 what you will need. In case your supply does not reach you by October 1, don't sit down and wait for it, but continue to work with The New World and Fighting for Liberty on the Home Front.

Companies that were advised they were not to receive a consignment may order at once, returning the card they received authorizing them to order now.

Where there is a group of special pioneers working in the same assignment, the consignments will all be shipped to one of the pioneers, who will divide up the shipment on the above basis.

### A Lot for Us to Work With

As freedom-lovers gathered at the 100 assembly points of the "Free Nation's" Theocratic Assembly, they awaited with keen anticipation whatever in the way of releases the Lord's channel would have to give. To put it mildly, they were not disappointed! Again, Jehovah gave a thundering reply to those who had accused His servants of following a man, and again He, the God of the free, gave proof to His people that their pathway would shine brighter unto the perfect day.

After the stirring keynote speech by Brother Knorr on "Standing Fast for Freedom", which burned into the minds of all present the great truth that "FOR FREEDOM DID CHRIST SET US FREE", the Society's vice-president and legal counsel introduced the first release, the booklet Freedom of Worship, in an address on that subject.

This booklet is a "first aid" manual for all ministers of Jehovah God, so that they may stand fast for freedom. Thoroughly understand it, so that you will know not only what to say and do, but also when and how to apply that knowledge. This booklet is not to be carried with you into the field.

However, Freedom of Worship was only an appetizer, or the "introduction"; the main dish, the "body" of the convention releases, came on Saturday afternoon at the end of the lecture "Free Education for Men of Good-will", and after the Declaration, when the book "The Truth Shall Make You Free" was released by the president of the Society. # The "Free Nation" in Assembly

## Educational Campaign Brought to the Fore

### Rich Blessings Ours

"The arrow of Jehovah's deliverance." Thus the theme of the keynote speech of the "Free Nation's" Theocratic Assembly was developed and echoed throughout the entire earth as assurance of victory and freedom to the Lord's people. Gathered together in 100 cities in the United States and many others in Mexico, South America, Cuba, Hawaii, Britain, Africa, Sweden, Australia, New Zealand, and many other localities, thousands of the citizens of the Free Nation and their associates from every nation, kindred and tongue were rejoicing as these words of triumph penetrated even the clamor of global war with a message of hope.

### The First Release

From this powerful keynote opening throughout the three climactic days of assembly the point of deliverance to FREEDOM for the Lord's people was stressed. All then returned to the field, which they had left for a short season, fully determined to STAND FAST FOR THEIR FREEDOM, and only again be entangled with the yoke of bondage. "FOR FREEDOM DID CHRIST NOT US FREE." Resolved now are Jehovah's witnesses to continuously smite the earthly visible organization of the tan with the bundled arrows of Theocratic truth as long as any part of it remains. In invincible unity of understanding of truth, with various instruments and having been instructed in their use, the witnesses of Jehovah continue to call on the people, giving us the wicked oppressors of free men notice of their doom. Day after day they go—from house to house and city to city, meeting people at the doors, on the streets, in the market places, in places of business, and everywhere—confident that somehow, in some way, the message will continue on and on, reaching each and every captive of the present wicked organization. They must continue 'shooting the arrow' and 'smiling' until every last one of the 'other sheep' has been gathered and offered freely an education in the textbook of freedom, The Bible, from now right on through to the complete establishment of the New World.

This offensive movement needs equipped fighters, and part of such equipment was revealed in the booklet _Freedom of Worship_, which will require study and application on the part of the brethren. Thus equipped you may prove to all, friend or foe, that our freedom to preach as ministers of God soars high above the reach of earthly power.

### Free Education

Although stressed, this work was not started at the Assembly. The free education of the people in God's Word has been going on for years, and must continue, and will continue, by the Lord's grace. The talk on "Free Education for Men of Good-Will" brought to the fore that educational work with the Kingdom truth is the most essential instruction that can be given to them. This point should be burned into the heart and mind of everyone as he engages in the witness work.

Keep in mind always the points of the DECLARATION unanimously adopted at the Assembly. Briefly: they were to the effect that Jehovah God has made us free by his truth and through Christ; that our freedom will be used for no selfish purpose, but we must perform one commissioned duties by locating and educating "men of good-will" in the way to life; that our work is not subversive of present governments, but promotes full respect for law, order, and righteousness; that the textbook of freedom is the Bible; and that in the postwar era we will extend the publication of the Kingdom truth and increase the number of free Bible-study classes. "We are in favor of free education of all men of good-will" concerning The Theocracy.

The thrilling release of the new book followed immediately, with the Society's president saying, "To everyone at this Assembly from five years old upward who will accept it in the spirit of this Declaration one copy of this book will be given FREE." All of us received free copies under these conditions; therefore let us not forget the obligation which falls upon us, but let us go on with the free education of "men of good-will" in the "Textbook of Freedom". Our book "The Truth Shall Make You Free" will remind us daily to do this.

The final day of assembly stressed the freedoms and blessings promised in the New World of righteousness, which are so marvelous that the men of this "old world" dare not even promise such to the people in their so-called "new order".

### August Report

The August report is very encouraging. Some of the outstanding items only are mentioned here, because of lack of space. A new peak of full-time publishers is one of them; 6,846 reporting, being 85 over July, which was the previous peak. The average hours for company publishers is 18; which is the best we have had since June 1940. This shows real activity on the part of the publishers, as considerable time was taken up with activities in connection with the Assembly, which could not be reported as field service.

Its weakest point seems to be the average number of back-calls for the company publishers, which was only 2.8. If, in the 100 cities, the publishers' devoting considerable time to locating rooms, working in conjunction with the preparation of territory, cafeteria, etc., for the "Free Nation's" Theocratic Assembly interfered to some extent with the August back-call work, then the September report should show a marked improvement because of the new interest created through Assembly activities being given attention by them. Undoubtedly the same stimulus indicated in the increased number of hours and of publishers will show up here from now on.

## "Trust Your Proved Faithful Brethren"

### Make Preparations for November

Time moves by swiftly for the Lord's servants. With the many unforgettable experiences of the "Free Nation's" Theocratic Assembly fresh in our memories and the messages there delivered still ringing in our ears, our Calendar shows us the month of November draws near. We see that the text for the month is Philippians 2:29, with the precept, "Trust Your Proved Faithful Brethren." That is good admonition. But who are the "proved faithful brethren"? How can we identify them? Why is it so important to have this counsel now?

The Lord sets forth His will and the requirements for faithfulness at all times. This he does for the benefit of men. Those who desire to prove faithful take heed. They consecrate themselves to Jehovah God and seize upon His will (including organization instructions) and make it their own will. They diligently follow His commands, consistently and zealously engaging in the work of singing His praises and carrying out the free education of "men of good-will". They have agreed to serve Jehovah without end and they set out to do so. By their works of righteousness you can easily identify them.

Today all the faithful ones work together in unanimity under the direction of Christ Jesus. His instructions are sent out through the Theocratic organization which the Lord has brought forth in the earth, "the Society." It is composed of the "proved faithful brethren", having been tried by the Lord and gained his approval. Therefore you should have perfect confidence in the tried

and proved faithful Society and stick closely to it for your good. Instructions sent out through the Society are confidential to the brethren and are to be discussed among the consecrated. They are not for general public disclosure. They are provided to aid you and comfort you; they are private to you. Do not disclose to the enemy the course you will take, and put no trust in the organizations and men of this old world. Safeguard the interests of the Kingdom and cooperate with your proved faithful brethren, serving the Lord with them. Mature ones are appointed as servants in the company organizations. Trust them. Their counsel and aid will prove priceless in helping you to keep free from the old world and in improving your service in the new world. The November text states: "Receive him therefore in the Lord with all gladness; and hold such in reputation." (Phil. 2:29) Have due respect for your proved faithful brethren and associate with them. That is the wise course now, because the days are evil and the enemy is ever alert to interfere with the Lord's work. Discuss private Theocratic matters among the consecrated and co-operate in carrying out the instructions well. Thus will oneness prevail; and the Lord's rich blessing be with all.

Among the unconsecrated ones talk about the Kingdom message only, not confidential matters involving the work. During the month of November the new book "The Truth Shall Make You Free" and the booklet _Freedom in the New World_ will be the principal literature offered, on the contribution of 25c. Prepare now for a full month of activity during November with your proved faithful brethren.
