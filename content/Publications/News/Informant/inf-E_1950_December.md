# INFORMANT

DECEMBER, 1950

"Preach the word."—2 Tim. 4:2.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## "Preach the Word" Testimony Period

### Strive for Efficient Use of the New Book

#### Follow Up All Interest

—Start Studies

¹ The apostle Paul was nearing the end of his long, faithful ministry. His trials had been many and some very difficult, but in bonds of imprisonment he counseled the young man. Timothy, impressing upon his mind the most important work for him as one of Jehovah's witnesses. Was that to seek financial security? Gain worldly prestige? Or climb the social ladder of success? No! Paul's inspired words pointed to the highest vocation and the most important thing for one desiring everlasting life. He instructed, "Preach the word!"—2 Tim. 4:2, _Moff_.

² Timothy did preach, but Paul's words pointed forward especially to our day when the life of every living creature depends upon the choice of the right way based on God's Word. Urgent it is for us to "preach the word". And this we have been doing. Reflect: As we look back over the past year's activity, we see Jehovah's rich blessing upon the word preached. Increase after increase has resulted, circuit assemblies have accentuated our joy, and the climax was reached at the wonderful assembly in New York city. At the public meeting 123,707 in attendance attested to Jehovah's blessing upon the word preached. The world may call this preaching impractical if it wishes, but the results show the opposite. Gratefully we go forth in December, the last month under this yeartext.

#### Effective Preaching

³ December brings us to the second month of presenting "This Means Everlasting Life", on a contribution of 35c, and endeavoring to leave a copy of _Can You Live Forever in Happiness on Earth?_ in every home. Present the book enthusiastically and with conviction. Work for efficient presentation. Chapter 2, for example, presents excellent talking points. This age of science and advance in human wisdom tends to exalt man. He can peer millions of light-years into space, probe the secrets of atomic energy and erect great buildings. His theories lead him still farther until the layman looks to man and his greatness and becomes subject to such leaders to do their will. Great men, however, when seen from God's viewpoint are very little. They have made advances, yes, yet the Lord says man is as a grasshopper, the nations as dust in the balance! God alone is great and he is the Giver of life. Should we not humble ourselves and learn of his requirements for life?

⁴ What kind of life has God promised those who will meet his requirements? Chapter 27 points out that it is God's purpose to restore to this earth the paradise of Eden with all its blessings. That means everlasting life with peace, freedom and

happiness. What a prospect for righteous, God-fearing persons to-day! To learn of this righteous new world is of greatest importance.

⁵ Many other presentations may be used, but, in all cases, "preach the word" as commanded and then offer the book as an aid to study. The holiday season brings added opportunities for presentation. Since it has been impossible to fill all domestic and foreign orders, companies that do not have supplies of "This Means Everlasting Life" may use "Let God Be True".

#### "Preach the Word"

by Following Through

⁶ The brief witness and placing of the book is only the start. Hearing and learning are required, as John 6:45 points out. That means back-calls. Perhaps you make the back-call and they say they haven't read the book. What then? Words to this effect might be used to strike their interest: 'I realize that many things come up that prevent us from doing all we would like to do. However, you will agree that if you don't read the book it will be of little benefit to you. I desire to aid you to learn the precious contents of the book you have. It has brought much comfort and understanding to me and I'm sure it will bring this to you also. The chapter on the Author of Life is very interesting... etc. Or if they seem indifferent about the book, you might ask if they have any Bible questions they would like answered. If not, you ask one: 'Do you believe there will be people that will live on the earth forever in peace and happiness?' You might go to chapter 27 for your discussion. In this way you start them talking and the way is opened for you to preach the Word. Be friendly. Talk to the people as your neighbors, which they are.

#### Start Studies in

"This Means Everlasting Life"

⁷ If the people of good will want life they must take in knowledge of Jehovah and Christ Jesus. (John 17:3, _NW_) So, study is essential. Go into a discussion on some subject in the book on your back-call and then show where you got your information. They too can get such information by studying the book and you will be glad to come to their home and discuss the book subject by subject with them. This is what they need to understand the Bible and God's requirements for life. The idea that one must go to college, or a theological seminary, or learn Greek and Hebrew before he can understand the Bible is wrong. The Bible was written for the common man, and everyone can understand it through informal study. Remember, our goal is one study per publisher. Have you started yours? # Sharing in the Final Witness

## Prepare for 13th Annual Watchtower Campaign

1 During the last days of this old system of things, Jehovah has decreed that a final witness be given. The big question then before all mankind is, Who will share in it? The obvious answer resounds. His people! The ones he has given a clear picture of this present system's impending destruction, shown the way of escape and commissioned to proclaim 'this good news of the kingdom for a witness to all nations before the accomplished end'. (Matt. 24:14, NW) Before that accomplished end, a yet unnumbered multitude of 'other sheep' will gladly hear the good news of the kingdom and share in the final witness. Jehovah's present-day witnesses are happy to aid the 'other sheep' not yet gathered begin a life of praise to him.

2 Because of the importance of this final witness and the mammoth part _The Watchtower_ plays in it, the Society has scheduled the 13th annual _Watchtower_ campaign from the first of January through April. Four grand months to offer from door to door, on back-calls, Bible studies, street witnessing, yes, wherever and whenever the opportunity presents itself, a year's subscription to _The Watchtower_, for a \$1 contribution, with a premium of 3 booklets. The premium should include _Evolution versus The New World_, _Can You Live Forever in Happiness on Earth?_ and any other self-covered booklet. A remittance of 90c should accompany each such subscription. _Awake!_ may be offered to _Watchtower_ subscribers but without a premium.

3 The quota for this campaign has been set at 2 subscriptions for each company publisher and 16 for each

planner. All indications point to this 13th annual _Watchtower_ campaign to be the biggest yet, for not only are thousands more sharing in the final witness, but the many new appealing features of _The Watchtower_ will play an important part in obtaining many more new subscriptions.

# New Schedule for Theocratic Ministry School

## To Feature 'Pure Language' of the New World Translation

1 With what joy we received the _New World Translation of the Christian Greek Scriptures_ at Yankee Stadium last summer! How we thrilled to learn of a new translation whose sole purpose 'is to exalt the name of the living, true God and spread the fragrant knowledge on his kingdom by Christ Jesus'! A version which would commend itself to every honest searcher for the truth of the divine revelation because of its faithfulness, courage and correctness.

2 Four months have gone by since we received this new translation. How far have we progressed in our reading and study of it? Have we carefully considered the wealth of information appearing in the Foreword and the Appendix so as to fully appreciate the arguments there presented? To what extent are we becoming familiar with the word chains and the theocratic margins references? Yes, what progress are we making toward mastering the 'pure language' contained in this latest and choicest provision of Jehovah through his 'faithful and discreet slave'? That all may thus become fully familiar with the _New World Translation_ it will be the sole textbook in our theocratic ministry schools for the next two years.

### Outline of Study

3 Beginning with the week of November 24, 1950, the _New World Translation_ will be the basis of our instruction talks, student talks, and audience participation as well as the 'homework' of our ministry school.

4 _Instruction talks:_ The first instruction talk will give a general summary of the translation as a whole. The next five instruction talks will be based on the Foreword, to be followed by eighteen talks based on the Appendix. Thereafter instruction talks will discuss the more significant word chains.

5 _Student talks and audience participation:_ The first and second student talks will be based on the translation itself, each talk covering about three and a half pages. Rather than merely repeat the account or the arguments presented, the purpose of these talks will be to highlight the fine points of the _New World Translation_. After the school servant has given his counsel, the audience will be given one minute to comment on points that may have been overlooked. The third student speaker will use a subject taken from the word chains. Instruction talks will be fifteen minutes long, student talks more than six but less than eight minutes. There will be no oral or written reviews.

By careful individual study and regular attendance at the theocratic ministry school, we will be fully convinced of these things and will be able to intelligently and confidently put to silence all criticism leveled at the _New World Translation_ because of its radical departures from generally accepted versions.
