MARCH, 1941

"Salvation unto our God . . . and unto the Lamb."—Rev. 7: 10.

BROOKLYN, N.Y.

## "The Sign" Apr. Testimony Period

### Two New Booklets to Aid Publishers

### Memorial Comes During Final Month of Campaign

#### Should Have 100,000 New Subscriptions by End of Campaign

". . . we are now well advanced within the times and the seasons, but the 'sign' immediately preceding Armageddon is not yet discernible. Until that sign is apparent in every part of 'Christendom', and until every avenue is closed to the giving of testimony openly and boldly, the remnant and the 'other sheep' . . . must continue to sound out the message of the great Theocrat and his government under Christ Jesus the King. This must be done to the name of Jehovah God and that those who are of good-will might find the way of escape."

With those thoughts in mind, from _The Watchtower_ relative to 'The Sign', which name identifies the special Testimony Period during April, Jehovah's witnesses push ahead in the third month of the _Watchtower_ Campaign featuring a year's subscription for the _Watchtower_ magazine at the regular contribution of \$1.00 a year, with a free copy of the book _Religion_ and of Brother Rutherford's two new booklets _Theocracy_ and _God and the State_.

#### The Lord's Provisions for Publishers

Again the Lord has graciously provided his Theocratic publishers with added equipment, and instead of offering the people a year's subscription for _The Watchtower_, with a book and a booklet free, we can now offer _The Watchtower_, the book, and two brand-new booklets, on a contribution of \$1.00. The fact that there is no better message, and surely no better offer, to bring to the people should be emphasized to every person.

Having in mind the three-month quota of 100,000 new subscriptions, 1,000,000 bound books and 6,750,000 booklets for the United States, early reports indicate that we are behind in our quota for subscriptions, only 9,000 having been received at the time this went to press. Each company publisher and pioneer has his individual quota to work for, and should know how well he is doing. All of these individual quotas ultimately make up the quota for the United States; therefore each Theocratic publisher has his part in making this _Watchtower_ Campaign successful. Have you reached your quota? or will you? If not, carefully consider some of the following ways which may help you.

#### How to Get Subscriptions

The first thing necessary is to put in the time and put forth diligent effort with the special offer. The second thing is to talk _The Watchtower_ at every home, that is, the

special premium offer at $1.00. Publishers should not try to predetermine the circumstances of the person to whom they talk, but rather should keep in mind that they are out to place *Watchtower* subscriptions, above anything else, and that unless they tell the people about *The Watchtower* at $1.00 a year, with the free book _Religion_ and the two new booklets _Theocracy_ and _God and the State_, the person will not know that such a wonderful offer is available. We feel sure, as past experiences have shown, that the publishers who consistently talk this offer at every door and to everyone they meet will get more than their quota of subscriptions, books and booklets. Try it!

#### Other Points to Aid Publishers

After playing the record 'Instruction' to introduce _The Watchtower_, many publishers take the _Watchtower_ magazine and point out 'Its Mission' or what 'The Scriptures Clearly Teach', show the large-size type of the leading article, point out the secondary articles, and even read the opening sentences of the main article, or excerpts, which clearly place the matter before the people. Publishers not having a photograph or not able to carry one may present such outstanding points in connection with the testimony card.

#### Back-Calls and Studies

Is everyone attending your model study or book study a _Watchtower_ subscriber? Have you talked to your friends, relatives, neighbors, business associates, the grocer, the milkman and the butcher about the _Watchtower_ magazine with the special premium offer? You may be instrumental in starting them to read _The Watchtower_ or open the way for a back-call and ultimately place the \$1.00 combination with them. Since

it is much easier to place _Watchtower_ subscriptions on back-calls, thoroughly work your individual territory, keep a record of all who manifest interest, together with other leads you may obtain in the field, call back on such persons a few times stressing the need of reading _The Watchtower_, and finally take a year's subscription for the _Watchtower_ magazine, leaving free, _Religion_, _Theocracy_, and _God and the State_.

If a party contributes 25c for the book and booklets, you may suggest to him that you will apply the 25c toward a year's subscription for _The Watchtower_ and that you will call back each week until the \$1.00 contribution is received for the entire combination. Publishers having magazine routes should show their regular obtained the advantages in taking a year's subscription. Also make use of opportunities to place subscriptions in the magazine work by being prepared with the entire combination while you are street witnessing.

#### Three Booklets on 10c Contribution

To neither the \$1.00 combination nor the offer of the book and two booklets on a 25c contribution is taken, the publisher will offer three booklets on a 10c contribution, two of which will be _Theocracy_ and _God and the State_. Any other booklet may be used for the third. Either of the two new booklets may be left on a 5c contribution, or _God and the State_ on any contribution given.

Consignments of these two new booklets are being sent, and all companies should have theirs in time for a good start on April 1. Does your company have sufficient distributors' copies of _The Watchtower_ for all publishers? Is the company sending subscriptions promptly to the Brooklyn office? All details should be taken care of now for 'THE SIGN' Testimony Period.

## New Peak for April

### How Near to 70,000 Shall We Come?

The quota of publishers for this year, as brought out in the January _Intendant_, is 70,000. Let us see to fit the April small, as a good step toward that mark. By the Lord's grace this will be done. This means everyone on the job! Not only such advantages as better weather, easier traveling in rural territory, etc., should aid in attaining this new peak, but also the added incentive of taking the two new publications, _Theocracy_ and _God and the State_, for the first time into the field, including them in the special offer as instructed. During this campaign every publisher of _The Theocracy_ will be out doing his part, and the best habit to this end is to arrange for each week in advance what time can be set aside for Kingdom service. There is no time now to waste. semble to celebrate the Memorial; and, with renewed zeal and appreciation of the Lord's purposes, will go forth in the remainder of 'THE SIGN' Testimony Period and _Watchtower_ Campaign to surpass all previous records. Everyone who supports the Theocratic Government of Jehovah is anxiously looking for 'the sign'; and as we do let's continue to press on with the Kingdom message, giving the 'great multitude' every opportunity possible to learn about his government of peace, which is so clearly set forth in _The Watchtower_ and kindred publications. Concerning 'the sign' and its appearance, _The Watchtower_ says: 'There are still opportunities of service, and none of the faithful will neglect to avail themselves of such.' May the Lord give us a keen appreciation of our privileges and make us to faithfully do his will. # They Can't Say, "I Did Not Know" An Added Feature to "Watchtower" Campaign

## Influential Men to Be Called On

In addition to using the _Theocracy_ and _God and the State_ booklets in the regular house-to-house campaign beginning April 1, there is an additional special campaign in which these booklets are to be used, in carrying the Kingdom message to prominent officials and influential men.

1. Both booklets have been presented to the press of the country. This work has been taken care of by the Society; so it will not be necessary for the companies to do it. Every newspaper and magazine (not Catholic) has been presented with a copy of _Theocracy_ and of _God and the State_.

2. The _Theocracy_ booklet is to be presented to every Protestant clergyman in each company's assignment. A list of these individuals should be made up as soon as possible and several names and addresses turned over to each publisher who will see that a copy of the _Theocracy_ booklet is presented to them. There will be no occasion to present this to the Catholics, but the Protestant clergy should have a copy in order that they might see how grossly the Lord's people have been misrepresented. Pioneers, to the best of their ability, will look after this matter in their assignment.

3. _God and the State_. This booklet is to be presented to the school boards and members of the state legislature. To avoid confusion, the following suggestion is made for this:

Each company will see that the city and county officials and the school boards in their respective territory are presented with a copy of _God and the State_. A list of these individuals should be assembled and given to the publishers in the same way that the Protestant clergymen's names are distributed, and each publisher is to see that those whose names he receives are properly called upon.

The companies holding the territory in which the state capitals are located will see to it that the members of the state legislature are properly served, and the company in Washington, D.C., will be responsible for seeing that those of the national legislature are served with a copy of this booklet.

Work can start on this as soon as you receive your literature, and should be pretty well cleaned up before the end of March. If there are any state capitals that are not in a company assignment, the zone servant having supervision over the territory in that area is to see to it that some nearby company serves the legislature in that city. This work should be handled very thoroughly and systematically, endeavoring to reach each individual with their copy of the booklet. If, for any reason, the legislature should not be in session, those who are supposed to serve the various ones will try to get the home addresses of the representatives or senators, as the case may be, and see that a copy of the booklet is mailed to their homes.

# "The Lord Gave the Word" (Ps. 68 : 11)

## Company That Published It Should Reach New Peak

This completely negatives the claims that any man produces the message of truth. Hence we may go forth with the WATCHTOWER publications that contain the Theocratic message with the full confidence that it is Jehovah's message and is published at His command, "The Sovereign Lord giveth the word."—_Rotherham_.

"The word," or "speech," is a comprehensive form applied, not to only one specific message, but to the whole series of messages of truth which Jehovah gave and which he continues to give over since the lightnings have been flashing from the temple and upon the record of his Word. "The word," therefore, includes every revelation of truth down to and including the publications _Religion_, _Satisfied_, _Theocracy_, and _God and the State_, and whatsoever shall be revealed and published, by the Lord's grace, as long as the "remnant" is on earth. Hence the importance of getting subscriptions for _The Watchtower_, because through its columns Jehovah is continuing regularly to give "the word" twice a month.

The members of "the company" on earth are "great", but not in themselves. Their greatness is by reason of being a part of God's army, hence "strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might."—Psalm 68 : 11.

Jehovah has given the word, for no other reason than that his devoted people should in turn give it to others. That given "word" includes the _Watchtower_ magazine, _Religion_, and other publications, which comprise an especially fine offer for this _Watchtower_ campaign now on. Hence, with great appreciation of the gift and the responsibility the receiving of it entails, let us one and all get into this campaign and publish the word to others.

Jehovah foretold he would have a company to do the publishing. If you appreciate the rare privilege of having part in the publication in fulfillment of the prophecy you will no longer hold back, but will become a publisher at the earliest, during this current _Watchtower_ campaign. You will be anxious to do as much publishing as you can, 60 hours a month on the average, if possible. If you can devote your entire time thereto, so much the more favored you are of the Lord, and the opportunity is yours to become a full-time pioneer. In fulfillment of Psalm 68 : 11 we here in this yet democratic land should be attaining the year's peak of publishers, of at least 60,000 and soon our quota of 50,000, especially during April, and from there on go ahead to yet higher numbers of publishers. This should be an "silent" "blitzkrieg" against the ramparts of religion, for the vindication of Jehovah's name.

## The Memorial a Time of Joy

### How to Report

Announcement of the time for the annual celebration of the Memorial, the feast to the honor of Jehovah and to the sacrifice of the Lamb of God, Jesus Christ, appears in the February 1 _Watchtower_. This year the date is Friday, April 11, 1944, after six p.m. At that time all companies of the Lord's people will meet together for that purpose. All people of goodwill should be invited to attend and witness this feast, thus becoming better acquainted with Jehovah's provisions and his purposes.

Appropriate service announcements should be made by the individual conducting the Memorial, informing those present of the arrangements made for all, both assisted and Jonadabe, to share in the proclamation of Jehovah's name and Theocratic Government, the special offer during "Tax Says" Testimony Period (in April), and the time of the local meetings of the company, _Watchtower_ studies, service meetings, and book studies. All should be offered literature, testimony cards, and other equipment necessary to carry forward the work. Have these on hand.

Pioneers who attend Memorial with companies should make no notation on their report cards; but where isolated pioneers meet together, one of them should make such notation. Use the following method: Memorial attendance ... Number partaking ... If your company or a pioneer attends the Memorial service of another company, then state on the bottom of your report card "In attendance with ... company" (dilux in the name of the company).
