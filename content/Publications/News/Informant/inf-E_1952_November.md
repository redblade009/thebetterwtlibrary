## "Hold a Good Conscience"

### Everyone a Publisher During November

#### Assist Others to Gain a Good Conscience

¹ During the month of November publishers will endeavor to "hold a good conscience" by presenting three bound books on a contribution of \$1.50: _What Has Religion Done for Mankind?_ "Let God Be True" and "This Means Everlasting Life". To the majority of publishers this will mean simply transferring the offer they were making in their unassigned territory to their regular company assignment. To others who had no unassigned territory it will mean extending this very excellent presentation to a greater number of persons in their regular assignment.

² Regardless of whether you work in unassigned territory or regular territory you meet people, you talk to them about the Lord's kingdom, and you endeavor to place bound books with them. If you did that in unassigned territory, you can do it in your regular assignment. People, generally speaking, are the same. If you can show them that you have something they need, they will take it. Therefore, if you present the combination with the same vigor and conviction in your present assignment as you did in your unassigned territory, they will take it, and you will place books. Actually our biggest job is that of adjusting and preparing ourselves to do what the Lord directs and in the manner he directs.

³ In this connection the following comparison is interesting. Prior to the unassigned territory campaign we had a three-bound-book campaign in November, 1951. The average for book placements per company publisher was 1.3. In December, 1951, we offered one bound book, _What Has Religion Done for Mankind?_ Average books per publisher, 1.2. During the three-bound-book campaign in unassigned territory, we averaged in June 1.6, in July 1.5 and in August 1.4 books per company publisher. The slight decrease in placements from June to

August is undoubtedly due to rapid completion of unassigned territories by many companies and their return to regular company assignments. But the figures indicate that publishers placed many more books in unassigned territory, and we believe this was mainly because the publishers went to that territory expecting to place books. They diligently and enthusiastically presented books and hence actually placed them. The combined total of 924,811 books placed in the United States in the three months proves this.

⁴ Let us "hold a good conscience" during November and present the three bound books with the same

conviction, and see if we do not bring our placements up to the level of our unassigned territory results. Examine the book placements for June, July and August from your company records and ascertain your publisher average. Make it your goal to equal or exceed that figure right in home territory. Arrange demonstrations by publishers who were efficient in placing bound books. Encourage those who are having difficulty making bound-book placements. Let experienced publishers give personal assistance and generally work up enthusiasm for the presentation. Make your quota for each publisher at least two hours of house-to-house work every Sunday followed by back-calls on previous placements. Be sure each publisher has at least two combinations with which to work, and also have plenty of company supplies on hand for the campaign.

## Maturity Essential for Regular Service

### Do You Preach Every Month?

The mature Christian understands fully his commission from God. His richness of understanding keeps the will of the Lord clearly before him and he delights to do it. Thus Paul prayed that the Colossian brothers might "be filled with the accurate knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual discernment, in order to walk worthily of Jehovah to the end of fully pleasing him as you go on bearing fruit in every good work".—Col. 1:9, 10, _N.W._

Yes, "become full-grown in powers of understanding," for it is then that a Christian moves forward steadily, confidently and properly equipped to preach the good news of the Kingdom. This is why we received so much good counsel on growing to maturity at our recent district assemblies. That there is a great need at present for increased maturity is shown by an examination of the figures on regularity in the service. Of the total number of publishers in the United States during the past 6 months, only 56 per cent were regular publishers each

month, while 44 per cent were irregular. This means that only a little over half the total publishers engaged in the preaching work every month, while the remainder for some reason did not arrange their affairs to engage regularly in this blessed work of aiding others. How does your company compare?

If we are to reduce this number of irregular publishers considerably, it will require that each servant in the company and all mature publishers work diligently to help new and irregular publishers to participate in the service. This should be true, not for just one month, but every month. (See the September 1951 _Informant_ and the Society's letter of February 20, 1952, for suggestions.) ## Around the World

with Jehovah's Witnesses

# "Obey God Rather than Men"

The apostles, when before the Sanhedrin and commanded to cease preaching the good news regarding Christ Jesus, replied: "We must obey God as ruler rather than men." (Acts 5:29, NW) The 1952 "Yearbook" shows that the good example they set is being followed by Jehovah's witnesses today.

The witness work in a certain Syrian town was forging ahead by leaps and bounds, especially the public meeting campaign, partly due to the fact that a clergyman took his stand for the truth. So the Devil influenced his agents to command the brothers to refrain from assembling themselves together. However, they obeyed God rather than men and so the company servant was arrested. At the trial the false charges were refuted and a good witness was given, causing the judge, in dismissing the case, to say: "I have found Jehovah's witnesses to be the only real Christians." (170, 171)

Our brothers in Switzerland followed a like course. Although the Supreme Court at Lausanne refused to properly classify their house-to-house activity as preaching and ruled that they must obtain permits, they chose to obey God rather than men and even arranged for a circuit assembly in Lausanne, where the court resided, and featured the house-to-house work. Not a single arrest was made, and the largest attendance ever was seen at the public meeting! (233)

In Yugoslavia the brothers continue to preach and increase despite a five-year ban. They depend upon Jehovah for strength. (244)

### Home Bible Studies

One cannot read the reports contained in the 1952 "Yearbook" without being struck with the vital role that the home Bible studies play in releasing the prisoners; where "anyone who is being orally caught the word \[shares] in all good things with the one who gives such oral teaching". —Gal. 6:6, NW.

In Bolivia a clergyman told a young couple with whom a Bible study was being conducted that Jehovah's witnesses were antichrists. However, having just concluded the chapter on Jesus Christ, they were well equipped to refute him. Result: two more publishers of the Theocracy. Without the home Bible study the Devil's bird might have snatched away the seeds of Kingdom truth that had been so painstakingly planted in the hearts of those interested ones. (91)

In China one sister invited her former school friends to her own home for a Bible study, and before long there were three more witnesses for Jehovah. One of these in particular received much opposition from her husband and so-called friends, but that only made her stronger. Could she have withstood that heat of persecution without the help obtained from that Bible study? (110)

In France a back-call was made in a town where there were none of Jehovah's witnesses. This grew into a study with ten persons. "Within two months, four were publishing, and by the time of the next circuit assembly six of the original ten were immersed. At the close of the service year there were 12 publishers in that little town, and as many as 30 persons were attending the public meetings held there. Home Bible study work is certainly an effective means of obeying Jesus' injunction, 'Feed my sheep.'" (137) And also an effective way of telling the prisoners, "Go forth!"

# YOUR SERVICE MEETING

Some publishers devote a great deal of time to street work, but little if any time to house-to-house preaching. The greatest results are achieved by a balanced field schedule. In October many 'Awake!' subscriptions were obtained in house-to-house witnessing. The second demonstration below offers suggestions on how to follow through on 'Awake!' subscriptions.

Conductor opens with remarks about door-to-door work's being backbone of our service to God. Not enough door-to-door work is being done by publishers. Last year in the U.S. 193 million hours spent in field, 8 million pieces of literature placed, plus 11 million magazines. Roughly this amounts to one book, Bible, booklet or magazine placed every hour, the great majority being booklets and magazines. But why not increase book placements? Are we not educators? And do not books provide basis for more studies? Many hours of street work are good, no doubt; but most effective use of time requires us to channel more activity into house-to-house work. This will increase our placement average.

**SCENE I (3 min.)** Two brothers talking. Brother Progressive tries to encourage Brother Backward to do house-to-house work with him. Campaign this month is to place as many bound books as possible in hands of people. Backward says secular work allows him only two or three hours a week, and his street work at Main and Broadway seems to tire him less. Progressive tries to get him to put in one hour in house-to-house work, but to no avail.

**SCENE II (6 min.)** Progressive calls at a door. Householder says: 'I have seen a Watchtower man standing on corner of Main and Broadway.' Witness: 'Yes, it is one way we have of reaching many busy people.' Householder: 'It doesn't seem that many stop to talk to him, but I suppose every religion has a different way of preaching.' Witness: 'That's just one small feature of our preaching. But may I step in for a few minutes to explain how you have come into contact with the greatest educational work ever undertaken?' Progressive is invited in, leads into three-book offer, showing how they aid in study. Places books; arranges for return visit. (This scene can demonstrate effective presentation of three books.)

**SCENE III (3 min.)** Two brothers again meet and Progressive's experience is recounted. Brother Backward is impressed. Progressive shows how doing only street work is one-sided. Street work good, yes, but think of greater opportunities offered in house-to-house work. God gives real increase when we talk to people, when we give of ourselves to people by explaining his purposes, when we participate in all features of service. Why not put half of his street time in house-to-house publishing? He invites Brother Backward on back-call.

**SCENE IV (4 min.)** Householder glad to see Brother Backward. Says he did not appreciate work he saw on street until Brother Progressive talked to him and showed him it was only part of great educational campaign of Jehovah's witnesses. Systematic arrangement of books, questions, etc., convinces him we are real Bible educators. Study is arranged.

Conductor concludes, showing Brother Backward that he should now think very, very hard. How much more Progressive accomplishes with his time! If you are a street publisher only, why not give some of that time to house-to-house work for better results?
