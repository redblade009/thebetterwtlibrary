# INFORMANT

APRIL, 1950

"Preach the word."—2 Tim. 4:2.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## "Announcing Messiah's Presence" Testimony Period

### Watchtower Campaign Enters Final Month

#### Memorial Week-End Sets Stage for 100,000 Goal

In 1914, amid world-shaking events, Jehovah God asserted his universal sovereignty and sent forth his Messiah-Son to rule in the midst of his enemies. Christ's invisible reign means everlasting life to all who believe and obey. But how can they believe unless they first hear? and how can they hear unless his presence is announced? And who is there today announcing Messiah's presence? You are, if you really believe the signs that prove that Christ is now present and ruling.—Matt. 24:37, Roth.

Just pause a moment to consider what a unique position and privilege we have in daily telling others that Christ is present, ruling in the heavens, and how his Kingdom will shortly "satisfy the desire of every living thing". Of all the world's millions, Jehovah's witnesses alone are engaged in this ministry of salvation. One of the best instruments we have to aid us in ministering to "this generation" is the _Watchtower_ magazine. Did not _The Watchtower_ help us to see with our eyes of faith that Christ is present and ruling? Logically, then, it is the magazine we should use to assist others likewise to appreciate this all-important truth. Therefore, during April, we will continue to offer the _Watchtower_ subscription and a gift of eight booklets on a contribution of one dollar.

As we enter this final month of the _Watchtower_ campaign let us redouble our efforts to meet our individual quota of two subscriptions per publisher for the four months. Reports coming into the office thus far show we are surpassing last year's figures for this same period. Continue your diligent efforts by calling back on last year's subscribers, whose expiration slips are regularly mailed to each company. Rekindle the spark of interest they once had in _The Watchtower_, and demonstrate its practical benefits for them. Also, be sure to remember those who subscribed during the early months of the campaign. Visit them. Aid them to understand and study _The Watchtower_. Help them to realize that _The Watchtower_ is "meat in due season" for them.

#### Follow Up Memorial Week-End Activity

The Memorial week-end should give an enthusiastic impetus to all our April activities, setting the stage for a new peak in publishers. No doubt then many new ones will be assisted into the field, and also many more will eagerly participate during the remainder of the month, if given the proper encouragement and loving assistance by zealous publishers. Remember not to neglect these once they start publish-

ing, but continue to work with them and assist them until they become regular "announcers", able to bear their own burdens. As they engage in field service, encourage them to report their activity. Also visit delinquent publishers, infirm and sick ones to obtain their reports of time spent in preaching.

Our goal is 100,000 publishers by convention time, announcing Messiah's presence. We have already reached the 90,000 mark, so the desired quota is by no means an impossibility. It can be reached if we do not relax in our service. If all publishers are diligent and regularly, report the time profitably spent in preaching, both "in season" and "out of season", a great stride will be made toward reaching a new peak and our goal of 100,000.

#### Public Meetings Aid in Announcing

Another very effective means of announcing Messiah's presence is through public meetings. The new series should now be under way, and will undoubtedly give renewed force to this feature of service. Brand new subjects! What striking titles: "It Is High Time to Awake!" "Have Christendom's Religions Failed?" "Hell Used as a Scare." What vital, absorbing issues! "The Pathway to Peace," "Who Is Your God?" "Preserving Godliness amid World Delinquency." What comfort and assurance! "The Truth Shall Make You Free," "Surviving Global War." Yes, what a joy it will be to announce such good news.

True, for these talks to fulfill our expectations, those assigned to deliver them must make proper advance preparation. Their real success, however, will depend on the energetic support of each publisher. The company servant, together with the advertising servant, should ascertain the number of handbills needed for the company and order accordingly. The wide distribution of leaflets plays an important part in announcing and advertising. Advertise, too, by inviting your back-calls and home Bible studies. Are you doing your part?

Once you have advertised, then attend the public meeting and show genuine interest in the strangers present. Help them to further understand and appreciate the truths they have heard, by discussing with them any question they may have and by arranging to call on them at their homes at some convenient time.

Let us bring this, the twelfth _Watchtower_ campaign, to a climactic close. The prospects are bright. Memorial week-end has set the stage, and by further zealous endeavor, making the most of our opportunities, the way will be opened for new peaks and countless joys and blessings. So forward! announcing Messiah's presence.

## Pioneering, a Work with a Future Are You Arranging Your Affairs to Be One?

#### Watchtower Article to Be Considered at Service Meetings

Consider, if you will, representative types of work in which you might engage. After each put down the brightest future you might expect to result from devoting all of your ability and energy to it. You may find on your list such things as wealth and ease, social position, security or fame; perhaps even the satisfaction of benefiting humankind to some degree by discovery or invention.

Now record the future prospects that pioneer service holds out to those who diligently strive to succeed therein. These will include "the joy of the Lord", the full satisfaction of really aiding your fellow creatures to lasting benefits, countless blessings from the hand of a provident Creator both in richness of life and sufficient to meet present physical needs, a new world full of bounties just ahead and, best of all, for those who maintain integrity, everlasting life!

Study your lists and thoughtfully make comparison. Do you honestly feel that the transient rewards of any secular position can in anywise compare with those of the pioneer work? Or, are they as sure of fulfillment? Why, it even seems a waste of time to make the comparison! Truly, the pioneer work is the work with a future!

#### The Pioneer Service is for All

Surely you, like all other conscientious servants of God, have considered more than once these blessings that accompany pioneer service; but then you have dismissed the prospects from your mind. True, pioneering presents remarkable opportunities for others, but it cannot be for you, you conclude. You have good reasons. Well, list your reasons or excuses. Consider each one. Then know for a certainty that whatever your hindrance may be, there is somebody on the pioneer list who has overcome that same problem. If one can overcome it, doesn't it seem possible that by placing complete faith in Jehovah you too can overcome it?

Now is the time for each servant of God to carefully and prayerfully consider his own circumstances, and work toward the goal of entering the pioneer service. Many who have already been planning and working toward that end will be able to send for an application immediately and enjoy the added blessings of attending the 1950 convention as a pioneer. But do not let inability to begin before the convention move you to "put off" pioneer service into the indefinite future. Send for your application anyway, fill it in and return it, letting us know what month you wish to start; whether immediately, or soon after the convention. Then you will be able to begin

at the time you have planned, without delay.

Some may wish to sign up for the pioneer work while at the convention. In that case, it would be wise to bring with you the letter of recommendation from the company servant, which must accompany all pioneer applications. Then your application can be considered without unnecessary and time-consuming correspondence.

#### Vacation Pioneering

A large number of those now enjoying full-time service have been at one time or other vacation pioneers. This in itself proves that vacation pioneering is the best possible way for children to spend their vacation days, for, by busying themselves in Kingdom service, they grow in appreciation of their responsibility to Jehovah and are protected from undue influence by the world. But vacation pioneer service is not restricted to young people alone. Anyone who can remain in the pioneer work for at least two months at any time of the year and who can meet the necessary requirements of having been immersed and a publisher for six months can qualify for vacation pioneering. For the past two years we have had new peaks in the number who have enjoyed this service. This summer may we not hope to exceed last year's \$32 vacation pioneers?

#### "Watchtower" Stresses Pioneering

So important is pioneering now to the welfare of the servants of God and to the expansion of gospel- preaching that _The Watchtower_ has devoted the April 1, 1950, issue to this subject. The second article of this issue, entitled "More and More Pioneers of Good News", because of its service theme, will be considered at the service meetings instead of at the regular _Watchtower_ study. It will be taken up during the last three service meetings in April, by questions and answers in the same manner in which _The Watchtower_ is customarily studied. Not more than thirty minutes should be spent on each of these studies, which will be apportioned to cover paragraphs 1-10 at the first meeting, paragraphs 11-21 at the second and paragraphs 22-31 at the last.

All publishers should make every effort to be present at each of these service meetings, to benefit from these studies. Careful study of the article beforehand and enthusiastic participation will enhance the value of the material to you.

Remember, then, pioneering isn't only for somebody else. Pioneering is also for you. Make your decision now to be a pioneer, and plan for the future. Send for an application blank, fill it out, and return it with a letter of recommendation from the company servant. Then, at the time you have decided upon, enter the pioneer service, the work with a lasting future.

# Singing the Taunt-Song Against Satan

## Public Meeting Work to Be Intensified

### Offer for May Features Three Bound Books

While men and nations continue to sing this old world's praises and to extol its god, Jehovah issues a command to His servants: "You shall raise this taunt-song against the king of Babylon, crying: How the tyrant is hushed!" (Isa. 14:4; Moffatt). This taunt-song was not composed for the benefit of Satan and his irreformable world organization. Rather, it is for the long time victims of this powerful tyrant, vast numbers of whom doubt that he even exists. For these enslaved ones the lyrics were composed, for they give a note of hope and assurance of liberation.

**Join the Taunt Singers During May**
During the month of May you are invited to join in the chorus of joyful singers. People of good-will everywhere will rejoice at the sound of the melody of truth that will be on the lips of Jehovah's witnesses. This refreshing message will be brought to the people in the form of the books "Let God Be True", "The Kingdom Is at Hand" and "The Truth Shall Make You Free" all on a contribution of \$1.00.

In accordance with our lessons on "Argumentation" and "Presenting the Gospel at the Doors", found in "Theoretic Aid to Kingdom Publishers", many methods of presenting the above books can be developed. For example, in the chapter entitled "Satan the Devil", in "Let God Be True", it is pointed out that "the whole world lies in the power of the evil one". From this it can be shown that the only hope for mankind lies in the righteous rule of God's kingdom by Christ Jesus. Literally hundreds more presentations can be found by alert publishers in the three books offered during May.

Will you be one of the thousands of singers of the taunt-song against Satan during May? Are you preparing suitable and varied presentations so that your voice will be effectively added to the growing chorus world-wide? Surely your answer will be a ringing yes! Let your conviction of the truthfulness of the

taunt-song be shown by your regularity and zeal in Jehovah's service. Out of love help others to harmoniously blend their voices with yours.

**All Avenues of Service Open**
Singers of the taunt-song rejoice that all features of the witness work can be engaged in during May. Set a goal to participate in every type of work arranged for by your local company. This should include house-to-house witnessing, back-out activity and conducting home Bible studies. Magazine Day should not be overlooked, for with the arrival of warmer weather more stress can be placed on this branch of service.

Our theater of song can be extended to include outdoor public meetings in parks, bands, and other locations, private lawns, etc. Appropriately, the Society has arranged new lectures that will edify the brethren as well as the "stranger." Intensified public meeting activity will automatically cause improvement in other features of service. With such abundant opportunities, are you planning to take advantage of them?

The company servant, together with the stock servant, should check to see that there is sufficient literature available. The territory servant should line up for group witnessing those sections of territory not covered during the winter. In many companies this will include rural and outlying sections where public meetings can be held. Practical service meetings on presenting the offer of the month, calling back on Watchtower subscribers and welcoming new interest at public meetings should be thoughtfully planned by the company servant.

Everyone consecrated to Jehovah should prepare now, not only to add his own voice to the chorus of singers, but to aid the new and inexperienced publishers as well. Let each one endeavor to assist at least one person to be an active and regular singer of Kingdom truth during May. Thus, by all working together, the anthem will grow in volume to the delight of those of good-will and to the chagrin of Satan, who will be hushed at Armageddon.

### Pioneer Assignment Cards

Assignment cards held by pioneers should always coincide with the territory in which they are working. This is not always the case at present, for different reasons. In some instances the name of the company or unit may have been changed, because of a shift in the location of the company headquarters or the formation of new units. Hence, even though the pioneer is still working in the same general territory or with the same group, his card may not agree with the official name of the unit or company. Other pioneers may have started to work with other companies, overlooking the necessity of being assigned to that territory by the Society.

It is therefore requested that all pioneers immediately check their assignment cards. If the card does not show the name of the company you are now with, please return the card to the Society immediately and request an up-to-date assignment card. Be sure to state where you wish to be assigned to avoid unnecessary correspondence. Circuit servants can assist in this respect by emphasizing this point at the time of the pioneer interview.

### Reports of All Preaching Activity to Be Made

Jehovah gives his people the opportunity both to manifest love for him and to encourage their brethren by allowing them to report Kingdom service activity. Love for Jehovah is shown by following the example he set, and, since compiled service reports make known the great amount of praise being rendered to Jehovah's name, the results are a real source of encouragement to all.

Sometimes a publisher is heard to say, "Why should I report?" The Lord knows what I am doing. "To be sure, he does. Each time that we obediently open our lips to praise him he is aware of it, and he is pleased with this acceptable sacrifice. But God also knew what "the man with the writer's inkhorn" had done. Nevertheless, God commanded him to report the matter, and this he obediently did. This example, recorded at Ezekiel 9:11, shows the propriety of reporting after the work is done.

Furthermore, many reports were made by the Creator himself. He recorded in remarkable detail events from creation to the ultimate destruction of wickedness, and coming Kingdom blessings for those who now obey. His report is a loving provision for the comfort of all who love him.

### Bethel Applications

Recently a number of brothers were invited to come to Bethel and Kingdom Farm because they had filed applications some time ago. But now their circumstances are changed, so they found it impossible to accept the invitation.

In order to keep the Bethel application file up to date, all Bethel applications now on hand at Brooklyn will be canceled as of April 1. Those who have had applications on file for some time and who wish to keep their names on file must make new applications. Those who have not previously applied for Bethel service may do so now, if they wish.

Single men and women between the ages of 18 and 55 may qualify for Bethel service. They must be in good health and seriously interested in permanent full-time service. Another requirement is that each person must have been consecrated and active in the service for a full year. Pioneers and company publishers may apply.

Send requests for application blanks to: President's Office, 124 Columbia Heights, Brooklyn 2, New York.

When blanks are filled out and returned to the President's Office, they will be put on file for consideration when openings occur for additional servants at Bethel.
