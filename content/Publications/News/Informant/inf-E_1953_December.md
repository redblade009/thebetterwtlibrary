## Around the World with Jehovah's Witnesses

### Overcoming Obstacles

In 127 different lands Jehovah's witnesses encountered and overcame all kinds of obstacles in order to be able to preach to men of good will. Rather than stop or slow down when confronted with problems, they obey his command: 'Do with your might what your hands find to do.' Blessings result, as shown in the 1953 'Yearbook.'

In order to work unassigned territory in Norway publishers had transportation and other obstacles to overcome. By careful planning these were worked out, only to find additional and unexpected ones on arriving in unassigned territory. These, too, were surmounted with grand results. (233, 234) Many new congregations are in the process of being formed because Kingdom publishers overcame obstacles to successfully preach. (164, 165)

Pioneering is the ultimate goal of all appreciative servants of Jehovah on this side of Armageddon. In order to enjoy the full-time service, often obstacles in this materialistic world must be overcome. A publisher in Burma removed obstacles and now enjoys greater happiness in New World society activity. (125) Have you tried doing so?

On obtaining a subscription we have been counseled to call back with issues of the magazine until the subscriber's copy starts coming through the mail. How would you like to deliver all 24 copies to each subscriber? In Belgium a ban prohibits the delivery of magazines by mail. This obstacle was overcome with good results by publishers' personally delivering all subscription copies. (105)

### Defending and Legally Establishing the Good News

Under favorable and unfavorable conditions Jehovah's witnesses take advantage of all opportunities to preach the Kingdom message. Adverse conditions do not stop them, though at times they do result in their being brought before local police authorities, into court and before rulers. To comfort all that mourn God's ministers press on 'defending and legally establishing' their God-given right to preach.

Are you acquainted with the Society's booklet 'Defending and Legally Establishing the Good News'? Could you use it if called upon to do so? Its effectiveness in defending our right to preach is shown on page 65.

For years much time, effort and expense have been directed toward establishing our right to preach from house to house with literature in the Province of Quebec. How good it is to know that the 800 court cases that have been pending may be put aside because in October, 1953, Jehovah gave his servants the victory in the case in the Supreme Court of Canada! (128) Now literature can be used in house-to-house witnessing.

In Switzerland police officials are no longer able to charge Jehovah's witnesses as peddlers or disturbers of Sunday peace. The courts of that land have ruled: 'The peddling law has no application to the activity of Jehovah's witnesses.' (274) Italian courts, too, have given favorable decisions. (198)

When called before governors and rulers Jesus and his apostles established their God-given right to preach. Modern witnesses follow Jesus' example (287) and have Jehovah's blessing.

## From House to House During December

1 December is a religious month for so-called Christians. During the month much emphasis is placed on the Christmas celebration. Over and over the story of the birth of Jesus is told and many persons become more receptive to reading the Bible than at any other time. During this period it will be our privilege to present the _New World Translation of the Christian Greek Scriptures_ and a book.

2 In presenting the Bible at the door, using a three- to eight-minute sermon, you might call attention to the importance of understanding the reasons for Christ's coming to earth. One could say: 'During this season of the year much is said about the birth of Jesus and our attention is drawn to the Biblical account found in Luke and Matthew. Have you taken note of what the angel Gabriel said to Mary about the child she was to bear? It's found at Luke 1:32, 33.' (Read it to them from the _New World Translation_, or let them read it.) Then continue: 'We hear much about the virgin birth and the tiny babe, but note here that the babe was to become king of a kingdom that would have no end. Because they knew the part he was to have in establishing God's kingdom. This kingdom is the same one you pray for in

the Lord's prayer. (Matt. 6:9, 10, NW) A study of the Bible will give you complete information about that kingdom and its blessing. To help you get that understanding, I'm glad to be able to offer this modern translation of the Greek Scriptures and this Bible study aid book to you on a contribution of only \$2.'

3 Another presentation might be: 'Probably you have heard that Jesus said in the sermon on the mount: 'Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.' I would like you to read that verse in the _New World Translation_. \[Read Matthew 5:3.] See how much clearer the thought is? It is not just a matter of those who have a weak or poor spirit learning about and living under the reign of the King, but it is those who have become conscious of the need for spiritual instruction who will become happy ones. Further, here at John 17:3, Jesus tells us that everlasting life is gained only when we take in knowledge of the only true God and the one he has sent forth. \[Read it.] That is why we encourage you to study the Bible and bring you this translation in modern English. It is easier to read and the thought is so much clearer that studying it is a real pleasure. To further assist you, this book with many chapters highlighting not only the birth of Jesus but also the blessings of his reign as King will help you in satisfying your spiritual need. Together they are yours on a contribution of only \$2.'

# YOUR SERVICE MEETING

The first demonstration should be put on early in December. It emphasizes the practicalness of the monthly offer. The second demonstration deals with the training work being carried out in all congregations.

**SCENE I (2 min.)** Publisher is in theocratic court for failing to engage in house-to-house work with Bible and book offer. Attorney presumes charges against publisher, emphasizing the dangerous position the publisher is putting himself in by withholding life-giving truth from others. (Acts 5: 42, NW) Lovingly the publisher has been brought into court so as to receive theocratic correction.

**SCENE II (3 min.)** Publisher is called to the stand and questioned by attorney, who establishes from publisher that house-to-house work has not been done in September and November, and doubts if it will be done in December. Why? Because publisher feels the "New World Translation of the Christian Greek Scriptures" is for witnesses, and, besides, \$2.00 is more than most people have for the offer.

**SCENE III (4 min.)** "NW" takes stand and on being questioned by attorney testifies it is not for witnesses only. Its clear understandable language can be a blessing to all lovers of truth and is what the people need. Armageddon is impending! The Kingdom is established! People must understand the trend of events if they are to live. "NW" is glad it clears up doctrines, magnifies Jehovah's name and can assist people of good will. As for being expensive, "NW" says it contains knowledge necessary for people to find pearl of great price. "NW" urges it be used in house-to-house work along with book, on contribution of \$2.

**SCENE IV (4 min.)** Book takes stand and testifies to questions of attorney that it goes well with "NW" and aids in systematic study. Material on various subjects is gathered within its pages for convenient study. Bible and book have helped most persons in theocratic court to understand more of Jehovah's purposes, and the two will help many persons of good will—if publishers place them from house to house.

**SCENE V (4 min.)** Householder next takes stand. Attorney uses points "NW" and book made in asking householder if he needs them. Householder replies that they sound to be just what he lacks in understanding more about God, for he wants to have peace of mind in this troubled world. Attorney questions him on \$2 contribution for Bible and book, pointing out the need for spiritual food that the combination fills. (Matt. 4:4, NW) Householder agrees that spiritual diet must not be neglected and asks if he can obtain the offer now. Attorney then asks if others in his neighborhood might be interested. Householder relates discussion he had with neighbor who did not understand events of the day and expresses the hope that publisher will go from house to house calling upon them.

**SCENE VI (3 min.)** Judge calls publisher to the stand and summarizes points made by Bible, book and householder. Then he asks publisher if his error in reasoning is seen. Publisher says "Yes" and wants to correct matters. Judge sentences publisher to comply with 1 Timothy 4:15, 16 (NW), and concludes with exhortation for all to join in worship of Jehovah in holiness by sharing in house-to-house work regularly with Bible and book offer during December.

## The Proper Mental Attitude

**SCENE I (5 min.)** Study conductor is talking to publisher New View, who is qualified to teach and train others in house-to-house work. New View is assigned to assist Old View who has not been doing house-to-house work for some time. New View is hesitant and asks how he can approach Old View, who has been in the truth much longer. Conductor emphasizes instructions received at the New World Society Assembly and how mature publishers should tactfully encourage publishers not doing house-to-house work to join in this feature of Jehovah's strange work. Whether young or old in the truth all publishers should do house-to-house preaching as set forth in the Bible. New View agrees the new arrangement is a loving provision, and will gladly do his part by making definite arrangements to assist Old View.

**SCENE II (5 min.)** New View calls on Old View and tactfully and in a loving manner discusses the encouragement all of Jehovah's witnesses received at the New World Society Assembly. Also, the need for all to do house-to-house work is continually stressed in the "Informant." New View relates the joy that one has finding other sheep in house-to-house activity, and comments on Jehovah's wisdom in having the witness work done from house to house. He has a territory that has not been worked with current offer, and asks Old View to come along with him Sunday. Old View replies he would like to, but his son and family are coming over Sunday. He just couldn't make it. New View understandingly acknowledges Old View's position and says he will call again so definite arrangements can be made in the near future.

**SCENE III (5 min.)** Talking to himself Old View ponders over his discussion with New View. He used to go out in house-to-house work, but hasn't for years. It is true, as New View said, at the convention the necessity of engaging in house-to-house work was emphasized, and the "Informant" continually highlights it. Old View admits he should do the work and really wants to. Why, then, did he turn down New View's kind invitation? Was it his pride that made him say what he did when he knew right well his guests were not coming until the afternoon? Brother New View was right. The other sheep are found mostly in house-to-house work, and what joy there is to find one interested in the Kingdom. Why should he keep on depriving himself of the happiness of giving? And, too, Acts 20:20 outlines the need to preach from house to house. God is not partial; it means me as well as others. It is about time I get back into the good habit of going from house to house. If everyone else can do it, so can I. When New View comes and invites me to go with him next time, I'm surely going to accept the opportunity to get started in the house-to-house preaching work, and, undoubtedly, before long I'll be in position to help someone else. I'm surely glad Jehovah has instituted this new arrangement and that his servants are willing to help persons like me.

4 For a lively, interesting meeting to result, advance preparation on the part of the conductor and those attending is necessary. ## CONGREGATION ORGANIZATION

### Following the Circuit Servant's Visit

$^{1}$ The benefits of the circuit servant's visit to a congregation are realized not only during the week he is with the congregation. During the week of his visit publishers are taught and instructed in the best ways of performing the ministry. The weeks that follow his visit should see the things learned put into practice and should result in more effective witnessing. This emphasizes the importance of being alert during his visit and sharing in all the activities arranged, in order that we might 'not be forgetful hearers but doers of the work.'

$^{2}$ At the present time the circuit servant is spending much of his visit training publishers to do effective house-to-house preaching. He concentrates his attention on training and working with the more mature publishers so they in turn will be able to teach and train others in the congregation in the months to follow. Mature publishers assigned to work with less effective and irregular publishers should take the assignment seriously. The goal should be to eliminate irregularity in the service and have all become mature ministers. The training program established during his visit should be kept well organized under the congregation servant's supervision. Each publisher should co-operate fully. Already the Society is receiving very encouraging reports as to the outstanding results achieved by this training program's being used and by all publishers' endeavoring to give three- to eight-minute sermons at the doors.

$^{3}$ In addition to the training given

in house-to-house preaching, the circuit servant gives good admonition from the platform. His talks encourage and edify the congregation and at the same time bring to the attention of all publishers the weaknesses that should be overcome, along with counsel for doing so. Servants should pay particular attention to these weaknesses and the counsel for overcoming them. However, the admonition is for all publishers. Everyone should make it his responsibility to do his part in improving the activity of the congregation.

$^{4}$ At the end of his visit the circuit servant prepares a report on the condition of the congregation and sends it to the Society. A copy of it is left with the servants for future reference in working to overcome weaknesses that manifest themselves in the congregation. All servants should read the report and apply the counsel. Parts of the report might be used from time to time in developing parts on the service meeting as well as assisting in general congregation organization. On receiving the circuit servant's report the Society writes a personal letter of acknowledgment to the congregation and gives pertinent admonition and counsel.

$^{5}$ By utilizing all the counsel received during the circuit servant's visit and in the Society's letter, the congregation is sure to improve and progress in its ministry. This is the desire of all members of the New World society, and we are thankful to Jehovah for so many provisions, such as the circuit servant's visit, to keep us growing to maturity and preaching the good news more effectively.
