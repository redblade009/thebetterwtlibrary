BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## "Living Up to the Name" Testimony Period

Offer of Three Books Continued

### Advertise the International Assembly

One of the things that particularly identifies the Lord's consecrated servants today is the name they bear as Jehovah's witnesses. What a lofty privilege it is for insignificant man to bear the majestic name of the glorious Creator who alone is Jehovah! Those that accept the name, however, must also accept the responsibility that such a privilege places upon them. They cannot be witnesses in name only; they must live up to the name.—Isa. 43:10, A. S. Y.

How do Jehovah's witnesses live up to the name? By living exemplary Christian lives, which are built around concentrated activity in field service. Out on the busy streets and to the homes they go, praising Jehovah and magnifying his name before all who will hear. While in this service of the King they deport themselves as befitting ambassadors of his heavenly kingdom. Their speech at the doors is pleasant, graced with good things about Jehovah. Their conduct in the homes is irreproachable so as not to stigmatize or cast reflection on his holy name and cause some to stumble. In all their service they display Theocratic tact and the attributes of the God they worship.

During June part of living up to the name will be offering to the people three bound books on a contribution of \$1.00. In most cases the three latest books will be used, but any of the campaign volumes in stock may be presented. Demonstrations should be planned around the literature you will be using. The value of these various publications cannot be overstated, for they make known the name of Jehovah, how to serve him and live up to his name. Isn't this the people's greatest need?

There are yet thousands of good-will persons who will accept Jehovah's name. Before these can live up to the name they must be found and instructed. A house-to-house search must be made for them. In view of this we cannot ignore this important feature of service. These initial contacts furnish the basis for by far the majority of our future back-calls and home Bible studies. Those living up to the name should make it a point to engage regularly in the house-to-house activity; and group witnessing for this purpose should be arranged from the company book-study locations and the Kingdom Hall.

### Stress Back-Calls and Talk Convention

As you extol Jehovah's name in your door-to-door witnessing be sure to make a record of all placements and where interested ones live. Not only should you inform these prospective praisers that you will re-

turn, but keep your word and call back. Jehovah is a dependable God; those living up to his name must likewise show dependability as well as patience and loving consideration toward interested ones. The sheep-like ones who invite you into their homes look to you as a minister of God. Their future progress will be influenced by the example you set for them.

The international assembly in New York city will make an excellent talking point on your back-calls and Bible studies. Review past assemblies for them. What their appetites for these joyous spiritual feasts which Jehovah serves to those bearing his name. Though the Society does not have any in stock, it may be that publishers have personal copies of _The Messenger_ from previous conventions, which can be reviewed to arouse interest for this grand event. Many of them will desire to attend, and there is yet time for them to make plans. Perhaps they can be assisted in this regard.

Also, let us not neglect to call on the regular subscribers to the _Watchtower_ and _Awake!_ magazines. Keep their interest alive, and ask them to renew their subscription before it expires. Expiration slips for those subscriptions which do expire will, of course, provide an additional source of back-calls, and you should continue to contact these as their names are sent to your company. Since assemblies play such a vital part in magnifying Jehovah's name, an enthusiastic discussion about the international convention may be just the stimulus needed to reawaken interest in _The Watchtower_ or _Awake!_ and in the Kingdom. Make the most of these opportunities.

### Live Up to the Name at All Times

Living up to the name is not restricted to our direct service activities. It extends further than that. It is a full-time obligation. Though field service reports show that publishers, on the average, spend less than one hour each day in actual preaching, we, nevertheless, bear Jehovah's name all day long. This means, then, that our daily lives must be a living testimony exalting Jehovah's greatness. In our dealings with this world we must reflect all the dignity and superlative qualities that his name stands for. ## Advance Convention Preparations Begin

### President Opens Rooming Work

On Saturday night, May 13, at St. Nicholas Arena, in the heart of New York city, 4,837 publishers from the city and surrounding companies assembled together to discuss their share in the 1950 International Convention. Gladly and with great enthusiasm they accepted the role of hosts. To until the time of that meeting they had been looking ahead, but with it came a first taste of the convention itself. The president of the Society, Brother Knorr, opened the meeting, describing the program to be accomplished and showing the responsibilities of it all upon the local publishers. Instructions were given as to how to select rooming accommodations, what to look for and how to eliminate the undesirable ones.

Since there is very little hope of a trailer camp's being established, many more rooms in private homes will have to be found. It is true there are many more hotels in New York city than in other cities, but most of the hotels are keeping permanent guests and not so many rooms are available. The Convention Bureau helped by having its member hotels pledge rooms, and so at the time of the meeting there were 8,700 rooms pledged for convention use, which might accommodate approximately 20,000 people. More rooms will be obtained from other hotels in the city, but the majority of the conventioners will be housed in private homes. That will be a good thing, too, for it is in the

homes of the people that impressions are made and a witness is given.

The report of the Convention Committee showed that a few thousand room request forms had been received and that the mails were pouring in with hundreds more. It will be a help to the Committee to have the room request forms early, so the _Informant_ here reminds all to send in room request forms as soon as possible.

An outline of the convention departments is being drawn up, and the volunteer service applications have come in handy in this work. Now is the time to lay the foundations of a smooth-working convention organization. Let us know what you can do to help. If you have not sent your company's forms in, please mail them soon.

We also learned that approximately two thousand brethren are listed as coming from countries outside of the United States and Canada, from all corners of the earth. More than fifty countries and islands of the sea will be represented at the New York convention.

This year it will not be necessary to call in hundreds of pioneers months in advance for the pre-convention work, because the publishers of Greater New York are taking on the work. They are a joyful lot. Yes, the convention spirit is really evident in New York city. If you ask the publishers here, this is what they'll tell you: "We're getting everything ready for you. Be sure to come. Be here all eight days, and don't miss any part of the convention."

Think of it; an increase of 12,148 in only two months!

This remarkable growth has called for strenuous and consistent effort. Servants, individual publishers and pioneers have all had to plan, assist and work hard. But it has been worth the added effort, hasn't it? Do you think we should stop now? one, and sincere efforts will be blessed indeed.

Remember, bearing Jehovah's name is the grandest privilege any human creature can have conferred upon him. It is also a serious responsibility, a twenty-four-hours-a-day job, requiring all of our time and attention. With all publishers in each company uniting their efforts and working closely together, a tremendous witness can be accomplished. So hold on to your privileged position as Jehovah's witnesses and rejoice in the blessings reserved for those who are truly living up to the name.

Or should we strive for even higher goals?

Certainly, we all hope for further increases; none want to drop back now. And how can we keep up this healthy progress? Keep pace with the Theocracy! Its triumphant forward march will continue until the whole earth rings with praise to Jehovah! There can be no decrease if each publisher will keep in step, "preach the word" regularly each month and aid another who is not as strong. We, his servants, sow and water, and to show his approval "Jehovah gives the increase".

# "Pay God What Belongs to God"

## Plan on Being a Regular Publisher

### July Offer to Feature One Bound Book

Jehovah God created the universe and every living thing in it. Therefore, everything in the universe belongs to Jehovah. That means that we, then, as human creatures are a part of his possession, and everything that we have and enjoy belongs to him. In appreciation of this fact, we have indicated our love to Jehovah by making a vow or consecration and offering our lives and everything we possess in unselfish service to him. "Caesars" of this world, under the influencing power of Satan, try to encroach upon or even demand that we not keep this vow. Attempts are made, and will continue to be made, to regiment our time; but we will be careful to pay Caesar only what belongs to Caesar, and pay God what belongs to God. With life-insuring wisdom the wise man declared: "When thou oweest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools: pay that which thou hast vowed."—Ecol. 5:4.

### Arrange to Keep Service Activities Alive

With joyous anticipation, most of the Lord's servants are making preparations to attend the rapidly approaching convention. However, this should not completely overshadow our regular service activities during this convention month. Conscious effort should be made to keep all features of service reasonably well supported, and certainly every publisher will want to devote some time to Kingdom service, so as to be a regular publisher right through the convention period. To aid us in fulfilling our vows to Jehovah during the month of July, the offer that will be featured is a bound

book, on a contribution of 35c. If persons have already obtained "Let God Be True", then any other campaign book can be substituted.

Rather than waiting till after the convention to prepare to carry on public meetings, companies will want to make preparations in advance so that the public lecture series can take up immediately thereafter. Also, to take care of the good-will interest and the publishers who do not attend the convention, companies can make plans now to organize the remaining brethren so that field service arrangements and meetings can continue during the convention period. All of the brethren should keep in mind to render whatever assistance is possible to aid both those of good-will and brethren in less fortunate circumstances to come to the assembly and share in its wonderful blessings.

On the way to the convention many opportunities will be presented to witness and thus pay our vows to God. By taking advantage of all opportunities to preach the Word, and by conducting ourselves as true ministers of God, a heap of witness will be given from one end of the country to the other. Be sure to keep a record of all the time spent in witnessing while going to, attending and returning from the convention, as this should be turned in to the local company upon return. Companies should send their July report to the Society as soon as possible after the convention.

With all of Jehovah's servants keeping in mind their consecration vows to God, they will not neglect their service activities during the month of July, nor allow Caesar and this world to interfere with such service. Instead, they will make full use of all their opportunities and thereby "pay God what belongs to God."—Matt. 22:21, AnAmer. Trans.

### Watchtower Campaign Exceeds 180,000 Quota

The 12th annual Watchtower subscription campaign came to a conclusion with 180,829 new Watchtower and Awake! subscriptions obtained in the United States by the end of April. This great campaign has again proved its value by opening up thousands of new homes and many more minds to the message of truth presented through the pages of these two Christian magazines. In view of our campaign quota of 180,000 new subscriptions for the United States, the total obtained reflects sincere effort on the part of God's servants and his blessing on their activity. Another quota exceeded!

The total of 180,829 subscriptions is based upon field service reports turned in by United States companies and pioneers.

During the months to come these new subscribers will benefit to varying degrees from The Watchtower depending upon whether they read it; and whether after reading they understand. Hence it is essential that each publisher call back upon those persons who subscribed from him and aid them to realize the full value of The Watchtower. Are you conscientiously doing this? If you are, then next year there will be fewer expiring subscriptions. Instead, many subscribers will have renewed; yes, and some will be active witnesses themselves, taking the good news to others.

### Circuit Servant Visits Discontinued During Convention

On the week-end of July 23, 1950, all circuit servants will discontinue visiting the companies. Their work with the companies will be resumed on Tuesday, August 15. This will allow a three-week period to attend the international convention in New York and enjoy a vacation from the regular routine of the circuit work.

Circuit assemblies, which have been suspended since March 20, will be started again on Friday, September 1, 1950. Undoubtedly the circuit assemblies and the visits of the circuit servants will do much to emphasize the blessings of the international convention and further strengthen the publishers for the important work of gospel-preaching yet ahead.

If your company is notified that the circuit servant's visit or the circuit assembly will closely follow the convention, you should make as much of the preparation as possible before you leave for New York.

### The Call for Pioneers Continues

Gratifying, indeed, is the response to the call for pioneers. Why, in one week alone over 400 applications for pioneer service were sent out? True, it may be that not every one of these publishers will realize his goal to enter the pioneer service, but it is evident that they are thinking, planning and working toward that end. There is every reason to believe that the majority of these brethren will continue on, with Jehovah's help, to complete this forward step. But there are undoubtedly many others with the same heart's desire and whose circumstances might be altered sufficiently to make pioneering possible. To these the call for pioneers continues.

With this issue of the Informant's another pioneer letter is being sent. There is a personal copy for each publisher, so that it can be prayerfully considered in private study in addition to being discussed at the service meeting.

Do not conclude that pioneering is only for others. Have you calmly and dispassionately reviewed your circumstances before the Lord? Can you foresee adjustments which might be made to overcome those things which prevent you from being a pioneer? If, as a result of this personal examination of your circumstances, you believe you can embark on the wise course and heed the call for pioneers, send now for your application. Having taken that step, continue to work positively toward being a pioneer, looking to Jehovah for guidance and help.

### Magazines and Mail During Convention

During the latter part of July and the first part of August, the majority of the publishers will be away from their home companies to attend the international convention in New York. Arrangements should be made in advance to take care of personal and company mail during this absence so that it will not be returned to the Society. If you will carefully note the suggestions given below much expense and inconvenience can be avoided.

Pioneers and individuals who receive The Watchtower and Awake! direct in individual rolls or by subscription should arrange for a friend, neighbor or another witness, who is not attending the convention, to pick up and care for their magazines and other mail regularly. It might be well to mention to your postman that you will be gone for a short while but that your mail should still be delivered and you have arranged to have it cared for.

If the one who regularly receives the distributors' copies is to be away, please see that someone is appointed to take care of them and arrange for their distribution in the field. Company servants and others who may be expecting literature shipments or other mail from the Society should leave definite instructions as to how it is to be cared for to avoid return of mail or costly storage charges on literature shipments. Please do not leave these matters until the last minute and then forget to care for them, as it will mean much needless expense and inconvenience.
