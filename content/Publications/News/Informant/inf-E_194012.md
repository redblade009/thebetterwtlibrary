## Let's Go! "Courage" Testimony Period Begins Will You Place 3 Combinations the First Week?

### More than 10,000,000 Booklets Ready for Witness

By this time the preparations for the greatest of all booklet campaigns are completed. There are now over 10 million booklets on hand with the companies and pioneers, and both have ordered several million more. Therefore companies and pioneers are fully equipped, and all company and pioneer publishers prepared to start off in this campaign with a BANG. It is estimated that 5 million of the 10 million booklets now on hand will be placed during the month of December. It will be done. That is a vast amount of literature; life-sustaining food for the people of good-will, a great joy and blessing in store for all who have a part in the distribution, and a terrifying blow to the Devil's organization. All good-will names, territory, and sound equipment should be in order and ready. Special wrappers also should be pasted and handed out to facilitate the booklet distribution. Everyone should be thoroughly familiar with the details of the special offer and the quota set for company publishers and pioneers, as outlined in the November _Informant_.

### Every Publisher Ready for Campaign

A splendid example is given us in Phud's being fully prepared when he went forward to meet the enemy. He trusted in the Lord for guidance and strength, and when the proper time came he quickly drew the sword and with the greatest force plunged it into the belly of the fat king Egion. The same thing

with this booklet campaign—_preparation, courage, and speedy action_ are essential requisites. There should be a peak of publishers in December. There are ten days in particular on which everyone should get out, the "Christmas" holiday, when practically everyone will be free for field service, and the Saturdays and Sundays. All publishers should arrange their affairs so that every minute of time possible will be used in getting out into the field to place this special offer with the people. Each company publisher should be equipped with his quota of 10 combinations for the month. Remember also the foreign territory. Proper covering of the foreign territory with this special offer and preparations as outlined in the November _Informant_ should be stressed.

At each service meeting a weekly report should be given. This weekly report will show the progress that the company is making in attaining its quota of combinations placed, week by week. This will enable the companies to be better prepared to attain their quota and push forward this campaign in their territory with increasing force. Every publisher will be on his toes to do his part. To make this campaign have a fitting climax it is being extended to include New Year's Day, on which day every publisher will get out into the field and begin with the new booklet _Satisfied_ in every combination, as well as begin covering territory preparatory to the February campaign. Let's go! Make "Courage" Testimony one of the greatest witnesses ever given.

## January Witness, "Satisfied" Will It Be 60,000 Publishers by January? Millions of Booklets Being Shipped on Consignment

### Pioneers Are Ordering

The _November Informant_ called our attention to a new booklet by Brother Rutherford—_Satisfied_. We commence distributing this booklet on January 1, using it exclusively throughout that month in order to prepare the field for the _Watchtower_ campaign that is to follow.

To the anointed and Jonadales alike this booklet will be found thrilling and refreshing beyond words; and to the people of good-will, indispensable. These are located in the homes, in the business offices, on the streets, and even in the air-rail shelters. In fact, everywhere where people congregate such people of good-will may be located.

### Why the Campaign?

1. To carry to these hungering souls the message contained in this booklet.

2. To prepare the field for the _Watchtower_ campaign to follow.

3. To direct the people of good-will to look for and expect to see Jehovah's witnesses on the street with _The Watchtower_.

The publishers who are faithfully availing themselves of their great privileges now are being used by the Lord in this manner to feed and comfort the people with provisions from the Lord's storehouse.

The booklet _Satisfied_ is consigned to each company on the basis of 30 copies for each publisher in the company, and each publisher should have a part in the campaign and see that his 30 copies are placed in the hands of the people. This should be done in the regular house-to-house work and in your magazine routes. They may be placed with the magazines on the streets, and, of course, all names on your back-call files should be supplied

with a copy. This campaign should be so complete that the booklet _Satisfied_ should be discussed among the people everywhere, and this can be done by Jehovah's witnesses' enthusiastically carrying on this campaign. Take them with you to your work. Have your associates in the office and factory reading and discussing it. Take them to the market places; magazine publishers holding them before the people in the streets and in every other manner possible keep this booklet prominent during the month of January; and if we do that, much will be accomplished in preparing the field for the _Watchtower_ campaign.

### A New Peak in January

There are over 60,000 publishers associated with the companies. Practically all of them can get out some time during the month. At 30 booklets each, this will run close to two million copies. There are some four thousand pioneers and regional and zone servants on our list, who will be doing their share, devoting all their time to the Kingdom service. Therefore it is reasonable to expect that we can distribute between 2½ and 3 million of these booklets during the month of January. If each one tries conscientiously to do his part there is not any doubt about the results. Each one who receives a copy of the booklet is an excellent prospect for _The Watchtower_. We can call back on them and talk _The Watchtower_ understandingly because of what they have read in the booklet, and take their subscription or at least place a copy of _The Watchtower_ with them on a contribution of five cents—with good prospects of calling back later and getting a subscription or including them in our regular magazine route.

# 1941 "Yearbook of Jehovah's witnesses"

When you read the report by Brother Rutherford as furnished in the 1941 Yearbook of Jehovah's witnesses you will appreciate more than ever before the power of Jehovah. The world-wide activities of Jehovah's witnesses under continuous enemy fire during the past service year will be a joy to everyone. Not only will this amazing report bring a thrill to those already associated with the Theocratic Government, but the people of good-will will be astonished when they read the 1941 Yearbook.

Order your copy immediately. Your contribution of 50 cents will help cover the cost of this limited edition. Large companies would do well to get a few extra copies for the new interest that will be coming in during the year. A remittance should be sent in with all orders. We hope to be filling orders about the middle of December.

## 1941 Calendar

The 1941 Calendar is exceptionally beautiful and expressive under the text "Salvation unto our God . . . and unto the Lamb." (Rev. 7:10, A.R.). The artist portrays the Scriptural symbolisms in modern detail of Jehovah's "strange work" of judgment now fast reaching a climax amongst the nations.

The date pool sets out in two colors the testimony periods for 1941, along with other field-service instruction and suggestions. The 25-cent contribution is asked for each copy, or one dollar for five copies when mailed to one address. Companies should order the Yearbook and Calendar at the same time, along with remittance. The Calendar will be ready December 15.

## Trouble Affidavits

In response to notice in the July, 1940, Informant under the heading "Mobs", brethren filed with the Department of Justice several hundred affidavits showing clear violations of Sections 83 and 52 of Title 18, United States Code Annotated.

Where local state or municipal officials have either participated in mobs or knowingly permitted mobs to assault Jehovah's witnesses, or have arrested and oppressed them, and affidavits of such occurrences have been filed with the Department of Justice, it is suggested that a letter be written now to that Department at Washington, calling attention to the violation of the above statutes as shown by your affidavit on file with the Department. In such letter you can ask the Department's cooperation and also offer to assist them in any possible way.

Specific request should be made in the same letter as to what action has been or will be taken against such officials. When you receive reply send to legal desk at Brooklyn the original or a copy thereof, together with a copy of what you wrote.

## Magazine Distribution

As heretofore outlined, the magazines Watchtone and Consolation may be placed in the hands of the people (1) by obtaining subscriptions, (2) by placing them on routes, (3) in sidewalk witnesses, and (4) in connection with the regular door-to-door work. That all four methods have been used to good advantage as evidenced by the year's report appearing in the 1941 Yearbook.

### Door to Door

There is no reason why we should allow old magazines to accumulate. I, because of rain or some other such reason, the magazine publishers are unable to get out on the street on a Saturday afternoon or night, or any other scheduled night, the magazines should be pushed in the regular door-to-door work at all times, placing them singly at 5c (or 6d), ones free as samples to be used as 'seed' for a subscription later on) or in combination with books or booklets. For instance (except during the special booklet campaign), a Watchtone or Consolation could be placed with a bound book and a self-covered booklet on contribution of 30c, or with booklets on a 10c contribution.

### Routes

As a more publishers see the end of holding and properly covering individual territories, a corresponding increase of magazines placed by the route method is apparent. And this method aids the publishers in becoming better acquainted with those of good-will in their territory and builds up their back-calls and model studies. Weather conditions will not cause placements of magazines on routes to fluctuate.

### Street Work

The street work continues to be an excellent means of placing magazines, especially with persons who would not otherwise have the opportunity of receiving them, due to living in inaccessible territories. Not only does the street work provide this additional outlet for the magazine, but, just as important, it serves as a means of public identification of Jehovah's witnesses, as well as identification of both the "other sheep" and the "goats" by their course of action toward the Lord's people. The current issues of the Watchtone and Consolation should be offered on the street; never offer "out-of-date" issues. However, in the case of continued articles, if some are left over it would be in order to have the preceding issues containing those articles in the event request is made therefor. Magazine work should be arranged for several nights during the week. Select another evening as well as Saturday.
