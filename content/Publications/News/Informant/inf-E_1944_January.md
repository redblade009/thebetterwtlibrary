# INFORMANT

JANUARY, 1944

"Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God."—Ps. 143:10

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## Continue Keeping Truths!

Jehovah's Purpose Shall Be Sustained

### January Activities Should Increase and Prosper

"There is no furlough in war." With this principle in mind, the members of the Free Nation and their associates press forward. Although having just finished another Testimony Period, proving themselves "more than conquerors," there is yet much work to be done.

The offer for the month of January is the book "The Truth Shall Make You Free" and the booklet Freedom in the New World, and 25c contribution. If your stock of this is depleted, use the latest book you have until your new supplies arrive. When making your first back-call on the book obtainers, be sure to present the Study Questions booklet and show them its advantages.

Those companies who have a supply of the 1944 Yearbook of Jehovah's witnesses, above the needs of those associated with the company, should arrange to distribute these during January to those persons of good-will who are interested in the truth, at the rate of 50c.

#### Company Organization

The servants of the company should serve their brethren. Territory should be lined up, giving particular attention to that city and business territory not already worked with the latest book. January provides an excellent opportunity to thoroughly work this, especially the business. Group witnessing should be arranged for those brethren who are unable to hold an individual assignment. Group back-call arrangements should also be made to call back within the month on those book obtainers, by the same group. Perhaps demonstration at service meetings could be given to aid this, showing the presentation of the book and the directing of their attention to pages 375-376 of the book, and then the follow-through back-call, using the Study Questions booklet.

#### Personal Publisher Organization

In order to be assured of sustaining your purpose for the month, prepare. Do not leave matters to chance. Obtain territory near your home. See that you have an adequate supply of literature and Kingdom News. Your photograph should be supplied with records and needles. Have your Testimony Card properly filled out and signed. Your Jehovah's Servants Defended booklet should be available. Get back-call slips, follow-up reports, House to House Record forms, book marks, and other personal witnessing equipment, handy for use. Do not always have to 'search' for these before you go witnessing. Keep all instruments in 'firing position' and confine your 'searching' to the territory. Further, budget your time. Be systematic and arrange for a dependable schedule

and then follow it out. Routine accomplishes the real work.

The most important part of the personal preparation is your own study, mental preparation. Be able to discuss the message intelligently. Know the contents of the publications you offer. Have clearly in mind what you want to tell the people concerning the literature, arrange it in logical order, and then present that, brief though you may be, to the best advantage. In order that you may be an able minister, always ready for defense, a thorough knowledge and understanding of the contents of the booklet Freedom of Worship is necessary; hence advance study.

This preparation cannot be done by any other than the individual Kingdom publisher, and we feel sure that each one will gladly do this ever appreciating the serious work ahead. Of course, when made, this must be carried out. However, it is first realizing the purpose, and then making practical preparation for upholding that purpose, that will sustain it. This will bring Jehovah's blessing, and the prosperity of increase will result. Concerning this, the October 1 Watchtower says:

"As Jehovah's witnesses, in obedience to his command to properly have gone to as many of the nations as they could penetrate by personal house-to-house witnessing, and by printed page, radio, telephone, and underground, our only Lord God has added to the nation. . . . The increasing of the Lord's Nation has therefore not yet reached to the populousness he has decreed for it before Armageddon finishes off the envious opposers of such increase. The increase thus far is to Jehovah's glory."

### November Report

The field service report for November, 1943, which appears below, should be carefully considered by each individual publisher as well as by the company at the service meeting. It is better than the previous November's report; however, having in mind the April peak, the report shows that there still are a considerable number who permit a month to slip by without once heeding the call for service. Each publisher should resolve to be a regular publisher and not to let a week go by without participating in some feature of the field service, even though it be only for an hour, and then report same. Did you do your part in November? What are you doing now? Make January the best!

## 90,000 Book Studies a Week!

### Conduct Your Quota of Book Studies

During the year 1943 there were, on an average, 40,000 book studies conducted every week. This is very gratifying indeed, and the brethren who conducted these studies received a rich blessing from the Lord. The coming year, 1944, promises to be far greater in this vitally important feature of the witness work. It may well be stated that book studies are the most important part of the witness work today, as it is there that the work of "free education for men of good-will" is most effectively carried on. Here is where, through His Word and the WATCHTOWER publications, the people learn the Lord's requirements and how to properly serve him. Everyone should concentrate on starting and conducting BOOK STUDIES. Let every placement and every effort in other features of the work be a step in the direction of starting a book study.

To accomplish this effectively each publisher should set a goal and strive to attain it. To aid each one in this respect the following quotas in back-call book studies for all Kingdom publishers have been set forth. The special pioneers will strive for a goal of at least one back-call book study each day, or seven studies a week; general pioneers, a goal of at least four back-call book studies a week; and company publishers, at least one back-call book study a week, during the coming year. If this schedule is kept it will mean that between 85,000 and 90,000 studies in the book "The Truth Shall Make You Free" will be conducted every week in the United States.

#### Consider 1944 Yearbook in Service Meetings

That every publisher may be thoroughly familiar with what is being done in conducting studies and may keep a check on his own activity, it is recommended that each company consider the 1944 Yearbook, pages 60-67, on "Book Studies", for at least twenty minutes of a service meeting in January. It should also be referred to from time to time throughout the year. The company servant will assign in advance some capable brother to consider this section of the Yearbook. This brother will then prepare for this portion of the meeting, and the company will be advised in advance what is going to be considered. He will arrange to bring out all the points mentioned in the Yearbook article. The Yearbook will not be read in service meetings; the entire discussion should be extemporaneous.

The progress of the local company in number of publishers conducting book studies should be mentioned and compared with the above quotas, and plans be made to bring the company activity up to the quotas. Book studies can be carried on even when it is difficult to engage in other features of the service. Some children, as well as adults, can conduct stud-

ies by simply following the questions in the Question booklet and the paragraphs in the book. Everyone can conduct book studies, and you will be amazed at how many studies you can start by merely being "study-minded". Make every statement and every placement one step in the direction of starting a book study. Merely placing literature does not free men of good-will from the bondage of religion; it requires BOOK STUDIES. It is as much your covenant obligation to the Lord to conduct book studies as it is to engage in the regular house-to-house witness work.

The quota for book studies is a righteous requirement of the Lord. If all publishers will conduct their quota of book studies, then they will be better able to make their quota in back-calls, as every individual study can be reported as a back-call each week. This should also see the back-call average increase considerably. Let us all keep our quota for studies uppermost in mind during this year and carry the 'truth that makes free' into every home possible. Remember, "90,000 BOOK STUDIES A WEEK!"

### Theocratic Ministry Library

The "Course in Theocratic Ministry" has been in operation in most companies nearly one year. The enrollment generally is very gratifying and the progress made by the individuals applying themselves to the course is very marked, and clearly shows the Lord's blessing upon their efforts. Our younger brethren not only are better publishers, but also excel in their school work as a result of this course.

One drawback that some brethren encountered was the lack of proper reference works. To overcome this, many companies have installed what they call a "Theocratic Ministry Library" at the company headquarters. This library is accessible to all the brethren at certain convenient times, usually Saturday afternoon and evening before and after magazine street work, and at other times mutually agreed upon.

This library usually contains a complete set of the current books from The Harp of God to "The Truth Shall Make You Free"; a complete set of the booklets; a file of the Watchtower and Consolation magazines (they generally take these copies from their distributors' supply); a good concordance and a Bible dictionary; as many different translations of the Bible as they can obtain; a good dictionary; some good books on composition, outlining and grammar, and any other material that will assist the brethren in their studies. These books are marked "The Property of the Company of Jehovah's witnesses". Many local brethren who have such works contribute them to the library for the benefit of the company. The Society is in full accord with and thoroughly approves these arrangements.

# "Feed the Flock" Testimony Period

## Education in God's Word

### Watchtower Campaign Begins

How fitting the name "FEED THE FLOCK" Testimony Period? For many years past, Jehovah God has fed his "little flock", and educated them in his Word, using as his instrument _The Watchtower_. In recent years that food has been extended to the "other sheep", the "stranger" outside of the "little flock", by means of an extensive offer of _The Watchtower_ to such ones. February 1944 marks the beginning of another _Watchtower_ Campaign, this time to be more extensive than ever before. The "elders" (those of the remnant), the mature "Jonadahs", and the servants in the companies will take the lead in this campaign and will "feed the flock" of God among you in your own individual territories. The tremendous proportions that this campaign will reach are well indicated by the quota the Society has set: for _The Watchtower_ alone, 1000,000 new subscriptions. So you see, brethren, it will require real hard work and organization.

### Make Full Preparations Now

That we may press forward with unbroken ranks, the company organizations and the individual publishers composing such will of necessity need to get "streamlined" for this special campaign. Company servants, each acquaint yourself thoroughly with the work to be done during this campaign, and see also that each of the servants assisting you is well acquainted with his respective duties. The servants or "elders" will first take the lead in the field, but their additional responsibilities and privileges, such as office work, must be taken care of also. The assistant company servant should have plenty of Subscription Record Sheets on hand. Also, see that plenty of _Watchtower_ and _Consolation_ subscription slips are provided for the publishers. The back-call servant will thoroughly check his files and get them in readiness for the campaign. See that each present subscriber and each back-call name in the file is given an opportunity to get _The Watchtower_. The stock servant should see that plenty of stock is on hand or, if not on hand, should arrange with the company servant to immediately order a good supply of the needed publications for this campaign. The territory servant will see that each territory in the company is thoroughly worked at least two times during the campaign. The advertising servant should check immediately upon receipt of this _Informant_ and see that an order is sent in for sufficient additional distributors' copies of _The Watchtower_ and _Consolation_ to supply the publishers with plenty of sample copies for the door-to-door work with the campaign offer. This order should reach Brooklyn by the 10th of January. Also, see that all photographs available in the company are put to use, and all records, particularly the "Instruction" (P-154) record. Push the doorstep setup.

The offer will be _The Watchtower_ for one year (at the regular rate) with a gift of a book and a booklet. Offer "_The Truth Shall Make You Free!_" and _Freedom in the New World_ first. If they have these, then offer _The New World_ or some other book and booklet. If their _Watchtower_ renewal is due, offer to renew it at the regular rate, and then offer

_Consolation_ at the campaign rate. The special offer does not apply to renew all subscriptions. The campaign offer is good for all foreign-language subscriptions, giving a subscription and a book and booklet at the regular subscription rate for such language.

### Directions

Space will not permit detailed instructions here. However, the February, 1943, _Informant_ article on "Timely Directions for Sending in Subscriptions" should be thoroughly covered during a service meeting in January.

Let every publisher walk close to the Lord and His organization and redeem the time. Withhold not the food from the scattered flock, but feed them! Use your freedom to show the ungartered flock how they can make their break for solid ground and substantial food under the Thesaurus. Faithful elders, "feed the flock"! Let all other publishers follow their example!

### Open Pioneer Territory

Today there are many people who are seeking after God, if happy they may find him, who have not yet been reached by the good news of Jehovah's kingdom. This is so even in the United States, where the ratio of publishers to the population is approximately one to two thousand. True, if all the publishers were evenly distributed, and if all the pioneer and company publishers engaged regularly in the house-to-house work and made diligent efforts to meet their quotas, the accessible population of this country would come fairly close to being covered four times in six months.

But the publishers are not evenly distributed and many are not meeting the requirements. As a result, there are sections of the country (counties and small cities) that, year after year, are not receiving proper attention. Through the "special publisher" arrangement practically all of the isolated cities of four thousand or more population have been witnessed to at least four times. But for isolated counties and cities under four thousand, the witness depends upon general pioneers.

We feel sure that there are many general pioneers who would be glad to fill this breach of isolated territory so long idle, if they had more information on the matter, particularly the location and the length of time since it was last witnessed to. Many of these counties have towns with populations ranging from 2,000 to 3,500, which general pioneers with only limited facilities could easily work. For the benefit of all who would like to respond to the call for service where the need is greatest, we are beginning, with this issue of the _Informant_, to publish a list of counties available, showing the location and the date last covered. When making request, please give two or three selections, as others may also be considering the same. Also state who will work with you in the assignment and when you will begin to work. # How to Plan a Service Meeting

### Advance Preparation Necessary

The service meeting should be one of the most interesting meetings held by a company of the Lord's people. The very name "service meeting" suggests a meeting that, above all, stresses service. It is the one meeting that applies in a practical manner the information contained in the columns of _The Watchtower_. It aids us as Jehovah's servants to effectively carry out our covenant obligations in a manner pleasing to the Almighty God and in harmony with his visible organization on earth. It is one of the most important meetings held by the company, and, for this reason, should be carefully planned in advance.

### The Central Theme

The main thing around which a service meeting should be centered is, of course, the _Informant_. All articles appearing in the _Informant_ should be carefully studied at the service meeting. They are to be discussed thoroughly, and then the information contained therein applied locally. The publishers should be shown how that information affects them as individuals and how it will aid the company as a whole in the service activities. As soon as the _Informant_ is received, the company servant should go over all the material and plan the order in which each article is to be used during the month. If the main article is one dealing with the current Testimony period, this article should be considered at the beginning of the Testimony period, so that the publishers will be thoroughly familiar with the offer and ready to start off the campaign with all the zeal and enthusiasm they can muster.

The company servant should strive for good continuity throughout the service meeting. One subject should logically lead into the one following. If at all possible the brethren assigned parts on the service meeting should get together with the company servant and discuss their assignments in advance so that they will harmonize. ### The Chart and the Poster

Another thing that should be used at service meetings is the company chart. Each month the company's activity should be analyzed, using the chart to compare your present activity with that of previous months, and your present activity with the quota your company set. Then try to increase the service record of your company. Also, posters that the Society sends out should be discussed and the important service features portrayed there brought to the fore. Here the use of good supporting scripture is invaluable. Information contained in the front of the _Yearbook_ can be utilized to a good advantage, as well as points brought out in the _Course in Tenacitax Ministra_. From time to time _Freedom of Worship_ (as outlined in December _Informant_) and _Jehovah's Servants Defended_ should be considered.

With these points in mind, and with a _careful planning ahead of time_, the service meeting will become one of the most interesting meetings held by the company and will be a real aid to the brethren to see and grasp their service opportunities.
