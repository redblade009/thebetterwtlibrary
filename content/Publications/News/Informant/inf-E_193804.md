We have come to the time that all the Lord's people have been looking forward to and preparing for during the past three months or more: to the beginning of the greatest world-wide campaign ever carried on by the Lord's visible army.

All the details have been taken care of, either in former _Informants_ or in another part of this _Informant_. Everyone, by now, should know what he is going to do, how to do it, when he is going to enter the campaign, and to what extent he is going to participate in it.

The campaign commences Saturday, April 9, the first day of "COMPANIONS" testimony period. This will be a red-letter day for all those who are on the Lord's side, the anointed and their companions. The campaign should start with enthusiasm and zeal. The first week has a number of "holy" religious days in it—Palm Sunday, Good Friday, and Easter Sunday. This is one season of the year when the Hierarchy crowd think they reign supreme and all they have to do is to sit back and gather in the shekels from their poor deluded supporters. Many persons will be sighing and crying because of the abominable conditions that exist in the religious organizations controlled by the old "harlot". It is a fine time to begin distribution of _Cure_ and get a real wallop at the religious racketeers before they get over their drunken stupor.

For practically all of us the Saturdays and Sundays, April 9, 10, 16 and 17, as well as Friday the 15th, will be the days on which we will devote most of our time to the field service. With five full days for most of us, and witnessing parties arranged for every day for those who can get out, along with the many back calls to be made, we shall get off to a wonderful start. Company publishers should easily get in 10 hours or more, and the pioneers and auxiliaries will be able to do much more.

A number of the brethren who are schoolteachers or pupils in school will have an opportunity to put in still more time, because of the Easter holidays which go into effect in most sections of the country. Those who really love the Lord will demonstrate how these holidays can be used to the glory of His name by taking advantage of the occasion to enter the field service. January 31, 1938

To the witnesses of Jehovah throughout the earth.

Dear Brethren and Companions:

A world-wide witness campaign to the name and glory of Jehovah and his King begins April 9, 1938. That campaign specially features the message of testimony of the Lord set forth in the books "Riches" and "Enemies" and the booklet "Cure". Such contains a warning from the Lord's Word to "Christendom" and a message of hope to those who are looking for consolation. The obligation to get this message to the people is laid upon everyone who has covenanted to do the will of God.

It appears from the Scriptures that the people of God are now entering upon a period of unusual importance, during which time every one of the anointed and companions must do his, or her, duty. It is my privilege and duty to call your attention to the importance of the hour and to the message of the Lord that must now be delivered to the people. All witnesses must now be at complete unity in action, dwell in peace, and work harmoniously in righteousness.

Will you put forth your best endeavors to carry this testimony to those who have a hearing ear and to give warning to others?

Never in times past has there been such an opportunity to sing forth the praises of Jehovah and speak of his glorious name as now. He has commanded all who are of his organization to rise up against the enemy in war, and our part in that warfare is to bear testimony to his name and sing the praises of Jehovah and his King. Be of good courage and do your part, and may the God of peace and righteousness shield and protect you and richly bless you as you go forth. To you the Most High speaks as at Isaiah 51:16.

The interest of the Kingdom is of paramount importance and is the only thing worth while.

With much love and best wishes,

Yours for the Kingdom interest,

President

## How to Obtain

Consolation Subscriptions

Before you can intelligently present anything to the people you must be acquainted with the article. The reason why the book _Enemies_ is going so well is that the brethren have read and studied it. ## How to Do It

In order to handle this tremendous increase in subscriptions, the office at Brooklyn will need all your co-operation. With this _Informant_ there is being sent to every company, pioneer and auxiliary a special form to use in transmitting subscriptions to the office. Please follow the suggestions set out herein.

Check each subscription slip turned in, for complete address. If not written plainly, make it clear, so it can be read here without any trouble. Be sure the street, avenue or route number is shown. If these are not filled out completely, immediately have the publisher who took the subscription and signed the slip give you the correct information. If subscription is obtained through the special offer, the company servant will receive 75c to result to the Society. The other 25c of contribution is to take care of the cost of _Enemies_ and _Cure_.

The company servant should select a capable brother or sister to check all slips and to fill in all the

names and addresses on the "transmittal form". Keep a carbon copy for your own records. Number the forms consecutively and send in a remittance to cover the subscriptions listed at 75c each, and those at \$1 you may list as "renewals". When mailing, have money order to cover, special form with all the names and addresses listed, subscription blanks to correspond with the "transmittal form", all enclosed in one envelope. These should be mailed in at least once a week, and oftener if you get more than a sheet full.

We cannot overstate the care that should be exercised in handling these subscriptions, not only on the part of each publisher in turning them over promptly to the company servant, but also on the part of the person who gathers the subscriptions and sends them in to the Society. The more care that is taken all the way through, the better the handling here in the office will be, and the fewer the complaints we shall receive. We know you will co-operate.

## New Model Phonograph

The Society is pleased to announce a new model of the small portable phonograph. The new model is very similar in every respect to the small model previously manufactured, except that it is 11/16 of an inch deeper than the older model, which permits the tone arm and reproducer to be left in the playing position when no more than three discs are being carried in the machine. There is a small coil spring in the lid of the case to hold the records on the turntable when carrying the machine. The removable tone arm feature is retained so that on making back calls the tone arm and reproducer can be placed in the bottom of the case as before, thus leaving room for carrying up to 10 or 11 discs in the phonograph.

The Society is nearly 2500 machines behind in filling orders, but we hope to be able to fill all of these orders in the next three months. All of these orders will be filled with the new model which we are now producing.

## Convention

A convention of Jehovah's witnesses and companions will be held at San Antonio, Texas, April 15-17 inclusive. Both the Spanish- and English-speaking people of the Lord will be served at that convention. The convention will open at the meeting place, 323 Matamoros Street, at 8:30 a.m. on Friday the 15th. All brethren should bring their phonographs, with the necessary records. Those desiring accommodations will please address J. D. Carter, 517 Stonewall St., San Antonio, Texas. A joint meeting of English and Spanish will be held on the evening of the 15th to celebrate the Memorial.

## What to Do When

You Take a Subscription

Each publisher, when obtaining subscriptions for _Consolation_, should leave with the individual a current copy of _Consolation_. Also leave with the party the _Enemies_ book, the _Cure_ booklet and a carbon copy of the subscription slip, which is his receipt. Sign this receipt.

The original slip, together with the remittance of 75c, you will turn in at the very first opportunity during the week to the company servant or the one designated to receive subscriptions and transmit them to the Society.

You will also make out a Back Call slip. Hold this Back Call slip yourself, so as to make a back call within a week or two with phonograph records.

Before leaving the subscriber, state to him that his first issue of _Consolation_ will come in two or three weeks. In a few words tell him about the world-wide campaign, so that he may understand that a tremendous number of subscriptions are now coming in to the office and that therefore a short delay should not cause him any concern. PLEASE DO NOT TELL SUBSCRIBERS THAT THEY WILL RECEIVE COPIES OF _CONSOLATION_ NEXT WEEK.

If the party wants his subscription to begin with any particular number, mark on the subscription slip coming in to the Brooklyn office "Begin with Issue No. \_\_\_\_." If your slip is not marked, we will mail him the current issue.
