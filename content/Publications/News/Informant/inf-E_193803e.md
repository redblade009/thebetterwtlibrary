BROOKLYN, N.Y.

## Jehovah Gives World-wide Warning to "Christendom" "Enemies," "Cure" and "Consolation", the Message

Call to Action! All to 'Rise Against Her in Battle'!!

This Siege to Continue Three Months

### Eleven Million Pieces of Literature to Be Distributed

Some months ago Brother Rutherford outlined a campaign which calls for the greatest united effort ever put forth by the Lord's anointed and their companions. This campaign begins April 9 with the "COMPANIONS" testimony period and will continue to the end of June. This is a period of nearly three months, and it includes two testimony periods, "COMPANIONS" and "BOLOGIES".

The personal letter by Brother Rutherford to the witnesses of Jehovah throughout the earth which you receive with this _Informant_ sets out the importance of this witness and shows that a warning from the Lord's Word must be given to "Christendom". Jehovah's witnesses and their companions are charged with the responsibility of giving this warning. So that this warning may be clear and understandable to the people, the literature that will be offered will be _Enemies_ and a new 32-page booklet, _Cure_, which is full of fire and contains a pointed message that every person in the world should have. The first printing of this booklet in English alone will be ten million copies. The foreign languages will greatly add to this total. With Jehovah's blessing and favor all of these _Cure_ booklets and one million _Enemies_ (or _Riches_) will be distributed in the three-month campaign. All the _Cure_ booklets must be distributed during these three months because of the special offer for _Consolation_ made on the back of the booklet, which expires June 30.

Study the _Cure_ booklet carefully. Know its contents and be acquainted with the special offer. You will then most certainly be one of the "companions" who will 'rise against her in battle'.

#### A World-wide Quota

For this three-month period, April, May and June of 1938, a world-wide quota is being set for the distribution of the Kingdom message. It is confidently expected that the greatest number of publishers ever engaging in the field at one time will share in this mighty witness. In the United States alone we expect that there will be upward of 30,000 different persons 'putting forth their best endeavors to carry this testimony to those who have a hearing ear and to give warning to others'. So that this warning and testimony may be given the world-wide quota is set at

10,000,000 Booklets
1,000,000 Bound Books
100,000 New Subscriptions

This will require a very extraordinary effort on the part of all those

fighting for God's kingdom. It means more than double anything ever done heretofore in the same length of time. For months past every branch of the Society in the world has made great preparation in announcing this campaign and seeing to it that it will be carried forward to the honor of Jehovah's name. Think of it! 10,000,000 of the _Cure_ booklet, a million of the book _Enemies_ (or _Riches_), and 100,000 new subscriptions for _Consolation_!

#### The Special Offer

As stated on the back of the _Cure_ booklet, beginning with April 9 the brethren of the Lord and their companions will offer _Cure_, _Enemies_ (or _Riches_), and one year's subscription for _Consolation_, on contribution of the amount usually received for _Consolation_ alone, which is a dollar in the United States and \$1.25 in other countries. This is the finest offer that has ever been made to the peoples of the world, and surely never before has such a powerful message of warning been carried to them.

#### Phonograph Introduction

All publishers who are fortunate enough to have a phonograph and the "Enemies" record will introduce this special offer by running this record. After persons have heard the "Enemies" record, which most forcefully shows them why they should study the book, it will be easy to present the special offer of _Consolation_ along with _Enemies_ and _Cure_ on a contribution of only a dollar. If

the people already have _Enemies_, then run the "Riches" record or some other disc that we know will arouse interest in the minds of those people who are seeking righteousness. The point is that we should use our phonographs extensively and show the people what a great advantage it is to them to have _Enemies_ and _Cure_ and to have _Consolation_ coming to them every other Wednesday.

#### Testimony Card

The Society is providing every publisher with a testimony card which sets out briefly why people of good will should take advantage of this special offer. You may find that sending the testimony card to the person first and then introducing the phonograph will be to your advantage in gaining entrance to the home. Or, if you find it to the best interests of the work to use the phonograph first, then do that. Many of the publishers will find it very helpful to direct the attention of the people to the announcement on the back of the _Cure_ booklet early in their discussion to show why the people should have _Enemies_, _Cure_ and _Consolation_. Always bear in mind that this special combination is limited to these three months. At every home offer the dollar combination. If the people do not desire to have the magazine and cannot afford to contribute a dollar, then point out the great benefits that will be theirs by their reading the book _Enemies_ and _Cure_, which you may leave on a contribution of only 25c. If they cannot take even this, and have shown sufficient interest to listen to your mes-

sage and would like to read something, then leave them the _Cure_ booklet on a contribution of a penny or anything they wish to give. If they cannot contribute, and are willing to read, and promise to read it, leave them the _Cure_ booklet free. We must leave the _Cure_ booklet in the homes of more than ten million interested persons in these three months.

#### Three-Month Siege

It is the privilege of the publishers of the Kingdom in the United States to place seven million of the ten million _Cure_ booklets printed in the English language. To place these seven million of the _Cure_ booklet is a tremendous but possible task. To do this it will be necessary to reach at least fourteen million homes. That will mean that each company publisher in the United States must call on at least 500 homes in these three months and leave the _Cure_ booklet in 200 of them. The individual quota for each company publisher is 200 booklets, 20 bound books, and three subscriptions for _Consolation_, in this three-month period. This tremendous task of distributing seven million of the _Cure_ booklet and hundreds of thousands of the book _Enemies_ (or _Riches_), and obtaining thousands of subscriptions for _Consolation_ can be accomplished in each individual takes upon himself the responsibility of doing his share of the work. The auxiliaries and the pioneers have a higher quota than that of the company publishers. Each one of the auxiliaries during this entire period will, by the Lord's grace, place 500 booklets and 75 bound books and obtain six _Consolation_ subscriptions. The full-time publishers—the pioneers and the special pioneers—have the privilege of making a quota of 1,000 booklets, 150 bound books, and 15 _Consolation_ subscriptions. If everyone in the United States attains this personal quota for three months, the quota for the United States will be attained and the world quota will be met! What an opportunity! What a witness this will be to Jehovah's name!

#### If You Have Never Been a Publisher

Carefully consider Brother Rutherford's letter to you. This letter, along with this _Informant_, has gone to more than 43,000 persons. Instructions of how to carry on the field work, as set out in the _Informant_ month by month, go to the same number regularly. This shows that there are many interested persons now reading the _Informant_ and the _Watchtower_ magazine who do not report as publishers of the Kingdom. The greatest number of publishers ever engaging in the Kingdom work at one time in the United States has been 27,004. ## Brother Rutherford Writes to Witnesses 'Entering Period of Unusual Importance'

### Letter Going to Every Watchtower Subscriber

This _Informant_, which sets out the details of the campaign, is being sent along with Brother Rutherford's letter of January 31, 1938, which is addressed "To the witnesses of Jehovah throughout the earth". A testimony card also is supplied. This letter is a great comfort to everyone who loves righteousness and who is willing to fight for the Kingdom under Christ. Because of the importance of this message from the president of the Society, it has been translated into every foreign language in which the _Watchtower_ magazine is published. All readers of _The Watchtower_ will receive this letter in their own language, and we feel sure that after considering its contents all those who are not al-

ready publishers will become such during this great witness with the book _Enemies_ and the _Cure_ booklet, and _Consolation_.

Brother Rutherford states that we "are now entering upon a period of unusual importance" and that NOW we must deliver to the people the message of the hour. Will you deliver that message? If you are not a publisher now, provision is made in this _Informant_ for you to get a supply of literature, so that you may engage in this greatest of all campaigns. If you are located in territory where a company organization already exists you will be furnished the name of the company servant, whom you should contact immediately and avail yourself of the privilege in working with the witnesses of Jehovah throughout the earth.

## Have You Studied _Cure_?

The suggestion is here offered that the companies substitute the _Cure_ booklet for _Enemies_ in their _Enemies_ study period for the next few weeks, so that all will be thoroughly acquainted with _Cure_ by the time the campaign begins. When the booklet is completed, then again take up _Enemies_ where you left off.

## Memorial, April 15 After Six P.M. Anointed and Companions to Assemble in One Place

As announced in _The Watchtower_, all of the anointed of the Lord who partake of the Memorial, and their companions who will be observers at the feast, should assemble at one place on the evening of April 15, after 6 p.m. The Society desires to keep a report on the Memorial. This report should appear on your April report card, at the bottom. Please show the following: Number of persons attending the services; number partaking of the emblems. All of those present at this memorable occasion will want to be witnesses for the Kingdom; and if you are not a regular publisher you should contact the company servant that evening or sooner, so that arrangements can be made to begin in the work.

## Something That Has Never Been Done Before

### Preparations for This Campaign

Never before, to our knowledge, has there ever been printed any publication whose first printing was ten million copies. Never before has there been a booklet released in 15 different languages at the same time for world-wide distribution. Never before has the same piece of literature been prepared and held in readiness in every principal country of the world for distribution on a given date. All of these things which have never been done before are now being done with the _Cure_ booklet. In the English language alone we have nearly completed the printing of ten million copies. My April 1 all of these will be printed. Added to this there are hundreds of thousands of copies of _Cure_ in 14 other languages being prepared for the countries where they are to be used as well as for those people in the U.S.A. who speak those languages. April 9 will be the beginning of the greatest campaign that has ever been waged against the Devil's organization by Jehovah's witnesses and their companions.

## Reports for April, May, and June

Report cards will be made out the same as heretofore for April, including the report from the first day to the last, and the report for the testimony period alone in the space provided for this purpose. At the bottom of the report each company servant, pioneer, auxiliary and special pioneer should make the following notations: Number of _Consolation_ subscriptions sent in to the Society during the month; number of back calls made during the month; and on the April report, of course, the Memorial attendance and the number of partakers of the emblems. Write it as follows:

"Consolation" Subscriptions...

Back Calls...

Memorial Attendance...

Memorial Partakers...

_Consolation_ copies should not be reported as booklets, whether you leave them as sample copies or not. Pioneers and auxiliaries may report Memorial attendance and Memorial partakers in only such cases as are isolated from a company gathering. Where a group of pioneers gather for Memorial in isolated territory, one of them should make the report for the entire assembly.
