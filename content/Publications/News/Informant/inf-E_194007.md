## "Judge Rutherford Uncovers Fifth Column"

That's the Title of a New 32-Page Booklet Released July 13

2,000,000 First Printing

To Be Distributed in 10 Days

Jehovah's witnesses in the United States have encountered an intense wave of persecution on every side. A few weeks ago, in Texas, organized mobs, formed at the instance of the Hierarchy in "get" Jehovah's witnesses, went into action. Since then magazine publishers have been stormed on street corners by mobs and run out of town; automobiles have been upset, and run into lakes; officers of the law have protected the monsters and incarcerated Jehovah's witnesses. Jehovah's witnesses' halls have been burned, people have been dragged out of their homes, many have been put in jail without trial or just complaint; no one has been allowed to see them. They have not even been permitted to communicate with a lawyer. Zone assemblies have been broken up; decrees set forth by city officials that no longer may Jehovah's witnesses meet in halls; conventions canceled, and brethren being ordered out of the state. These conditions have prevailed in many states in the Union. The same Nazi, Fascist, Catholic Hierarchy method is being used in this country to stop the witness work as has been used throughout Europe. The Hierarchy declared years ago that 1940 was their year to take America. They are trying to do it through mob action. The time has come for them to strike and they are striking. The only organization on earth today that is completely free from their influence is Jehovah's Theocratic organization.

What to Do

It is the privilege and responsibility of the Lord's people to reveal the Roman Catholic Hierarchy in all its hideousness so that reasonable-minded people of good will may be able to distinguish between what comprises the Lord's organization and what constitutes the Devil's organization and make an intelligent choice. The booklet _Judge Rutherford Uncovers Fifth Column_ is unquestionably the divinely provided instrument for this purpose.

We are not going to try to describe the contents of this booklet for you. We could not do justice to it if we tried. Read it yourself and receive the thrill of your life. Then go forward and put it out in the strength of the Lord.

## A Petition Sweeps the Country

"Locust Army" Period
Winds Up with Swift Action

It came as a shock and great surprise to all the publishers of the Kingdom that the Theocratic Convention of Jehovah's witnesses might not be held in the Ohio State Fair Grounds because of the cancellation brought about by the politicians in charge under pressure from the Catholic Hierarchy. Not only on Columbus was the strenuous opposition by the Catholic Hierarchy brought to bear, to stop this convention, but also in many other parts of the country. It was necessary to take legal action to try to counteract their influence.

Preparations for the convention are going on, however, and it is a source of real joy to all who have a share therein. Never before has there been so much opposition to the preparations for any assembly of the Lord's people. The Devil knows his time is short, and he is "out to get" Jehovah's witnesses, but, by the Lord's grace, he shall not be victorious.

When Brother Rutherford authorized a petition sent out, first in Ohio, the brethren went into the work with a zeal peculiar to the Lord's house. Mobs were formed in many parts of the state. Petitions were taken away from the brethren and torn, in some places. This, however, in no wise intimidated them; they went forward.

Following close on the heels of the petition to the State of Ohio, Brother Rutherford authorized a country-wide petition sent out. The

brethren throughout the land were thrilled. As soon as the companies received their sample copies of the petition and letter of instructions a great many of them began mimeographing forms so that they could start to work immediately and later use up the regular petition forms which were coming to them by mail.

A total of more than 100,000 petition blanks have been sent out from here, in addition to all the homemade ones. This would be sufficient for over three million names. If half of these are filled in, what a wonderful witness against the rulers it will be! Scores of telegrams and hundreds of air mail and special delivery letters have been received stating that all blanks are filled and more are needed. The results of this work have been unparalleled. It is evidently the Lord's doing, and marvelous in our eyes. You will be advised of the results of the campaign after the presentation.

The time spent in obtaining petition signatures should be counted as hours in the field.

Record of Petition Signers

As the petition sweeps the country it is encountering considerable vicious opposition from the enemy, showing that those who do sign it are people of good will.

A list should be made of all petition signers and placed in the company's back-call file. All of these people will want to hear the series "Religion as a World Remedy" as soon as it is available. Therefore make a list of the names of all petition signers for that purpose.

# 100,000 Subscriptions by Convention Time! One Thousand New Pioneers by Convention!

## Can You Make Your Quota of Books?

### New Booklet Will Help on Your Booklet Quota

The special offer of a year's subscription for _The Watchtower_ magazine, a bound book and a _Refugees_ booklet, on a contribution of \$3.00 ends July 23. The importance of the people's having the literature now, of all times, cannot be oversupplied. This is one of the reasons why the publishers of THE THEOCRACY are anxious to do everything in their power to persuade as many people as possible to subscribe now. We realize that all people who love righteousness and hate lawlessness must quickly take their stand on the Lord's side if they are to receive his protection in Armageddon; for, as _The Watchtower_ and the physical facts clearly indicate, the 'strange work' is rapidly coming to a close. It is only by studying the _Watchtower_ magazine and kindred publications that they can get the needed life-giving knowledge. There is no other source.

All the 50,000 and more publishers of THE THEOCRACY should, during the remaining days of the _Watchtower_ Campaign, earnestly push this special offer. Between now and convention time each publisher should make it his or her business to get one more subscription, and so help us to go over the top of our worldwide quota of 100,000 new subscriptions. Even while distributing the latest booklet, _Judge Rutherford Cuccovers Fifth Column_, keep your 'eyes wide open' and grasp every chance

to make known to all these who show unusual interest in the message the special _Watchtower_ offer. Many additional subscriptions can be so obtained, if the brethren will only be on the alert.

We are behind in our world-wide quota of subscriptions, bound books and booklets. Each company and publisher is aware of what must yet be done to reach the quota set for the company and himself. Therefore every publisher should strive hard to get at least one more subscription and to place many more bound books and particularly _Refugees_ booklets. **CONCENTRATE ON THE HOUSE-TO-BOUND FIELD SERVICE, MADE YOU AS MANY BACK-CALLS AS YOU CAN.** Company servants should organize evening witnessing groups with the _Refugees_ booklets, and, after July 13, with the new booklet. Arrange to quickly cover all the territory that has not yet been gone over with this special offer. By all means leave a _Refugees_ booklet or one of the new ones in every house where they will read it.

The enemy is doing everything it can to break up the witness work. But, by the Lord's grace, the work is going forward, and with added zeal and power! This is the time to set aside everything that can be humanly ignored and to turn one's full attention and devotion to serving Jehovah and the people of good will—and to arrange to personally attend the convention at Columbus or nearest convention city.
