"My heart shall rejoice in thy salvation."—Ps. 13: 5.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## Jehovah Pours Out Rich Blessing

Conventioners Overjoyed by Brother Rutherford's Talks

### President Releases New Book "Religion"

#### Enemies' Bluff Failed

##### Publishers Return Home with Renewed Vigor

Jehovah in his goodness and mercy poured out upon his people a blessing to overflowing, a blessing all of which could not be contained by his grateful people. The Theocratic convention of Jehovah's witnesses came to a conclusion Sunday night, July 28, but what occurred at the convention will never be forgotten; for a witness began there which will be carried to the ends of the earth. Brother Rutherford was richly blessed by the Lord and, because of Jehovah's favor, was able to come to the convention and address the assembly at Detroit and 17 other convention cities. It was a most joyful occasion to hear the instruction of the Lord brought to us, and the climax of the convention came when the president of the Society delivered the lecture "Religion as a World Remedy". How true it was, as Brother Rutherford stated, he had to present both sides of the argument because of the failure of the Roman Catholic Hierarchy to put up their man to talk in favor of religion!

Religion received a real beating at Detroit, and the exposing of that wicked organization continues as the dirt of that wicked organization is brought out and made plain to the people.

##### New Book Released

The companions, alongside of Jehovah's witnesses, cheered and applauded with real joy the presentation of the new book _Religion_ by the president of the Society. This was one of the most thrilling moments of the convention. There is no question in the minds of the brethren who attended the Theocratic Convention of Jehovah's witnesses that it was the Lord's will that his people assemble this year in Detroit at the Convention Hall and the other convention cities tied in by direct wire. The Lord's hand was manifest from beginning to end in all the preliminary arrangements, in trying to find a place for the convention, and in all the detailed activity of the convention itself. Even as the Lord has directed his organization for many years and continues to do so, so did he direct what should be accomplished at this convention.

##### Bluff Failed

It was a glorious fight from start to finish. The enemies of The Theocracy manifested their violent oppo-

sition to the convention soon after it was announced, and did everything they could to prevent the assembling of Jehovah's people in the Theocratic convention; and they continued to howl until the close of the convention, but showed they are afraid to come out in the open to carry out their boasts. It was a tremendous witness against them and for The Theocracy, Jehovah gave the victory!

_The Messenger_ will set out the full report of the activity leading up to the convention and what happened at Detroit and the other places, and this will be read with real joy by all the publishers of the Kingdom.

Suitless to say here is that the Theocratic Convention of Jehovah's witnesses was arranged and directed of the Lord, all to his praise. Jehovah's servants were refreshed and strengthened and they have returned home with renewed vigor to serve in the interests of the Kingdom and assist the other sheep to find the place of safety. (For details, read _The Messenger_.)

# "Judge Rutherford Uncovers Fifth Column"

## September Will See More Distributed

The first barrage of this new booklet was laid down just prior to the Theocratic convention. This was nation-wide and so thorough that it took away the enemy's breath. They had desperately tried to stigmatize Jehovah's witnesses by excusing them of being the fifth column in this country, and then they set their demonized hoodlums to develop and execute mob action against these publishers of the Kingdom. They felt they had things pretty well their own way and had Jehovah's witnesses on the run, and then came the withering blast of two million Fifth Column Uncovered booklets widely distributed from coast to coast in less than one month, exposing who are the real fifth column.

The evidence in support of the argument contained in the booklet was indisputable. The facts supporting the charges made were so clear and forcibly presented that there was no getting around it. This message in the hands of the reading public was a terrific blow to the Hierarchy. The effect of it upon the Hierarchy can be approximated by their desperate attempt to stop Jehovah's witnesses

at the Theocratic convention and ever since; but, by the Lord's grace, they were not permitted to succeed. The Lord has still kept the door open so his people can continue in the service. Let everyone, therefore, avail himself of the opportunity of participating in the service with greater zeal than ever before.

### Keep Going

Let the enemy know they have not heard the last of the Fifth Column Uncovered booklet, by any means! Two million more booklets run and will be distributed during the month of September! Leave one of these booklets in every home where the people will read it. Leave one with each placement of Religion. Where they cannot take Religion, drop to this booklet, on any contribution; and where they want to read the booklet but cannot contribute, let them have it free. Our slogan is, Two million more Fifth Column Uncovered booklets during September!! It is apparent that the Hierarchy does not fear Jehovah's witnesses but they do fear and hate the hard, irrefutable truths presented in the booklet. Let them have more of it.
