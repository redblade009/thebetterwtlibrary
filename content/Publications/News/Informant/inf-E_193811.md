# INFORMANT

NOVEMBER, 1938

'Be not afraid: the battle is God's.'—2 Chron. 20:16.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## 'Vindication' Books for 'Faithful and True' Period

### Thrilling Offer! 'Vindication' 1, 2, 3, on Contribution of Only 35c

#### Every Publisher Should Place at Least One Set

##### Warning Also to Be Offered

##### Shall We Have 35,000 Publishers???

Jehovah has revealed to his faithful and true witnesses, in the publications _Vindication_ 1, 2, and 3, his glorious universal organization; also the wicked and cruel organization of Satan, and his (Jehovah's) purpose soon to utterly annihilate Satan and all his religious cohorts, that all people who love righteousness may know that Jehovah is the only true and almighty God. Therein is disclosed also the grave responsibility of the anointed and their companions of new widely warning the people of this impending destruction, and the urgent necessity that the people of good will ride in the chariot of the Greater Jehu.

By the most gracious provision of the Lord, we are privileged to offer these three books on a contribution of only 35c, thus bringing this message well within the reach of all people who love righteousness. Also, during December, if you have no _Vindication_, you may offer _Preparation_ and _Warning_ on a contribution of 25c, dropping to _Warning_ alone on a 5c contribution if they do not take books.

Necessarily now the obligation is laid upon all the anointed and their companions to bring this unusual offer to the attention of as many people as possible throughout the 31 days of December. Each company should set as its goal the distribution of all the _Vindication_ books in stock and as many _Preparation_ and _Warning_ as possible. Make it at least one set of _Vindication_ per publisher.

##### Offer Most Seasonable

This wonderful offer comes at the most appropriate time of the year. It is during December that the people are 'Christmas'-shopping-conscious,' so called—they are on the lookout for the best bargains that they can get as presents for their friends and for themselves. This false sense of 'good will toward men', which has been foisted upon them by the religionists, can be used to their advantage by bringing to their notice an offer that they cannot duplicate elsewhere. _Vindication_, Books 1, 2, and 3, on a contribution of 35c cannot be best!

##### Use Own Stock

Most of the companies have a plentiful supply of _Vindication_, _Preparation_, and _Warning_; so there will be little need of companies' ordering for this campaign, except _Vindication_ to make complete sets. The stockkeeper should arrange to have an exact count of this literature on hand as of Sunday, November 13, and post this information so that all publishers may know what

is to be put out. This information is to be kept up from week to week until the campaign is over. The company servant should see to it, by the time of the last service meeting of November, that all the publishers have on hand at least one or two sets of _Vindication_, some _Preparation_, and a good supply of the _Warning_ booklet. All should be fully armed to start this campaign December 1 and place all of them by December 31.

##### Pioneers and Auxiliaries

Pioneers and auxiliaries located in company territory should arrange to draw on the company stock of _Vindication_, _Preparation_, and _Warning_, ONLY. The company servant is hereby authorized (during this campaign only) to transfer to pioneers and auxiliaries as much of this stock as they can use, on credit, on their word that their account is in good condition. At the end of December, that proper adjustment may be made on the different accounts, the company servant is to send in a statement signed by himself and the pioneer or auxiliary showing the literature so transferred. This ONE NOTICE, covering literature turned over to each individual, will be all that will be necessary to send in to this office. It only causes confusion if both parties notify this office of the transfer.

##### Isolated Pioneers and Auxiliaries

Pioneers and auxiliaries not located in company territory, as well as those companies that have but a limited supply of this campaign literature, should send in their orders at once to Brooklyn for whatever _Vindication_, _Preparation_ and _Warning_ are required. We will promptly arrange to fill your order from our own, or, if possible, from some nearby company stock. Brethren whose

accounts are in poor condition should accompany their order with remittance to cover.

##### Territory to Be Worked

During December the brethren should return to their urban territory, to the large towns and cities. The territory servant should immediately line up and assign such territory as can be best witnessed to in cold and inclement weather. The purpose should be to aid the publishers in calling on as many people as possible during the winter months.

Additionally, the back cell servant should arrange for the publishers to call on all the interested ones on his list with this special _Vindication_ offer, not only that they can take advantage of this opportunity for themselves, but that they can also be encouraged to get a set for their friends.

##### Carry Your Phonographs

Introduce the phonograph as quickly as you can. Run records 'Relief' and 'Resolution', P-151, P-152. Briefly present the _Vindication_ offer, pointing out the need of studying them at this crucial time. If they cannot take these books, endeavor to leave _Warning_ at least. Those brethren who because of infirmity or for other reason cannot carry along sets of _Vindication_ should stress _Preparation_ and _Warning_ on a contribution of 25c or _Warning_ alone on a contribution of 5c.

Where there is interest manifested, arrange for a back call for the 'Warning' and 'Violence' series. The every means at your command to aid the interested people in recognizing that Jehovah is supreme and that their safety lies in aligning themselves under his organization. The sound servant should see that all phonographs are in working condition and in use and that the brethren have record P-151, P-152.

## 'Warning' Going Well! Don't Stop!

### Our Work During November

In another part of this _Informant_, the 'FAITHFUL AND TRUE' testimony period, during December, is outlined. While the booklet _Warning_ will be offered in connection with the bound books and should be left at every call, it will be impossible to cover a large territory, due to handling more literature, and it will be necessary to concentrate on getting this booklet into as many houses as possible before the December campaign opens.

Therefore we will continue throughout the month of November to offer the _Warning_ booklet in the people on a contribution of 5c, and leave the booklet in quantity of 6 on a 25c, 13 on a 60c, or 26 on a \$1.00 contribution, with those who show

particular interest and desire to have a share in this important work.

'Warning' is not just the title of another booklet; the booklet contains an ultimatum from the Lord to the Fascist-religious totalitarian crowd and an invitation to those of good will to serve God and Christ the King and live. The obligation is laid upon Jehovah's witnesses and their companions, the Jonadabs, to deliver this message—an obligation that cannot be side-stepped.

Continue to use the new record, 'Relief,' 'Resolution,' P-151, P-152, in the regular house-to-house witness, which will materially aid you in arranging for studies in the homes of the interested ones. At these back calls, use the 'Warning' and 'Violence' series of records.

##### December Report

On the December report card, in the column 'Books' is to be shown the total number of _Vindication_ and _Preparation_ (as well as any other books) that have been placed during that month. In addition, the companies and auxiliaries are to show at the bottom of their December report card the number of complete sets of _Vindication_ placed, by stating there, 'Placed ... sets of _Vindication_,' and giving the number. All companies and auxiliaries giving this information will receive a credit on their account of 25c and 13c a set respectively. If this information is not on the December report, it will be assumed that the company or auxiliary so reporting is desirous of helping the Society to bear the burden of this special reduction. As the cost to pioneers is already very low, no credit will be given on sets placed at this special rate.

##### Warriors All

This 'FAITHFUL AND TRUE' period should see all the soldiers of the Kingdom wielding the sword of the spirit to the honor and praise of Jehovah and his King, Christ Jesus, and to the continued perplexity and pain of the religionists and their Godless allies. This period should start us well on our way toward our goal of 35,000 publishers as well as clear the decks for the great battle to be fought during the world-wide campaign of January-April.

# “Face the Facts” Going to 12,000,000 Homes

### Greatest of All Witnesses

It is not the purpose here to go into the details of the next world-wide campaign, when the booklet _Face the Facts_ will have a tremendous distribution, but merely to make announcement of that campaign so that you may prepare, arrange territory and good-will names, and get ready for the offensive battle ahead. _Face the Facts_ is to have the greatest distribution of any message, and, by the Lord’s grace, this will be accomplished by his chosen people and their companions.

#### Time

On January 1, 1939, everyone will be prepared, world-wide, to carry to the people of the earth this message and will press forward, even though it is winter in the Northern Hemisphere when the campaign begins. It will continue until April 30, by which time spring will have begun and we shall be able to wind up the campaign with a tremendous rally. The four months of the campaign will include two testimony periods; the one usually held in February, and the other the world-wide testimony period.

#### The Offer

_Face the Facts_, a 64-page booklet, will contain the lecture by that title as well as “Fill the Earth”, the speech delivered by Brother Rutherford at the Royal Albert Hall on Saturday, September 10. In the latter speech it is pointed out that more information about Jehovah’s mandate to righteous creatures to fill the earth is supplied in _The Watchtower_. It is essential, then, for the lovers of righteousness who read this booklet to have access to _The Watchtower_. Therefore the offer will include the _Watchtower_ magazine. In fact, it will be a special offer: _Enemies, Riches_, or any other bound book written by Judge Rutherford, and the booklet _Face the Facts_, will be left with the people free when they subscribe for _The Watchtower_ for a year. Only $1.00 in the U.S.A., or $1.50 in foreign countries.

Companies, pioneers and auxiliaries, by now, have thousands of names in their files. All of these persons will be called on with _The Watchtower_, one of the bound books and _Face the Facts_. It is hoped that during these four months 100,000 new subscriptions for _The Watchtower_

In every home where the people are interested and will read, they should be left a copy of _Face the Facts_ on contribution of 5c or less, or even free if they are unable to contribute. Such contributions will make it possible to print more. This message must go far and wide. Where persons are really desirous of reading but their circumstances will not permit them to make even the small contribution of a penny, leave _Face the Facts_ with them and arrange for a back call with the records.

#### Sound Equipment

During this world-wide campaign with _The Watchtower_, _Face the Facts_ and a bound book, records will play a very important part, because many back calls will have to be made. It is therefore essential that every company have on hand a good supply of records, particularly the ones announced in this _Informant_, which records are “Face the Facts”, “Fill the Earth,” and “Fascism or Freedom”. Everyone in the theoretic organization will appreciate the real need of preparing for this, the greatest of all campaigns, and by the Lord’s grace it will equal the last previous campaign of this kind and surpass it. It will mean real work, hard work, and a progressive fight, and therefore we announce at this early date a few of the points relative to the campaign. Your next _Informant_ will carry all the details. In the meantime we will add to all company orders a consignment of _Face the Facts_. Pioneers and auxiliaries must order their supply.

Let us make the coming fall and winter the greatest period of back-calling we have ever had to date.

## 35,000 Publishers for 1939

### New Year — New Quota

The month of October ushers in another year of service in the Kingdom for Jehovah’s witnesses and their companions, the Jonadabs, who recognize Jehovah as supreme and delight to acknowledge his new Theocratic Government, under Christ, and place ourselves under its laws. We rejoice to have had a share during the past year in heralding the Kingdom tidings, and confidently look forward to a year of increased privileges of serving Jehovah, now that the time has arrived for a better and more completely harmonious organization for the service of the Lord. He having made his approved servants peace and righteousness, and who are in complete harmony and united in action.

The two important messages from London during the recent world-wide convention and the one from New York city on October 2 have renewed within the Lord’s people, both anointed and Jonadabs, a determination to let nothing separate us from the love of God and participation in his service. With confidence, knowing that the battle is Jehovah’s and the victory certain, let us fearlessly face the enemy, alert and subject to the commands of God’s great Field Marshal, Christ Jesus.

The world-wide convention witnessed many newly interested Jonadabs taking a firm stand for Jehovah and his righteous kingdom, engaging for the first time in the actual field service, joyfully saying to those with a hearing ear: “Whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely.”

The August _Informant_ stated: “In the United States alone \[during June] there were 31,110 publishers in the field—a new peak—showing the continued increase of the multitude.” In view of the advent of Jehovah’s Theocratic Government, with many new ones now appreciating the privilege of service as sub-

subjects thereof, the peak of publishers reached in June will be far surpassed, and so a new quota is set for the fiscal year 1939, namely, THIRTY-FIVE THOUSAND PUBLISHERS. This will call for wide-awake action on the part of every true lover of righteousness in the Lord’s army of witnesses. Each publisher will determine to be consistent in service (_turning in service reports regularly and promptly_) and also to place the matter of now being servants of Almighty God in its proper light before the interested ones at back calls and at study and service meetings. This is a responsibility on all Kingdom publishers and especially company, zone and regional servants.

Companies should keep a close check on the matter of increase in publishers. Each company should have an up-to-date list of all company publishers on file. All associated with the company organization and not now publishers should be encouraged to have a share in this ‘strange work’ of the Lord now progressing.

In the strength and by the favor of Jehovah of Hosts, we will reach and exceed the new quota of THIRTY-FIVE THOUSAND PUBLISHERS!

Some companies are wondering why on October 1 the zone servant did not call on them. He will within the next few months.
