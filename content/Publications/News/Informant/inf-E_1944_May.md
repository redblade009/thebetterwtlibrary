MAY, 1944

"Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God."—Ps. 143: 10

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## "Teach All Nations"

### Booklet Distribution

#### Clean Up

#### Watchtower Campaign

The scope of the great command of the Lord forming our 1944 service calendar text for May is not limited to any particular section of the earth, but takes in the entire globe. His command to his discipline was, "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, . . . teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you." (Matt. 28: 19, 20) This command applies now to the "faithful and wise servant", the Lord's Society of his present disciples, and which Society is devoted to carrying out this command. It also applies to their companions and all people of good-will who now have a knowledge of the Lord's purposes. The Lord's favor and blessing is evident on his organized people in that great headway is being made toward the end of expanding the campaign of free education to all nations. This is clearly shown by the Lord's blessing on the course in Theocratic ministry and on the Watchtower Bible College of Gilead.

That the greatest number of persons throughout all nations may be fed from the Lord's great storehouse and taught his precepts and law, the Society has used and continues to use every legitimate means of getting the teaching work done. One means of taking the message of free education to the people is that of booklets containing the Kingdom message in concise and compact form. Throughout the country there are thousands of colored-cover booklets that have been printed since about 1829 and shipped to the Lord's people for distribution but, for one reason or another, have not been placed in the hands of the people. All of these abound in the truth that the prospective "other sheep" of the Lord must receive. These publications the Lord caused to be printed for a purpose. They should not stay in storage as they have been with many companies and pioneers for some time. No, these are to be put to their intended and proper use now. The work for May is arranged for that purpose. The Lord is no waster, and he is directing that these be not wasted by deterioration simply because there is such an abundant supply of later publications on hand. Each booklet contains its own specific Theocratic message essential to a full knowledge of the Lord's purposes. Therefore it is imperative that these be placed in the hands of the people now.

#### Combination Offer

The offer will be three booklets on a 10c contribution. These booklet combinations will, by most companies and pioneers, be made up of two surplus colored-cover booklets and the booklet _The Coming_

_World Regeneration_. The latter should be included in every combination. If you run low on colored-cover booklets, make it one colored-cover booklet and two self-covered. If you run completely out of colored-cover booklets it will be in order to offer three self-covered ones on the ten-cent contribution. If they do not wish the combination, stress the importance of their reading _The Coming World Regeneration_, stating that they may contribute a small amount for it. If they do not wish to contribute, but will promise to read it, leave it with them.

#### Assist New Publishers

May will afford a splendid opportunity for the servants, regular publishers and book study conductors to assist the new publishers, particularly those reporting activity in April for the Memorial special service week-end, and also the ones attending Memorial that expressed definite interest and wish to have part in Theocratic service. No doubt you have territory that needs covering with the new _Kingdom News_ prior to the regular current offer. Give these new publishers the opportunity of covering a section of the territory with _Kingdom News_, and point out how they have the privilege of calling back on the people in such territory a week later with the current booklet offer. Show them how to present the literature, and then suggest that they try witnessing alone in the territory. Provide them with the necessary forms for reporting: "Publisher's Field Service Report" slips, "Back-Call" slips, "Back-Call Follow-Up" slips, "House to House Record" forms, and instruct them as to the proper use of such. Also, the blank testimony cards can be furnished the very new ones, such cards being obtainable on request at the Society's headquarters in Brooklyn. Suggest the use of the phonograph and the doorstep setup if the phonograph is available.

The company servant should see that group-witnessing arrangements are made to cover territory not assigned individually and isolated urban (thickly populated) sections. The servants, study conductors and regular publishers should take the initiative and see that definite arrangements are made so that all associated and new interest desiring a part in the work will have the opportunity to participate. By special concentration, making personal affairs conform to increasing Theocratic requirements, along with the assistance that the new publishers will give in covering the territory, each territory in the company should be covered approximately one time during May.

The Theocratic ministry study is an integral part of the Theocratic requirements for the Lord's people. Every company, regardless of size, should by now be conducting the course. #### Schedules

One of the schedules is to be posted on the company information board of each company headquarters, and the other retained by the Theocratic ministry instructor in his book. # "Educators in Freedom" Testimony Period

## Another New Booklet

Jehovah is always far ahead of the enemy. Right at this time, when Satan's visible organization is straining every possible resource to train and educate thousands, yes, millions, of men and women for jobs to advance the "affairs of this old world" and to patrol the earth in future years, Jehovah has established the government of his new world and provides the "EDUCATORS IN FREEDOM" Testimony Period, designating the month of June, for the enlightenment of people of good-will who wish to live in that new world.

The greatest educational work ever inaugurated on the earth is now being sponsored by the Lord. All other educational and intellectual enterprises are destined to certain, complete and everlasting destruction at Armageddon. The efforts of educating the people of good-will in freedom will not be defeated by the enemy, but will continue on and bear fruit through out eternity. You who are privileged to be educators in freedom are not laboring in vain, as are the so-called "educators" and "teachers" of the old world, whose efforts will soon pass away and be for ever forgotten. Those who love the Lord delight to do his well as educators in freedom and will say, "I will walk at liberty; for I seek thy precepts, I will speak of thy testimonies also before kings, and will not be ashamed." (Psalm 119: 45, 46) Thus, the freedom-lover will walk at liberty through the cities, towns, villages, streets, from door to door, and every other place where he can "speak of thy testimonies" unashamed before the people of the land, as well as before the rulers.

"Religion Reaps the Whirlwind" is the title of a new attractively designed colored-cover 64-page booklet that will be released for distribution far and wide during June. This is the first colored-cover booklet published for over three years. The booklet is perhaps the most striking that has yet been printed.

There is little doubt that the message contained in this booklet will be joyously received by thousands of good-will persons. This has already been demonstrated by the enthusiastic reception given to its message at a number of assemblies addressed by the president of the Watchtower Bible & Tract Society, Brother N. H. Knorr, on his recent extended tour. Now that the Lord has provided us with such a marvelous weapon and comforting message for all of good-will, the proper thing is to make definite plans for utilizing it in the most efficient and effective manner possible to advance the Kingdom interests. Therefore, every company and pioneer should plan to get the entire consignment out into the hands of the people before the end of June.

The days are longer now and it will be possible to spend two or three hours in the evening distributing this booklet. The company servant, assistant company servant and stock servant should arrange for special service campaigns with the booklet. Stress this feature at the service meetings during this month. Emphasize the need that every publisher and good-will person have part in this all-important work.

All book-study conductors should have a supply on hand for new publishers and to supply the newly interested to distribute among their friends and relatives. This will afford a splendid opportunity for them

to get started in the house-to-house work with the booklet. Be sure to contact every party on your back-call list with this special booklet, and give particular attention to those people who subscribed for The Watchtower during the recent campaign. Call back on these people and discuss its wonderful message with them.

### The Offer

The offer for the "EDUCATORS IN FREEDOM" Testimony Period (June) will be the new booklet Religion Reaps the Whirlwind, the self-covered booklet The Coming World Regeneration, and the book "The Truth Shall Make You Free", all on a contribution of 30c. If they do not take this offer, then you may drop down to "The Truth Shall Make You Free" and the self-covered booklet The Coming World Regeneration on a 25c contribution. From there you may drop to the new booklet, Religion Reaps the Whirlwind, on a 5c contribution.

### Consignments

Consignments of this new booklet are now being sent out and all should have their supply in time to begin the June campaign. Consignments for pioneers who are associated with a company will be shipped together with the company consignment. Every pioneer is being sent 130 of these booklets. Each company's past records have been considered, and they will be sent as many booklets as it is believed they can dispose of during this campaign, on the average of about 20 to 25 booklets per publisher. These should be divided by among the publishers so that each may have a portion to distribute in his territory.

June will be a very busy month for all "educators in freedom". Let us all put our shoulder to the wheel and push harder than ever before during June, caring for our good-will interest, and unmasking religion and proclaiming Jehovah's coming whirlwind by distributing our latest provision, Religion Reaps the Whirlwind.

## Letter of Appreciation to Pioneers

With the crumbling and passing away of the present old world affairs, the service of the incoming New World of righteousness takes on ever-increasing importance and delight to those who love Jehovah. In review of six months of the pioneer activity, the Society has sent a letter of appreciation to those full-time servants who have given themselves freely to the service of God, rejoicing with them in the many evidences of Jehovah's favor upon their activities.

Hours, back-calls, and book studies have all been increased by the pioneers, in keeping with the advancement of the mighty campaign of free education now being carried forward under the supervision of our great King, and it is apparent that many more brethren are desirous of sharing in these blessings by devoting themselves to full-time service. These realize that this is not an easy way out of present world difficulties; in fact, times may be harder while engaging in pioneer action; but where else can the joy, peace, and evidence of God's favor that comes from full-time service be equaled? May Jehovah bless and guide all who contemplate devoting their entire time to His service.

# Report on "Watchtower" Campaign

## All Previous Records Shattered!

Since the inception of Watchtower Campaigns, several years ago, many thousands of subscriptions have been obtained and thousands of people of good-will have come into semimonthly contact with the Lord's visible organization through The Watchtower and thousands have also seen their privilege of direct and constant association with the organization. The 1944 campaign extends to thousands more this much-needed and vital contact.

In spite of the magnitude of past campaigns, none have approached the immensity that this 1944 campaign promises. Already, with only the February and March subscriptions entered, a total of approximately 100,000 subscriptions (English and foreign) have been put through. The two-month report in round figures reads:

|  The Watchtower | 80,000  |
| --- | --- |
|  Consolation | 20,000  |
|  TOTAL subscriptions | 100,000  |

Though this is the highest two-month figure yet, this does not mean that the quota set for the campaign has been reached. No, that quota is for 100,000 Watchtower subscriptions alone. All expectations are, however, that the quota will be more than reached when the complete campaign returns are received at headquarters.

Publishers have co-operated well in following instructions on sending in subscriptions, which has greatly facilitated the necessary office work of handling these thousands of new subscriptions. However, a number of the brethren working in cities that have the new postal zone arrangement either full to list the zone number or put down the wrong number on the subscription slip. Please watch this.

The pioneers are doing very well and have reached a new peak in back-calls.

Now that summer is near, with its good weather, longer days, and with two new booklets, vacation coming, and a few holidays to give us more time for the Lord's service, all should make definite plans for improving their activity during the coming month. Assist newly interested ones to get into the service, so that a new peak in publishers may be reached.

## Back-Call Servant

The continuous expansion of the educational activity of God's able ministers causes the duties of the back-call servant to occupy a position of much responsibility in the company organization. The gathering together or assembling of the "other sheep" with those of Zion depends to a large extent upon the faithfulness of each company in carrying forward the back-call service to acquaint those of good-will with the way to the Kingdom.

For these reasons the back-call servant must have a clear vision of the timeliness of diligent back-call activity, take the lead in this feature of service, and keep it continually before the company for its advancement. He will check the publishers' record cards with the intent of aiding all not doing back-call work to see its urgent necessity and have a share in it.

### All Essential Forms Provided

Many forms have been provided and a complete system developed by the Society for keeping the record of all interest located in the different territories. The back-call servant must see to it that these forms are utilized by all in the company. Always alert to discern good-will interest, the publisher will record the names of all definitely interested. After making the first back-call, a follow-up slip is promptly made out and given to the back-call servant. After it has been established by three back-calls that genuine interest exists, the back-call servant will then make out a permanent record slip and keep it in his permanent file. In order to see that all names are being properly cared for he will then check all the follow-up slips received each month with the permanent back-call slips on hand. If some names are not being followed up, he should ascertain the reason. If the person is still interested, the back-call servant will make all effort possible to see that the good-will person is regularly called on. The permanent slip should be destroyed only if interest no longer exists.

### Proper System of Filing

Advancement of the back-call activity will result from continual checking by the back-call servant with all study conductors and publishers in the company, and keeping all back-call information neatly and accurately filed. The first essential, then, is to get all publishers back-call-minded and engaging freely in this work, and to exercise much care in keeping the records thereof. A working back-call file will be kept with all the names located in unassigned territory, which names will be handed to publishers whenever the territory is taken out. In this connection there must be close co-operation between the back-call and territory servants. Permanent back-call slips will be filed according to territory, arranging such in alphabetical order within each territory. This will be the permanent or duplicate file for the company's records.

The whole company can be thoroughly instructed in back-call activity by frequent appearance of the back-call servant on the service meeting programs. Demonstrations can be made to illustrate the development of good-will interest from its first location until definitely associated with the organization. To assure that new interest is being properly tied in with the local organization the back-call servant should co-operate closely with all study groups.
