## Around the World

with Jehovah's Witnesses

### Happy Giving

Regardless of surrounding circumstances or conditions, Jehovah's witnesses joyously give of the truth freely. This may well be illustrated by our brothers in Taiwan, who, in spite of persecution, increased from 417 publishers in 1951 to 1,752 in 1953. What method of teaching is used? Oral teaching. Daily, taught ones happily talk to others, so the truth remains fresh and clear to them. Brothers who were privileged to donate material needs forwarded by the Society to Taiwan may feel especially happy as they view the increase there. (189, 190)

On being contacted by a witness a Methodist Sunday-school teacher in New Zealand sought the help of three clergy to defend Methodists' teachings, to no avail. After four studies in "Let God Be True" he was convinced he had found the truth and started teaching it to his Bible class. His name was soon struck from the church list, but now he and one of his Methodist friends are having their names recorded in Jehovah's book of life. (205)

When no brothers are available for servant appointments, sisters are appointed to serve and care for congregation matters. By happily giving out the truth and caring for these additional privileges, they are blessed in their efforts, as illustrated in Puerto Rico, where a congregation increased from seven to eighteen publishers. (238)

The congregation at Sao Tome in Angola has had much happiness in giving. In 1950 the congregation was started with six publishers. Now there are twenty-three publishers, all of them regular. That they appreciate the greater happiness in giving is shown by their field service report. Truly it is a wonderful report of integrity and love for Jehovah. (248)

### More Happiness

The more you give the happier you are, said Jesus. Thus the more hours spent preaching to others, the more joyful experiences one of Jehovah's witnesses has. Since pioneers and missionaries devote more hours to the ministry they enjoy more blessed experiences.

Have you ever thought while working a particular territory: "Can there possibly be some of the other sheep in this area?" A pioneer in Cameroon working a Catholic town thought that very thing. But that did not stop him, for he knew the Kingdom message must be preached. Today a former Catholic, believed by the local inhabitants to be more devout than the pope, preaches to his neighbors and a congregation flourishes in the town. (210)

Just as a person is admonished not to judge a book by its cover, so Jehovah's witnesses do not judge the inhabitants of a house or village by its size or appearance. A pioneer in Austria worked a village of 800 persons. And what has resulted thus far? Eight subscriptions, at least 15 persons attending studies he conducts and two publishers reporting 25 hours the first month. Yes, the next door may be the location of one of the other sheep. (95)

Does one ever become too old to pioneer or to share in happy giving as a congregation publisher? For a good answer turn to page 87 and read of the 86-year-old pioneer in Argentina, who, in reporting only 145 hours one month, wrote on his report "please see letter why I have not been able to make more hours." How evident this pioneer desired to share more and more in the happiness of giving!

# YOUR SERVICE MEETING

## Prepare and Use Sermons

**INTRODUCTION (3 min.)** Speaker conveys necessity for all to prepare and deliver three- to eight-minute sermons. They are easy to prepare and allow for a good Kingdom witness. They show our desire to help people understand the Bible, and the householder is given something to think about, even if no placement is made. Using sermons adds joys to witnessing. Note these points as illustrated in three different four-minute sermons that publisher gives while training another at the door.

**SCENE I (5 min.)** To householder: 'My name is Mr. —, and I'm calling on you as a friend who is sincerely interested in your welfare. I'm here to share with you the kind of information that I'm sure you will be interested in. We are living in a world that leaves much to be desired. It's a divided world, a world that winks at corruption and unrighteousness. It's a world filled with sorrow, sickness and death. Definitely it's a world in which the people are unhappy and are unable to enjoy life the way they would like. Men continue to promise that they will build a new world and eliminate these conditions. Many sincere efforts have been made, but it is now apparent that men cannot create a world where there will be no sorrow, sickness or death.

'But the Creator of the universe, Jehovah God, can and will create such a new world. Over twenty-six centuries ago, through his prophet Isaiah, he promised he would do this and told of conditions that would then prevail. \[Isa. 11: 4-9] Preferably read from Bible, or give substance of this and other scriptures used.] Seven hundred years later, to Christians of his day, the apostle Peter emphasized how such conditions would be brought into existence. \[2 Pet. 3: 13] And the apostle John, in the last book of the Bible, elaborated on the wonderful conditions that will exist in it. \[Rev. 21: 1-5] Wouldn't you like to live in that new world? This book 'New Heavens and a New Earth', which I have here, will aid you in getting vital information. Right here in chapter 1 you can learn more about the reliable Creator of the new world. You may have this copy on a contribution of fifty cents and with it this most interesting booklet.' (This sermon must be four minutes long.)

Leaving door, one being assisted, surprised at ease and effectiveness of witness, asks how sermon was developed. It is explained sermon was prepared in advance by selecting a theme and picking a few key scriptures for support. For the one just given he used chapter 1 of the book. Enlarging, he says that to quicken interest he told householder right away who he was and why he was calling. Then he contrasted man's inability to fulfill promises with Creator's assurance of a new world, shown by the scriptures used. By questions and by illustrating the use of the book, householder was shown means of gaining additional vital information.

**SCENE II (5 min.)** At second door the sermon mature publisher developed from chapter 17 on 'Sure Evidences of the End's Approach,' as set forth in the following general outline is used.

He states that he is working in conjunction with a world-wide society of ministers, calling to tell of good news of God's kingdom. Jesus taught followers to pray for the establishment of his kingdom. (Matt. 6: 9, 10) He said it would not be established then but forefold evidences that would mark the time of his second presence and of the Kingdom's establishment. (Matt.

24: 3-8) As these things were taking place on earth, the Bible, at Revelation 12: 7-12, tells what took place in heaven. With Satan cast from heaven to earth woes were to increase, and they have. With the establishing of the Kingdom in heaven and having the sure evidence from things happening on earth that we are living in the last days, we can appreciate the importance of Matthew 24: 14. To escape destruction and gain everlasting life under the Kingdom we must be acquainted with Jesus' words concerning our time and follow the course that he directed. Chapter 17 of 'New Heavens and a New Earth' will be a source of interest to householder in understanding how events today are in fulfillment of Bible prophecy.

One being assisted expresses surprise that a different sermon was used. Mature publisher explains it is unnecessary to stick to only one sermon, and suggests trying still another at next door.

**SCENE III (5 min.)** At this door theme selected from chapter 19 on 'End of the Heavens and Earth That Are Now,' outlined as follows, is used.

Minister engages in Bible-education work. Seriousness of times prompts call with information necessary for householder to make important and wise decision. Present system is corrupt and wicked, due to its god, Satan. (2 Cor. 4: 4) Righteous persons will be glad to see end of such system. God soon to destroy present heavens and earth. (2 Pet. 3: 7) Literal heavens and earth are not destroyed. (Isa. 66: 1; Eccl. 1: 4) Satan's symbolic heavens and earth to be destroyed at Armageddon. Satan and demons now influence rulers to oppose God's King and kingdom. (Rev. 16: 14-16) Christ is now judging peoples and separating sheep class destined for everlasting life and goat class destined for everlasting destruction. (Matt. 25: 31-34, 41, 46) Accurate knowledge necessary for wise decision. The book 'New Heavens and a New Earth' gives further information in chapter 15 on end of present heavens and earth and aids one to get accurate knowledge to make right decision.

Assisted one exclaims all three are different, yet effective. Experienced publisher points out how a sermon can be given in four minutes, holding householder's attention and stimulating interest.

**CONCLUSION (3 min.)** Speaker sums up advantages of each publisher's being able to make up and give three- to eight-minute sermons. They will result in a better witness, more placements, more back-calls and more home Bible studies.
