## "War of Vindication"

Will Dec. "Locust" Swarm Be 50,000?

### Government and Peace the Month Feature!

The month of December has been designated as "WAR OF VINDICATION" Testimony Period, and appropriately so; for during this month all of Jehovah's witnesses will be using their time and faculties in performing a service which will be both pleasing to the Lord and a means of vindicating His name before men. The vindication of Jehovah's name is the most important doctrine of the Bible, God's Word of truth. All who honestly believe this (and to believe means to obey), will have an opportunity of demonstrating their active belief by obeying the Lord's command: 'Preach this gospel in all the world for a witness unto all nations.'

There is no better way to do this than by bringing forcefully to the attention of the people the information that Jehovah has made available for those who desire his blessings. Such information is contained in the latest publication of the Society, Government and Peace, which booklet gives the complete text of the memorable speech by that title by Brother Rutherford at Madison Square Garden and, in addition, its companion speech "Victory", which defaiblity outlines for us, as well as for all others, our stand for The Theocracy, and Jehovah's challenge to the Devil and his hosts of wicked servants.

This month the booklet Government and Peace together with two other booklets (colored-cover or self-covered—preferably those with which we are overstocked) should be offered to every person in our territory, each being permitted to make a small contribution of life to cover the cost of printing. Those who do not have even a penny to contribute, but desire this all-im-

portant message, should be given a copy of Government and Peace. Also arrange for a back-call. In your foreign territory be prepared to offer any three booklets on the same contribution, taking advantage of the opportunity of getting out the old large-style booklets if any of these are still on hand.

During this month all publishers engaging in the Kingdom service will also be equipped with the "Snare and Racket" record and run this at every available opportunity, using the doorstep method with the phonograph. Of course, you will have some of the books Salvation and Enemies along with you. Many are impulsive due to our marches with the "Religion is a snare and a racket" placards, and so the Kingdom News No. 5 should be left in every home in your territory so that all may be informed of the Scriptural proof on the subject. Where the people are not at home, it would be well to call back on them some evening, and remind them that a copy of Kingdom News was left and that you have additional information along that line, and offer to play the "Snare and Racket" record for them. This can easily be arranged where one holds individual territory.

December, "WAR OF VINDICATION" Testimony Period, should be the best booklet month ever, because more publishers are in the field in the United States. Be sure that every effort is made in each company to 'swarm the locusts' for this "WAR OF VINDICATION". Will there be an "army" of 50,000 publishers? Five Saturdays and five Sundays, as well as the added "holidays", will enable those who are engaged in secular work to devote much time in the Kingdom service during this period.

## A New Peak in Publishers

### Did Your October Report Show an Increase?

Thrilling beyond words is the majestic onward march of JENOVAN'S THEOCRATE GOVERNMENT. The evidences of the Lord's favor on his organization are multiplying daily. That the "other sheep" are taking their stand on the side of the King is clearly seen in each month's report.

During the month of October 45,300 joyful Kingdom publishers, which includes the pioneers and company publishers in the U.S.A. and outlying countries reporting to the Brooklyn office, participated in the Lord's strange work; an increase of 12,471 over October a year ago.

The Lord describes his people today as warriors in battle array and as locusts overrunning the land, an army that cannot be stopped or turned aside. Others, who have neither the joy nor the love of the Lord, are frightened and intimidated by the enemy and turned aside because of adverse weather conditions or things of that nature; but not so with the warriors of the Lord. Instead of looking for a falling-off because of the severity of the winter weather, we confidently look forward to a definite increase in number of publishers each month and to attaining the 50,000 quota very early in the fiscal year 1940.

The Kingdom is on the increase, and nothing can stop it. Not only did

the month of October show an excellent increase in publishers, but there was also a very fine increase in the average number of hours per publisher. The company publishers in the United States increased from an average of 13.1 hours per publisher last October to 19.1 hours per publisher this year; practically a fifty-percent increase. This marks a definite stride toward our quota of 60 hours a month.

This increase in the average of hours per publisher for the month of October also explodes the theory of some who contend that increase in number of publishers means a decrease in average of hours. The Lord says that 'his people shall be willing in the day of his power'. The Jonadala enter into the work now with a fire and soul for Jehovah and his kingdom that is irresistible, and with them "time" means something to be used to the glory of the Lord and the advancement of the Kingdom.

How does your local company chart compare with these results throughout the United States?

## 1940 "Yearbook of Jehovah's witnesses"

It would be well for all company organizations to have two or three extra copies of the Yearbook on their shelves so that when the newly interested come to the meetings and want to know more about the Society you may offer them a Yearbook, on a contribution of 50c. Publishers making back-calls on people with whom they have placed the regular books, and The Watchtower and Consolation, should take along on these later back-calls a copy of the Yearbook, and place that too.

This is a book that should be used every day in the week by the publishers, because it contains the text for each day and comments on the same. No home in which there are publishers for The Theocracy should

be without the Yearbook. Order it now through the company servant. The 50c contribution for each copy covers the cost of this edition. The Yearbook and Calendars will be ready for shipment about the 15th of December.

The report by the president of the Society, certain to thrill the heart of everyone who is for the Theocracy, is set out in the 1940 Yearbook of Jehovah's witnesses. You will rejoice in reading about the most successful year of the proclamation of the Kingdom. Without question, the people of good will who are not already publishers, upon reading this report, will take a definite stand for The Theocracy.

## Calendar for 1940

Very impressive is the picture on this year's Calendar, which on careful consideration gives the observer much to think about. The year's text, "My heart shall rejoice in thy salvation," is one directly connected with our work at the present time, the privilege we have of distributing the Salvation book, that all the people of good will may do as this text sets out.

The Calendar pad this year is unique in that the name of each testimony period is set out clearly on the pad, on the month of the period. The other months have each

a very appropriate text which supports our general activity throughout the year.

Every home in the country where there are publishers for the Kingdom should have one of these calendars, so as to keep before their minds the importance of the Lord's work during certain times of the year. The Calendar is 25c; five Calendars for one dollar when mailed to one address. Company servants should obtain orders from all publishers and send in one blanket order, for the convenience of the Society, along with remittance to cover.

# January a Bound-Book Month Are You Ready? Have You Supplies?

## Most Unusual! 3 Bound Books on a 35¢ Contribution

To follow the December campaign, 'WAS OF VINDICATION,' the Society has arranged for a wide distribution of bound books in January, 1940. These will be placed on a contribution of 35¢ for any three of the Society's bound books from _The Harp of God_ to _Jehovah_ inclusive.

The brethren will recall our experience last year in the distribution of the three books _Vindication_ on a similar contribution. Let us profit by the experiences of that campaign. At that time almost all companies and pioneers underestimated the result of the campaign (many of them running out of their _Vindication_ stock before the campaign was half over; others, when it was two-thirds over), and very few of them had enough stock on hand to last them until the end of the campaign. There were many contributing causes which are familiar to the brethren and servants in each company; therefore all should watch and see to it that they do not make the same mistake again. To do so would show negligence and indifference toward the Kingdom interests committed into our care.

To the enlightened child of God it is clearly evident (from the ballyhoo in the press, on the radio, and from the religious and political publishers everywhere) that the people are perishing, not for bread or for water, but for the hearing of the word of the Lord. The books we have to offer to the people contain Jehovah's life-giving food for a perishing world. We are obligated before the Lord to raise us a standard for the people, and, at the contribution on which this combination is offered, the Lord has made it possible for practically everyone to obtain at least one combination. Let us, therefore, by the Lord's grace, accept this grave responsibility which Jehovah has placed upon us and go forward with zeal and thanksgiving carrying this life-giving food to a perishing world during this campaign.

### The Offer

All of the Society's bound books from _The Harp of God_ to _Jehovah_ may be offered during this period, 3 on a contribution of 35¢, 6 on 70¢ contribution, 12 on \$1.40 contribution, etc. Foreign-language books are also to be used in this campaign; any 3 to be offered on a contribution of 40¢, 6 on 80¢ contribution, etc. Note that _Globes_, _Enemies_ and _Salvation_ are not to be included in this combination offer.

The enemy is closing down on the Lord's work throughout the earth. In America we still have the privilege of service. Let us, therefore, work while it is day; for the night cometh, when no one will be able to work. Each individual publisher, each company servant, zone servant, and regional servant, should start planning and preparing for this campaign now, each in his respective position seeing that every necessary detail is taken care of. Pioneers also, in their field, are to participate in this campaign, so that on January 1 the entire organization will roll into action as one man.

We learned from the _Watchtower_ Campaign, last January to April, that if an offer is attractive enough and is presented with zeal and conviction the people will take it. Therefore there should be no backwardness or timidity about presenting a

35¢ combination to the people, but it should be done with the conviction that what you have to offer to them is far more valuable than any amount of money they could possibly contribute for it.

During the much-underestimated _Vindication_ campaign last December we placed 290,516 bound books. With proper advance planning and preparation, with from 10,000 to 15,000 more publishers in the field all averaging about 6 hours more each month. It would not surprise us if that figure were almost doubled this year. 'According to your faith be it unto you.' Let each one do his part with faith and confidence, and the Lord will give the increase.

### Details

On November 13 the Society sent a letter to each company and zone servant regarding this campaign. This letter should be read to the company. In addition to other things, it contains a basis for carrying on this campaign. The following figures are recommended: 12 bonus books for each company publisher. That amounts to one combination a week for the month. This is a very reasonable expectation and should be attained without difficulty. This estimate applies to all companies large and small. Detailed instructions regarding the manner of obtaining the stock, and other details, were also contained in this letter of November 13. The instructions therein should be followed out carefully by all the servants. In addition, the company servant is responsible for checking up at the last service meeting in December, seeing to it that each publisher has the required combinations, territory and other details arranged for, so that all can start right into the work at the beginning of the month.

During this period individual territory adjacent to the publishers' houses is particularly recommended, and, for group work, territory in the vicinity of each study group. The study conductors should arrange to take the study group out at least once each week in this territory in addition to the individual witnessing done by the brethren themselves. By working such convenient territory repeated back-calls can be made at any time on those who obtain the literature and manifest interest. Arrange for back-calls on each one obtaining a combination. The real purpose behind leaving the literature with the people is to have them read it. Therefore, call back and see how they are progressing, and show an interest in assisting them, and the advantage of having a model study. Arrange for such model studies. In fact, the procedure should be that at each door where the publisher comes there is a prospective obtainer for the combination; each obtainer is a prospect for a back-call; each back-call is a prospect for a model study; and each attendant at the model study should be viewed as a prospective active publisher and should be invited to the company meetings as soon as possible.

The back-call servant should see to it that all interested ones on his files are called upon with this special offer. This is a work that new publishers can participate in without difficulty. Assign some names to them. The people they call upon are acquainted with the work, in sympathy with it, and will receive their gladly. The person should be given the advantage of this special offer because of previous interest.

## How Companies Are to Get Their Books and Rebate

As set forth in detail in our letter of November 13, the companies with insufficient stock are to get the books they need from any near-by company which has an oversupply. This should be done at once, and the Society should be notified of such transactions on the regular Transfer of Stock blanks, which blanks are to be duly signed by the respective company servants. The blanks are then to be sent in to the Society and an adjustment will be made on your accounts. Only one blank is to be sent in covering each transaction; do not send in two notices about the same transfer; otherwise, duplicate charge and credit will be made.

However, if there is no company near by to supply the books needed, then send in your order immediately to the Society for the 'total quantity of books' needed. That is, say, order 120 books, and leave it up to us to send you whatever books we find it

convenient to ship to you from the nearest company or depot. Of course, if the company has broken sets of _Vindication_ and _Light_ books, you should order specifically such books and quantities as you need to match your sets. Whatever you do, be sure to order plenty of books and to send in your order without any further delay. It should be on the way now.

A rebate of 25¢ a set of three books, English or foreign) will be given to each company for the sets placed and properly reported on the January field service report card. At the bottom of the company's January report card show the total number of sets of three books placed: 6 books placed, 9 books, and 12 books, placed. The company account will be given a rebate from the information so shown on the January report card; and this is the ONLY WAY in which the company account will be credited. So make out your January report card correctly, and thus avoid unnecessary correspondence and work.

## The Pioneers' Part in the January Campaign

Many of the pioneers during the _Vindication_ campaign barely got going before they had to stop, due to the fact that they didn't supply themselves with sufficient _Vindication_ or that they got started too late. Notwithstanding all this the pioneers averaged 53 books each during that campaign. Knowing that there are sufficient books for all, and looking forward to the pioneers' starting January 1, we strongly urge the pioneers to immediately check their stock and send in their orders to the Society for what additional books will be needed. Surely it is reasonable to expect the pioneers to average at least one combination of three books a day. You should order accordingly.

Pioneers should send in their orders as soon as possible after receiving this _Informant_. Where there are two or more pioneers working in the same city, they should arrange to send in their orders together, in the same envelope, with instructions to send the orders to one address. ## How Publishers Are to Get Their Books and Rebate

The publishers are to get their books for the January campaign from the stockroom at the usual cost. Each day the publisher engages in the service he should make out a Publisher's Field Service Report slip, showing on the bottom of the report slip the number of 5-book combinations placed at 35¢. The publisher is to turn in these reports to the stockkeeper or treasurer, who in turn will give the publisher a rebate of 25¢ (either in cash or in literature) for each set of 3 books reported placed. Each report slip so handled is to be signed by either the stockkeeper or the treasurer, to show that a rebate has been made. These report slips are to be kept on file for two months or until such time as it seems certain all the publishers are satisfied they have received proper rebate for the sets placed and reported.

The company will be reimbursed for such rebates by credit on its account. The sets reported placed, and shown on the bottom of the company's January report card to this office, will determine the credit allowed.
