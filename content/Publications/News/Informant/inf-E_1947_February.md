# INFORMANT

FEBRUARY, 1947

"Oh praise Jehovah, all ye nations."—Ps. 117: 1, A. S. V.

BROOKLYN, N. Y.

## "God's Truthfulness" Testimony Period Campaign Enters Second Month

### All of Jehovah's Servants Eager to Uphold His Truthfulness

Soon the issue of truth versus error will be settled forever with a smashing victory for truth. Jehovah the God of all truth will vindicate himself and all those who have stood for his truthfulness. Those who have been deceived by the lies of God's enemies will be confounded, but those who have been faithful in the proclamation of God's Word as truth will be justified with him, and, additionally, they have the blessed assurance that they will share Jehovah's ultimate triumph. (See Romans 3:4, our service text for February.)

The privilege of sharing in this glorious fight is not a future matter, something that can be put off or attended to whenever we "feel like it". Right now is the time to vigorously defend God's truthfulness, and during February those who really see and appreciate the issue will have an active part in the present mighty _Watchtower_ Campaign.

### Present the Full Offer of 2 Subscriptions and 2 Bound Books

Early reports show that the two magazine subscription offer of _The Watchtower_ and _Awake!_ is being accepted very readily. Subscriptions for most magazines are very expensive these days, but when a year's subscription for two valuable magazines is presented at the very nominal sum of \$2.00, and two truth-packed books, such as "_Let God Be True_", "_The Kingdom Is at Hand_" or "_The Truth Shall Make You Free_", given free as a premium, people can easily see that it is not a commercial scheme. Thus the publisher has the opportunity of showing that it is actually part of the free educational program that Jehovah is conducting throughout the earth to point men to the highway of life. So do not feel that you cannot place the offer. Present it with enthusiasm, and you will be surprised at the results. Where a subscription cannot be obtained, always endeavor to leave the new book "_Let God Be True_", so that the way may be paved for holding a book study in the house of those accepting it.

### Follow-up of Interest Will Bring Big Increase in Back-Call and Book-Study Activity

The need of aiding those who obtain _The Watchtower_ to understand its contents is clearly manifest. It is not a story magazine, to be skimmed through lightly. Understanding of its contents requires diligent study and, in many cases, assistance by those who know God's truthfulness. Just as Philip willingly explained Isaiah's prophecy to the Ethiopian, so Jehovah's Christian witnesses will gladly aid the

"other sheep" by arranging home studies in _The Watchtower_ and "_Let God Be True_", as outlined in the _January Informant_.

Through this means thousands more studies will be started and back-call activity will increase. The distribution of _The Watchtower_ and "_Let God Be True_" is laying the foundation for a tremendous educational program. Shortage of publishing material may in time affect the quantity of literature we have to offer, but the need for personal study and application of the truths learned will actually increase as other conditions in the earth become worse. Be zealous in assisting these new students to become associated with God's people in joining the efforts to uphold his truthfulness.

Our Share in Attaining the Quota
A quota of 200,000 subscriptions for _The Watchtower_ and 100,000 subscriptions for the _Awake!_ magazine has been established for all the publishers throughout the earth. Without dividing this amount proportionately between the various countries, we will all diligently strive to do our full share, trusting in the Lord as to the result. If the company has territory not worked with the subscription offer in recent years, special effort should be put forth to see that all such territory is covered during the present _Watchtower_ Campaign.

Keep the present offer foremost in every feature of Kingdom activity, at the doors, on the streets, to acquaintances, on back-calls, at studies; wherever you are, try to get people interested in having both _The Watchtower_ and _Awake!_ come to their home in weekly magazine service. Above all, look well to the spiritual needs of all those who are searching for truth. Feed them by establishing home studies, and go farther by extending the welcome hand for them to come and join you in maintaining Jehovah's true worship.

## New Feature Added to Theocratic Ministry School

### Written Reviews on Bible and "Equipped for Every Good Work"

The 1947 _Yearbook of Jehovah's witnesses_ not only gives eloquent testimony to the great expansion work being carried on in all parts of the earth, but also reveals that there is a tremendous work yet to be done. That each Kingdom publisher may have a still greater part in this expansion work the Lord has provided the Bible handbook "_Equipped for Every Good Work_". And so that all may get the greatest possible benefit from the study of this new instrument arrangements have been made for written reviews. For the first twenty lessons of the book the following procedure will be used.

## All-Time High in Company Publishers Reached!

### Circuit Assemblies to Stimulate Further Progress

Topping off a year of expanding praise to Jehovah, the highest number of company publishers ever reporting, 66,210, together with thousands of pioneers, united in extending gladness to the nations. Most remarkable is the fact that over 12,000 more publishers were active in service during December, 1946, than during December a year ago! A total of 69,876 publishers shared in the work. What a fitting manifestation of the Lord's blessing upon our inviting "nations" of goodwill to "be glad, with his people"! We have real reason to rejoice and press forward with increased determination to expand in Kingdom preaching during 1947.

To what may this manifest blessing of our God be attributed? Nation-wide, servants in companies in disastrously planned and put into effect arrangements to aid new and weak publishers to see and taste the gladness of serving Jehovah. Simultaneously, 144 servants to the brethren, and in many cases their wives, spending one week with each company, large and small, gave practical assistance in _field publishing_ to hundreds of irregular publishers, "inactive" and other goodwill persons, in addition to those publishing each month. The newly arranged servant to the brethren activity with its emphasis on field publishing has the evident favor of the Lord, and will continue to stimulate many to better and more Kingdom service.

Mainly responsible for the new high in company publishers, however, is the support and whole-hearted effort given to the field during the "Nations' Gladness" Testimony. Period by the individual publisher. It was you, the publisher, who faced inclement weather and other obstacles to go from home to house, preaching the good news. It was the individual publisher who made back-calls and carried on book studies, and then took such interested persons into the field that they might make others glad by active service. And this concerted,

determined endeavor of hundreds of publishers has had its effect in the attainment of an all-time high, with increased praise to Jehovah. The new interest who have begun to publish will need attention during the months to come. Let's give it to them regularly and unite toward further increase while inviting, "Oh praise Jehovah, all ye nations."

### Circuit Assemblies to Forward Expansion

Contributing to further expansion are the circuit assemblies, which started country-wide January 3-5. Enthusiasm runs high over these assemblies, where the same spirit of gladness and unity prevails as did at Cleveland. Publishers show keen interest in the activity of other companies and the circuit as a whole and are alert to suggestions for improving various features of the service locally.

The well-planned service meeting and Theocratic ministry school have proved instructive to all. The extensively advertised public talk "Blessed Are the Peacemakers" has been attended by hundreds of responsive strangers. But Saturday evening's circuit activity meeting takes first place as the assembly's most outstanding feature. In Florida, Michigan, Washington, D.C., Iowa, Tennessee and New Mexico fishermen, miners, lumbermen, farmers, factory laborers, business and professional men, housewives, and pioneers, brethren from all walks of life, united to discuss the common problem of vital concern to all: Kingdom interest in their circuit. They wanted to talk over and compare their problems with others, to discuss local activity in an informal, Theocratic manner, to offer suggestions for consideration. And this they have done, to the delight and cultivation of all assembled. Moreover, many made notes of the constructive counsel from the district servant and have been moved to expend greater effort during the next six months to magnify Jehovah's name as one indissoluble people. Much practical aid and a real blessing were missed by some few weak companies by not being represented. These assemblies are especially designed to assist such. The resulting benefits should motivate every company to be fully represented at every assembly.

The individual publisher's effort, the servant to the brethren work, and now the circuit assemblies, are all receiving Jehovah's rich blessing. Let each one give thanks to Jehovah and be moved to greater service activity during the coming year, so that ever-increasing praise from all nations will be given to Jehovah.

### Foreign-Language Awake!

Beginning in January, 1947, the German and French editions of _Consolation_ changed to _Awake!_ The Hollandish _Consolation_, which was announced available in the _January Informant_, has been discontinued, due to paper shortage in the Netherlands.

### Written Review Procedure

Every fifth week, instead of oral review, instruction talk and student talks, there will be a written review on the material covered in the previous four lessons. This will be a closed book review, which means that the students will not be permitted to consult any notes, the Bible or the textbook. It will consist of some twenty-five questions printed on a form provided by the Society and will also have space for the writing in of the answers. A copy for each one attending the school as well as a set of answers for the school servant will be sent a month in advance to the school servant in care of the company servant. These questions are to be strictly confidential until the time of the review, and no one but the school servant should have access to the answer sheet until after the review has been held.

A half hour will be allowed for the written review, after which the papers will be collected by the school servant. He will then read to the class the questions and the correct answers. ### Lessons on Bible Books

After the first twenty lessons, under the heading "Preparing the Way for Bible Study", have been studied, the student speaking assignments will be based on the book of the American Standard Version Bible covered in the lesson for the evening. The instruction talk will feature the facts regarding the book, such as authenticity, by whom written and when, time period covered, the theme of the book as a whole, as well as touch on the outstanding events or truths of the book. It should not be necessary to do any research outside of what appears in the Watchtower publications.

The student talks will be limited to the actual contents of the chapters within the assignments, and no reference should be made to what a previous speaker may have said or a subsequent speaker may have to say regarding other parts of the book under discussion. # April 5, 6 to Witness World-wide Memorial Activity

## Plan Now for Special Service, Start of New Public Meeting Series

To Jehovah's people earth-wide the celebration of the memorial of our Lord's death is the most significant event of each year. It is the one celebration yet binding upon the Lord's people, and has been enhanced since the second advent of the Lord and his coming to the temple. Jehovah's and other witnesses and their companions in every company or unit will assemble locally to observe the Memorial, Sunday evening, April 6. It is appropriate that all unite in a week-end of extensive witnessing activity in fulfillment of their commission.

So that a real world-wide witness may be given in recognition of the purpose of the Memorial the Society is outlining a special two-day program of service for April 5, 6. This is to be put into effect by every company in the world. Saturday, April 5, should be marked by all-day house-to-house witnessing and advertising of Sunday's public talk. There will be no circuit assemblies held this week-end; each company or unit will organize for service locally. Many newly interested persons may express their appreciation by distributing handbills. This evening everyone will assemble for study of The Watchtower, the issue scheduled for study the following day. This study will be followed by a 30-minute service talk outlining the company's summer activity, particularly as it pertains to the rural work during June, July, August and September.

Sunday morning, April 6, house-to-house service will continue, using the literature offer and public meeting handbills. Invite all persons of good-will to attend the Memorial service that evening, as well as the public talk. The afternoon talk will be the first of the new series, outlines for which will be supplied with the March Informant. Every company should make plans now for this series, with servants to the brethren aiding in making advance preparation. If no speaker is available, the book to develop it.

Proper preparation for the written reviews on the books of the Bible, beginning with Lesson 21, will require that each one read through the entire book from the American Standard Version Bible prior to the giving of the instruction talk on that particular book. This would mean, then, that by the time the textbook has been completely covered each one will have read through the entire Bible. By each one's reading the book of the Bible as well as the lesson in "Equipped for Every Good Work" beforehand, making note of the outstanding points in both, hearing the instruction and student talks on it at the school, partaking in the oral review the following week, refreshing his mind on the high points during the week before the written review, he should become well acquainted with the contents of and the facts regarding God's Word of truth, and thus be "perfectly equipped for every good work".
