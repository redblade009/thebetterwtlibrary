## Display Conduct Worthy of the Good News

¹ Our fine Christian conduct is a recommendation for the good news. Do we not represent the established kingdom of God? As representatives of God's glorious kingdom, may we all conduct ourselves worthily of the good news, so recommending it to others. "Only behave in a manner worthy of the good news about the Christ." (Phil. 1:27; 2 Cor. 6:3-7) As we reflect credit upon the good news by our actions and our speech, we do well to examine the progress we have made in advancing the good news thus far during the Watchtower campaign.

### Our Subscription and Bible Study Goals

² With the Watchtower campaign entering its third month, publishers should have obtained at least one subscription, pioneers ten and special pioneers fifteen. If any of us have not reached our goal yet, we would do well to reconsider the many helps we have for obtaining subscriptions. They are: Being out regularly in the house-to-house ministry; having a schedule that will enable us to spend ten or more hours a month in the ministry; being well acquainted with the sermon "Will Sincerity Alone Please God?"; remembering that The Watchtower is available in sixty-six languages, when we meet a householder who does not read English; making effective use of the information on page two, "The Purpose of 'The Watchtower'"; being diligent to follow up subscription promises and expired-subscription slips. Many publishers have found that offering the Watchtower and Awake! subscriptions together has met with success. ### Help Them to Meetings and Memorial

⁴ Since March is Memorial month, it is most fitting that we concentrate this month on aiding all interested persons to get to the meetings. How can we really expect a new one to be zealous in the ministry if he does not attend meetings? Only by going to the meetings can new ones be spiritually alive and so want to conduct themselves worthily of the good news. Servants and others will call on all inactive and irregular publishers and those who need encouragement. Help them from the outset of the month, inviting them to attend congregation meetings. Put forth special effort to invite them to Memorial on Saturday, March 28.

Then help them to keep on coming to meetings by encouraging them not to miss the stimulating special talk on April 5, "What Will 'God's Kingdom Come' Mean to You?" Especially do we want interested ones to attend the service meetings during the month so they will know what they can do to share the good news with others during March and April. All of us who regularly attend meetings will want to help others attend also so they will be anxious to share in the ministry and be spiritually alive in April.

⁵ So it would be well for all publishers to think of interested persons to invite during March, encouraging them to attend Memorial and the special talk, as well as the other meetings. To help us, the accounts servant will list on pieces of paper the names and addresses of all subscribers shown on the Subscription Record form since January 1963, giving the names to the publishers who obtained the subscriptions. In addition to subscribers, there are undoubtedly many others we would like to invite to the regular meetings and Memorial and the special talk.

⁶ In harmony with our special goal of helping all to attend meetings, there is something we should give serious thought to: Can we help others with transportation? Let us think now of those interested persons or brothers who do not have autos or easy access to public transportation systems. Are some unable to get to meetings simply because they live on the outskirts of the city? Are there elderly persons who find it difficult to use public transportation? Be alert to give help. It will take some of our time, yes, but is not this display of love in providing transportation conduct worthy of the good news? Indeed it is!

# CONGREGATION ORGANIZATION

## Progressively Aiding New Ones (Part 1)

1 Will you have the blessed privilege of starting a new Kingdom publisher in the service in April?

2 A powerful stimulant to starting new ones in the service is right association. Right association is encouraging and mutually strengthening. Why not put forth an extraordinary effort during March to accompany the newer ones to the congregation book study, the Watchtower study or other congregational meetings?

3 One of the first things to determine in inviting a new one is this: Is he willing to share the good news? Does he have the desire to do so? In short, no one should serve under compulsion, for each one should know what he is doing, why he is doing it and want to share in it to Jehovah's honor. Moreover, the new one should be morally clean and in a fit position to represent the Most High God. (Please see "pt," par. 51.)

4 When should young ministers turn in a field service report? First, when they understand what they are doing, and second, when they

show a desire to do that work because they know it is Jehovah's will and are able to make their own presentation in some feature of the service, even if just with the magazines. The Society feels that once a young minister begins to report, his parents or the one aiding him should be willing to assume the responsibility to progressively aid him so that he will be regular in the service and make further progress toward maturity.

5 When aiding new ones it is a good idea to reflect on our own initial efforts in the ministry. How did you feel when you started out in the preaching work? Did you appreciate the loving aid offered you by the one who trained you? Were you pleased that he did not keep you out too long when you first went in the field service? Were you grateful for the help offered in preparing the sermon, even the simplified sermon or a magazine presentation? Were you not made happy when commended even for small things? Just as you appreciated all these things, the one you are progressively aiding will likewise appreciate them.

## Shoulder Your Responsibility —As a Young Publisher

1 Are you one of the more than 65,000 publishers in the United States under fifteen years of age? How happy we older ones are to be associated with you in the New World society! How marvelous it is to see your devotion, your zeal, your good example!

2 One of the big problems that confronts youthful ministers is: How can I be regular in the service, meet the goals set by the Society and still keep up with my schoolwork? The answer lies in having a schedule for weekly service. Many of our younger publishers have been aided by their parents in this respect. And what splendid examples in zeal and faith there are to copy! Think of Samuel, Jeremiah and Jesus, to mention just a few. (1 Sam. 2:18, 19, 26; Jer. 1:4-10; Luke 2:40-50) What made these youths outstanding? The fact that they listened to the counsel of Jehovah and paid more than the usual attention to it.

3 A good way to be introduced to the ministry is in the magazine work. Many youthful publishers are placing three or more magazines each week and are reaching the goal of twelve magazines per month. Some set aside an hour or so each week to engage in the magazine work after school hours, under the direction of a mature brother. Have you tried this yet? Perhaps overseers can make such arrangements in the congregations, if advisable. After training is received in magazine work, help can be obtained in giving the simplified sermons. From this point it is only a small step ahead to give the full sermon.

4 And what about conducting home Bible studies? An ever-increasing number of our youthful publishers are finding it possible, with good parental training, to conduct studies with their schoolmates in the Paradise book, and some have been aided to get the truth in this manner. You younger publishers, are you responding to all the fine training offered by your parents? Also, do you have vacation pioneering as your goal from time to time as well as advancing on to the regular pioneer work, missionary work or Bethel service?

5 How true the statement is, "Train up a boy according to the way for him; even when he grows old he will not turn aside from it." (Prov. 22:6) In this connection, the Society encourages all you young ministers to fortify yourselves for the future by accepting God-given responsibilities now, and may Jehovah bless you richly for this.

## WHAT HAPPENED HERE IN JANUARY?

The second-highest number reporting for this service year! That is our fine January report. It was indeed good to see 290,488 share in the preaching work.
