## Walk by Faith amid Materialism's Deceptions

¹ As we begin the new 1964 service year this month, all of us will want to determine to continue improving our service to Jehovah. We are better equipped to do so now than we were a year ago. Not only have we had one more year to progress toward maturity, but on September 8 we will have concluded the stimulating Around-the-World Assembly. How this has enriched us spiritually! Yes, Jehovah continues to provide what we need to aid us toward further growth in the ministry so we can continue walking by faith amid the materialistic deceptions of this old world.—Matt. 13:22.

### Objectives for New Year

² What is our primary objective for the new service year? It is to take advantage of every opportunity to praise Jehovah and make known the "good news of the kingdom." All publishers are encouraged to arrange their schedules to shoulder their individual responsibilities as ministers. Included in this is the aiding of others so they too may progress toward Christian maturity. Is there a publisher in the congregation who would be strengthened and helped to grow to maturity if you took a personal interest in him, perhaps helping him to improve his personal study and encouraging him to comment regularly in meetings? Of course there is! Lovingly take the initiative to offer that help. Servants will be glad to offer suggestions on where the most good can be done.

³ 1964 can be another year of fine increase, as the brothers return to their congregations encouraged by the recent assemblies, eager to put into practice the things they have learned. All publishers will be interested to know the 10-percent goal for increase in their congregations for the coming year. The new chart will be put up at the end of this month and will show this suggested congregation goal. Take a

personal interest in it. Know what your congregation is working toward and then work constructively to help someone with whom you are studying to be included among those who start in the service this year.

⁴ The summer is almost past and children will be going back to school. This means that many sisters will have time to vacation pioneer. Also, with people back from vacation, Bible studies discontinued during the summer months should be resumed. Call on all who were studying before the summer and who, for one reason or another, stopped the study. Their study of God's Word is vital if they are to meet Jehovah's approval and be put on the way to life. In addition, try to start new studies with other interested ones. Let each one of us be Bible-study conscious and endeavor to conduct one or more studies regularly during the 1964 service year.

This meeting, which can be held before or after a regular meeting of the congregation, is for the purpose of seeing that all is well organized within each service center so that each one there will receive the needed assistance. Among the points to discuss that will aid the publishers are the following: to see that meetings for field service are functioning as suggested, that midweek activity is arranged for the con-

### Ten-Hour Goal

⁶ Another goal that should receive attention is that of devoting a minimum of ten hours to the field ministry each month. Every publisher who can will want to arrange a reasonable schedule for this. The goal is surely within reach for many who have not yet attained it, especially when we consider that it means just two and a half hours a week in the ministry. Much fine assistance and encouragement can be rendered by servants and study conductors who will patiently and lovingly help newer and weaker ones to progress toward the goal. They will keep in mind that each publisher has his own routine for the week, and what may be a practical schedule for one may not be so for another.

⁷ Many publishers are already devoting more time to the ministry than the suggested ten hours, and that is fine. Wherever possible, work diligently toward that reasonable goal. Where it is not possible because of infirmity, old age, family responsibilities or various hindrances, remember, our service to Jehovah is acceptable when we give what we can with the available strength we have. Earnest service, even though limited by circumstances beyond our control, is pleasing to Jehovah, as was the widow's whole-souled contribution of two small coins. (Luke 21:1-4) However, while it may not be possible for some of us to grow in the quantity of our service, we still want to grow in the quality, increasing in our devotion and integrity.

## SECOND MEETING IN SEPTEMBER

5 min: Introduction, text and comments.

7 min: New World News.

18 min: Question-and-answer coverage of main article, "Walk by Faith amid Materialism's Deceptions."

20 min: Resuming Bible Studies Stopped During Summer Months.

CHAIRMAN (3 min.) Bible studies increased nicely January through April. During summer months, however, many have stopped due to vacation, children home from school, and publishers being away to assemblies. With things settled down now, assemblies and vacations drawing to a close, children back to school, all should make concerted effort to start studies stopped during summer.

SCENE I (5 min.) Bible study servant approaches sister inquiring why no study report was received last two months. Reasons given: Both families on vacation, children home, unending visiting relatives, etc. Goodwill person decided to stop study during summer. Too, sister attended assembly and took vacation. Servant asks when she plans to resume study. Sister replies person of goodwill said she would call when ready, has not yet called. Servant explains publisher should take initiative, as student does not have been appreciation we have nor does student realize full importance of study. Perhaps her interest has cooled somewhat and must be rekindled. Sister agrees, will call her on phone and suggest, since children are now in school and vacation period is over, why not study again. Servant suggests personal visit would show loving concern for individual and sincere interest in wanting to continue study, would be more effective than phone call. Sister sees value, asks what she would say. Servant suggests she build up on a business for study by sharing assembly highlights, attendance, points that stood out, newspaper clippings, showing some of the releases, etc. Emphasize how assembly stressed importance of Bible study. Get on theme last being studied, review a few highlights, suggest householder get Bible and, that simply, study is resumed. Sister elated over suggestions, says she will try them. Bible study servant desires to know outcome.

CHAIRMAN (1 min.) Following these suggestions, we will be able to resume many studies that have been discontinued.

SCENE II (6 min.) Sister makes call on goodwill person. Renews acquaintance and incorporates suggestions given above into presentation; scene concludes with study being resumed.

CHAIRMAN (2 min.) Studies have fallen off during summer months. Do your utmost to rekindle interest and resume study. The responsibility of assisting sheeplike persons to grow to maturity by regular Bible study is as important as finding them.

10 min: Concluding comments. (Cover "What Happened Here in July?" and appropriate announcements from Announcements column.)

## Dedicated Publishers, Shoulder Your Responsibility

¹ Having dedicated our lives to Jehovah and the doing of his will, there is only one direction in which we want to go and that is forward. Having received a commission from God, each one of us should have an overwhelming desire to fulfill it. That this duty falls upon each one individually Paul indicates when he says, "Each one will carry his own load."—Gal. 6:5.

² Before us lies the new service year with all its grand prospects for expansion. What an appropriate time not only to determine how we can individually shoulder more responsibility but also to make definite plans for doing so! What should those plans include? For each member of the family it should mean participation in door-to-door work with effective sermons and sharing fully in magazine work so that the territory is covered adequately and the meek are given the opportunity to hear. Further, it becomes our grand privilege to call back on all interested persons found and cultivate this interest so that Bible studies can be started. Finally, it means conducting Bible studies with a purpose; teaching so that accurate knowledge is imparted and new ministers begin serving.

³ Does shouldering your own responsibility mean receiving no help or encouragement? Not at all. In fact, assisting others is an additional responsibility to shoulder. How happy we should be to receive loving assistance from our brothers from time to time! Therefore, always welcome the assistance of the circuit servant, the district servant, the congregation servant and others in regard to making up a practical schedule.

⁴ Undoubtedly, family heads will want to work out family schedules, and proper training of children will be cared for in conformity with 1 Timothy 5:8. Responding to this loving assistance, new publishers should determine to move out from the "babe" class as quickly as possible and expand their ministry.

⁵ All of us have real cause to be optimistic as we apply ourselves to the work ahead. Every year sees advancement and increase in the New World society, and Jehovah's blessing continues richly upon his people for progress. With this prospect before us, may we individually shoulder our responsibilities during the 1964 service year, resulting in increased praise to Jehovah and continued growth toward maturity individually.

# CONGREGATION ORGANIZATION

## Value of Congregation Book Study

¹ It is good for all of us to stop and think what a loving provider Jehovah is in so many ways. For example, consider the congregation organization. There is a central meeting place at the Kingdom Hall; yet, in addition, Jehovah has wisely and lovingly arranged for an extension of this arrangement throughout the congregation's territory. How so? By arranging for congregation book studies and service centers at convenient locations.

² With congregation book studies wisely located throughout the congregation's territory, the largest number of publishers and persons of goodwill are able to attend regularly. Each one who does so is greatly aided to progress in knowledge and to press on to maturity. Since the study groups are kept small, there is frequent opportunity to comment, to be built up and to build others up. Regular attendance at the congregation book study will also aid us to be more proficient in conducting our own home Bible studies. How is this? Because the procedure followed at the congregation book study is the pattern that each one should follow in conducting effective home Bible studies with persons of goodwill.

³ As a service center, this arrangement is without equal. Here is a location where all in the vicinity can conveniently meet for field service, and this with a minimum of time and expense for traveling. Each one associated should make it his goal to have some share in every feature

of the ministry each week. The convenient location of the service center and personal assistance by the study conductor and others made available here make this possible.

⁴ All who attend are benefited by the 45-minute public talks that are arranged every month or two. When properly organized by servants, and advertised and supported by publishers, splendid attendance has resulted. It is thrilling to bring a person of goodwill to a talk or to the study. Often it is the first contact new ones have with congregation meetings of the New World society. All in attendance at the study should make every effort to assist persons of goodwill to attend these talks. Build toward this by taking others from the service center with you to your Bible studies, thus gradually getting those goodwill persons acquainted with different ones who attend the study. This will make it easier for them to come to the 45-minute talks or to the regular studies when the invitation is extended to them. When such new ones begin attending, how important it is for each of us to demonstrate warmth and friendliness, especially toward these new ones!

⁵ With all of us appreciating Jehovah's provision of the congregation book study, we will take advantage of the benefits to be derived by our regular attendance and participation in all of its meetings and field service arrangements. Thus we will be helped to fulfill our responsibilities in the work Jehovah has given us to do. At the same time we will be helping others.
