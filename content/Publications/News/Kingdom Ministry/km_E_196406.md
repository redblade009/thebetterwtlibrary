## Maintain Maturity with Our Brothers

¹ We all know, of course, that pressing forward to maturity is absolutely essential, but equally vital is keeping our maturity. This maturity we have attained we must keep with our brothers, associating with them at meetings and in the ministry. Keeping regular in the ministry helps us guard and maintain our maturity and offers us the opportunity also of aiding new ones to press forward to maturity. You well know that to begin progressing toward maturity people need the Bible, and this month we are privileged to offer it in all seven languages in which the New World Translation is published.

² As we have already experienced, the Bible is a most pleasurable thing to offer. The New World Translation is so easy to understand, so helpful, that once people begin to realize its value, they eagerly want a copy. ## THIRD MEETING IN JUNE

**5 min:** Introduction, text and comments.

**12 min:** Talk on "Fruitage of the Spirit" Assemblies and "Showing Christian Love at Assemblies," from June 1, 1964, "Watchtower."

**15 min:** Question-and-answer coverage of "Shoulder Your Responsibility as a New Publisher."

**20 min:** Demonstration on use of "Renderings of Scriptures Compared." (Publishers should bring their copy, along with Bibles, to meeting.)

**CHAIRMAN** (3 min.) Material in "Renderings of Scriptures Compared" can be used at conclusion of sermon or in answering objections such as "I already have a Bible." In either case, the bookholder can be invited to get his Bible, if this appears to be best. Then comparisons can be made to show the superiority of the "New World Translation." In certain scriptures the "Douay" or "King James Version" may not be too different from the "New World Translation." After determining what Bible version of the bookholder has, appropriate comparisons can be made.

**DISCUSSION** (6 min.) Chairman directs audience in lively and informative discussion as comparisons are made in various texts. Three minutes can be taken in discussing those texts where the "New World Translation" is greatly superior to the "King James Version" and the same can be done with reference to the "Douay Version." Publishers may make notes.

**DEMONSTRATION** (4 min.) Capable publisher gives presentation, making comparison with "King James Version." Householder says: "I already have a Bible." Householder is invited to get Bible and attention is directed to Matthew 5:3. After reading scripture the question is asked: "Just what does it mean to be 'poor in spirit'? Are we to understand from this that having a small amount of God's spirit is a good thing? \[Let householder comment.] Notice how clear the thought is made by the 'New World Translation.' \[Read.] If 1 Corinthians 10:24 is used, the householder could be asked: "Just what do you understand from this? Was the apostle Paul telling us that we should try to get someone else's money? \[Let householder comment.] Notice how clear the 'New World Translation' is when it says: \[Read.]"

**DEMONSTRATION** (4 min.) Capable publisher shows how scripture comparisons can be used for the "Douay Version." After reading Matthew 11:12 the question is asked: "Just what do you understand by the words 'suffereth violence and the violent bear it away'? Are violent persons able to interfere with God's kingdom? Let us read this verse from the 'New World Translation' and then see if the meaning of Jesus' words is made

clear." If the scripture at 1 Corinthians 10:25 is used, the question could be asked: "Just what does the word 'shambles' mean? \[Allow time for comment by householder, but no embarrassing silence.] Notice how any difficulty is cleared up when this text is read from the 'New World Translation.' \[Read.]"

**CHAIRMAN** (3 min.) Scripture comparisons should be pasted in back of Bible and appropriate notations can be made. Which texts are used will depend upon the circumstances.

**8 min:** Concluding comments. (Include remarks on slogan, based on Galatians 6:1, at bottom of page 4.)

# CONGREGATION ORGANIZATION

## The Assistance Arrangement (Part 1)

¹ As we have experienced, Jehovah, through his organization, has always provided assistance and training so his ministers could grow to maturity and improve their effectiveness in declaring the good news. (Eph. 4:11-13) And it is true today. For several years now we have received the benefits of the training program and the assistance arrangement.

² The training program is for the more advanced publishers who can, with additional training, be assigned to train still others. (2 Tim. 2:2) The assistance arrangement is primarily designed to assist new, less experienced publishers who are not spiritually strong. It will help these publishers to become more regular and effective in Jehovah's service, mature ministers, by providing publishers to work with them consistently in the different features of the service, encouraging them to attend meetings and studying with them when necessary. The assistance arrangement, therefore, is really a steppingstone to bring publishers on to the level of spiritual development where they may benefit from the more advanced training program.

³ The overseer plays a prominent role in organizing the assistance arrangement. As he visits the service centers he should arrange for the more mature and experienced

publishers to work with those who need help and encouragement. Before visiting the group he will make an analysis of the Publisher's Record cards so he will know who needs assistance as well as those who can provide the assistance. Then he will talk to the more mature and experienced publishers, asking them to work regularly with the publishers who need the assistance, outlining the help needed.

⁴ In large congregations, where it will be some time before the overseer gets around to all the service centers, study conductors will arrange for the more experienced ones to assist those who need help. Study conductors will first discuss their suggestions for the group with the overseer before putting them into operation.

⁵ Who will be doing the assisting under this arrangement? Publishers starting out new ones in the service will not only continue their studies with them but will also want to help them in the field service. The experience and knowledge of mature publishers, pioneers and dedicated parents should be utilized in helping new publishers. Study conductors themselves will also assist weaker and less experienced ones. In each case, the mature ones taking the lead should be advised of the particular needs of the one(s) being assisted. Then they can work on improving one point at a time.

## Shoulder Your Responsibility as a New Publisher

¹ Did your career as a publisher of the good news commence recently, possibly in April? Your fellow publishers welcome you warmly into the ranks of active praisers of Jehovah's name. Now that you have taken this initial but very important step, what will your course be during the months to come? One of constant progress, assuredly, shouldering responsibility even though you may have many things to learn as a new publisher.

² First of all, learn to appreciate that you are not alone in this grand work. You have many sources of help. Reliance should be placed in Jehovah and not in self, as he points out at Zechariah 4:6, "Not by a military force, nor by power, but by my spirit." In addition to this help, the one who has been conducting the study with you will be happy to offer assistance.

³ Taking in spiritual food is an unending process and is specially vital now at this point in your preaching career. Do not recommend to your teacher that the home Bible study that has been conducted with you be stopped just because you have started in the field activity and are attending congregation meetings. Continue the study and seek to improve your own personal study habits too. You should also read and study The Watchtower and Awake! regularly along with the Society's other publications.

⁴ Association with other active servants of Jehovah is likewise essential. This means that you will want to attend all the meetings, preparing well for them and then participating in them.

⁵ Having started to serve actively in the field, you will want to preach and teach regularly from this point on, having some share in the service every week, if possible. Mature publishers have a schedule to guide them, and a weekly one is best. Schedules can be discussed with one of the mature brothers.

⁶ As a new publisher you will be greatly blessed as you shoulder your responsibility in serving God, keeping in mind that this is now a part of your worship.
