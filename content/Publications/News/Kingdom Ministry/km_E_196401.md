# CONGREGATION ORGANIZATION

## Regularly Attend the Congregation Book Study

¹ One of our most stimulating meetings of the week is the congregation book study. Usually it is here that interested persons are first introduced to meetings of the New World society. Here, too, there are more opportunities to comment, building up our faith. It is also here that many new truths are brought out from the publications. Then, too, we are given much personal encouragement to press on in the ministry, bringing about greater individual maturity. What compelling reasons we have to attend the congregation book study regularly! Yet only 76 percent of our brothers do. How can we help the more than 75,000 publishers who do not yet attend?

² Very likely our brothers not attending regularly need loving assistance to appreciate the importance of the congregation book study more fully. Perhaps a friendly word

of encouragement or, better yet, a promise to drop by and pick them up or accompany them to the study will start the good habit of regular attendance. Some brothers engaging in prestudy work have found it advantageous to call at a publisher's home and indicate they would be happy to call again in 30 or 40 minutes and go with that one to the book study.

³ And what a blessing the book study is to newly interested persons! Here they meet others from their neighborhood who are learning of Jehovah and his promises. The warm and friendly atmosphere is uplifting to them, the love shown them is heartwarming and their introduction to the New World society becomes a happy experience. Soon some are on their way to attending other meetings, sharing in the service and eventually dedicating their lives to serve Jehovah.

⁴ If we can improve our personal attendance at the congregation book study, why not do so immediately?

## Shoulder Your Responsibility —As a Family Head

¹ It has been said that good families make good congregations. How vital it is, then, to shoulder your responsibility as a family head. As such you have assigned to you the privilege and responsibility of leading your wife and children in true worship. How are you discharging this responsibility?

² As a family head, are you taking the lead in arranging for a family home Bible study? Is this conducted at least once a week so that the entire family is spiritually built up? Does your schedule also include time for group study through attendance at all the congregation meetings?

³ Essential too is family prayer. Have you taken time to explain the purpose and value of prayer to your family? Set the example at mealtimes, at meetings and in the evening before retiring.

⁴ Regular weekly field service for each member of the family is also of great importance. This calls for good family organization. First, patiently teach your children simplified sermons, how to do magazine work, and then let them gain the joy of advancing to the point of being teachers of others. Arrange a family schedule of field service to put into practice the things they have learned. Take them along with you in all features of the ministry. Your own leadership will provide them with an incentive to serve Jehovah. Regular weekly service, for at least two and a half hours a week, will see the entire family reaching the ten-hour goal.

⁵ Recently two fine theocratic families of four each reported on the excellent improvement they made in their activity after working out a family schedule and sticking to it. Both families nearly doubled their average hours. In one family each member averaged 15.8 magazines before and 30.5 after. In the other family, back-calls increased from 2.6 to 6.8. True, they were both doing well before, but what a fine increase after making a schedule! In the first six weeks these two families started nine new studies. One of the sisters reported that, even with the increased activity, with the schedule she has more time than ever before.

⁶ Even though no children are involved, you husbands should lead your wives in serving Jehovah. This responsibility is enjoined upon you when you become "one flesh" through marriage. Wisely accept and discharge this responsibility for which you are accountable to Jehovah.
