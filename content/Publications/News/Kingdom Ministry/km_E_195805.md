## KEEP FILLED WITH THE SPIRIT

### Permit God's Spirit to Work Through You

¹ As magnetic forces permeate the area around a magnet, so does God's spirit radiate from him and permeate his organization. That invisible force activates his earthly servants to be joyful proclaimers of his good news. But they cannot expect to keep filled with it unless they stay close to the organization, where its field of influence is. To remain there it is essential to keep active in the ministry. We must speak the truths the spirit has given us and permit it to work through us. "Now we received, not the spirit of the world, but the spirit which is from God, that we might know the things that have been kindly given us by God. These things we also speak, not with words taught by human wisdom, but with those taught by the spirit, as we combine spiritual matters with spiritual words."—1 Cor. 2: 12, 13.

² What is the most effective way for our spiritual words to bring comfort and hope? It is by making back-calls and by conducting Bible studies. Not only does this bring good-will persons a knowledge of the truth but it brings them under the influence of God's spirit.

³ We need to pray for them to get a mental grasp of the whole scope of Jehovah's purposes, just as Paul did for the Ephesians. "On account of this I bend my knees to the Father, in order that you may be thoroughly able to grasp mentally with all the holy ones what is the breadth and length and height and depth and to know the love of the Christ which surpasses knowledge."—Eph. 3: 14, 18, 19.

⁴ When our prayers are accompanied by zealous ministerial activity on our part God's spirit can operate through us to answer them. Make a special effort to let it operate through you during May and June. Let these two months be outstanding in your personal back-call and Bible study activity. If all publishers fol-

low the spirit's leading by engaging wholeheartedly in these two features of service we will have new peaks in back-calls and Bible studies. It can be done by calling on new subscribers and magazine placements.

⁵ Besides back-calls and Bible studies May has something special for us. It is the opportunity to "comfort all that mourn" by visiting the cemeteries on this country's Memorial Day. By means of timely issues of The Watchtower and Awake! we will be able to comfort and give hope to those who have lost loved ones.

Approach them tactfully, and speak to them with kindness. Each congregation can then send a report to the Society giving the number who engaged in this work, the number of magazines placed and any interesting experiences.

⁶ By enthusiastic activity during May and June show that you are filled with the spirit and permitting it to operate through you. Use all that the spirit provides for Jehovah's service. Use the new sermon, use the timely magazines, make back-calls, conduct Bible studies. Be aglow with zeal for Jehovah's kingdom and for his truth.

### Increase Home Bible Studies by Assuming Responsibility!

¹ As noted elsewhere in this issue of Kingdom Ministry, it is the home Bible studies that get results. Weekly study of the Bible with the aid of basic publications matures persons of good will, making them want to dedicate themselves to do God's will.

² During May and June we are aiming for a peak in home Bible studies. To attain that we must, of course, also build up our back-calls, for unless we make back-calls there will be no Bible studies.

**OFFER FOR MAY**
Two books and two booklets on a contribution of \$1.

**THEME FOR MAY**
Keeping Filled with the Spirit of God.
—Eph. 5: 18.

³ True, we are making progress. From September, 1957, to March, 1958, we have increased in studies from 140,618 to 177,584. Congregation publishers have improved during that time from an average of .6 to .7; pioneers from 4.9 to 5.8; and special pioneers from 7.6 to 8.1. While the Society has never set a

quota of studies for pioneers it would be well for each pioneer to try to have at least five different studies a week and each special pioneer no less than eight, even as each congregation publisher should have at least one home Bible study.

⁴ While the circuit servant has set apart his afternoons for back-calls and studies, many congregations are not fully taking advantage of this provision to improve and increase in these. This is a mistake and shows a lack of appreciation! Congregation servants should arrange for a full schedule so that a maximum number of publishers can work with the circuit servant and receive needed back-call and home Bible study training.

⁵ The home Bible study work is not only the most fruitful but also the most joyful part of our Kingdom ministry. There is a warm and friendly relationship between the minister and the good-will student. True the conducting of home Bible studies means responsibility, but there is so much joy and satisfaction in connection with it that the burden seems light indeed!

## FIRST MEETING IN MAY

Theme: Keeping Filled with the Spirit of God.

5 min: Welcome, text, comments.

25 min: Develop and demonstrate a model sermon. A competent brother should be used as chairman.

# CONGREGATION ORGANIZATION

## Aiding the Other Sheep Through the 'Watchtower' Study

$^{1}$ To Jehovah's people the _Watchtower_ study is the most important meeting of the week. Rightly, it should have one hundred percent support. Not only does it provide spiritual food for the little flock, but the other sheep are also invited to share in this feast. What can we do to aid the other sheep to share more abundantly in these progressive revelations of truth from _The Watchtower_ that illuminate the path of the righteous ones?—Prov. 4: 18.

$^{2}$ We must first of all build up proper respect for the unique position _The Watchtower_ has in Jehovah's arrangement. It is Jehovah's provision for supplying food at the proper time. When studying with prospective sheep from the very beginning _The Watchtower_ study should be held before them as the most desirable of all studies. Mention it often and aid them to see the value of attending so that they can get the benefit of association and of all the mature comments.

### Take Them with You

$^{3}$ Also, do not just tell them about the meeting; accompany them. It is noted that even some of the older publishers do not come to the public meeting and remain for _The Watchtower_ study. In fact, the attendance is only seventy-two percent of the total publishers associated; and since many persons of good will are

included, it is obvious that greater appreciation for this study needs to be built up. Vital is the role it plays in gaining greater maturity for all who attend. Therefore, attend regularly. Participate and do your part to create a warm and friendly atmosphere at the _Watchtower_ study so that persons of good will desire to return.

$^{4}$ The study conductor can do much to help new publishers and those newly associated to attend. He can note who among his group are missing at the _Watchtower_ study and then display loving interest by encouraging them, yes, visiting them personally, aiding them to attend the study. Additionally, at the end of the congregation book study he can, from time to time, pose several questions to be answered at the _Watchtower_ study. This will serve to whet their spiritual appetites.

### Help Them Comment

$^{5}$ Each one, including the newer publishers, should see the value of commenting. Help the newer ones to prepare their answers if necessary. Perhaps they may want to tell the conductor beforehand which paragraph they wish to comment on.

$^{6}$ _The Watchtower_ contains vital food for the other sheep. It is the responsibility of those who are more mature to direct good-will persons to this unlimited source of spiritual truths. Aid them to attend the _Watchtower_ study with you.

## Your Letterhead

A number of congregations have been printing letterheads, and they may do so if they think it necessary. Some congregations have printed letterheads with just the words 'New World Society.' This is meaningless, as it does not connect the Society with Jehovah's witnesses. It could be any kind of a society. It is suggested that, if congregations are going to print letterheads for their use, a more explicit form be followed. For example, it could read: 'New York, N.Y., Congregation of Jehovah's Witnesses, Brooklyn Heights Unit.' Then on the right-hand side of the letterhead it could have 'Kingdom Hall, 136 Columbia Heights, Brooklyn 1, New York.' On the left-hand side the meeting times could be shown, the hour and the day.

Letterheads printed in this manner advertise the name of the congregation and Jehovah's witnesses, as well as directing people to the Kingdom Hall. This has meaning and identification.

## Continue to Help New Ones

$^{1}$ New publishers that have been helped in April to go out in the service should not be forgotten nor neglected during May. Even though they have been aided in the field once or twice, they are still very new and need much training and encouragement before they become sturdy, regular, mature ministers of God. Our desire should be to build these new ones up until they 'all attain to the oneness in the faith and in the accurate knowledge of the Son of God.' We do not want them to remain babes, being tossed about by doubts and tricked by crafty men. See, then, that they grow up strong in faith, rich in love.

$^{2}$ For over three years Jesus taught and trained his apostles and disciples how to carry on the ministry. It had its effect, because after Pentecost people recognized by the apostles' outspokenness 'that they used to be with Jesus.' We should desire that our training be as thorough. Our heart should be set on developing ministers of quality, publishers that are efficient in every feature of the ministry. This takes time—sometimes many months—depending on the ability, appreciation and zeal of the new one. If we conscientiously assume our responsibility as teachers and preachers, the new publishers generally come along and do their part. So let us help them grow to Christian maturity through love.—Acts 4: 13; Eph. 4: 16.

$^{3}$ What is the best way to aid the new publisher to maturity? By gradually increasing his theocratic privileges and responsibilities. If he started to serve in the magazine work, keep up with that feature of service with him but increase his privileges by asking him to accompany you in the house-to-house work, then the back-call and the Bible study work. Teach him to give the three- to eight-minute sermon. Train him how to overcome objections and refute falsehoods. See that he continues to study the publications by studying with him.

$^{4}$ Preparing for the international assembly, no doubt, will keep you busy. But do not let this cramp your service to Jehovah. Just hustle a little more. Stay close to your studies and help them in the field. Let your advancement be manifest to all persons. 'Pay constant attention to yourself and to your teaching. Stay by these things, for by doing this you will save both yourself and those who listen to you.'—1 Tim. 4: 15, 16.

# Presenting

THE GOOD NEWS

## Essential Qualities of Sermons

Part 8: Friendly,
Conversational and Direct

¹ Jehovah's people have the most interesting subject in the world—the good news of the Kingdom. However, even though our sermon may have a good theme with excellent material, how does it appeal to the listener? Not only is an intellectual appeal necessary but we need to stir our listener emotionally. Otherwise he will not be moved to action. To achieve this the minister needs to be friendly, conversational and direct.

² First of all, we need to examine our attitude. Does each home contain potential sheep until you find out otherwise? Then do you get off to a good start with the householder? Avoid undue controversy by starting with a point of common interest. This will gain a listening ear. Further, be friendly disposed toward the people, just as Jesus was. A long face or an unhappy expression has no place with our message. Good news makes one radiate with gladness, thus showing the effect the truth has upon us. Remember friendliness often begets friendliness and has much persuasive power.

³ To make your sermons appealing never lecture to the people. Converse with them and you will get better results. Conversation, like teaching, is an art. Therefore it must be cultivated. It is sometimes defined as a

friendly interchange of views. Hence, do not do all the talking; it is only natural that the householder will want to say something. By being a good listener you may learn just what is needed to put your sermon across. Also, remember to include your companion in the conversation. He should pay attention to what is being said and not appear indifferent or preoccupied. This would detract from the sermon.

⁴ Always look directly at the person spoken to, since this will enable your facial expression to convey the proper thoughts. Helpful to such directness is proper sermon preparation. It is difficult to convince someone else of the importance of what you are saying if you are not thoroughly convinced yourself. Failure to look right at the householder may indicate lack of sincerity, shyness or self-consciousness.

⁵ Gain respect for the message by not delivering it in an apologetic manner but rather in a direct, friendly and conversational style. Use these essential qualities to make your sermons even more effective.
