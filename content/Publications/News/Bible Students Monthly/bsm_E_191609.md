By C. J. Woolworth.

The average man believes in hell, but thinks few people go there and nobody knows much about it. The Bible is the only authority in the world, and he can can know anything about it, aside from the Bible. Few know that he is a teacher that too thousand go to hell to see that stays out.

The population of the earth is 1,000,000,000 and the number of adult professed Protestant Christians is 14,000,000, or one in 100. Many of these Christians are rich, but we read, "A little Knot, a little to hardly enter into the Kingdom of Heaven."—Matt. 19:23, 24. Many are wise, influential and under-minded, but we read, "Not many wise men after the death, not many mighty, not many noble men called."—1 Cor. 1:36.

Many believing Christians will be disappointed, for the Lord will say to many men, "I know you not whence ye are; do not fear me." (Luke 12:27.) Many praying and worship Christians will be disappointed, for prophesying, casting out devils, praying, Lord, and doing many things, and for the Lord will say to all the people of the earth, and for the reward of heaven.—Matt. 7:21-25. When we consider Christ's statement that unless a man loves Him more than father, and mother, and wife, and children, and brethren, and sisters, yea, and his own life also, he cannot be my disgrace. (Luke 1:22.) And reflect that probably not one professed Christian in a hundred that reached either the standard or the other one which He set in the same chapter, that "Whosoever be he of you that forsaken out all that he hath, he cannot be My dispripe" (Luke 14:20), it should mean the willing to consider carefully what is to become of the 3,000 and of every 10,000,000,000,000 population that do not meet these conditions.
