# Religious and Scientific Gleanings

REFORMATION IN SURGERY.
"The wonders performed by surgery constantly invaders, and perhaps in its direction has the beneficence of the science been so much demonstrated as in the direction of making mental and physical cures through surgical operations. From time to time a number of cases have been reported whereby—with an operation on the head, removing some depression from the brain, or perhaps opening the skull to allow greater brain expansion—backward and vicious boys have been given normal mental and moral conditions and a fair chance in life."
"Edward B. Grimmett, when 14 years of age, received a blow on the head from a patient, which fractured the skull and left a discernible depression. He prevents paid little or no attention to the incident, but the boy grew up and from time to time showed criminal tendencies. He made little progress at school and was vicious, and was frequently the subject of police attention. After serving three terms in State's prison he was finally arrested and tried for forgery. He did not deny any of the facts, and his counsel sentenced that his criminal houses were the result of injuries to the head.

Release of Pressure on Brain Changed Course of Mace Life.
"The Judge did not place credence to the claim made in his behalf, considered him a clever professional crook, dangerous to the community, and sentenced him to the Dannenora prison. The man begged the prison surgeon to perform an operation, before he could not resist life, but could not resist the opportunity to commit crime whenever he had a chance. He preferred death to a continued career of crime, such as he felt he was doomed to have. He simply attracted the attention of an eminent surgeon to freedom, who performed the operation, relieved the pressure on the brain and changed the whole tendency of the man's thought and action, and thus having the most perverse and criminal nature prior to the operation, he has since become, to all appearances, a moral man of the highest impulses.

"It has been found that surgical operations, removing adenoids and other growths of nose and throat, allowing proper breathing has changed many cases of stupid children into bright and active ones. Crime comes ordinarily through an abnormal physical condition; visionomes is considerably the result of ignorance through the presence of abnormal conditions."—Plattsburg Sentinel.

If accidents to the skull can produce degradation of morals, who will deny that the shape of the brain of the skull is largely influenced by heredity—particularly by the mother? This agrees with the Scriptural declaration, "I was shapen in iniquity; in sin did my mother conceive me."—Psalm 15:6.

Who will deny, further, that the faith has much to do with the child's physical vitality and vigor of constitution? But additionally, he is largely responsible for the mother's surroundings and mental condition during the period of gestation. Injustice, harshness, cruel words at such a time from anybody, but particularly from the husband, would arouse in the mind of the mother grievous thoughts, sure more or less to have their impress upon the child she is carrying.

While enforcing law and order let us remember that a reign of sin and death is in progress. Let us sympathetically do all in our power to promote righteousness and to be helpful to those who are still in greater difficulty than ourselves along these lines. Not only do such thoughts give us great charity toward all, but they cause us the more reverently to look up to God as the only One competent to fully release the greasing creation. Looking into His Word we perceive that the entire Plan of Salvation for the race is along the lines of uplift from mental and physical differences to full perfection in God's likeness, and while praising the family, for this generous Plan soon to be put into operation for the world at the Second Coming of Messiah and the establishment of His Kingdom let us also note with the our own privileges—or "High Calling of God, in Christ Jesus."

# WHERE ARE THE DEAD?

OUR FRIENDS, OUR NEIGHBORS; THE HOLY, THE UNHOLY; THE CIVILIZED, THE WILE?

The proper answer to this question stands related to our own destiny, colors and influences our theology and the entire trend of our lives! The correct answer gives strength, confidence, courage, and assists towards the spirit of a sound moral!

"Men and brothers, let me freely speak to you of the pertinent David, that he is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day. For David is not ascended into the heavens." (Acts 2:20, 24.) "And no man hath ascended up to Heaven, but He that came down from Heaven, even the Son of Man."—John 3:12.

For a man to declare himself uninterested in this subject would be to proclaim himself idiotic—thoughtless. If the ordinary affairs of this present life, food, raiment, thence, politics, etc., which concern us but for a few years, are deemed worthy of thought, study, how much more concern should we have in respect to the eternal future of ourselves and neighbors and mankind in general?

Of course, so important a question has had the most profound study ever since the reign of Sin and Death began, six thousand years ago. By this time the subject should be threefold. The entire world should be so thoroughly informed respecting this question that there would be nothing new to any and nobody curious to hear. But the large advances of intelligent, thoughtful people which come to bear, and which listen with treatises interest to what we have to say, imply that, after all the study the subject heads, but few are thoroughly satisfied with their conclusions.

The Apposite Answers the Question.
Before presenting what we claim is the Scriptural and only satisfactory answer to our query, we think it but proper respect to the intelligence and thought of the day and of past centuries to make general inquiries on the subject and have before our minds the most profound thoughts of the most astute thinkers of our race. We cannot, however, go into the matter elaborately and give lengthy quotations. We must content ourselves with brief, synoptical answers, which will be stated kindly and truthfully, and with a desire not to offend anybody, however much we may disagree with his conclusions. We recognize the right of every man to do his own thinking and to reach his own conclusions, whether these agree with our conceptions or not.

We begin our examination by asking our apostle friends, who boast of their untrammeled freedom of thought, "What are you, Free-thinkers, in reply to our query, Where are the dead?" Their answer is, "We do not know. We would like to believe in a future life, but we have no proof of it. Looking the evidences, our conclusion is that man did not do so does the brute beast. If our conclusion disappears your expectations in respect to these being before the saints, it certainly should be comforting to all as respects the vast majority of our race, who certainly would be much better off perished like the brute beast than to be preserved in torture, as the majority believe."

We thank our apostle friends for the courteous reply, but feel that the answers is not satisfactory, either to our heads or to our hearts, which try out that there must, or should be, a future life, that the Creator made man with powers of mind and heart so superior to the brute that his pre-eminence in the Divine Plan should be expected. Furthermore, the brevity of the present life, it bears, its sorrows, its experiences, its lessons, will nearly all be valueless, useless, unless there be a future life—an opportunity for making use of these lessons. We must look further for some more satisfactory answer to our question.
