But let us for the moment lay aside the Bible and look at things from the standpoint of reason alone.

This who can look into the sky with a telescope, or even with his natural eye alone, and see there the immensity of creation, its asymmetry, beauty, order, harmony and diversity, and yet doubt that the Creator of these is vastly his superior both in wisdom and power, and who can suppose for a moment that such order came by chance, without a Creator, has so far lost or ignored the faculty of reason as to be properly considered what the Bible terms him, a fool (one who ignores or lacks reason). The Evolution Theory Lacks Proof.

Some who deny the existence of an intelligent Creator claim that nature is the only God, and that from nature all forms of animal and vegetable development proceeded without the ordering of intelligence, but governed, they say, by "the law of the survival of the Street" in a process of evolution.

This theory lacks proof, for all about us we see that the various creatures are of fixed natures which do not evolve in the environment; and though those who hold to this theory have made repeated endeavors, they have never succeeded either in blotting different species or in producing a new, fixed variety. No instance in known where one kind has changed to another kind. Though there are fish that can use their fins for a moment as wings, and fly out of the water, and frogs that can sing, they have never been known to change into birds; and though there are among bruise some which bear a slight resemblance to men, the evidence is wholly lacking that man was evicted from such creatures. On the contrary, investigations prove that though different varieties of the same species may be produced, it is impossible to lend the various species, or for one to evolve from another. For the same reason the donkey and the horse, though resembling such other, cannot be claimed as related, for it is well known that they offspring an imperfect and cannot propagate their species.

Surely if unintelligent nature were the creator or evolver she would continue the process, and there would be no such thing as fixed species, since without intelligence nothing would arrive at fixed conditions. # BIBLE AS A DIVINE REVELATION

VIEWED IN THE LIGHT OF HEASON.

THE Bible is the torch of civilization and liberty. Its influence for good in society has been recognized by the greatest human being, through the far the most part have looked at it through the various glasses of conflicting creeds, which, while upholding the Bible, grievously misrepresent its teachings. The grand old Book is unintentionally but worldly misrepresented by His friends, many of whom would lay down life on its behalf; and yet they do it more vital injury than its foes, by claiming its support to their long-reverse preoccupations of its Truth, received through the traditions of their fathers. Would that such would awake, re-examine their oracle, and put to confusion its enemies by disarming them of their weapons?

Since the light of nature leads us to expect a new revelation of God than that which nature supplies, the reasonable, thinking mind will be prepared to examine the claims of anything purporting to be a Divine revelation, which bears a reasonable surface evidence of the truthfulness of such claims. The truthfulness of such a revelation of God, and it does come to us with sufficient surface evidence as to the probable correctness of its claims, and gives us a reasonable hope that closer investigation will disclose more complete and positive evidence that it is indeed the Word of God.

The Bible Has Outlived the Storms of Thirty Centuries.

The Bible is the oldest book in existence; it has outlived the storms of thirty centuries. Men have endeavored to every means possible to banish it from the face of the earth; they have hidden it, buried it, made it a crime punishable with death to leave it in possession, and they must bitter to the relentless piety of nations have been waged against those who had faith in it; but still the Book lives. To-day, while many of its foes abumber its death, and hundreds of volumes written to discredit it and to overthrow its influence are long since forgotten, the Bible has found its way into every nation and language of earth, over two hundred different translations of it having been made. The fact that this Book has survived so many centuries, notwithstanding such unparalleled efforts to banish and destroy it, is at least strong circumstantial evidence that the great thing whom it claims as its author has been found to preserve.

It is also true that the moral influence of the Bible is uniformly good. Those who become careful students of its pages are invariably elevated to a purer life. Other writings upon religion and the various sciences have done good and made excellent and blessed to stand, to some extent; but all other books combined have failed to bring the joy, peace and blessing to the groaning creation that the Bible has brought to both the rich and the poor, to the learned and the unlearned. The Bible is not a book to be read merely; it is a book to be studied with care and thought for God's thoughts are higher than our thoughts, and His ways than our ways. And if these could comprehend the Plan and thoughts of the infinite God, we must bend all our energies to that important work. The richest treasures of truth do not always lie on the surface.
