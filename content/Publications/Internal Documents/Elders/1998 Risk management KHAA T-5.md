### Witnesses

Ask any witnesses to the accident to describe what happened. Have two brothers present to document the comments, questions, and answers. No one is obligated to answer these questions. The following questions should be asked: 1 What were you doing when the accident occurred? 2. Did you see the accident? 3. According to your understanding, what happened? ( Please do not ask any other questions; specifically, do not discuss who or what may have been at fault, the cause of the accident, or reference to any legal action.)
