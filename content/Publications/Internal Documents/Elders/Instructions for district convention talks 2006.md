# REMINDERS FOR THOSE ASSIGNED OUTLINE TALKS AT DISTRICT CONVENTIONS

- • Thorough preparation is essential in order to present an enthusiastic and motivating program part.
- • Not all cited scriptures need to be read. Give special attention to those that support the main points.
- • This material is to be presented extemporaneously. PLEASE DO NOT CONVERT THE OUTLINE INTO A MANUSCRIPT.—_Ministry School_, pp. 50, 166, 174-8.
- • Keep in mind that a talk given in the form of a fireside chat will not be suitable. Even the way you give a talk in your Kingdom Hall may not be suitable in a large auditorium to a sizable audience. Although it is good to be conversational, the situation calls for voice projection, sufficient volume, and good sense stress. On the other hand, guard against sounding bombastic.
- • Your talk is to be given entirely in the language of the convention. Do not revert to another language, thinking that some in the audience, such as the children, will get a better understanding of what is said.
- • When introducing scriptures, do not use innovative or otherwise “catchy” expressions such as “meet me at . . .” or “let us rendezvous at . . .” Instead, follow the guidelines in Study 20 of _Benefit From Theocratic Ministry School Education_, entitled “Scriptures Effectively Introduced.”
- • Please adhere to the material in the outline. We have purposely indicated the order of development of ideas; familiarize yourself with that order rather than changing it according to personal preference. Do not introduce outside material or personal viewpoints. Some speakers have, at times, indicated to the audience that there is an important point in the outline and that they would like to read it word for word. It is not necessary to follow such a practice. Just develop the points outlined.
- • Develop the outline in the time designated for each main subheading. This will mean not spending more than the allotted time on the introduction or in the use of illustrations and experiences.
- • Supporting material or illustrations should be in good taste and not interfere with the coverage of the main points of the outline, as they are to be developed within the allotted time. Avoid questionable illustrations that are dramatic or that might offend some listeners.—_Ministry School_, pp. 240-6.
- • Persons being interviewed should direct their comments to the brother conducting the interview. Depending on the length of the interview, prepare a number of questions. Replies should be brief, yet specific. Usually, the response to each question should require no more than 30 to 60 seconds. Those being interviewed should not be asked to offer their recommendations or to give advice to the audience. The speaker is responsible for making any application of points brought out in the interview that illustrate, support, or clarify the main point under consideration in the talk.
- • When selecting participants, be sure to check with their respective Congregation Service Committees to make sure that they are exemplary.
- • Make your participation in this convention a matter of prayer, and Jehovah will bless your efforts.
- • After you finish your part, you may be requested to report to the Chairman’s Office, as the brothers there may have some observations for you.
