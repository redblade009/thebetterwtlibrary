### STEP 1 Attach wheels to axle.

- Place the inner washer on the axle followed by the wheel, then the outer washer.
- Insert the cotter pin through the hole in the axle then bend the cotter pin back with the long-nosed pliers.
- Repeat this process for the other wheel.
