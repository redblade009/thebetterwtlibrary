# FOREWORD

_“Shining as Illuminators in the World”_ outlines a course of study for the Pioneer Service School. The school course is made up of 20 three-hour units. Three of those units allow for actual field service activity. Thus, you can put into practice the material you consider during class sessions. One extra subject is provided for use in classroom discussion if needed, as explained in Unit 1 (a).

By taking full advantage of this training and then by applying what you learn, you will become more effective in all aspects of the field ministry. You will be encouraged to continue making disciples who will be able to stand fast in the truth. As new situations arise in your life, you will be more aware of the Bible principles involved, and you will be better equipped to apply them. More important, this school will help you strengthen your relationship with our Grand Instructor, Jehovah God, and with the Head of the congregation, Christ Jesus.—Isa. 30:20; Eph. 4:15.

# Explanation of Pioneer Service School Course

CLASSROOM DISCUSSION

Education has long been a hallmark of Jehovah's people. From ancient times, Jehovah has concerned himself with teaching his servants his righteous ways. (Gen. 18:18, 19) He commanded family heads to instruct their households in his Law. Joyous conventions were arranged that included a public reading of God's Law. (Deut. 4:10; 6:4-9) During the first century C.E., Christians likewise met together and made good use of God's inspired Word. (1 Cor. 14:23-31) In modern times, "the faithful and discreet slave" has made available a wealth of spiritual food to educate God's people. (Matt. 24:45-47) This has included congregation meetings, assemblies, conventions, and various schools, such as the Pioneer Service School.

Therefore, a discussion of the information in this unit will be beneficial, both to the students and to the instructor. While considering valuable information regarding the operation of the school, you will be able to get acquainted with one another. By paying close attention to the points here outlined, you will be able to contribute to the success of the school and to enjoy warm Christian fellowship because of your mutual interest in Kingdom service. Also, the taking in of stimulating spiritual instruction should have a profound effect on your ministry in the days ahead.

Yet, his love of righteousness is balanced with hatred of wickedness. God’s love seeks the lasting welfare of his creatures, so the things that he provides are truly for their good. Even when faithful ones are experiencing adverse circumstances, his love for them will never diminish.
